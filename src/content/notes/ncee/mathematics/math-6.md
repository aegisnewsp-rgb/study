# Word Problems and Applications

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

A word problem is a short story that hides a calculation. Your job is to read the story, pull out the numbers and the unknown, write an equation, then solve it. NCEE word problems test six recurring situations: ages, profit and loss, simple interest, distance–speed–time, work-rate, and averages. All use Naira (₦) for money and standard metric units (km, m, h, s).

- **The Big Six formulas you must memorise:**
  - Simple Interest: **I = P × R × T / 100** (P in ₦, R in % per year, T in years).
  - Total Amount: **A = P(1 + RT/100)** (in ₦).
  - Distance–Speed–Time: **D = S × T** (match units: km/h with km and h).
  - Work–Rate (combined): **1/T = 1/T₁ + 1/T₂** (T, T₁, T₂ in the same time unit).
  - Mean: **x̄ = Σxᵢ / n** (Σxᵢ is sum of values, n is the count).
  - Profit%: **%Profit = (SP − CP) / CP × 100**; Loss% = (CP − SP) / CP × 100.

> 💡 **High-Yield Memory Hook:** **"Read → Write → Solve → Check"** — *Read* for numbers, *Write* an equation, *Solve* with the matching formula, *Check* that the answer makes sense (no negative age, no speed faster than the limit). For work problems, remember **"Add the reciprocals, not the rates"**: 1/T = 1/T₁ + 1/T₂.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### Core translation skills

Every NCEE word problem hides three ingredients: a **known quantity**, an **unknown** (the letter x), and a **relationship** between them written as an equation. Translate clue phrases word-for-word:

| English phrase | Algebraic translation |
|---|---|
| "5 more than x" | x + 5 |
| "twice as old as A" | 2A |
| "5 years ago, A was x" | A − 5 = x → A = x + 5 |
| "shared in the ratio 2 : 3" | parts are 2k and 3k |
| "at the rate of ₦200 per kg" | total cost = 200 × kg |
| "left in 2 hours" | remaining = total − distance covered |

Money always works in **₦ and kobo**, where **100 kobo = ₦1**. If your answer ends in kobo, write it as ₦x.yy (two decimal places).

#### Concept Comparison Matrix — easily confused pairs

| Looks similar | Actually different | How to tell them apart |
|---|---|---|
| Profit % vs Profit margin | Profit % is on **cost price (CP)** | Margin is on **selling price (SP)** — NCEE always asks % on CP |
| Rate vs Reciprocal of rate | Rate = jobs **per hour**; 1/rate = hours **per job** | In work problems, you add the **reciprocals** of the times |
| Simple Interest vs Compound Interest | SI uses P only; CI uses last year's amount | NCEE Mathematics tests **Simple** Interest only at this level |
| Average vs Total | Average = total ÷ count | "Find the average" → divide; "Find the total" → multiply average × count |
| Speed in km/h vs m/s | 1 m/s = 3.6 km/h | Convert **before** using D = S × T, never after |

#### Key steps for setting up linear equations

1. Let x = the unknown quantity (state the units clearly: "Let x be Ade's age in years").
2. Convert all clue phrases into expressions in x using the table above.
3. Form the equation from the equality given in the problem.
4. Solve: isolate x using inverse operations.
5. Substitute back and **check the answer against the original story**.

#### 🎯 Exam-Level Worked Problem

**Question:** A trader bought 12 cartons of drinks at ₦2,400 each. He sold each carton at ₦2,800. Find (a) his profit and (b) his percentage profit to the nearest whole number.

#### Solution:
(a) Cost Price (CP) per carton = ₦2,400.
    Selling Price (SP) per carton = ₦2,800.
    Profit on one carton = SP − CP = ₦2,800 − ₦2,400 = **₦400**.
    Total profit on 12 cartons = 12 × ₦400 = **₦4,800**.

(b) %Profit = (SP − CP) / CP × 100
    = 400 / 2,400 × 100
    = 1/6 × 100
    = 16.67%
    ≈ **17%** (to the nearest whole number).

> ⚠️ **Examiner Trap:** Many candidates divide by SP (₦2,800) instead of CP. The question asks for "% profit", and profit is **always measured against the cost price**, not the selling price. Dividing by SP gives 14% — a wrong answer that costs the mark.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Advanced traps and exceptions

1. **Hidden two-step equations.** A problem like *"A father is three times as old as his son. In 12 years he will be twice as old."* requires two equations: F = 3S and F + 12 = 2(S + 12). Students often solve only the first and report F = 3S without using the time-shift.
2. **Ratio changes after a transaction.** *"Two brothers share ₦12,000 in the ratio 3:5. The elder gives ₦1,000 to the younger; find the new ratio."* — solve the first ratio, then subtract/add ₦1,000, **then** form the new ratio. Don't divide ₦1,000 by the ratio parts.
3. **Speed conversion mid-problem.** A bus leaves at 08:00 at 60 km/h; a car leaves the same point at 09:00 at 90 km/h. To find when the car catches up, **convert the head-start distance** (60 × 1 = 60 km) into the relative speed gap: 90 − 60 = 30 km/h, so catch-up time = 60 / 30 = 2 h.
5. **Simple interest for partial years uses the actual fraction.** A deposit of ₦20,000 at 6% per annum for 90 days: I = 20,000 × 6 × (90/365) / 100 ≈ ₦296. Always convert days to years as a fraction before plugging into I = PRT/100.
6. **Average with a missing value.** If the mean of five numbers is 12 and four of them are 10, 14, 9, 15, then the fifth = 12 × 5 − (10 + 14 + 9 + 15) = 60 − 48 = **12**. NCEE loves this trick.

#### Common Mistakes Catalogue

| Mistake | Why it loses marks | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Computing %Profit on SP | Wrong denominator | Always divide by CP |
| Adding times in work problems | 5 + 3 = 8 is **wrong** | Add reciprocals: 1/5 + 1/3 = 8/15 |
| Leaving answer in kobo as a whole number | Loses the ₦ symbol | Express as ₦x.yy |
| Reporting a negative age | Sign error in setup | Re-check the equation; ages ≥ 0 |
| Forgetting to convert minutes to hours | Distance comes out 60× too large | 30 min = 0.5 h **before** using D = S × T |
| Dividing by the wrong total in ratio | Ratio parts reversed | Sum the ratio numbers **first**, then share |

#### Advanced Practice Prompts

1. *A loan of ₦80,000 at 5% per annum simple interest is partly repaid at the end of 2 years with ₦8,000 interest. Find the amount still outstanding.* (Apply A = P(1 + RT/100), then subtract the interest paid only if principal was reduced — here interest paid equals computed interest, so the **entire** ₦8,000 is the interest, and A = ₦88,000.)
2. *Pipe A fills a tank in 6 hours, Pipe B in 9 hours, and Pipe C empties it in 18 hours. How long will all three together take to fill an empty tank?* (Combined rate = 1/6 + 1/9 − 1/18 = (3 + 2 − 1)/18 = 4/18 = 2/9 per hour; time = 9/2 = **4 hours 30 minutes**.)

#### Links to adjacent topics

Word problems sit on top of four earlier skills: **fractions and percentages** (for profit/loss and SI), **rates and ratios** (for sharing and mixing), **linear equations in one unknown** (for ages and work), and **unit conversion** (for D–S–T). If any of these feel shaky, revisit them before the exam — most NCEE word-problem marks are lost because of a weakness in the supporting skill, not in translation.
