# Typical Content Domains

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

**Subject Knowledge** is the content-specific academic section of UI's entrance pathway (UM, MTK-UM, or RUANG-AJAR), carrying **3% of the total selection score**. It tests **factual knowledge and conceptual understanding** of materials relevant to your chosen **faculty / study programme** (e.g., Kedokteran, Farmasi, Hukum). Items are **single-answer multiple choice**, drawing on **logical reasoning, propositional logic, sets, basic algebra**, or programme-specific content.

- Check your **paket soal** before studying — not every prodi administers Subject Knowledge.
- Strategy: allocate study time **proportional to weightage** (3%), not equal to heavier sections.
- Refresh material **close to exam day** — the question bank is refreshed annually.

---

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### Scope and Weighting

Within UI's selection architecture, Subject Knowledge is a **satellite sub-test** layered onto the core reasoning modules. Its 3% weight makes it the lightest scored section in most paket soal, yet it still rewards candidates who can convert one or two extra correct answers into a meaningful rank shift.

#### Typical Content Domains

The actual subject matter depends on the target faculty:

| Faculty / Programme | Likely Content Emphasis |
|---|---|
| Kedokteran, Farmasi, Kesehatan | Biology, Chemistry, basic scientific reasoning |
| Hukum | Logical argumentation, reading-based legal concepts |
| Ilmu Komputer, Teknik | Sets, propositional logic, algebra fundamentals |
| Komunikasi, Psikologi | Literacy, data interpretation, concept mapping |

#### Question Format

Items are **pilihan ganda with one correct answer**. They lean on **pengetahuan faktual** rather than calculation-heavy problem solving, so fluent recall of definitions, taxonomies, and standard propositions is the dominant skill tested.

#### Exam Strategy

> Treat Subject Knowledge as a **tie-breaker, not a foundation**.

Because SNBT, TKPU, and penalaran modules carry far larger weightage, candidates often over-invest here. Limit prep to the **last 3–5 days** before the exam, use only **UI's official PMB silabus**, and verify against `penerimaan.ui.ac.id` which prodi paket soalan includes this section for the current cycle.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Edge Cases and Programme Variability

Subject Knowledge does **not** appear uniformly. Some prodi bundle it with another mata uji; others drop it entirely in favour of heavier penalaran or sains dasar weighting. Always cross-check the **paket soal announcement** for your intended prodi on `penerimaan.ui.ac.id` — assuming its presence can waste days of revision on the wrong exam shape.

#### Connection to Adjacent Modules

Logical-reasoning items in Subject Knowledge overlap with **penalaran induktif** tested elsewhere in the UI battery. Mastering propositional logic (AND, OR, NOT, implication) and set operations (union, intersection, complement) here **double-counts** as preparation for higher-weight reasoning sections.

#### Common Mistakes

- **Misallocated hours:** spending 20+ hours on a 3% module while ignoring 25–30% modules.
- **Stale material:** relying on last year's question bank without checking for annual refreshes.
- **Wrong-source preparation:** using UTUL UGM or SNBP practice sets that don't match UI's silabus.
- **Assuming universality:** preparing Subject Knowledge for a prodi that doesn't administer it.

#### Practice Prompts

1. Construct a truth table for the proposition *(p → q) ∧ ¬p*, and identify the rows that satisfy it — then explain why this mirrors a typical logical-reasoning Subject Knowledge item.
2. Given two finite sets A and B with |A| = 8, |B| = 5, and |A ∩ B| = 2, compute |A ∪ B| using the inclusion–exclusion principle and frame a one-sentence justification in the style UI examiners favour.
