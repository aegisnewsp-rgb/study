# Famous Personalities of Bangladesh and South Asia

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

**Sheikh Mujibur Rahman** (born 17 March 1920, Tungipara, Gopalganj) is the **Father of the Nation** of Bangladesh and the principal architect of the 1971 Liberation War. He led the historic **6-Point Movement** of 1966 and the **Mass Uprising of 1969**, leading directly to the independence of Bangladesh.

| Personality | Title / Distinction |
|---|---|
| Sheikh Mujibur Rahman | Father of the Nation, Bangladesh |
| Rabindranath Tagore | Composed Bangladesh's national anthem *Amar Shonar Bangla* (1972); Nobel in Literature, **1913** |
| Kazi Nazrul Islam | National poet of Bangladesh (*Bidrohi Kobi*); composed *Joy Bangla* (national march) |
| Mahatma Gandhi | Father of the Nation, India |

- DU MCQs frequently test the **Tagore vs Nazrul** swap and which leader was head of the Provisional Government of Bangladesh (Tajuddin Ahmad) versus Commander-in-Chief of Mukti Bahini (M. A. G. Osmani).

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### Founding Fathers of the Three South Asian States

Bangladesh, India, and Pakistan each recognise a distinct "Father of the Nation". Memorising the correct assignment is essential because DU questions deliberately mix them.

| Country | Father of the Nation | Founder / Architect |
|---|---|---|
| Bangladesh | **Sheikh Mujibur Rahman (Bangabandhu)** | Founder of independent Bangladesh, 1971 |
| India | **Mahatma Gandhi** | Independence 1947, non-violent movement |
| Pakistan | **Muhammad Ali Jinnah (Quaid-i-Azam)** | Founder, 14 August 1947 |

Allama Iqbal is the **national poet of Pakistan** and the philosopher whose 1930 Allahabad Address conceptualised the two-nation idea; he is **not** the founder.

#### Two Women Leaders of Bangladesh

- **Sheikh Hasina** — longest-serving Prime Minister of Bangladesh, daughter of Bangabandhu, leader of the Awami League.
- **Begum Khaleda Zia** — **first female Prime Minister of Bangladesh** (1991), widow of President **Ziaur Rahman**, leader of the Bangladesh Nationalist Party (BNP).

#### Liberation War (1971) Leadership Structure

| Role | Person |
|---|---|
| Supreme Commander (proclaimed) | Sheikh Mujibur Rahman |
| Head, Provisional Government | Tajuddin Ahmad |
| Commander-in-Chief, Mukti Bahini | M. A. G. Osmani |
| Sectoral commanders | Ziaur Rahman (Sector 1), Major Khaled Mosharraf (Sector 2), among others |

#### National Symbols of Bangladesh

Bangladesh has two anthems of distinction — a **national anthem** (*Amar Shonar Bangla*, by **Tagore**) and a **national march** (*Joy Bangla*, by **Nazrul**). **Ekushey February 21** commemorates the 1952 Language Movement martyrs (Salam, Barkat, Rafiquddin, Jabbar, Shafiur Rahman) and is observed globally as **International Mother Language Day** (UNESCO, 1999).

#### Subcontinental Nobel Laureates to Recognise

| Laureate | Country | Year | Field |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rabindranath Tagore | India | 1913 | Literature |
| C. V. Raman | India | 1930 | Physics |
| Mother Teresa | Indian citizen (Albanian-born) | 1979 | Peace |
| Abdus Salam | Pakistan | 1979 | Physics |
| Amartya Sen | India | 1998 | Economics |
| Malala Yousafzai | Pakistan | 2014 | Peace |
| Kailash Satyarthi | India | 2014 | Peace |
| Abhijit Banerjee | India | 2019 | Economics |

> Trap: **Mother Teresa is never classified as Bangladeshi**, and **Abdus Salam is the sole Pakistani Nobel-winning physicist**. Confusing these two 1979 laureates is a frequent MCQ distractor.

#### Literary and Cultural Icons of Bengal

- **Begum Rokeya Sakhawat Hossain (1880–1932)** — Bengali feminist writer, author of *Sultana's Dream*, pioneer of women's education in Bengal.
- **Lalon Shah** — Baul mystic and philosopher whose egalitarian songs transcended Hindu–Muslim boundaries in Bengal.
- **Jasimuddin** — known as *Polli Kobi* (rural poet) for his pastoral Bengali verse.

#### Common MCQ Traps

- Tagore composed *Amar Shonar Bangla* (anthem), not *Joy Bangla* (march). Mixing these is the single most-tested error.
- Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose led the **Indian National Army (INA)** and founded the Forward Bloc — he never served as India's Prime Minister (that was Nehru).
- Swami Vivekananda (1863–1902) is celebrated for the **1893 Chicago Parliament of Religions** speech; he is **not** a Nobel laureate.

#### Practice Prompts

1. Match the title to the figure: *Father of the Nation (Bangladesh)*, *Quaid-i-Azam*, *Bidrohi Kobi*, *Netaji*. Which personality is commonly mislabelled as the founder of Pakistan, and which as Tagore?
2. Identify the Nobel laureate from each: 1913 Literature, 1979 Physics (Pakistan), 2014 Peace, 1998 Economics. Why is Mother Teresa often wrongly credited to Bangladesh in MCQs?
