# Assessment Criteria — Task Response, Coherence, Lexical, Grammar — IELTS Writing (Qatar)

In the IELTS Academic Writing test, examiners score your Writing on
**four equal-weighted criteria**, each contributing **25% of the band
score for Writing**. The four criteria are: **Task Response** (TR),
**Coherence and Cohesion** (CC), **Lexical Resource** (LR), and
**Grammatical Range and Accuracy** (GRA). To score band 7.0+, you must
score **at least band 7.0 on at least three of the four criteria** and
**no criterion below band 6.0**. The four public-band descriptors are
the same for Academic and General Training Writing.

This is the **seventh and final topic** in the ielts-writing subject
(weight 3). It follows ieltsw-006 (Task 2 — Cohesion, Lexical Resource
and Grammar w=4). The final topic focuses on **band 7.0-9.0 patterns**
that mark a strong Academic Writing response: a **clear position
throughout**, **support with relevant main points**, **error-free
complex sentences**, **precise word choice**, and **natural cohesive
devices**.

The examiner uses **public-band descriptors** (published by Cambridge
Assessment English, IDP, and British Council). These descriptors are
publicly available at https://www.cambridgeenglish.org/exams-and-tests/ielts/
and https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/. The examiner applies them in
a **standardized way** to all candidates worldwide, ensuring fair
comparison across countries.

This note shows the LCA version: a **Lite** (5-minute) read, a
**Standard** (15-minute) read, and the **Exam Essentials** table a
Qatari candidate can use to revise in the last hour before the test.

## Lite (4-Step Method)

1. **Memorize the four criteria and their band 7.0-9.0 descriptors.**
   TR: clear position, supported with relevant main points. CC:
   logical organization, clear progression, appropriate cohesive
   devices. LR: wide vocabulary, precise word choice, minimal errors.
   GRA: wide range of structures, majority error-free sentences,
   good control of grammar and punctuation.
2. **Self-assess using the public-band descriptors.** Before the
   exam, score your practice essays using the band descriptors. Aim
   for band 7.0-7.5 on all four criteria.
3. **Identify your weakest criterion and target it.** Common Qatari
   cohort weak points: CC (overuse of cohesive devices) and LR (basic
   vocabulary).
4. **Practice all four criteria in every essay.** A Task 2 essay
   should address all four criteria simultaneously: clear position (TR),
   logical organization (CC), varied vocabulary (LR), and varied grammar
   (GRA).

**Insight: The four criteria are equal-weighted (25% each).** A Task 2
that scores band 8 on TR but band 6 on LR scores 7.0 overall (the LOWEST
criterion determines the ceiling). A Task 2 that scores band 7 on all
four criteria scores 7.0 overall. ALWAYS aim for at least band 7.0 on
all four criteria.

### 6 Examiner Traps (Assessment Criteria)

- **The single-criterion-focus trap** — candidates focus on one criterion
  (e.g. GRA) and neglect the others (e.g. CC, LR). ALWAYS address all
  four criteria in every essay.
- **The weak-TR trap** — candidates take positions without supporting
  them with relevant main points. ALWAYS support with main points.
- **The weak-CC trap** — candidates overuse cohesive devices or have
  illogical organization. ALWAYS use 2-3 cohesive devices per paragraph,
  NOT in every sentence.
- **The weak-LR trap** — candidates use basic vocabulary (*very,
  important, big, good*). ALWAYS use academic vocabulary (*significant,
  considerable, substantial, fundamental, essential*).
- **The weak-GRA trap** — candidates write only simple sentences or
  have subject-verb agreement errors. ALWAYS mix simple, compound, and
  complex sentences (60% complex).
- **The lowest-score trap** — the lowest criterion determines the
  ceiling. ALWAYS aim for at least band 7.0 on all four criteria.

### Qatari-Context Matches (2025–2026 cohort signal)

the assessment criteria emphasis commonly addresses:

- **Task Response** — taking a clear position, supporting with
  relevant main points.
- **Coherence and Cohesion** — logical organization, clear
  progression, appropriate cohesive devices (NOT in every sentence).
- **Lexical Resource** — varied vocabulary, precise word choice,

featured in the Qatari cohort.

## Standard (7-Step Method with Long-Form Guidance)

### Step 1 — Memorize the four criteria and their band 7.0-9.0 descriptors

Before the exam, memorize the band descriptors. The four criteria and
their band 7.0-9.0 patterns are:

- **Task Response (TR)** — band 7.0: clear position throughout the
  response; presents, extends and supports main points but may
  over-generalize or lack focus. Band 8.0: sufficiently addresses all
  parts of the task; presents a clear position throughout the
  response; presents, extends and supports main points.
- **Coherence and Cohesion (CC)** — band 7.0: logical organization;
  clear progression; uses a range of cohesive devices appropriately
  though with occasional under/over-use. Band 8.0: logical
  organization; clear progression; uses a range of cohesive devices
  appropriately with no or only occasional under/over-use.
- **Lexical Resource (LR)** — band 7.0: sufficient range of
  vocabulary; allows flexibility and precision; uses less common
  lexical items with some awareness of style and collocation; may
  produce occasional errors in word choice and collocation. Band 8.0:
  wide range of vocabulary fluently and flexibly used to convey
  precise meanings; skillfully uses less common lexical items with
  occasional inaccuracies in word choice and collocation.
- **Grammatical Range and Accuracy (GRA)** — band 7.0: uses a
  variety of complex structures; produces frequent error-free
  sentences; has good control of grammar and punctuation but may
  make a few errors. Band 8.0: uses a wide range of structures;
  majority of sentences are error-free with only occasional
  inappropriateness and/or basic errors.

### Step 2 — Self-assess using the public-band descriptors

For each criterion, ask:

- **TR** — did I take a clear position? did I support with main points?
- **CC** — did I have logical organization? did I use 2-3 cohesive
  devices per paragraph (NOT in every sentence)?
- **LR** — did I use varied vocabulary? did I use academic vocabulary
  (*significant, considerable, substantial, fundamental, essential*)?
- **GRA** — did I mix simple, compound, and complex sentences? are my
  complex sentences error-free?

### Step 3 — Identify your weakest criterion and target it

Common Qatari cohort weak points:

- **CC** — overuse of cohesive devices (*moreover, furthermore, in
  addition, firstly, secondly, thirdly, in conclusion*).
- **LR** — basic vocabulary (*very, really, big, important, good,
  bad*).
- **GRA** — subject-verb agreement errors (*Education are
  important*); tense mixing.

Target the weakest criterion by:

- **CC** — practice using 2-3 cohesive devices per paragraph; remove
  excessive cohesive devices from practice essays.
- **LR** — build a list of academic vocabulary (*significant,
  considerable, substantial, fundamental, essential, crucial, vital*);
  practice using them in essays.
- **GRA** — practice subject-verb agreement; practice complex sentences
  (*because...; although...; whereas...*).

### Step 4 — Write a band 7.0+ Task 2 essay

A band 7.0+ Task 2 essay has:

- **TR** — clear position; 2-3 main points; each main point supported
  with explanation + example.
- **CC** — 4 paragraphs (introduction + 2 body + conclusion); 2-3
  cohesive devices per paragraph.
- **LR** — academic vocabulary (*significant, considerable,
  substantial, fundamental, essential, demonstrate, indicate,
  suggest, propose*); minimal errors in word choice.
- **GRA** — mix of simple (15%) / compound (25%) / complex (60%)
  sentences; majority of complex sentences error-free.

### Step 5 — Review and self-assess (10 minutes after writing)

After writing, examine your score on each criterion:

- **TR band 7.0+** — did I take a clear position? did I support with
  main points?
- **CC band 7.0+** — did I have 4 paragraphs? did I use 2-3 cohesive
  devices per paragraph?
- **LR band 7.0+** — did I use academic vocabulary? did I avoid basic
  vocabulary?
- **GRA band 7.0+** — did I mix simple/compound/complex sentences?
  are my complex sentences error-free?

### Step 6 — Practice with band descriptors

Practice with the public-band descriptors weekly:

- Take a Task 2 prompt.
- Write a 280-320 word essay in 40 minutes.
- Self-assess using the band descriptors.
- Identify weak criteria and write another essay targeting them.

### Step 7 — Final pre-test self-assessment (1 week before test)

In the last week before the test, write 3-5 practice essays under timed
conditions and self-assess. Aim for band 7.0+ on all four criteria.

## High-Yield Band 7.0-9.0 Patterns (7 rows)

These are the band 7.0-9.0 patterns that mark a strong Academic Writing
response.

1. **Clear position throughout** — every paragraph reinforces the
   thesis; no wavering.
2. **Main points with explanation + example** — each main point is
   developed, not just stated.
3. **2-3 cohesive devices per paragraph** — appropriate use at
   transitions, NOT in every sentence.
4. **Academic vocabulary** — *significant, considerable, substantial,
   fundamental, essential, demonstrate, indicate, suggest, propose*.
5. **Varied sentence structures** — mix simple (15%) / compound (25%)
   / complex (60%).
6. **Error-free complex sentences** — most complex sentences should
   be grammatically correct.
7. **Precise word choice** — use collocations and less common items
   (*substantial implications, considerable merit, fundamental
   principle*).

## Previous Year Patterns (IELTS 2018–2025)

The four criteria have been consistent since 2018. Over 2018–2025:

- 2018–2020: TR was the most-discriminating criterion; CC and GRA were
  common weak points.
- 2021–2023: LR became more discriminating; use of academic vocabulary
  was emphasized.
- 2024–2025: All four criteria are equally discriminating; the
  lowest criterion determines the ceiling.

The pattern: The four criteria are equal-weighted and equally
discriminating. Candidates who score band 7.0+ on all four criteria
score 7.0+ overall.
