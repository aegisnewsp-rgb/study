# Wastong Gamit ng mga Salita (Correct Word Usage)

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid review of high-frequency word distinctions and grammatical usage rules for the UPCAT Filipino Language Proficiency subtest.

**Core Word Pair Distinctions — High-Yield Essentials**

1. **Ng vs. Nang:**
   - **Ng:** Used as a nominal marker for direct objects (*Bumili siya ng libro*), possessive marker (*Bahay ng aking guro*), or passive actor marker (*Kinain ng pusa ang isda*).
   - **Nang:** Used as a subordinating conjunction meaning 'when' (*Nang dumating ang guro...*), to connect repeating verbs (*Takbo nang takbo*), to indicate manner answering 'how' (*Sumulat siya nang maingat*), or as a contraction of *na + ng* (*Sobra nang pagod*).
2. **Din/Daw vs. Rin/Raw:**
   - **Rin / Raw:** Used when the immediately preceding word ends in a **vowel** (a, e, i, o, u) or semi-vowel (**w, y**): *Ikaw raw*, *Masaya rin*, *Giliw raw*.
   - **Din / Daw:** Used when the immediately preceding word ends in a **consonant** (except w and y): *Sulat daw*, *Tahimik din*, *Pagsusulit daw*.
3. **Subukin vs. Subukan:**
   - **Subukin (To test/try an ability, quality, or mechanism):** *Subukin natin ang bagong sasakyan.*
   - **Subukan (To spy on, monitor, or secretly observe someone):** *Subukan mo kung saan siya pupunta mamayang gabi.*
4. **Pahirin vs. Pahiran:**
   - **Pahirin (To wipe off / remove something from a surface):** *Pahirin mo ang luha sa iyong mga pisngi.*
   - **Pahiran (To apply / smear something onto a surface):** *Pahiran mo ng mantikilya ang tinapay.*
5. **Pinto vs. Pintuan / Hagdan vs. Hagdanan:**
   - **Pinto / Hagdan:** Refers to the physical barrier or physical steps (*Isara mo ang pinto*; *Umakyat sa hagdan*).
   - **Pintuan / Hagdanan:** Refers to the doorway / passage or the staircase area (*Huwag kang humarang sa pintuan*; *Tumambay sa hagdanan*).

⚡ **UPCAT Exam Trap:** The words ending in semi-vowels **w** and **y** (e.g., *araw*, *bahay*, *buhay*) mandate the **r-form** (**rin / raw**), NOT the d-form! A common distractor in UPCAT is writing *"araw daw"* instead of the correct *"araw raw"*.

## 1. Morphological and Semantic Word Distinctions

The Filipino subtest of college entrance tests evaluates precise semantic and morphophonemic word choices:

| **May vs Mayroon** | Existential particles | **May:** Precedes nouns, verbs, adjectives, pronouns (*May kotse*, *May ginagawa*). **Mayroon:** Precedes personal pronouns (*Mayroon siyang pera*), particle pauses, or answers questions (*May pera ka ba? Mayroon.*) | *May magandang bukas ang masipag.* vs *Mayroon pa ba tayong oras?* |
| **Operahin vs Operahan** | Verb affixation focus | **Operahin:** Refers to the specific organ or biological defect being surgically excised/repaired. **Operahan:** Refers to the patient or anatomical person undergoing the surgical operation | *Ooperahin ang kanyang apendiks.* vs *Ooperahan si Maria bukas ng umaga.* |
| **Walisan vs Walisin** | Locative vs Object focus | **Walisin:** Refers to sweeping away specific trash/dirt (Object focus). **Walisan:** Refers to cleaning the physical area/floor (Locative focus) | *Walisin mo ang mga tuyong dahon.* vs *Walisan mo ang sala.* |

## 2. Punctuation, Hyphenation (Gitling), and Morphemic Joining

In KWF orthography, the hyphen (**gitling -**) has strict syntactic functions:
1. **Pag-uulit ng Salita:** Full or partial root reduplication requires a hyphen (*araw-araw*, *gabi-gabi*, *sali-salisi*).
2. **Tambalang Salita:** Compound words retaining distinct metaphorical balance (*bahay-kubo*, *dalagang-bukid*, *hatinggabi* vs full fusion when meaning mutates completely: *dalampasigan*, *bahaghari*).
3. **Pagsasama ng Panlapi sa Pangngalang Pantangi:** Attaching affixes to proper nouns requires a hyphen (*maka-Pilipino*, *taga-Maynila*, *i-Cebu*).
4. **Pagsasama sa Salitang Banyaga:** Affixing native morphemes to unassimilated English/foreign loanwords (*mag-upload*, *i-scan*, *nag-dry run*).
5. **Katinig + Patinig Glottal Break:** When a prefix ending in a consonant meets a root beginning with a vowel, a hyphen represents the glottal stop (*tag-ulan*, *pag-asa*, *pang-akit*).

## 3. Bilingual Study Guide: English Grammatical Analysis for UPCAT Candidates

For bilingual Filipino high school seniors preparing for university entrance examinations, understanding the structural differences between Tagalog/Filipino focus systems and English transitive alignments is vital:
- **Austronesian Focus Alignment (Symmetrical Voice):** In English, grammatical voice is limited to active and passive. In Filipino, verbal affixes signal the semantic role of the trigger (ang-phrase) across multiple cases: Actor focus (*um-*, *mag-*), Patient/Object focus (*-in*), Locative focus (*-an*), Benefactive focus (*i-*, *ipag-*), and Instrumental focus (*ipang-*).
- **Phonological Alternation and Epenthesis:** The alternation between *d* and *r* represents an intervocalic lenition process characteristic of Central Philippine languages. Consonant *d* spirantizes to *r* between vowels to minimize articulatory effort, whereas word-final stops block this shift.
- **Particle Positioning and Clitic Ordering:** Clitic particles (*na*, *pa*, *daw*, *din*, *ba*, *nga*, *lang*) must obey rigid linear precedence hierarchies immediately following the first prosodic word of a clause.

## 4. UPCAT Language Proficiency Practice Questions

**Question 1 (UPCAT Filipino):** Piliin ang pangungusap na may wastong gamit ng salita:  
*(A) Pahirin mo ng gamot ang sugat ng bata.  
(B) Walisan mo ang mga kalat sa bakuran.  
(C) Ooperahin bukas ang bukol sa kanyang dibdib.  
(D) Ikaw daw ang napiling kinatawan ng paaralan.*  
**Answer:** (C) Ooperahin bukas ang bukol sa kanyang dibdib. (Paliwanag: Ang 'ooperahin' ay tumutukoy sa partikular na bahagi ng katawan o bukol. Sa (A), dapat ay 'pahiran'. Sa (B), dapat ay 'walisin' dahil kalat ang tinutukoy. Sa (D), dahil nagtatapos sa katinig na w ang 'ikaw', dapat ay 'raw').

**Question 2 (UPCAT Filipino):** Alin sa mga sumusunod ang tamang paggamit ng salitang "nang"?  
*(A) Kumain siya nang mangga.  
(B) Dumating ang mga bisita nang gabi na.  
(C) Ang payo nang guro ay kanyang sinunod.  
(D) Mayroon nang bagong mag-aaral sa klase.*  
**Answer:** (B) Dumating ang mga bisita nang gabi na. (Paliwanag: Ang 'nang' ay ginagamit bilang pangatnig na kasingkahulugan ng 'noong' sa pagtukoy ng panahon. Sa (A) at (C), kailangan ang 'ng' bilang pantukoy ng layon at pag-aari).
