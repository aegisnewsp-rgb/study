# Indian Economy — Growth and Development

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

India's economic performance is measured through national income aggregates, sectoral composition, and reforms that reshaped the post-1991 trajectory. The SBI PO General Awareness section tests these concepts through static fact-recall: base year, scheme launch dates, reform years, and aggregate differences.

| Aggregate | Formula | What it captures |
|---|---|---|
| **GDP (Market Price)** | C + I + G + (X − M) | Value of all final goods/services produced within India |
| **GNP** | GDP + NFIA | GDP plus net factor income earned abroad |
| **NDP** | GDP − Depreciation | Net output after consuming capital stock |
| **NNP at Factor Cost** | GNP − Depreciation − NIT | National income earned by factors of production |
| **Per Capita Income** | National Income ÷ Mid-Year Population | Average income per person (₹) |

- **Base year for national accounts:** 2011-12 (set by MoSPI, January 2015).
- **LPG reforms:** Liberalization, Privatization, Globalization launched in **1991** under Finance Minister **Manmohan Singh**.
- **Planning Commission → NITI Aayog:** replaced on **1 January 2015**; NITI Aayog is advisory, with no fund-allocation power.
- **Sectoral GVA split:** Agriculture ~18%, Industry ~28%, Services ~53%.

> 💡 **High-Yield Memory Hook:** "**GNP = GDP + Abroad**" — think **G**eography for GDP, **N**ation for GNP. India earns sizeable NFIA, so **GNP > GDP**. For factor-cost vs market-price: "**NIT is the bridge**" — Net Indirect Taxes (Indirect Taxes − Subsidies) convert one to the other.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### National Income Aggregates and Identity Chain

National accounting moves from gross to net, and from domestic to national, by adding or subtracting specific flows. Each step is a separate MCQ trap.

- **GDP at Market Price** = value of final output inclusive of net indirect taxes.
- **NDP at Market Price** = GDP − Depreciation (Consumption of Fixed Capital).
- **NNP at Factor Cost** (true *national income*) = NDP at Market Price − Net Indirect Taxes.
- **Real GDP** = (Nominal GDP ÷ GDP Deflator) × 100; the deflator strips out price effects to reveal actual growth.

> 📌 **Formula Check:** All aggregates are denominated in **₹ crore** at current prices (nominal) or constant 2011-12 prices (real). Per Capita Income carries dimension **₹·person⁻¹**.

#### Comparison Matrix — Easily Confused Concepts

| Concept A | Concept B | Key Distinction |
|---|---|---|
| GDP | GNP | GDP is **geographic** (within borders); GNP adds **Net Factor Income from Abroad** |
| Market Price | Factor Cost | Gap = **Net Indirect Taxes** (Indirect Taxes − Subsidies) |
| Fiscal Deficit | Revenue Deficit | Fiscal = **Total Expenditure − Total Receipts (excl. borrowings)**; Revenue = only the revenue component |
| FDI | FII | FDI is **lasting management interest** (≥10% equity); FII is **portfolio investment** by foreign funds |
| Planning Commission | NITI Aayog | Commission **allocated funds** to states; NITI Aayog is purely **advisory** |
| Green Revolution | White Revolution | Green = **foodgrains** (M.S. Swaminathan, 1966-70s); White = **milk** (Verghese Kurien, Operation Flood) |
| FERA 1973 | FEMA 1999 | FERA was **restrictive**; FEMA is **liberal**, aligning with LPG reforms |
| Current Account Deficit | Fiscal Deficit | CAD is **external** (BoP); Fiscal is **domestic** (budget) |

#### Key Reforms and Scheme Launch Map

- **1991 LPG reforms:** dismantling of License Raj, rupee devaluation, abolition of quantitative restrictions, opening to FDI.
- **GST launched:** **1 July 2017** — unified indirect tax replacing Central Excise, Service Tax, VAT.
- **PMJDY (Jan Dhan Yojana):** **28 August 2014** — financial inclusion, zero-balance accounts.
- **MUDRA Yojana:** **April 2015** — micro-finance for non-corporate, non-farm small borrowers.
- **Make in India:** launched **25 September 2014**.
- **Startup India:** **16 January 2016**.
- **Digital India:** **1 July 2015**.

#### 🎯 Exam-Level Worked Problem

**Question:** If India's GDP at Market Price is ₹270 lakh crore, Depreciation is ₹40 lakh crore, Net Indirect Taxes are ₹30 lakh crore, and Net Factor Income from Abroad (NFIA) is ₹(−)5 lakh crore, what is the National Income (NNP at Factor Cost)?

#### Solution:

1. **NDP at Market Price** = GDP − Depreciation = 270 − 40 = **₹230 lakh crore**
2. **NNP at Market Price** = NDP at MP − NIT = 230 − 30 = **₹200 lakh crore**
3. Convert to **NNP at Factor Cost** = NNP at MP − Net Indirect Taxes (which equals NDP at FC directly):
   - NNP at FC = 230 − 30 + (−5) = **₹195 lakh crore**

Verification via the identity: NNP at FC = GDP − Depreciation − NIT + NFIA = 270 − 40 − 30 + (−5) = **₹195 lakh crore**.

> ⚠️ **Examiner Trap:** Students frequently omit NFIA or treat it as always positive. For India, NFIA is *typically negative* because income paid to foreign investors exceeds income earned abroad by residents. Always insert NFIA with its sign.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Sectoral Dynamics and the Structural Shift

The services (tertiary) sector has overtaken agriculture as the dominant GVA contributor since the 1990s. Agriculture's share fell from ~55% at independence to roughly 18% today, while services rose from ~30% to ~53%. Industry hovers around 28%, but its composition shifted toward manufacturing and construction post-liberalization.

- **Primary sector:** agriculture, forestry, fishing, mining.
- **Secondary sector:** manufacturing, electricity, construction.
- **Tertiary sector:** trade, transport, financial services, IT-BPM, real estate.

#### Inclusive Growth and Human Development Indicators

| Indicator | Measures | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **HDI** | Health, Education, Income (0–1 scale) | UNDP Human Development Report |
| **Gini Coefficient** | Income inequality (0 = perfect equality) | NSSO / World Bank |
| **MPI** | Health, Education, Living Standards | UNDP + OPHI |
| **Per Capita Income** | Average income per person | MoSPI |

> 📌 **Formula Check:** Gini = (Area between Lorenz curve and equality line) ÷ (Total area under equality line). Dimensionless index between 0 and 1.

#### External Sector and BoP Architecture

- **Current Account Deficit (CAD)** = (Imports of goods + services + income) − (Exports + services + income). Sustainable CAD threshold is debated; RBI monitors it as a **% of GDP**.
- **Capital Account** records FDI, FII, external commercial borrowings, and NRI deposits.
- **FEMA 1999** replaced the restrictive **FERA 1973**, liberalising forex flows for residents and NRIs.

#### Advanced Traps and Exceptions

1. **"NITI Aayog allocates funds"** — false; it only advises. The Finance Commission handles devolution; schemes get funds via Union Budget.
2. **"India's GDP > GNP always"** — false generally, but direction depends on NFIA. India has run a *negative* NFIA recently, so **GNP < GDP**.
3. **"GST is a single rate"** — false; GST has **five slabs** — 0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%.
4. **"Base year is 2004-05"** — outdated; the current base year is **2011-12** since January 2015.
5. **"White Revolution = foodgrain self-sufficiency"** — wrong; that's the Green Revolution.
6. **"Fiscal Deficit = Borrowings"** — close but imprecise; Fiscal Deficit equals **total expenditure minus total non-debt receipts**, which is financed by borrowings.

#### Practice Prompts

1. India's GDP at Market Price = ₹300 lakh crore, Depreciation = ₹35 lakh crore, Indirect Taxes = ₹25 lakh crore, Subsidies = ₹5 lakh crore, NFIA = ₹(−)4 lakh crore. Compute NNP at Factor Cost.
2. Distinguish between a 1 percentage-point rise in GDP Deflator with constant Real GDP versus a 1% rise in Real GDP with constant prices — what happens to Nominal GDP and per capita income?
