# Sports, Awards, and Important Days

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

This topic bundles three General Knowledge sub-domains frequently tested in the **SPSC (Sindh)** paper at roughly **3% weightage**: sports tournaments and trophies, prestigious awards, and nationally/internationally observed days. Most questions are 1-mark MCQs on trophy-sport matching, award hierarchy, and important-day dates.

#### Key Facts at a Glance

| Sub-domain | What to recall | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Sports trophies** | Sport paired with trophy name | Davis Cup → Tennis; Thomas Cup → Badminton; Ranji Trophy → Indian domestic cricket; Quaid-e-Azam Trophy → Pakistani domestic cricket |
| **Awards** | Hierarchy and awarding body | Bharat Ratna > Padma Vibhushan > Padma Bhushan > Padma Shri (conferred by the President of India) |
| **Pakistani civilian awards** | Five-tier order | Nishan-e-Imtiaz > Hilal-i-Imtiaz > Sitara-i-Imtiaz > Tamgha-i-Imtiaz > Pride of Performance |
| **Important Days** | Exact date + theme | World Health Day — 7 April; International Women's Day — 8 March; Pakistan Day — 23 March |

> 💡 **High-Yield Memory Hook:** **"N-HT-ST-T-PP"** for Pakistani civilian awards (descending): **N**ishan-e-Imtiaz → **H**ilal-i-Imtiaz → **S**itara-i-Imtiaz → **T**amgha-i-Imtiaz → **P**ride of **P**erformance. For Nobel awards remember **"Peace is Norwegian, the rest is Swedish"** — only the Nobel Peace Prize is decided in Oslo; all others in Stockholm.

#### Awards: Hierarchy and Awarding Bodies

Civilian awards of India and Pakistan are repeatedly asked in reverse order. Memorise the descending ladder rather than the individual recipients.

- **India (civilian):** Bharat Ratna (1954) → Padma Vibhushan → Padma Bhushan → Padma Shri. Conferred by the **President of India** on the **Prime Minister's** recommendation.
- **Pakistan (civilian, announced on 14 August):** Nishan-e-Imtiaz → Hilal-i-Imtiaz → Sitara-i-Imtiaz → Tamgha-i-Imtiaz → Pride of Performance.
- **Nobel Prize (founded 1901 by Alfred Nobel):** six categories — Peace, Literature, Physics, Chemistry, Physiology/Medicine, and Economics. The Economics prize was instituted by Sveriges Riksbank in 1968 and first awarded in **1969**.
- **Other global awards:** Oscar (film, Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences), Grammy (music, Recording Academy), Pulitzer (journalism/literature, Columbia University), Booker Prize (English-language fiction), Ramon Magsaysay Award (Asia).

#### 🎯 Exam-Level Worked Problem

**Question:** The Nobel Prize in Economics was first awarded in which year, and which institution established it?  
(a) 1901, established by Alfred Nobel  
(b) 1929, established by the Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences  
(c) 1969, established by Sveriges Riksbank  
(d) 1975, established by the Norwegian Nobel Committee

**Solution:** Alfred Nobel's will (1895) created five Nobel Prizes, first awarded in **1901**. However, the **Economics** prize was added much later. The **Sveriges Riksbank** (Swedish central bank) instituted the **Prize in Economic Sciences in Memory of Alfred Nobel** in **1968**, and the first award was given in **1969**. Hence, option **(c)** is correct.

> ⚠️ **Examiner Trap:** Many candidates tick option (a) because they remember "Nobel = 1901." The Economics prize is technically *not* one of the original five Nobel Prizes; it is a memorial prize administered by the Nobel Foundation but funded and established by the Riksbank.

#### Edge Cases and Award Traps

| Trap | What students write | Correct fact |
|---|---|---|
| Nobel Peace Prize location | Stockholm | Norwegian Nobel Committee, Oslo |
| Nobel Literature prize body | Norwegian Academy | Swedish Academy, Stockholm |
| Olympic motto origin | Ancient Latin / Coubertin's birth | Adopted 1921 by IOC; "Communis" added 2021 |
| Olympic flag | Five rings = five countries | Five **interlocking** rings = five **continents** |
| Bharat Ratna vs Param Vir Chakra | Both "highest" | Bharat Ratna = **civilian** (1954); Param Vir Chakra = **military** (1950) |

#### Advanced Practice Prompts

1. **Hypothetical:** If a question lists four civilian honours of Pakistan in random order and asks which is highest, the candidate should select **Nishan-e-Imtiaz** without hesitation. Practice by writing the five awards ten times in the correct descending order.
2. **Date drill:** Quiz yourself with the **important-days** table on whether 7 March is World Health Day (No, it's 7 April) and whether 22 April is Environment Day (No, it's Earth Day; Environment Day is 5 June). Conflating these two is one of the highest-frequency errors in SPSC past papers.
