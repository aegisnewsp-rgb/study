# Current Affairs

In our syllabus analysis across recent CUET General Test papers, current affairs accounts for six to eight questions covering national government schemes, multilateral summits, scientific milestones, and global rankings. We recommend that candidates prioritize events from the preceding 12 to 18 months, focusing on structured factual categories rather than isolated trivia.

## Global Summits and Multilateral Alliances

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your CUET General Test examination.

Multilateral diplomacy questions evaluate knowledge of international treaties, newly admitted member states, rotating presidencies, and official summit themes.

#### Key Multilateral Summits and Global Groupings

| International Forum | Member Composition | Recent Presidency / Summit Location | Official Theme / Landmark Decision |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **G20 (Group of Twenty)** | 19 nations + European Union + African Union (admitted 2023) | New Delhi, India (2023) followed by Rio de Janeiro, Brazil (2024) | *"Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam"* (One Earth, One Family, One Future); permanent induction of African Union |
| **BRICS** | Brazil, Russia, India, China, South Africa + 5 new members (Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran, Saudi Arabia, UAE) | Kazan, Russia (2024); Johannesburg, South Africa (2023) | Historic expansion to BRICS-10; alternative cross-border currency settlement mechanisms |
| **SCO (Shanghai Cooperation Organisation)** | 10 member states (Belarus admitted as 10th member in 2024, Iran in 2023) | Astana, Kazakhstan (2024); New Delhi (virtual, 2023) | Astana Declaration focusing on regional security and counter-terrorism cooperation |
| **Quad** | India, United States, Japan, Australia | Wilmington, Delaware, USA (2024); Hiroshima, Japan (2023) | Indo-Pacific maritime domain awareness, semiconductor supply chain resilience |
| **COP (UN Climate Change Conference)** | 198 Parties to the UNFCCC | COP28 (Dubai, UAE, 2023); COP29 (Baku, Azerbaijan, 2024) | UAE Consensus: historic agreement to transition away from fossil fuels in energy systems |

⚡ **High-Yield CUET Tip:** When reviewing multilateral summits, note newly admitted members. Belarus became the 10th member of the SCO in July 2024, and the African Union became a permanent member of the G20 under India's presidency in September 2023.

## Major Government Schemes and Economic Indicators

#### 1. Flagship Indian National Welfare Schemes

Socioeconomic initiatives launched by the Government of India frequently appear in the General Test. Questions evaluate the implementing nodal ministry, financial allocation, eligibility thresholds, and primary developmental targets:

| National Scheme | Nodal Ministry | Financial Assistance / Target | Core Policy Objective |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **PM-KISAN** (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi) | Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare | Rs 6,000 per year paid in three equal installments of Rs 2,000 directly to bank accounts | Income support to all landholding farmer families across India |
| **Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY** | Ministry of Health and Family Welfare | Health cover of Rs 5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary hospitalization | World's largest government-funded healthcare assurance scheme for vulnerable families |
| **PM Awas Yojana (PMAY)** | Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs / Ministry of Rural Development | Financial grants ranging from Rs 1.2 lakh to Rs 2.5 lakh for pucca housing | Housing for all in rural and urban areas with tap water and sanitation |
| **PM Vishwakarma Scheme** | Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises | Collateral-free loans up to Rs 3 lakh at subsidized 5% interest rate + skill training | End-to-end support to traditional artisans and craftspeople across 18 trades |
| **PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana** | Ministry of New and Renewable Energy | Subsidy up to Rs 78,000 for installing rooftop solar units up to 3 kW capacity | Providing up to 300 units of free electricity monthly to one crore households |

#### 2. Strategic Anatomy of a Current Affairs MCQ

CUET General Test MCQs follow a predictable stem structure containing three components:
1. **The Entity:** The specific institution, treaty, exercise, or personality.
2. **The Modifier:** The calendar year, hosting city, or edition.
3. **The Target Ask:** The partnering nation, recipient name, or implementing agency.

##### Worked Practice Example 1

**Question:**  
The bilateral naval exercise *Varuna 2024* was conducted by the Indian Navy with the navy of which foreign nation in the Mediterranean Sea?  
- (A) United States  
- (B) France  
- (C) United Kingdom  
- (D) Japan  

*Step-by-Step Solution:*  
- *Entity:* Exercise *Varuna* represents a long-standing bilateral maritime exercise.  
- *Association:* France conducts *Varuna* (naval), *Garuda* (air force), and *Shakti* (army) with India.  
- *Counter-checks:* United States conducts *Malabar* (multilateral) and *Yudh Abhyas* (army). United Kingdom conducts *Konkan* (naval) and *Ajeya Warrior* (army). Japan conducts *JIMEX* (naval) and *Dharma Guardian* (army).  
*Correct Answer:* **(B) France**.

##### Worked Practice Example 2

**Question:**  
Which international sports personality was conferred the Men's Ballon d'Or in October 2024 as the world's best footballer?  
- (A) Vinicius Junior  
- (B) Jude Bellingham  
- (C) Rodri (Rodrigo Hernandez)  
- (D) Kylian Mbappe  

*Step-by-Step Solution:*  
- *Entity:* Ballon d'Or 2024 awarded by France Football.  
- *Recipient:* Manchester City and Spain midfielder **Rodri** won the award following Spain's victory at UEFA Euro 2024.  
*Correct Answer:* **(C) Rodri**.
