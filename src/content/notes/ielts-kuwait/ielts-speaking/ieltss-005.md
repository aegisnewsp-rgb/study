# Lexical Resource in Spoken English — IELTS Speaking Notes

Lexical Resource is one of the four official IELTS Speaking band descriptors. It rewards a range of vocabulary, accurate word choice, and the ability to paraphrase the question. Strong lexical resource helps the candidate reach band 7+.

> Verify the live Speaking band descriptors on https://ielts.org/ before planning revision.

---

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)

#### Core facts in one pass

- **Paraphrasing:** restate the question using different words. Example: "Do you like cooking?" → "Yes, I enjoy cooking."
- **Range:** use a variety of vocabulary across the three parts.
- **Accuracy:** choose words that fit the context and collocate properly.
- **Collocations:** word combinations that sound natural to native speakers (e.g. "make a decision," not "do a decision").
- **Topic-specific vocabulary:** words related to common Part 1-3 topics.

#### Examiner traps

- Using the same word repeatedly — repetition caps the lexical resource band.
- Using a word that does not fit the context — accuracy matters.
- Using long words that you are not sure of — risk losing the band.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)

#### Paraphrasing the question

Paraphrasing restates the question using different words. Example:

- "Do you enjoy reading?" → "Yes, I find reading very relaxing."
- "What's your favourite type of food?" → "I would say I prefer Middle Eastern cuisine."
- "How often do you exercise?" → "I try to work out three times a week."

Paraphrasing shows you understood the question and have the vocabulary to restate it.

#### Topic-specific vocabulary

Build vocabulary by topic:

- **Family:** siblings, parents, relatives, extended family, upbringing.
- **Work:** profession, colleagues, schedule, deadlines, workplace.
- **Education:** university, degree, major, scholarship, graduation.
- **Travel:** destination, itinerary, accommodation, transportation, sightseeing.
- **Food:** cuisine, ingredients, recipe, flavour, delicious.
- **Sports:** athlete, training, competition, championship, fitness.

#### Collocations

Memorise common collocations:

- **make:** make a decision, make progress, make an effort, make a mistake, make sense.
- **do:** do homework, do research, do a favour, do well, do the dishes.
- **have:** have a break, have a look, have a conversation, have an experience.
- **take:** take a photo, take a break, take a shower, take responsibility.
- **pay:** pay attention, pay a visit, pay a compliment.

### 🔴 Deep — Long-Term Mastery (1mo–6mo)

#### Where Lexical Resource appears across IELTS Speaking

Lexical Resource is one of the four band descriptors and contributes to the Speaking band. Strong lexical resource helps the candidate reach band 7+.

#### Common misconceptions (and the correction)

- "Long words always score higher." No — accuracy matters more than complexity.
- "Memorised phrases score high." Examiners recognise them; the band drops.
- "Topic-specific vocabulary is enough." No — academic and abstract vocabulary is also required, especially for Part 3.

#### Exam technique

- Paraphrase the question in your answer.
- Use a variety of vocabulary across the three parts.
- Memorise common collocations.
- Build topic-specific vocabulary for common Part 1-3 topics.
