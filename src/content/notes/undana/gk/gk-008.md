# Topic in the UNDANA Admission Universitas Nusa Cendana

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

Topic 8 in the UNDANA Admission (Universitas Nusa Cendana) General Knowledge block tests **penalaran umum** (general reasoning) and **pengetahuan umum terapan** (applied general knowledge), not rote memorisation. The Ujian Mandiri follows the SNBT/UTBK-SNPMB style, so the eight thematic blocks together probe literacy, numeracy, and scientific-social reasoning on short passages.

- **Core skill set:** distinguish fact vs. opinion vs. argument in a wacana; identify the ide pokok and simpulan; map verbal information into a table or simple grafik; compute percentages, ratios, and proportions.
- **Subject mix in this block:** Bahasa Indonesia literacy, Bahasa Inggris reading comprehension, IPA terapan, IPS terapan, and Matematika dasar.
- **Exam pointer:** read every passage fully before answering; trap words like **TIDAK**, **KECUALI**, and **PALING tepat** invert the correct choice.

| Domain | What you must do |
| --- | --- |
| Verbal | Pull the main idea and tone from a 1-paragraph wacana |
| Numerical | Compute %, ratio, proportion from a stem |
| Visual | Translate a grafik/tabel into a one-sentence answer |

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### What Topic 8 actually measures

UNDANA's Soal Tes Potensi mirrors the SNBT UTBK-SNPMB framework. Topic 8 sits inside the **Pengetahuan Umum** section, which carries roughly 3% of total soal weight. It is not a history or geography recall test. Instead, every prompt supplies a short stimulus — a wacana, a table, or a diagram — and asks you to reason from it. The stimulus can be in Bahasa Indonesia or Bahasa Inggris, and the subject label (IPA, IPS, Matematika) tells you which lens to apply, not which textbook fact to recite.

#### Core question types you will meet

- **Fakta, opini, argumen:** classify each sentence in a 4–6 line editorial fragment.
- **Ide pokok & simpulan:** pick the option that best summarises a paragraph.
- **Sinonim, antonim, hubungan makna:** word-pair analogy in Indonesian context.
- **Visual-to-verbal translation:** read a bar chart, table, or pictogram, then answer a numeric or comparative item.
- **Numeric reasoning:** simple operasi dasar, persentase, rasio, proporsi, in many papers embedded in a one-paragraph wacana about a local NTT or national scenario.

#### Worked relationship between wacana and answer

The wacana is the only source of truth. Each item presents a 60–120 word passage, then asks one question. The correct answer must be (a) directly supported by the passage or a logical consequence of it, and (b) consistent with the domain label (IPA framing uses satuan and cause-effect; IPS framing uses actor-institution-context).

| Concept | Key point |
| --- | --- |
| Fakta vs opini | Fakta can be verified from the wacana; opini uses evaluative language like "sebaiknya", "menurut saya". |
| Ide pokok | In many papers the first or last sentence; check against the repeated noun phrase. |
| Trap words | TIDAK, KECUALI, PALING tepat — underlining them prevents sign-flip errors. |
| Numerasi dasar | % change = (baru − lama)/lama × 100%; ratio a:b = a/b. |

#### Common mistake pattern

Students answer from prior knowledge instead of from the passage. UNDANA items are deliberately written so that real-world familiarity can mislead — the passage can state a figure that contradicts common assumption, and only the passage counts.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### How Topic 8 fits the broader UNDANA Admission scheme

UNDANA Admission aggregates eight thematic blocks across the Soal Tes Potensi. Topic 8 is the **applied general knowledge** block, sitting between pure penalaran induktif/deduktif (earlier blocks) and domain-specific Matematika/IPA (later blocks). Its 3% weight is small, but it is the most *heterogeneous* block: a single sitting can mix an English cloze, a Batik/NTT budaya passage, and a profit-margin percentage problem.

#### Edge cases and traps

- **Konteks NTT and nasional:** passages in many papers reference local culture (tenun ikat, rumah adat, bahasa daerah) or national policy (pendidikan, ekonomi kreatif). Trap items use plausible but off-topic local details to bait surface readers.
- **Unit conversion inside IPA prompts:** kelvin vs Celsius, meter vs kilometre, gram vs kilogram. The wacana states the unit; misreading it flips the numeric answer.
- **Sinonim that are not exact synonyms:** UNDANA rewards *nuansa* (connotation), not dictionary lookalikes. "Tangguh" and "kuat" overlap but are not interchangeable in context.
- **Two-passage inference:** some items require combining info from two short stimuli — read both before eliminating options.

#### Connections to adjacent topics

Topic 8 shares its passage-reasoning DNA with **penalaran umum** blocks but differs by adding subject-specific framing (IPA/IPS/Matematika). Strong performance here raises your **literasi membaca** and **numerasi** sub-scores, which feed into the SNPMB-style composite used for prodi selection.

#### Practice prompts

1. A 90-word wacana describes a desa in Timor Tengah Selatan switching from corn to vanilla; a table shows yield and price. Compute the percentage revenue change per hectare and decide whether the switch is **paling tepat** described as "produktif secara ekonomi". Answer using only the table — ignore outside knowledge.
2. A short English passage states that three Batik motifs have different permitted uses. Which motif **TIDAK** can be used commercially? Underline TIDAK first, then match the negative constraint against each motif.

| Pitfall | How to avoid |
| --- | --- |
| Answering from memory | Re-read the relevant sentence before locking the option |
| Ignoring trap words | Circle TIDAK / KECUALI / PALING tepat before reading options |
| Mixing IPA and IPS frames | Check the subject label; switch your analytical lens accordingly |
| Skipping the grafik | Visual items hide the answer in axis labels or legend |
