# Medicine, Pharmacy and Health Sciences — IJMB for Medical Careers

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

IJMB (Interim Joint Matriculation Board) is an Advanced Level programme run by Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria, that qualifies candidates for Direct Entry (DE) admission into 200-level of Nigerian universities. For Medicine, Pharmacy and Health Sciences faculties, the required IJMB combination is **Physics, Chemistry and Biology (PCB)**, plus Use of English and a fourth elective (in most keys Mathematics or Economics).

| Term | Meaning for Health-Sciences DE |
|---|---|
| IJMB A/L | 9–12 month ABU-affiliated programme granting A/B/C grades |
| Direct Entry (DE) | Admission route into 200-level via A/L result, bypassing UTME screening |
| PCB | Compulsory trio: Physics, Chemistry, Biology at A/L |
| UTME prerequisite | 5 O'Level credits (English, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology) |
| JAMB CAPS | Upload portal where IJMB grades are screened by universities |
| Cut-off target | Most schools accept at minimum **BBC** across PCB; competitive courses need higher |

> 💡 **High-Yield Memory Hook:** **"PCB Saves Med-Life"** → **P**hysics, **C**hemistry, **B**iology are the THREE non-negotiable IJMB subjects; missing one means no Medicine, Pharmacy, Nursing or Medical Lab Science admission in Nigeria.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### What IJMB Actually Is

IJMB is not a written exam you "sit" once. It is a moderated A-Level programme delivered over roughly 9 to 12 months at ABU-accredited study centres across Nigeria. At the end, candidates receive graded A/L certificates in three to four subjects, which JAMB recognises as equivalent to Cambridge A-Level for the purpose of Direct Entry admission.

#### The Standard Subject Package

The Medicine, Pharmacy and Health Sciences combination almost always requires **Physics, Chemistry and Biology** as the three graded A/L subjects. A fourth subject (in many papers General Mathematics or Economics) is taken alongside, and Use of English is mandatory. Universities vary on whether the fourth has to be Mathematics — always check the DE brochure.

| Faculty | Required IJMB trio | Typical 4th subject | Minimum grade pattern |
|---|---|---|---|
| Medicine & Surgery | PCB | Mathematics (in many papers compulsory) | AAB or ABB at top schools |
| Pharmacy | PCB | Mathematics (most schools) | ABB to BBB |
| Nursing | PCB | Mathematics or Economics | BBC widely accepted |
| Medical Laboratory Science | PCB | Mathematics or Economics | BCC to BBC |
| Anatomy / Physiology / Public Health | PCB | Mathematics or Economics | BCC in most keys sufficient |

#### How Admission Actually Happens

A candidate's IJMB grade is uploaded to **JAMB CAPS** (Central Admissions Processing System) and screened by the DE committee of the chosen university. Selection is merit-based on the combined IJMB point score, UTME aggregate, and any post-UTME or DE screening score. The IJMB result is the principal screening credential — there is no separate "IJMB entrance exam" with a percentage weight.

#### Key Eligibility Chain

1. Register JAMB UTME form and obtain a valid UTME score (even if you plan DE).
2. Register with an ABU-accredited IJMB centre for the PCB programme.
3. Complete the 9–12 month intensive contact sessions and sit A/L papers.
4. Secure 5 O'Level credits including English, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology (at one or two sittings — university-specific).
5. Upload IJMB result on JAMB CAPS and apply to chosen university's DE admission list.

#### 🎯 Exam-Level Worked Problem

**Question:** A 17-year-old candidate wants to study Medicine and Surgery at the University of Lagos through IJMB Direct Entry. Which combination is correct, and what is the minimum grade pattern in standard papers demanded?

#### Solution:

- Required A/L trio = **Physics, Chemistry, Biology (PCB)**.
- Required fourth subject = **Mathematics** (UNILAG's Faculty of Medicine specifies Mathematics, not Economics).
- Minimum published IJMB grade pattern for Medicine at most top schools = **AAB** at A/L, meaning two A grades and one B in PCB.
- The candidate must also upload via JAMB CAPS after completing IJMB and hold 5 O'Level credits (English, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology) at one sitting.

> ⚠️ **Examiner Trap:** Picking **Economics** as the fourth subject when applying to UNILAG Medicine — UNILAG's DE brochure specifies Mathematics; Economics alone leads to automatic disqualification at screening, regardless of A-grades in PCB.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Edge Cases and Common Pitfalls

| Pitfall | Why it bites | How to avoid it |
|---|---|---|
| Skipping JAMB UTME before IJMB | DE form without UTME history is rejected by CAPS | Register UTME same year, even if score is low |
| Two-sitting O'Level credits | Schools like UNILAG, OAU demand one sitting | Confirm sitting policy in DE brochure before enrolment |
| Confusing IJMB with JUPEB | JUPEB uses a different syllabus and grading | Check the certificate header says "IJMB" / ABU Zaria |
| Targeting Medicine only | Slots are <30 per accredited college | Apply to Pharmacy, MLS or Nursing as backup DE choices |
| Missing catchment / ELDS policy | Some states favour indigenes for DE slots | Cross-check school's catchment list before paying DE fee |

#### Connections to Adjacent Pathways

IJMB sits alongside **JUPEB** (another DE A/L route, run by some federal universities), **NCE**, **NABTEB A/L**, and **Pre-Degree** as alternative entry paths into 200-level. JUPEB has a similar 9-month duration but a separate syllabus and grading scale. NABTEB A/L is accepted for DE but is widely considered less competitive for Medicine than IJMB. Pre-Degree is a 1-year remedial programme that does not, by itself, grant A/L status — it only leads to internal university admission in limited cases.

#### Strategic Considerations

IJMB carries a "weight" of 3% in the Subject-Combinations syllabus because the choice of combination is the gateway — every other health-science topic (admission screening, catchment policy, JAMB CAPS upload) flows from getting PCB right. Strong A/B grades in PCB dominate admission outcomes at UNILAG, OAU, UI, UNIBEN, UNIJOS, ABU, UNILORIN and other federal schools. For Pharmacy, several schools (e.g. UNIBEN, OAU) accept Mathematics as the fourth but permit Economics if Mathematics is passed at O'Level — always verify per institution.
