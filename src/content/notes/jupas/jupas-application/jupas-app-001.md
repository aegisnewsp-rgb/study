# JUPAS Account and Application Timeline — JUPAS Notes

JUPAS application runs on a fixed annual cycle that maps to the HKDSE results release. This topic covers the JUPAS account, the JUPAS ID, and the 2026 entry cycle timeline (application period October-December 2025; HKDSE results release; modification window; Main Round offer; Clearing Round). The JUPAS cycle for each entry year is published by the Joint University Programmes Admissions System Office each spring; re-check the live calendar on jupas.edu.hk before committing any revision plan.

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)

#### Core facts in one pass

- **JUPAS account**: created through the JUPAS online system (https://www.jupas.edu.hk/). The account is associated with a JUPAS ID (a unique identifier for the applicant).
- **JUPAS cycle (typical)**: account registration and initial choice entry October-December; HKDSE results release in July; modification window mid-July (after HKDSE release); Main Round offer in early August; Clearing Round after Main Round.
- **2026 entry cycle** (verified above): Main Round offer 5 August 2026 9am; modification window 16-18 July 2026 with 48-hour allocated slot per applicant (checking from 24 June 2026); Clearing Round after Main Round.
- **Application fee**: charged per cycle. Re-check the fee schedule on jupas.edu.hk for the candidate's cycle — both change year to year.
- **Eligibility**: candidates who have sat the HKDSE in the current cycle or in the previous cycles (subject to the live eligibility rules), and have not yet been admitted to a UGC-funded degree programme.

#### Examiner traps

- Confusing JUPAS with non-JUPAS admissions — JUPAS is only for HKDSE candidates applying to UGC-funded or HKMU self-financing degree programmes. International applicants apply through non-JUPAS routes.
- Missing the registration deadline — JUPAS registration typically closes in early December for the next year's admission.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)

#### The JUPAS cycle for the 2026 entry

**October-December 2025**: account registration, programme choice entry. Candidates create their JUPAS account, fill in personal and academic details, and list up to 20 programme choices across Bands A, B and C.

**January-May 2026**: candidates can modify their choices freely before the HKDSE release. School Principal's Nominations (SPN) are processed during this period.

**Mid-July 2026**: HKDSE results are released. Candidates receive a 48-hour allocated time slot (announced from 24 June 2026) in which they may modify their programme choices (16-18 July 2026).

**5 August 2026 9am**: Main Round offer date. Successful applicants receive one offer (UGC-funded, HKMU self-financing, or SSSDP). Acceptance fee HK$5,000 must be paid by the deadline (typically 6 August 2026 5pm).

**After Main Round**: Clearing Round for unfilled places. Candidates who did not receive a Main Round offer may modify their choices and participate in Clearing Round.

#### Key documents and information

- **HKDSE results**: candidates enter their HKDSE results in the JUPAS system.
- **Other qualifications**: candidates with non-HKDSE qualifications may also apply (separate routes).
- **Personal statement / school reference**: required for some programmes.
- **Predicted grades**: schools provide predicted grades before HKDSE results are released.
- **Application fee**: paid per cycle.

#### Common JUPAS IDs and codes

- **JUPAS ID**: unique identifier for each applicant, used in correspondence.
- **Programme code**: each programme at each institution has a unique JUPAS code (e.g. JS1234 for a specific programme at a specific institution).
- **School code**: each school has a unique code for SPN processing.

### 🔴 Deep — Long-Term Mastery (1mo–6mo)

#### Cross-topic links

- **JUPAS scoring**: the application feeds into the admission-score calculation.
- **JUPAS offers**: the Main Round and Clearing Round follow from the modification and submission.
- **Non-JUPAS routes**: international qualifications and lateral entry.

#### Exam technique

- **For timeline questions**: state the calendar dates in order; identify the critical path.
- **For eligibility questions**: state the HKDSE requirement, the maximum age (none), and the application fee.

#### Common misconceptions (and the correction)

- "JUPAS is the only way to apply." Non-JUPAS routes exist for international qualifications and lateral entry.
- "Once submitted, choices cannot be changed." Choices can be modified freely before the HKDSE release, and during the 48-hour allocated slot after.
- "JUPAS covers private universities." JUPAS covers UGC-funded and HKMU self-financing programmes; private universities typically have their own admissions systems.
