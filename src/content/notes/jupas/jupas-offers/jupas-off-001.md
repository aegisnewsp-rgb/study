# Main Round Offer — JUPAS Notes

This topic covers the JUPAS Main Round offer process. The Main Round is announced on a single date (for the 2026 entry: 5 August 2026, 9:00 am). Each successful applicant receives at most ONE offer from: a UGC-funded programme; an HKMU self-financing programme; or an SSSDP programme. Offers are made through the applicant's JUPAS account. Main Round offers must be accepted by paying an acceptance fee (HK$5,000 for the 2026 cycle) by the published deadline — typically the day after the offer.

> Verify the live Main Round offer date and acceptance fee deadline on https://www.jupas.edu.hk/ before planning revision.

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)

#### Core facts in one pass

- **Main Round offer date (2026 cycle)**: 5 August 2026, 9:00 am.
- **One offer per applicant**: each successful applicant receives at most one offer from one of three funding categories (UGC-funded, HKMU self-financing, or SSSDP).
- **Offer announcement**: through the applicant's JUPAS account and the published media channels.
- **Acceptance fee**: HK$5,000 for the 2026 cycle; must be paid by the deadline (typically the day after the offer).
- **Payment channels**: PPS, JPP, bank counters, JUPAS online system (check the live channels list).

#### Examiner traps

- Missing the acceptance fee deadline — forfeits the offer.
- Assuming offers are made to the highest-scoring Band A choice only — JUPAS uses a more complex algorithm.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)

#### How Main Round offers are determined

The JUPAS Main Round algorithm:

1. Process all candidates in order of their overall score.
2. For each candidate, identify the highest-band programme that admits them.
3. Assign that programme as the offer.

A candidate's offer is determined by:

- The candidate's overall score (calculated from the programme's scoring formula).
- The candidate's programme choices (band structure).
- The minimum entrance requirements of the chosen programmes.
- The number of places available in the chosen programmes.

Each candidate receives at most ONE Main Round offer.

#### Example scenarios

**Scenario 1: Top-tier score, Band A programme admits**.

- Candidate's score: 32 (top tier).
- Band A: Medicine at HKU, Engineering at HKUST, Computer Science at CUHK.
- Band B: Engineering at CityU.
- Band C: Engineering at PolyU.
- Result: Medicine at HKU offers; candidate receives Medicine offer.

**Scenario 2: Mid-tier score, only Band B admits**.

- Candidate's score: 25 (mid tier).
- Band A: Medicine at HKU, Engineering at HKUST, Computer Science at CUHK.
- Band B: Engineering at CityU.
- Band C: Engineering at PolyU.
- Result: No Band A programme admits (cutoffs are higher). Engineering at CityU admits; candidate receives Engineering at CityU offer.

**Scenario 3: Below-tier score, no programme admits**.

- Candidate's score: 18 (below tier).
- Result: No programme admits. Candidate does not receive a Main Round offer. The candidate may participate in Clearing Round.

#### Acceptance fee and procedures

**Acceptance fee**: HK$5,000 for the 2026 cycle. This is a non-refundable fee paid to confirm acceptance of the offer.

**Payment channels** (check the live list on jupas.edu.hk):

- PPS (Payment by Phone Service)
- JPP (JUPAS Payment Portal — online credit/debit card payment)
- Bank counters (in Hong Kong)
- JUPAS online system

**Deadline**: typically the day after the offer day. For the 2026 cycle: 6 August 2026, 5:00 pm (check the live calendar on jupas.edu.hk).

**Failure to pay**: forfeits the offer. The candidate may participate in Clearing Round but the forfeited programme is not available.

#### After acceptance

- The candidate is committed to the programme (subject to the published withdrawal rules).
- The candidate must enrol at the admitting institution during the published registration window.
- Failure to enrol forfeits the offer.

#### Exam technique

- **For "describe the Main Round process" questions**: state the offer date, the algorithm, the acceptance fee, the deadline.
- **For "explain the offer algorithm" questions**: state the band system and the one-offer rule.

#### Common misconceptions

- "The Main Round always offers the highest-band programme." Yes, but only if the candidate is admissible — i.e. meets minimum entrance requirements AND has a competitive score.
- "All programmes offer all admissible candidates." No — places are limited. Only the top candidates are admitted.
