### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### Sports terminology and tournament cycles

A **velodrome** is an indoor banked track for track cycling; basketball uses **5 players** per side, volleyball **6**, while cricket, football and field hockey use **11** each. The Summer Olympics, FIFA World Cup, ICC Cricket World Cup and Commonwealth Games each follow a **four-year cycle**, though the ICC event has both an ODI World Cup and a separate T20 World Cup on staggered four-year schedules.

> **Trap:** students often treat "World Cup" as a single event — the ICC runs distinct ODI and T20 tournaments, each on its own calendar.

#### Civil and sports honours

Pakistan's civil awards, in descending order, are **Nishan-e-Imtiaz**, **Hilal-e-Imtiaz**, **Sitara-e-Imtiaz**, and **Tamgha-e-Imtiaz**, with **Pride of Performance** granted for distinguished merit in arts, sports, or public service. India parallels this with the **Padma Awards** (Vibhushan > Bhushan > Shri), the **Arjuna Award** (instituted 1961) for sporting excellence, and the **Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award** — India's highest sporting honour, originally instituted in 1991–92 as the Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna and renamed in 2021.

| Award (Pakistan) | Domain |
|---|---|
| Nishan-e-Imtiaz | Highest state honour |
| Hilal-e-Imtiaz | Second tier, military/civil |
| Pride of Performance | Arts, sports, public service |

#### Nobel Prize essentials

Instituted in **1901** by Alfred Nobel's will, the prize has **six categories**: Physics, Chemistry, Physiology or Medicine, Literature, Peace, and the **Sveriges Riksbank Prize in Economic Sciences in Memory of Alfred Nobel** (added 1969, not an original 1901 category). The Peace Prize is awarded in **Oslo, Norway**; all other categories are awarded in **Stockholm, Sweden**. Pakistani laureates include **Abdus Salam** (Physics, 1979) and **Malala Yousafzai** (Peace, 2014).

#### Important days

Pakistan's four national days are fixed: **23 March** (Lahore Resolution, 1940), **14 August** (Independence, 1947), **9 November** (Allama Iqbal's birth anniversary), and **25 December** (Quaid-e-Azam's birth anniversary). Common UN observances include World Health Day (**7 April**), World Environment Day (**5 June**), International Literacy Day (**8 September**), World Students' Day (**15 October**), Universal Children's Day (**20 November**), and Human Rights Day (**10 December**).

> **Exam pattern:** SPSC typically frames 1–2 MCQs as "On which date is … observed?" or "The Nobel Peace Prize is awarded in which city?"

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Edge cases in awards

The **Pride of Performance** is administered by the President of Pakistan on the advice of the Prime Minister and is conferred for excellence in **sports, arts, literature, or public service** — broader in scope than the purely sporting Arjuna Award. The Padma Awards are announced annually on **Republic Day eve** and the three grades carry a fixed civil precedence; confusing Bhushan with Shri is a common MCQ trap.

The **Khel Ratna** carries a medal, a citation, and a cash component; renaming it after Major Dhyan Chand in 2021 was the first time India's highest sporting honour was renamed since inception.

#### Edge cases in Nobel facts

The Economics prize is technically **not a Nobel**, but the official branding, medal design, and ceremony at the Stockholm Concert Hall make it indistinguishable to most test-takers. Students should remember the **full statutory name** only when an option explicitly uses it.

#### Pakistan national days — deep distinctions

- **Pakistan Day (23 March):** commemorates the **Lahore Resolution of 1940**, not independence.
- **Iqbal Day (9 November):** Allama Iqbal's **birth anniversary**, not death.
- **Quaid-e-Azam Day (25 December):** Muhammad Ali Jinnah's **birth anniversary**; his death anniversary is 11 September.

> **Common mistake:** swapping birth and death anniversaries of Iqbal (21 April vs 9 November) and Jinnah (11 September vs 25 December) costs easy marks.
