# Clearing Round — JUPAS Notes

This topic covers the JUPAS Clearing Round. The Clearing Round is a second-round offer process for unfilled Main Round places and for applicants who received no Main Round offer. Clearing Round is announced after the Main Round acceptance deadlines. Applicants may modify their choices before Clearing Round (subject to the published modification rules); Clearing Round offers follow the same offer-day format as Main Round.

> Verify the live Clearing Round dates on https://www.jupas.edu.hk/ before planning revision.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)

#### How Clearing Round works

**Timing**: announced after Main Round acceptance deadlines. For the 2026 cycle, Main Round acceptance deadline is 6 August 2026; Clearing Round is typically later in August.

**Eligible candidates**:

- Applicants who did not receive a Main Round offer.
- Applicants who declined a Main Round offer (or did not pay the acceptance fee).
- Applicants who held a Main Round offer but wish to seek a different programme through Clearing Round (subject to rules).

**Choice modification**: applicants may modify their programme choices before Clearing Round (subject to the published rules). The modification may include adding new programmes from the Clearing Round list (which may differ from the Main Round programme list).

**Offer day**: Clearing Round offers are announced on a single date, following the same format as Main Round. Each successful applicant receives at most one offer.

#### Strategy for Clearing Round

**Strategy 1: Re-rank based on Clearing Round availability**.

Some programmes that were not in the Main Round choice list may be available in Clearing Round. Research the Clearing Round programme list carefully.

**Strategy 2: Consider less popular programmes**.

Programmes with unfilled Main Round places are typically less popular, but they may still offer good career prospects. Research the programme content and career paths.

**Strategy 3: Be flexible on band structure**.

Clearing Round choices can have a different band structure than Main Round. Use the band system strategically.

**Strategy 4: Have a backup plan**.

If Clearing Round does not work out, consider:

- Non-JUPAS admissions for the following year.
- Self-financing programmes.
- Sub-degree programmes (e.g. HKMU sub-degree, SSSDP).
- International programmes.

#### Common misconceptions

- "Clearing Round has the same cutoffs as Main Round." No — Clearing Round cutoffs may be lower because some programmes have unfilled places.
- "All programmes are available in Clearing Round." No — only programmes with unfilled places participate.
- "Clearing Round is for weak students only." No — many strong students use Clearing Round to switch to a different programme.
