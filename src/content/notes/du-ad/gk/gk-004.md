# Culture, Society, and Politics of Bangladesh

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

Bangladesh is a **unitary parliamentary republic** in South Asia, born from the 1971 Liberation War (26 March – 16 December) against Pakistan. The nation revolves around the Bangla language, secular constitutional values amended over time, and a unicameral **Jatiya Sangsad** (parliament) with 350 seats. Dhaka is the legislative and administrative capital, situated on the Buriganga River.

| Anchor | Exact Fact |
|---|---|
| Liberation War | 26 March – 16 December 1971 |
| Victory Day | 16 December 1971 |
| International Mother Language Day | 21 February (Ekushey February, since 1999 UNESCO) |
| Independence Day | 26 March |
| Parliament seats | 350 (300 elected + 50 reserved for women) |
| Head of state / government | President (ceremonial) / Prime Minister (executive) |
| Constitution | 1972, amended (notably 5th, 7th, 13th Amendments) |

> 💡 **High-Yield Memory Hook:** **"B-I-D-E-S-S"** — the four original pillars of the 1972 Constitution: **B**angladesh nationalism, **I**ndependence, **DE**mocracy, **S**ecularism, **S**ocialism. Add **L**ight = **B-I-D-E-S-S-L** for **L**iberation War 1971. Quick mnemonic to recall constitutional values + war date.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### Core Constitutional Pillars

The 1972 Constitution framed the new nation on four state principles: **nationalism, secularism, socialism, and democracy**. The **5th Amendment (1979)** inserted "Bismillahir Rahmanir Rahim" and made **Islam the state religion**, while removing secularism. The **13th Amendment (2011)** abolished the Caretaker Government system after the 15th Amendment reinforced the unicameral parliament.

#### Political Structure & Parties

The President is elected by parliament for a five-year term and acts as ceremonial head. The Prime Minister, leader of the majority party, holds executive power. The **Jatiya Sangsad Bhaban** (designed by Louis Kahn, completed 1982) houses the legislature. Major parties include the **Bangladesh Awami League (AL)**, **Bangladesh Nationalist Party (BNP)** founded by Ziaur Rahman in 1978, **Jatiya Party (Ershad faction)**, and **Bangladesh Jamaat-e-Islami**.

#### Society, Language, and Religion

The **Language Movement of 1947–1952** led to the martyrdom of Salam, Barkat, Rafiquddin, Jabbar, and Shafiur on **21 February 1952**, prompting the **Shaheed Minar** monument in Dhaka. Ethnolinguistically, ~98% are Bengali, with indigenous groups (Chakma, Marma, Garo, Santal, Tripura) concentrated in the **Chittagong Hill Tracts (CHT)**. Religion: Muslim ~90%, Hindu ~8%, Buddhist, Christian, others.

#### Comparison Matrix: Confusable Concepts

| Concept A | Concept B | Distinction |
|---|---|---|
| Bengali nationalism | Bangladeshi nationalism | Former is ethnolinguistic; latter is multi-ethnic civic nationalism (1972 Constitution original usage) |
| Victory Day (16 Dec) | Independence Day (26 Mar) | Victory marks Pakistani surrender; Independence marks the start of the war |
| Six-Point Movement (1966) | Four-State Formula | Six-point was Sheikh Mujib's autonomy charter for East Pakistan; Four-state was a 1947 federal proposal |
| Shaheed Minar | Savar National Monument | Language martyrs memorial (1952) vs Liberation War memorial (1971) |
| Caretaker Government | Interim Election Commission | CTG ran government; EC only administered elections |
| Jatiya Sangsad | Jatiya Sangsad Bhaban | Parliament (body) vs the building designed by Louis Kahn |

> 📌 **Formula Check:** Bangladesh has **one** legislative chamber (unicameral), not two. Any MCQ with "bicameral" or "upper house" is automatically wrong.

#### 🎯 Exam-Level Worked Problem

**Question:** Which constitutional amendment (a) abolished the Caretaker Government system, and (b) reinstated Islam as the state religion?

1. 5th Amendment / 13th Amendment
2. 13th Amendment / 5th Amendment
3. 7th Amendment / 4th Amendment
4. 15th Amendment / 13th Amendment

**Solution:** The **13th Amendment (2011)** abolished the Caretaker Government (CTGs had governed during 1996–2008 elections). The **5th Amendment (1979)** under President Ziaur Rahman made Islam the state religion. Hence the correct answer is **(2)**.

> ⚠️ **Examiner Trap:** Many candidates swap the two — they assume a "religious" amendment is later and a "governance" amendment is older. Memorize the order: **5th = religion, 13th = CTG abolition, 15th = 2011 consolidation**.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### River System and Geography

The **Padma-Meghna-Jamuna** system drains the Bengal Delta, fed by the Ganges (Padma) and Brahmaputra (Jamuna) before discharging into the **Bay of Bengal**. The **Sundarbans**, the world's largest mangrove forest, straddles the India-Bangladesh border and houses the Royal Bengal Tiger. Borders touch India (west, north, east) and Myanmar (southeast), with a maritime boundary defined by the 2014 ITLOS ruling.

#### Cultural Festivals and Identity

- **Pohela Boishakh** — Bengali New Year, 14 April (Mangal Shobhajatra festival declared UNESCO Intangible Heritage 2016).
- **Pahela Falgun** — Spring festival, 13 Falgun (Feb), celebrated at Dhaka University.
- **Ekushey February** — UNESCO International Mother Language Day (declared 1999).
- **Victory Day** — 16 December, national holiday with parade at Savar.
- **Independence Day** — 26 March.

#### Advanced Traps and Edge Cases

| Trap | Reality |
|---|---|
| Bangladesh is bicameral | Unicameral — 350 seats in Jatiya Sangsad |
| Caretaker Government still exists | Abolished 2011 (13th Amendment) |
| Liberation War = 16 December only | War spanned 26 March – 16 December 1971 |
| Ganges originates in Bangladesh | Ganges enters from India (Gangotri Glacier), exits as Padma |
| Constitution is permanently secular | 5th Amendment (1979) added Islam as state religion |
| Bangabandhu refers to a monument | "Bangabandhu" = title for Sheikh Mujibur Rahman ("Friend of Bengal") |

#### Regional and Economic Context

Bangladesh is a founding member of **SAARC (1985)** and **BIMSTEC**. The **readymade garment (RMG) sector** drives ~84% of exports, while **microfinance** (Grameen Bank, founded by Muhammad Yunus, 1983) reshaped rural credit access. The country ranks among the world's top remittance recipients, with labour migration to Saudi Arabia, UAE, and Malaysia.

2. **Factual trap:** Compare the **five** constitutional amendments (5th, 7th, 13th, 15th, and the original 1972 text) on the axis of religion-state relations and executive-legislative balance.
