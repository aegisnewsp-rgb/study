# General Science & Technology

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

The AILET **General Science & Technology** module tests Class 8–12 NCERT-level facts from Physics, Chemistry, Biology and a heavy dose of **Indian S&T current affairs** (ISRO, DRDO, nuclear, IT/AI). Expect roughly **5–8 MCQs** out of 150, sourced from the General Knowledge section (3% weight).

- **Memorise the six foundational formulas:** W = F·d·cosθ, KE = ½mv², PE = mgh, V = IR, P = VI = I²R, pH = −log₁₀[H⁺].
- **Lock in the SI base units** — newton (N), joule (J), watt (W), volt (V), ampere (A), ohm (Ω), kelvin (K) — unit traps are recurring.
- **Indian S&T high-yield names:** Chandrayaan-3 (2023, lunar south pole), Mangalyaan/MOM (2013), Gaganyaan (uncrewed test flights 2023–24), PSLV/GSLV launch vehicles, BrahMos missile, Tejas LCA, ITER participation, Kudankulam nuclear plant.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### Physics Core
Mechanics, heat, light, sound, electricity and modern physics form the static backbone. **Newton's three laws** govern motion; the work-energy theorem links W = F·d·cosθ with KE = ½mv², meaning the net work on a body equals its change in kinetic energy.

In electricity, **Ohm's Law (V = IR)** states that potential difference V across a conductor is directly proportional to current I, with R the constant of proportionality. Power dissipated equals P = VI, which expands to I²R or V²/R depending on the known variable.

**pH and acids/bases:** pH = −log₁₀[H⁺], where [H⁺] is the molar hydrogen-ion concentration. Values below 7 are acidic, above 7 are basic, and 7 is neutral at 25 °C — the logarithmic scale means each unit shift is a 10× change in [H⁺].

#### Chemistry Core
The **periodic table** trends — atomic radius decreases across a period, ionisation energy increases, electronegativity rises toward fluorine. Bonding splits into **ionic** (metal + non-metal, electron transfer) and **covalent** (non-metal + non-metal, electron sharing). Carbon compounds introduce functional groups (alcohol −OH, aldehyde −CHO, carboxylic acid −COOH) that drive organic chemistry questions.

#### Biology Core
The **cell** contains mitochondria (powerhouse — ATP generation), nucleus (genetic control), and ribosomes (protein synthesis). Human physiology covers digestion, blood circulation (4-chambered heart, oxygenated vs deoxygenated flow), and the nervous system (CNS = brain + spinal cord).

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Modern Physics Edge Cases
**Radioactivity** splits into three emissions: **alpha** (helium-4 nucleus, +2 charge, low penetration, stopped by paper), **beta** (electron, −1 charge, stopped by aluminium), and **gamma** (high-energy photon, no charge, stopped by thick lead). **Rutherford's gold-foil experiment** (1909–11) overturned Dalton's billiard-ball atom by showing a tiny dense nucleus; **Bohr's 1913 model** quantised electron orbits to explain hydrogen's line spectrum.

#### Biology — Genetics Precision
DNA uses bases **Adenine, Thymine, Guanine, Cytosine** (A–T, G–C pairing). RNA substitutes **Uracil** for thymine (A–U, G–C). **Mitosis** produces 2 identical diploid daughter cells (growth/repair); **meiosis** produces 4 genetically varied haploid gametes (reproduction). Confusing these is a classic AILET discriminator.

#### Indian S&T — Year-Specific Awareness
Beyond the headline missions, candidates should know that **PSLV** is ISRO's workhorse polar satellite launcher (~1,750 kg to LEO), while **GSLV** uses cryogenic upper stages to reach GTO. India's nuclear programme is civilian (NPCIL, Kudankulam, Kakrapar) plus strategic (BARC, thermonuclear devices tested in 1998 Pokhran-II). **Defence systems** to recognise: Agni-V (ICBM range 5,000+ km), Prithvi (short-range), Akash (SAM), INS Arihant (nuclear submarine), Tejas (light combat aircraft).

#### Environmental & Emerging Tech
**Greenhouse gases** (CO₂, CH₄, N₂O, CFCs) trap outgoing IR radiation; the **ozone layer** (stratospheric O₃) blocks UV-B and UV-C. **Renewables** (solar, wind, hydro, biomass, geothermal, tidal) contrast with **non-renewables** (coal, petroleum, natural gas, nuclear). Emerging tech — **nanotechnology** (1–100 nm scale), **biotechnology** (CRISPR-Cas9 gene editing, Bt cotton), **AI/ML** (Large Language Models, computer vision) — appear as one-line definition questions.

#### Practice Prompts
1. A resistor of 10 Ω carries 2 A. What is the power dissipated? (Answer: P = I²R = 40 W.)
2. pH of a solution is 3. By what factor must [H⁺] change to raise pH to 5? (Answer: decrease by 100×, since pH is logarithmic.)

> **Time-budget tip:** Allocate 60–90 seconds per S&T MCQ. Skip calculation-heavy Ohm's-Law or KE numericals until the second pass.
