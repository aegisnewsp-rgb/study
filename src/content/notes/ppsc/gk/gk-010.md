# Pakistan's Society and Demographics

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

Pakistan is the **world's 5th most populous country**, with a population of about **207.77 million** (2017 Census, results released 2023) and an annual growth rate of roughly **2.55%**. The **Pakistan Bureau of Statistics (PBS)** conducts the decennial Population Census, the Pakistan Demographic Survey (PDS), and the Labour Force Survey (LFS) — all PPSC-recommended primary sources.

- **Punjab** holds ~53% of the population, Sindh ~24%, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa ~15%, Balochistan ~6%.
- **Urdu** is the national language; **English** is the official language alongside Urdu.
- **Muslims** form ~96–97% of citizens; Hindus, Christians, Ahmadis, Sikhs, Parsis, and Buddhists are recognised minorities.
- **Literacy rate (10+ years)** is about 60–62%, with a persistent male–female gap.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### Core Demographic Indicators
Pakistan's demographic profile is defined by high fertility, declining mortality, and a young age structure. The three foundational rates are **CBR, CDR, and NGR**:

- **CBR (Crude Birth Rate)** = (Live births in a year ÷ Mid-year population) × 1000. Unit: births per 1000 population.
- **CDR (Crude Death Rate)** = (Deaths in a year ÷ Mid-year population) × 1000. Unit: deaths per 1000 population.
- **NGR (Natural Growth Rate)** = CBR − CDR, expressed as **% per annum** (divide the difference by 10).
- **TFR (Total Fertility Rate)** = sum of age-specific fertility rates for women aged 15–49. Unit: children per woman (dimensionless). Pakistan's TFR has fallen from ~6 (1980s) to ~3.5 today.
- **Dependency Ratio** = ((Population 0–14 + 65+) ÷ Population 15–64) × 100. Unit: percentage.

#### Comparison Matrix — Easily Confused Demographic Terms

| Term | What it Measures | Unit | PPSC Trap |
|---|---|---|---|
| CBR | Births per 1000 people | births/1000 | Not the same as TFR |
| CDR | Deaths per 1000 people | deaths/1000 | Don't swap with IMR |
| NGR | CBR − CDR | % per annum | Dividing by 10 to convert from /1000 |
| TFR | Lifetime births per woman | children/woman | Dimensionless, not a rate |
| IMR | Infant deaths per 1000 live births | deaths/1000 live births | Specific to under-1, not all deaths |
| Sex Ratio | Males per 100 females | number | Pakistan's is ~105 male:100 female |
| Population Density | Persons per km² of land area | persons/km² | Punjab has high density, Balochistan low |

#### Key Sociological Facts
- **Ethnic composition**: Punjabis (largest group, ~38%), Pashtuns (~15%), Sindhis (~14%), Saraikis, Muhajirs, Baloch, and smaller groups such as Hazara, Kalash, Brahui, and Chitrali.
- **Muhajirs** are Urdu-speaking Muslims who migrated to Pakistan mainly from Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, and Delhi at Partition (1947); concentrated in urban Sindh (Karachi, Hyderabad).
- **Major provincial languages**: Punjabi, Sindhi, Pashto, Balochi. Urdu and English are used in federal government, judiciary, and armed forces.
- **Religious minorities** are protected under Articles 20–40 of the Constitution; the Hindu Marriage Act 2017, Sindh Hindu Marriage (Restoration of Rights) Act 2020, and the National Commission for Minorities are frequent PPSC points.

#### 🎯 Exam-Level Worked Problem

**Question:** Pakistan's 2017 Census recorded 207.77 million people and a Crude Birth Rate of 28.8 per 1000 and Crude Death Rate of 7.0 per 1000. Calculate the Natural Growth Rate (NGR) in percent per annum.

#### Solution:
1. NGR (per 1000) = CBR − CDR = 28.8 − 7.0 = **21.8 per 1000**.
2. Convert to percent per annum: NGR % = 21.8 ÷ 10 = **2.18 % per annum**.
3. Check against published figure: PBS reports ~2.55% which also factors net migration; the demographic-only NGR is ~2.18%.

> ⚠️ **Examiner Trap:** Candidates often **skip the division by 10** and write "21.8%" — losing the mark. Always convert from "per 1000" to "percent" before writing the answer. Also, do **not** confuse NGR (demographic only) with overall population growth rate (which includes migration).

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Provincial Distribution and Density Edge Cases

| Province / Region | Population Share | Area Share | Density (persons/km²) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Punjab | ~53% | ~25.8% | Very high |
| Sindh | ~24% | ~17.7% | High |
| Khyber Pakhtunkhwa | ~15% | ~12.8% | Medium |
| Balochistan | ~6% | ~43.6% | Very low |
| ICT + AJK + GB | ~2% | small | varies |

The **area-vs-population inversion** between Balochistan and Punjab is a favourite PPSC trap: Balochistan is **largest by area but smallest by population**; Punjab is **second-largest by area but largest by population**.

#### Demographic Dividend and Youth Bulge
Pakistan's **median age is ~22 years**, and roughly **64% of the population is under 30**. This creates a demographic dividend — a window of high labour supply and low dependency — but only if education, female labour participation, and vocational training keep pace. Failure to absorb this cohort produces unemployment, urban migration, and social pressure.

#### Labour Force and Migration
- **Labour Force Participation Rate (LFPR)** is about 36% for males and ~21% for females (LFS 2020–21).
- Internal migration flows from KP and southern Punjab toward Karachi, Lahore, Faisalabad, and Rawalpindi/Islamabad.
- External remittances from Gulf and Malaysian labour migration exceed USD 27 billion annually — a demographic-economy linkage.

#### Advanced Practice Prompts

1. **Numeric:** If Punjab's 2017 Census population was 110.0 million and its land area is 205,344 km², compute population density. *(Answer: ~535 persons/km².)*
2. **Conceptual:** Explain why a high dependency ratio coexists with a youth bulge, and how it differs from ageing populations in Japan or Germany.

#### Common Exam Mistakes to Avoid

1. Quoting the **1998 Census** figure of 132.4 million as "current" — the 2017 Census (results released 2023) supersedes it.
2. Treating **Islamabad** as part of Punjab — it is the federally administered **Islamabad Capital Territory (ICT)**.
3. Calling Urdu the "only" national language — both **Urdu and English** enjoy official status under the Constitution.
4. Reporting population growth rate as 3.0% — that figure applies to the 1980s; current rate is ~2.55%.
5. Misidentifying Muhajirs as a province-based ethnic group — they are a **migration-defined community**, largely urban-based in Sindh.

> ⚠️ **Examiner Trap:** PPSC examiners frequently pair "largest by area" and "largest by population" in MCQs. Memorise the inversion: **Balochistan = area, Punjab = population**.
