# Subject-Specific Requirements and Minimum Entrance — JUPAS Notes

This topic covers subject-specific requirements and minimum entrance requirements for JUPAS programmes. Each programme publishes minimum entrance requirements (typically 3 core subjects at Level 3-4 plus one elective at Level 2-3). Some programmes specify the elective subjects (e.g. Physics required for Engineering; Biology or Chemistry required for Medicine). The minimum entrance requirement is a hard gate; failure to meet it removes the candidate from consideration regardless of score.

> Verify the live minimum entrance requirements on each university's admissions page before planning revision.

---

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)

#### Core facts in one pass

- **Minimum entrance requirements**: typically Level 3 in Chinese and English; Level 2 or 3 in Mathematics; Level 2 in one elective.
- **Specific subject requirements**: some programmes require specific electives. Examples:
 - Physics required for most Engineering programmes.
 - Biology and Chemistry required for Medicine.
 - Mathematics Extended Part (M1 or M2) for some mathematics, science and engineering programmes.
- **Hard gate**: minimum entrance requirements are a hard gate. Failure to meet them removes the candidate from consideration regardless of score.
- **Source**: published on each programme's admissions page; consolidated in the 2025 JUPAS Admissions Scores report.

#### Examiner traps

- Applying to a programme without meeting the minimum entrance requirements.
- Forgetting that "Level 3" or "Level 4" means the HKDSE grade (1-5**), not the JUPAS score (1-8.5).

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)

#### Typical minimum entrance requirements

The minimum entrance requirements vary by programme but typically include:

- **Chinese Language**: Level 3 (some programmes require Level 4 or 5).
- **English Language**: Level 3 (some programmes require Level 4 or 5, especially for English-medium programmes).
- **Mathematics (Compulsory Part)**: Level 2 (most programmes), Level 3 (some), Level 4 or 5 (competitive programmes).
- **Liberal Studies / Citizenship and Social Development**: Level 2 (most programmes).
- **One elective**: Level 2 or 3 (depending on the programme).

Some programmes have additional subject-specific requirements.

#### Subject-specific requirements

**Engineering programmes**: typically require Physics at Level 3 or above. Some require Mathematics Extended Part (M1 or M2).

**Medicine / Dentistry / Pharmacy**: typically require Biology and Chemistry at Level 4 or above. Some require English at Level 5.

**Mathematics, Statistics, Actuarial Science**: typically require Mathematics at Level 4 or above; many prefer or require Mathematics Extended Part (M1 or M2).

**Business / Economics / Finance**: typically require Mathematics at Level 3 or above. Some require Economics or Business at Level 3 or above.

**Law**: typically require English at Level 4 or above. Some require a specific category of subjects.

**Computer Science / Information Technology**: typically require Mathematics at Level 3 or above. Some prefer Physics at Level 3.

**Architecture**: typically require English at Level 3; some require Art at Level 3.

**Education**: typically require Chinese and English at Level 3.

#### How minimum entrance requirements work

The minimum entrance requirements are a hard gate. The applicant must meet or exceed each minimum to be considered for the programme. If any minimum is not met, the applicant is not considered for that programme, regardless of the overall admission score.

**Example**: A programme requires:
- Chinese: Level 3
- English: Level 3
- Mathematics: Level 3
- One elective: Level 3 (Physics preferred)

A candidate with Chinese 5, English 5, Math 5, Physics 4, Chemistry 4, Biology 4, Economics 5 meets all minimums. The candidate's admission score is calculated normally.

A candidate with Chinese 5, English 5, Math 4 (but not Level 3 in Physics or any other elective) does not meet the elective minimum. The candidate is not considered for the programme.

### 🔴 Deep — Long-Term Mastery (1mo–6mo)

#### Cross-topic links

- **"Best 5" and "Best 6" Formulae**: the scoring formula assumes minimum entrance requirements are met.
- **Weighted Subjects**: weighting is applied to the scoring formula, not the minimum requirements.

#### Exam technique

- **For "what are the minimum entrance requirements for programme X" questions**: state the minimums for Chinese, English, Math, electives, and any subject-specific requirements.
- **For "can a candidate be considered for the programme" questions**: check each minimum; if any minimum is not met, the answer is no.

#### Common misconceptions

- "Minimum entrance requirements are soft targets." No — they are hard gates.
- "Meeting the minimum guarantees admission." No — meeting the minimum makes the candidate eligible; admission depends on the score relative to other candidates.
- "Subject-specific requirements are always stated in the programme title." Not always — some subject-specific requirements are buried in the programme description.
