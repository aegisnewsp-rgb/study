# Task 2 — Essay Structure and Argument — IELTS Writing Notes

IELTS Academic Writing Task 2 asks the candidate to respond to a point of view, argument, or problem in 250+ words in about 40 minutes. Task 2 carries twice the weight of Task 1 in the final Writing band score. It is the most heavily weighted component of the Writing test.

> Verify the live Writing format and Task 2 mark weighting on https://ielts.org/ before planning revision.

---

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)

#### Core facts in one pass

- **Format:** respond to a point of view, argument or problem in 250+ words.
- **Time:** about 40 minutes.
- **Mark weighting:** Task 2 carries twice the weight of Task 1 in the Writing band.
- **Structure:** introduction (with thesis), body paragraph 1 (first reason), body paragraph 2 (second reason), body paragraph 3 (counter-argument + rebuttal), conclusion.
- **Cohesion:** appropriate linking devices (however, furthermore, in addition, on the other hand).
- **Lexical resource:** range of vocabulary, accurate word choice, no repetition.

#### Examiner traps

- Writing less than 250 words — Task 2 has a clear minimum.
- Forgetting to address both sides of the argument (for discussion essays).
- Missing the conclusion — the conclusion is the method mark.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)

#### Structure of a Task 2 essay

1. **Introduction (2-3 sentences):** paraphrase the prompt and state the thesis clearly.
2. **Body paragraph 1:** first reason supporting the thesis, with evidence or example.
3. **Body paragraph 2:** second reason supporting the thesis, with evidence or example.
4. **Body paragraph 3:** counter-argument + rebuttal, or third reason.
5. **Conclusion (2-3 sentences):** restate the thesis in different words, summarise the strongest reason, end with a forward-looking statement.

#### Argumentative essay (opinion)

The candidate must take a clear position on the issue. Two acceptable structures:

- **One-sided:** thesis + three reasons, no counter-argument.
- **Two-sided:** thesis + two reasons for + counter-argument + rebuttal.

Strong Task 2 responses take a clear position, support it with specific evidence, and acknowledge the opposing view fairly.

#### Discussion essay

The candidate must discuss both views and give their own opinion. Structure:

- Body paragraph 1: first view + reasons.
- Body paragraph 2: second view + reasons.
- Body paragraph 3: candidate's opinion + reasons.
- Conclusion: restate the opinion.

#### Problem/solution essay

The candidate must describe a problem and propose solutions. Structure:

- Introduction: paraphrase + state that solutions will be proposed.
- Body paragraph 1: describe the problem in detail.
- Body paragraph 2: first solution with rationale.
- Body paragraph 3: second solution with rationale.
- Conclusion: summarise the solutions.

### 🔴 Deep — Long-Term Mastery (1mo–6mo)

#### Where Task 2 appears across IELTS Writing

Task 2 is the second and most heavily weighted component of IELTS Academic Writing. It accounts for two-thirds of the Writing band score. Strong Task 2 performance is the single highest-leverage preparation for the Writing test.

#### Common misconceptions (and the correction)

- "Long essays score higher." Quality matters more than length.
- "Memorised templates score high." Examiners recognise memorised phrases; the band for lexical resource drops.
- "No conclusion is fine." The conclusion is the method mark — without it, the essay loses marks even if the body is strong.

#### Exam technique

- Spend 5-10 minutes planning the structure before drafting.
- Take a clear position in the thesis statement.
- Support each reason with a specific example or evidence.
- Address the counter-argument fairly, then refute it.
- Restate the thesis in the conclusion in different words.
