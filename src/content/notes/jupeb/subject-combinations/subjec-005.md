# A valid JUPEB Subject Combination

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

A valid JUPEB Subject Combination is a faculty-tagged set of exactly three approved subjects (coded SC-001 through SC-199) plus five O'Level credit passes that include **English Language** and **Mathematics**. The combination is the gate to A'Level moderation, the JUPEB certificate, and Direct Entry admission.

- **Rule 1:** Only combinations on the official JUPEB list for your target faculty count.
- **Rule 2:** The same three subjects can be accepted for one faculty and rejected for another (e.g., Government/Economics/Mathematics works for Social Sciences but not for Law).
- **Rule 3:** Subject changes after registration attract an administrative fee and risk result cancellation if missed.
- **Rule 4:** Always cross-check the combination against the JAMB/DE brochure of the receiving university.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### Anatomy of a JUPEB Combination

Every JUPEB Subject Combination carries a faculty tag, an SC-code, and an O'Level anchor. The three subjects sit under the **Principal Subjects** group (the A'Level examinables), while certain required O'Level credits (English, Mathematics, and subject-relevant credits) act as **Subsidiary gating credits**.

#### How Faculty Tagging Works

A combination is not just "three subjects" — it is a pre-vetted triple mapped to a faculty cluster. Candidates choose combinations such as **Accounts/Business Studies/Economics (ABE)** for Commercial and Management Sciences, **Government/Economics/Mathematics (GEM)** for Social Sciences, or **Biology/Chemistry/Physics (BCP)** for Pure and Applied Sciences. The same triple (e.g., Mathematics/Economics/Government) can carry tag SC-007 for Social Sciences and SC-091 for Education, so the faculty your course belongs to determines which tag you register.

| Element | Specification |
| --- | --- |
| Subjects per candidate | Exactly 3 approved JUPEB subjects |
| Combination code format | SC-XXX (e.g., SC-021) |
| O'Level credits required | Minimum 5, including English and Mathematics |
| Audit window | In standard papers the first 4 weeks of the A'Level session |
| Result status | Barred from moderation if subjects are off-list |

#### Cross-Verification Before Registration

Candidates must reconcile three documents before payment: the JUPEB-approved combination list, the JAMB Direct Entry brochure for the intended university, and the receiving university's own admission page. A mismatch in any one of them invalidates the entire pipeline — moderation can pass the candidate while the university rejects the result at screening.

- Verify the SC-code matches both your faculty and your exact course of study.
- Confirm the five O'Level credits were obtained in not more than two sittings.
- Retain evidence of a B-grade or higher in any subject the receiving university flags as a "must-reach" minimum.
- Avoid registering a subject outside the SC list; moderation will zero it.

#### Edge Cases and Boundary Conditions

The 3% weight on the JUPEB Subject-Combinations paper belies the cascade effect of a wrong choice. A candidate who picks Biology/Chemistry/Geography for a Medicine pathway fails the faculty tag at moderation — no resit rescues the registration within the same session. Another boundary case is the **Education double-faculty tag**, where one combination qualifies the holder for both Education and a content faculty such as Agricultural Science, provided the O'Level credits align with both.

#### Mechanism of JUPEB Moderation

Moderation reconciles raw scores across subjects and computing centres into a comparable A'Level grade (A–F). Off-list subjects have no moderation matrix, hence are recorded as "Not Presented" and disqualify the candidate irrespective of raw score.

#### Connections and Common Mistakes

| Misstep | Why it costs marks or admission |
| --- | --- |
| Treating all science triples as equivalent | Faculty tagging makes SC-014 ≠ SC-061 for Medicine |
| Assuming Government is compulsory for all arts | Management courses want Economics/Maths, not Government |
| Skipping JAMB cross-check | University screens override JUPEB moderation |
| Late subject change after week 4 | Trigger result cancellation and administrative fee |
