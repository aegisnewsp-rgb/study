# Assessment Criteria — Task Response, Coherence, Lexical, Grammar — IELTS Writing Notes

IELTS examiners score Writing on four criteria: Task Response (Task Achievement in Task 1), Coherence and Cohesion, Lexical Resource, and Grammatical Range and Accuracy. Each criterion is graded on a band from 0 to 9, then averaged to give the Writing band.

> Verify the live Writing band descriptors on https://ielts.org/ before planning revision.

---

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)

#### Core facts in one pass

- **Task Response (Task 1: Task Achievement):** addresses the prompt fully, presents a clear position, supports with relevant main points.
- **Coherence and Cohesion:** logical organisation, clear progression, appropriate cohesive devices.
- **Lexical Resource:** range of vocabulary, accurate word choice, no repetition.
- **Grammatical Range and Accuracy:** variety of complex sentence structures, accurate grammar.
- **Band scoring:** each criterion scored 0-9, averaged to give the Writing band.
- **Half bands:** 6.5, 7.5 etc. are possible.

#### Examiner traps

- Focusing on one criterion at the expense of others.
- Scoring band 9 on one criterion and band 5 on another — the average pulls down the band.
- Ignoring the public band descriptors when planning.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)

#### Task Response / Task Achievement

Band 7 requires:

- Addresses the prompt fully.
- Presents a clear position throughout.
- Supports with relevant extended main points.
- Uses specific evidence.

Band 8 adds:

- Sufficient length (Task 2: 250+ words, Task 1: 150+ words).
- Clear progression.
- Appropriate tone.

#### Coherence and Cohesion

Band 7 requires:

- Clear logical organisation.
- Clear progression.
- Appropriate cohesive devices.
- Clear topic sentences.

Band 8 adds:

- Seamless progression.
- Varied cohesive devices (not just conjunctions).

#### Lexical Resource

Band 7 requires:

- Sufficient range of vocabulary.
- Accurate word choice.
- Few errors in spelling or word form.

Band 8 adds:

- Wide range of vocabulary.
- Precise word choice.
- Awareness of style and collocation.

#### Grammatical Range and Accuracy

Band 7 requires:

- Variety of complex sentence structures.
- Few grammatical errors.
- Clear communication.

Band 8 adds:

- Wide range of structures.
- High accuracy.
- Flexibility in sentence structure.

---

### 🔴 Deep — Long-Term Mastery (1mo–6mo)

#### Where the assessment criteria appear across IELTS Writing

The four criteria apply to both Task 1 and Task 2. Candidates should aim for band 7+ on each, which gives a Writing band of 7 or higher.

#### Common misconceptions (and the correction)

- "All four criteria must be the same band." No — averaging produces half bands.
- "Band 9 requires perfection." It requires very high performance with rare errors.
- "Task 1 and Task 2 have different criteria." Task 1 uses "Task Achievement" instead of "Task Response" but the criteria are otherwise the same.

#### Exam technique

- Aim for band 7+ on each criterion.
- Use the public band descriptors as a self-check before submitting.
- Practice with sample tasks and grade your own work against the descriptors.
