# JUPEB Subject Combinations (2)

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

JUPEB Subject Combinations are the officially approved **three-subject groupings** that candidates register for at the start of their A'Level foundation year, each assigned a unique **SC code** (e.g., SC-021). Combinations fall into three broad groups — **Science**, **Arts/Social Sciences**, and **Commercial** — and the choice directly decides which degree courses the candidate is eligible to apply for at partnering Nigerian universities. Grades awarded run from **A** (highest) to **F** (lowest), with **A–E as passes**.

- **SC code** = the registration identifier used on the JUPEB portal (e.g., SC-021 for Accounting/Economics/Government).
- **Science group** in standard papers includes Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics — required for Medicine, Engineering, Pharmacy.
- **Commercial group** covers Accounting, Economics, Business, Government — feeds into Business Administration, Finance, Accounting degrees.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### What a JUPEB Combination Actually Is

A JUPEB programme is a one-session Advanced Level foundation spread across two semesters, leading to a Direct Entry (DE) qualification into 200 Level at Nigerian universities. Every candidate must register exactly **three subjects** drawn from the JUPEB-approved list, and the trio is locked to one of the official combinations published on the JUPEB portal. Each combination carries a unique alphanumeric identifier known as the **SC code**, which registrars use to verify the candidate's declared programme during online registration.

#### Grouping Categories

The Joint Universities Preliminary Examinations Board classifies approved combinations into three umbrella groups, and this classification decides admission tracks. The table below shows the typical subject cluster associated with each group:

| Group | Typical Subjects | Target Degrees |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Science | Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics | Medicine, Engineering, Pharmacy, Computer Science |
| Arts/Social Sciences | Literature, Government, History, CRS/IRS, Economics | Law, Mass Communication, Political Science |
| Commercial | Accounting, Economics, Business Studies, Government | Accounting, Banking & Finance, Business Admin |

#### Common Registration Rules

Candidates should memorise the SC code for their chosen combination because the JUPEB portal rejects mismatches between the displayed SC code and the candidate's subject picks. Universities also publish faculty-specific entry requirements that can add a compulsory fourth subject (such as English Language or Further Mathematics) on top of the JUPEB triple, so a high A'Level score alone does not guarantee admission.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Mapping Combinations to Course Eligibility

The most-tested skill in Topic 8 is recognising **which combination unlocks which course cluster**. A candidate aiming at **Medicine** must register the Science triple Physics/Chemistry/Biology (commonly SC-001 or its group equivalent), while a candidate targeting **Law** is in standard papers routed through an Arts/Social Sciences combination such as Government/Literature/CRS (e.g., SC-029). **Accounting** candidates in most keys pick the Commercial triple Accounting/Economics/Government (SC-021). Misalignment is the single largest cause of candidates scoring A's but still being screened out at the university DE stage.

#### Traps and Edge Cases

- Some faculties quietly require **Mathematics** even when it is not the dominant subject in the chosen SC code — verify before locking the combination.
- "Easier" Arts subjects still demand the same A–E grading scale, so under-preparation costs the same points lost as in Sciences.
- Combinations are **programme-specific**, not university-wide; SC-021 at UNILAG can differ in subject mix from SC-021 at another partner university, so always re-read the latest JUPEB brochure.

#### Worked Example

A candidate wants **Mass Communication** at a state university. Step 1: Identify the group — Mass Communication sits under Arts/Social Sciences. Step 2: Pick subjects — Government, Literature-in-English, and Economics is a common triple. Step 3: Locate the SC code from the JUPEB examination-subjects page (e.g., SC-029 area) and register that exact code on the portal. Step 4: Verify the university's DE brochure still accepts that SC code for Mass Communication for the current admission cycle.

2. Explain why a candidate with A's in Government, CRS, and Literature can still be denied Direct Entry into an Engineering programme.
