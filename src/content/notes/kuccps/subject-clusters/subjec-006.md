# Cluster 6 — Agriculture and Biological Sciences

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your KUCCPS placement.

KUCCPS Cluster 6 groups degree programmes in **Agriculture**, **Veterinary Medicine**, **Food Science and Nutrition**, **Horticulture**, **Forestry and Environmental Science**, **Wildlife Management**, **Marine Biology**, and **Biological Sciences**. Applicants compete for all available seats across these programmes using **weighted cluster points** derived from four mandatory KCSE cluster subjects plus one additional subject.

- **Mandatory subjects:** Biology, Chemistry, Mathematics or Physics, and English or Kiswahili.
- **Typical minimum grades:** Biology C+, Chemistry C+, Mathematics/Physics C (some programmes demand higher).
- **Placement rule:** KUCCPS ranks applicants by **weighted cluster points**, not overall KCSE mean grade.

| Agriculture | BSc Agriculture, Agricultural Economics |
| Biological Sciences | BSc Biology, Marine Biology, Wildlife Management |
| Veterinary / Animal | Bachelor of Veterinary Medicine, Animal Science |

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for KUCCPS applicants preparing their programme choices.

#### Cluster Composition and Purpose

KUCCPS uses clustering to widen access: a candidate who qualifies for any Cluster 6 programme is eligible to compete for **every** programme in the cluster where capacity exists. The cluster covers life-science and land-based degrees offered at institutions such as the **University of Nairobi**, **JKUAT**, **Egerton University**, **Moi University**, **Kenyatta University**, and **Maseno University**.

#### Qualifying Subject Combination

The four cluster subjects are **Biology**, **Chemistry**, **Mathematics/Physics**, and **English/Kiswahili**. Agriculture is an optional but useful sixth subject at KCSE level. Most Cluster 6 programmes require **Biology C+** and **Chemistry C+** as a minimum; Mathematics or Physics at **C** is standard, though Engineering-leaning programmes (e.g. Agricultural Engineering) can require **B-** in Mathematics.

#### How Weighted Cluster Points Are Computed

KUCCPS assigns subject weights based on relevance to the cluster. Biology and Chemistry carry the largest weights in Cluster 6 because they underpin the core syllabus. The student's four cluster subjects plus one additional subject are combined — the total is what determines placement ranking, **not** the KCSE mean grade.

#### Common KUCCPS Mistakes to Avoid

- Listing only Medicine or Engineering choices and skipping Cluster 6 programmes, which lowers the number of realistic preferences.
- Believing high KCSE mean guarantees a slot — placement uses weighted cluster points.
- Confusing Cluster 6 with **TVET diploma clusters**, which use different weightings.

| Mistake | Correct approach |
| --- | --- |
| Only listing one programme | List every qualifying Cluster 6 programme in genuine preference order |
| Checking overall mean | Verify weighted cluster points on the KUCCPS portal |
| Assuming Mathematics C is enough | Confirm the grade demanded by each target programme |

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for applicants optimising their KUCCPS selection.

#### Programme-Level Capacity and Cut-Off Behaviour

Each Cluster 6 programme declares an annual intake capacity. Highly subscribed degrees — **Veterinary Medicine** at the University of Nairobi and **Bachelor of Science in Nursing-adjacent biological programmes** — record the highest cut-offs, in many papers exceeding **70 weighted points**. Broader biological sciences and agriculture programmes in standard papers admit candidates in the **45–55 weighted point** band. Cut-offs shift yearly based on applicant volume and institutional capacity.

#### Strategic Preference Listing

KUCCPS allows candidates to list a limited number of programme choices in order of preference. A disciplined applicant places the most competitive programmes first, then cascades to realistic alternatives within the same cluster. Because clustering allows cross-programme competition, a candidate with Biology C+, Chemistry C+, and Mathematics B- can secure **BSc Biology** even when Veterinary Medicine remains out of reach.

#### Connections to Adjacent Clusters

Cluster 6 overlaps with **Cluster 7** (Medicine, Pharmacy, Nursing) on Biological Sciences and with **Cluster 19** (Agricultural Engineering, Environmental Engineering) on Agriculture. Candidates who fail to secure a Cluster 6 slot can legitimately apply to overlapping clusters where their subject combination qualifies, expanding placement odds.

#### Common Pitfalls in Practice

1. Omitting Mathematics when Chemistry and Biology grades are strong — Mathematics is a mandatory cluster subject in KUCCPS Cluster 6.
2. Treating Agriculture as a mandatory subject — it strengthens an application but is not a cluster requirement.
3. Ignoring capacity constraints at preferred universities — Moi University or Egerton can have lower cut-offs than the University of Nairobi for the same degree.

> **KUCCPS scoring tip:** Visit *students.kuccps.net/programmes/cluster/* before revision deadline to confirm current weighted-point cut-offs and adjust your preference list accordingly.
