# Quran — Surah Ash-Shura (Tajweed and Recitation) — Qatar Secondary Certificate Islamic Studies Notes

Tajweed (تحقيق التلاوة) is the science of reciting the Quran with correct articulation of every letter, its characteristics, and the rules of pause and continuation. The MoEHE Grade 12 Islamic Studies Term 1 textbook (MoEHE-issued, 1447 / 2025-2026 academic year edition, distributed via almanahj.com/qa/id=7313) opens the Quran domain with Surah Ash-Shura (الشورى) as the recitation and tajweed module. The module covers the makharij al-huruf (points of articulation), sifat al-huruf (characteristics of letters), and madd (elongation) rules applied to Surah Ash-Shura.

### Lite — Quick Review (1h-1d)

#### Core facts in one pass

- **Makharij al-huruf:** the 17 points of articulation from the chest (jauf) to the lips (shafatan). Letters grouped by articulation: 'ain, ghain, ha, kha from the middle of the throat; qaf from the deepest part of the tongue; kaf from the middle of the tongue; etc.
- **Sifat al-huruf:** characteristics of letters — hamd (with breath), hams (without breath), shiddah (firm), rikhwah (soft), isti'la (elevated), istifal (lowered), itbaq (rounded), infitah (open), idhqaq (thin), ismat (thick).
- **Madd:** elongation of a vowel for a specified count — madd tabi'i (2 counts), madd lazim (4 or 6), madd wajib, madd 'arid, madd lazim.
- **Surah Ash-Shura:** 53 verses, surah 42, Makki (with the exception of verse 38 which some scholars consider Madani).

#### Examiner traps

- Confusing shiddah (firm, the letter stops the airflow) with rikhwah (soft, the airflow continues).
- Treating madd tabi'i (2 counts) and madd lazim (4-6 counts) as the same.
- Mis-articulating the heavy letters (mu'qal) and the light letters (mukaffaf) — Arabic distinguishes ط from ت, ص from س, ض from د, ظ from ذ, خ from ك, غ from ق.

### Standard — Regular Study (2d-2mo)

#### Makharij in practice

The five main articulation zones:

1. **Al-jauf (empty space in the throat/mouth):** the long vowels alif, waw, ya.
2. **Al-halq (throat):** hamza, ha, 'ain, ghain, ha, kha from the deepest to the shallowest point of the throat.
3. **Al-lisan (tongue):** qaf, kaf, jim, shin, ya, dad, dal, ta, za, sad, thaw, dhad, nun, ra, lam, waw from the back of the tongue to the tip.
4. **Ash-shafatan (lips):** ba, waw, fa, mim (along with the labial letters).
5. **Al-khayshum (nasal passage):** ghunna (nasalisation) on the noon and the meem.

To articulate correctly: identify the point of articulation, then the characteristic of the letter, then the vowel context.

#### Sifat al-huruf

The 16-17 characteristics cluster into pairs of opposites: hamd (with breath) vs hams (without breath); shiddah (firm stop) vs rikhwah (flow); itbaq (rounded) vs infitah (open); idhqaq (thin) vs ismat (thick); isti'la (elevated) vs istifal (lowered); safeer (whistling) vs non-safeer.

The thick letters (مفخّم / mufakham): خ, ص, ض, غ, ط, ق, ظ, ر (under certain conditions). The thin letters (مرقّق / muraqqaq): all others except these.

#### Madd (elongation) rules

- **Madd tabi'i (natural):** 2 counts. Occurs when a letter of madd (alif, waw-saakin, ya-saakin) follows a letter with the same vowel.
- **Madd lazim (necessary):** 6 counts. Occurs when a letter of madd is followed by a shaddah.
- **Madd wajib (compulsory, of madd):** 4-5 counts. Occurs when a hamza follows a letter of madd.
- **Madd 'arid (occasional):** 2, 4, or 6 counts depending on the pause.
- **Madd lin (soft):** 2 counts. Occurs when a hamza preceded by a ya or waw-saakin follows the waw or ya.

#### Surah Ash-Shura: structure and themes

Surah 42, revealed in Makkah. Themes: divine consultation (shura) as a community principle, the unity of God's message across prophets, divine mercy and punishment, the Quran as a healing and mercy for the believers.

Key verses for tajweed practice: 42:1-5 (ha-mim, 'ain-sin-qaf openings — heavy letters); 42:11 (laysa kalihi shay'un — test of shamsiyyah assimilation); 42:49 (mulk al-samawati wa al-ard — heavy letters with shamsiyyah).

### Deep — Long-Term Mastery (1mo-6mo)

#### Why tajweed carries marks at the MoEHE terminal

The MoEHE Grade 12 Islamic Studies Quran module tests tajweed rules applied to specific verses from Surah Ash-Shura (Term 1) and the memorised portions of Surah Al-Hashr (Term 1) and Surah Al-Mujadila (Term 2). Correct makharij and sifat are marked directly; recitation fluency is the goal.

#### Exam technique

- For written questions, write the rule name and the type of letter or madd before the example — method marks.
- For oral recitation, focus on each heavy letter (ط, ض, ص, ظ, خ, غ, ق) — these are the most commonly mis-articulated.
- Apply each madd rule to the verse from Surah Ash-Shura as it appears in the textbook — the textbook verse list is the question pool.

#### Common misconceptions (and the correction)

- "Tajweed is about melody." No — tajweed is about correct articulation; melody (maqamat) is the art of recitation.
- "All letters of madd are the same." Madd tabi'i is 2 counts; madd lazim is 4-6; the count changes the meaning of the recitation.
