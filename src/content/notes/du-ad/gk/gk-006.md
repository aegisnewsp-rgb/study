# Bangladesh Liberation War 1971 — Key Events, Dates, and Figures

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

The Bangladesh Liberation War of 1971 was a **nine-month armed conflict** (March–December 1971) between East Pakistan and West Pakistan. It began with the Pakistani army's **Operation Searchlight** on **25–26 March 1971** and ended with the **surrender at Suhrawardy Udyan (then Ramna Race Course), Dhaka, on 16 December 1971 at 4:31 PM**, producing about 93,000 Pakistani prisoners of war and the birth of the People's Republic of Bangladesh.

#### Must-Know Anchors

| Date | Event | Lead Figure |
|---|---|---|
| 26 March 1971 | Declaration of Independence / Operation Searchlight | Sheikh Mujibur Rahman (arrested); Gen. Tikka Khan |
| 17 April 1971 | Proclamation of Mujibnagar Government (Meherpur) | Syed Nazrul Islam (Acting President); Tajuddin Ahmad (PM) |
| 27 March 1971 | First public reading of the Declaration | Major Ziaur Rahman, Kalurghat radio |
| 16 December 1971 | Instrument of Surrender signed | Lt. Gen. Jagjit Singh Aurora accepts from Gen. A.M.M.A. Niazi |

> 💡 **High-Yield Memory Hook:** **"S-M-A"** — **S**earchlight (March), **M**ujibnagar (April), **A**llied surrender (December). Pair the mnemonic with **"26, 17, 16"** (day-of-month triplet for the three anchor dates) — the most-tested numbers in DU admission MCQs.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### Political Trigger and Outbreak

The war began after the Awami League's 1970 election sweep was denied the right to govern. After months of non-cooperation, the Pakistani military launched **Operation Searchlight** on the night of **25 March 1971**, targeting Dhaka University, the EPR headquarters at Pilkhana, and Bengali police. Sheikh Mujibur Rahman declared independence early on **26 March** before his arrest; **Major Ziaur Rahman** broadcast the declaration from Kalurghat radio on **27 March** when Mujib could not.

#### Provisional Government and Armed Resistance

With the political leadership either arrested or in hiding, leaders regrouped at Meherpur. On **17 April 1971**, the **Mujibnagar Government** was proclaimed — Sheikh Mujibur Rahman as President (in absentia), **Syed Nazrul Islam** as Acting President, **Tajuddin Ahmad** as Prime Minister, and **Khondaker Mostaq Ahmad** as Vice President.

The guerrilla force **Mukti Bahini** was organised into **11 sectors** (official recognition includes 4 hill sectors and 7 plains sectors, though some classifications list 9 functional commands). **Colonel M.A.G. Osmani** served as Commander-in-Chief of Mukti Bahini with political oversight.

#### Indian Intervention and Allied Offensive

After millions of refugees poured into India, Pakistan struck Indian airfields pre-emptively (**Operation Chengiz Khan**, 3 December 1971). India formally entered the war, forming the **Allied Forces (Mitro Bahini)** under **Lt. Gen. Jagjit Singh Aurora**, who commanded joint operations alongside Mukti Bahini. The sector-wise land offensive ran **2–16 December 1971**, ending with the surrender at **Ramna Race Course** (later Suhrawardy Udyan) — **not** at Shaheed Minar or Curzon Hall, a frequent misattribution.

#### 🎯 Exam-Level Worked Problem

**Question:** On 16 December 1971, the Instrument of Surrender was signed at 4:31 PM in Dhaka. Who accepted the surrender on behalf of the Allied Forces, and at which venue was it signed?

(A) Lt. Gen. Jagjit Singh Aurora at Shaheed Minar
(B) Gen. A.M.M.A. Niazi at Suhrawardy Udyan
(C) Lt. Gen. Jagjit Singh Aurora at Ramna Race Course (Suhrawardy Udyan)
(D) Col. M.A.G. Osmani at Curzon Hall

**Solution:** The surrender was accepted by **Lt. Gen. Jagjit Singh Aurora** from **Gen. A.M.M.A. Niazi**. The venue was **Ramna Race Course**, the official name of the site in 1971, which was later renamed **Suhrawardy Udyan** after independence. Therefore the venue answer must use the contemporary name *or* acknowledge the renamed identity.

#### Answer: (C)

> ⚠️ **Examiner Trap:** Students often select (A) because Shaheed Minar is a famous Dhaka landmark; (B) confuses who signed versus who accepted; (D) misplaces Col. Osmani into the field command role of Aurora. The venue question tests whether you remember the **1971 name (Ramna Race Course)** — the current name (Suhrawardy Udyan) alone may be marked incomplete in strict DU papers.

#### Sector Architecture and Edge Cases

The Mukti Bahini sector map is the single most error-prone area in advanced MCQs.

| Aspect | Detail | Trap |
|---|---|---|
| Total sectors (official) | 11 (4 hill + 7 plains) | Some books cite 9 |
| Sector 1 | Belonia (Tripura border) under Major Ziaur Rahman | Often mislabelled as Sector 2 |
| Sector 2 | Teldhala, under Major Khaled Mosharraf | Sometimes swapped with Sector 1 |
| Sector 11 | North Bengal, under Subedar Major Abu Taher | Frequently omitted from older lists |
| Hill sectors | 1, 2, 3, 5 under Mizo, Meghalaya, Tripura fronts | Students miss that hill ≠ plains |
| EFR / EPR | East Pakistan Rifles and East Bengal Regiment defectors formed nuclei | Confused with Mukti Bahini proper |

#### Conflicting Narratives and Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Correction |
|---|---|
| War lasted only 13 days | Only the **Allied offensive** lasted 13 days (2–16 Dec); the war itself ran **9 months** |
| Tajuddin Ahmad read the Declaration | He *authored and broadcast* it; **Ziaur Rahman** first *read* it on 27 March |
| Surrender venue = Shaheed Minar | It was **Ramna Race Course** (now Suhrawardy Udyan) |
| Khondaker Mostaq Ahmad = Acting President from 17 April | He became acting head later; **Syed Nazrul Islam** was the original Acting President |
| Operation Chengiz Khan was an Indian attack | It was a **Pakistani pre-emptive strike on Indian airfields** that triggered India's entry |

#### Advanced Practice Prompts

1. **Date-sequence question:** Arrange (i) Operation Chengiz Khan, (ii) Proclamation of Mujibnagar Government, (iii) Declaration of Independence, (iv) Allied offensive — earliest to latest.
2. **Role-match:** Match each of the seven figures above to the correct office (Acting President, PM, C-in-C Mukti Bahini, Commander Allied Forces, surrendering general, first radio reader, Father of the Nation).

> ⚠️ **Examiner Trap:** Two recurring errors cost top marks in DU papers — confusing the **16 December 1971 venue** (Ramna Race Course, not Shaheed Minar) and swapping **Ziaur Rahman** with **Tajuddin Ahmad**. Memorise who *authored* versus who *first read* the Declaration.
