#### 2. Cavity Theorem and Electrostatic Shielding

If an uncharged cavity exists inside a conductor containing no enclosed charge, the electric field within the cavity is zero regardless of external fields. If a charge $+q$ is placed inside the cavity, an induced charge hBcq$ distributes on the cavity wall, while $+q$ distributes on the outer surface.
