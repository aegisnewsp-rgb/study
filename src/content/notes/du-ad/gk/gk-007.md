# SAARC, BIMSTEC, and International Organizations

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

- **SAARC** = South Asian Association for Regional Cooperation, founded in **Dhaka on 8 December 1985**, with **8 member states** (Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Maldives, Nepal, Pakistan, Sri Lanka). Secretariat: **Kathmandu**.
- **BIMSTEC** = Bay of Bengal Initiative for Multi-Sectoral Technical and Economic Cooperation, established in **Bangkok on 6 June 1997**, with **7 members** (Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Myanmar, Thailand). Secretariat: **Dhaka** since 2014.
- Key contrast: **SAARC = South Asia only**, politically stalled since the 2014 Kathmandu summit; **BIMSTEC = Bay of Bengal rim**, increasingly active and excluding Pakistan/Afghanistan.
- Bangladesh is also a member of the **UN, Commonwealth, OIC, NAM, WTO, IMF, World Bank, WHO, UNESCO, UNDP, FAO, ILO**.

> High-yield tip: Memorise founding year + city + member count + secretariat city for both bodies — DU MCQs frequently combine these into a single 4-option question.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### Origins and Membership
**SAARC** was created through the **SAARC Charter signed in Dhaka on 8 December 1985**. Its founding principles are **sovereign equality, territorial integrity, non-interference in internal affairs, and mutual benefit**. The bloc has eight members, with **Afghanistan** admitted in 2007 and **China, the US, EU, Japan, and Iran** holding observer status.

**BIMSTEC** originated from the **Bangkok Declaration of 6 June 1997**, linking five South Asian states with two ASEAN members (Myanmar, Thailand). It gained operational shape after the **Kathmandu Declaration of 2004**, and its permanent secretariat was set up in **Dhaka in 2014**.

#### Structure and Areas of Cooperation

| Feature | SAARC | BIMSTEC |
|---|---|---|
| Founded | Dhaka, 1985 | Bangkok, 1997 |
| Members | 8 (South Asia) | 7 (Bay of Bengal rim) |
| Secretariat | Kathmandu, Nepal | Dhaka, Bangladesh |
| Key sectors | Trade, poverty, environment, education | Trade, investment, energy, transport, tourism, tech, fisheries |
| Charter document | SAARC Charter | Bangkok Declaration |

#### Bangladesh's Role
Bangladesh acts as **SAARC's birthplace** and **BIMSTEC's secretariat host**, giving it diplomatic leverage. Dhaka has chaired BIMSTEC's trade and energy ministerial meetings and pushed the **BIMSTEC FTA framework**. Within SAARC, Bangladesh leads initiatives on climate cooperation, the **South Asian University (established 2010)**, and poverty alleviation.

#### International Organizations
Bangladesh is a member of the **United Nations (1974)**, **Commonwealth**, **Organisation of Islamic Cooperation (OIC, 1974)**, **Non-Aligned Movement (NAM)**, and key Bretton Woods institutions (**World Bank, IMF**). It actively participates in the **WTO, WHO, UNESCO, UNDP, FAO, and ILO**, drawing development aid, trade concessions, and technical assistance.

> Examiner's trap: Do not confuse SAARC's Kathmandu secretariat with BIMSTEC's Dhaka secretariat — reversing the two is a classic one-mark loss.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Why SAARC Stalled and BIMSTEC Rose
The last SAARC summit was held in **Kathmandu in November 2014**; the planned 2016 Islamabad summit was cancelled after the Uri attacks, and tensions between India and Pakistan have frozen the body since. Consequently, India and several members now treat **BIMSTEC as the functional alternative**, evident in invitations to BIMSTEC leaders for G20 outreach summits.

#### Edge Cases and Common Mistakes
- Pakistan is **NOT** a BIMSTEC member; Afghanistan is **NOT** either — only the original seven signatory states belong.
- SAARC's first summit was held in **Dhaka (1986)**, but the organisation's founding charter is from 1985 — a subtle but frequently tested distinction.
- BIMSTEC's **14 areas of cooperation** are grouped into **7 core sectors**: trade, investment, transport & communication, energy, tourism, technology, and fisheries.
- The **South Asian University (SAU)**, headquartered in New Delhi, is a SAARC project, not a BIMSTEC body.

#### Worked Identification Example
Question pattern: *"Which organisation has its secretariat in Dhaka and excludes Pakistan?"*
Answer: **BIMSTEC** — secretariat in Dhaka since 2014, members are Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Myanmar, and Thailand.

#### Strategic Outlook for Bangladesh
Bangladesh benefits from BIMSTEC's **connectivity push** (the BIMSTEC Master Plan for Transport Connectivity 2018) linking Chittagong and Mongla ports to India, Myanmar, and Thailand, supporting its vision of becoming a regional trade hub. Bangladesh's OIC and Commonwealth memberships meanwhile sustain diplomatic networks beyond Asia.

#### Practice Prompts
1. State two differences between SAARC and BIMSTEC in terms of membership and secretariat location.
2. Identify three international organisations of which Bangladesh is a member, along with the year Bangladesh joined (where applicable).

> Scoring tip: Always pair the **founding city + year** with the **secretariat city** for any regional bloc — DU MCQs often separate these across options to test precise recall.
