# Contemporary Islamic Thought: Reform, Extremism, and Modern Challenges

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

**Contemporary Islamic Thought** is the body of Muslim intellectual responses since the 19th century to colonialism, the nation-state, secularism, and globalisation. It is organised along a spectrum from **Modernist** (Sir Syed Ahmad Khan, Muhammad Iqbal, Fazlur Rahman) through **Reformist/Islamist** (Maududi, Hasan al-Banna) and **Traditionalist/Salafi** (Ibn Abd al-Wahhab, Albani) to **Takfiri-Jihadist** (Sayyid Qutb, Abu Muhammad al-Maqdisi, TTP ideologues). Each movement answers one question: how should Muslim societies relate revelation (wahy) to reason (`aql`) and modern state structures?

| Term | One-line meaning | Movement/Thinker |
|---|---|---|
| **Ijtihad** | Independent legal reasoning from primary sources | Modernist/Reformist tool |
| **Taqlid** | Following established juridical authority | Traditionalist default |
| **Tajdid** | Periodic religious renewal | Maududi, al-Banna, Iqbal |
| **Takfir** | Declaring another Muslim an apostate | Takfiri-Jihadist weapon |
| **Maqasid al-Shariah** | Higher objectives: Din, Nafs, Nasl, Aql, Mal | Fazlur Rahman, Ibn Ashur |

> 💡 **High-Yield Memory Hook:** **"M-I-R-T-S"** spectrum — **M**odernist → **I**slamist/Reformist → **R**evivalist (Sufi/Brelvi) → **T**raditionalist Salafi → **S**alafi-Jihadist (Takfiri). Each letter shifts further from ijtihad toward rigidity.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### Reformist vs Extremist Movements Compared

| Movement | Founder / Period | Method | Position on State |
|---|---|---|---|
| Modernist (Aligarh) | Sir Syed Ahmad Khan, 1860s–90s | British-style education, Quran–science reconciliation | Loyal collaboration with colonial state |
| Reconstructionist | Muhammad Iqbal, *Reconstruction* (1930) | Khudi (selfhood); re-read Quran in modern idiom | Muslim-majority federated states |
| Double Movement | Fazlur Rahman, *Islam* (1966) | Historical-then-ethical Quranic reading | Democratic welfare state |
| Islamism | Abu Ala Maududi (Jamaat-e-Islami, 1941) | Theo-democracy / *hakumat-e-ilahiya* | Shariah sovereign state |
| Salafi / Wahhabi | Ibn Abd al-Wahhab (1740s); Albani (20th c.) | Return to salaf, reject bidah | Often quiescent (Saudi model) or activist |
| Salafi-Jihadist / Takfiri | Sayyid Qutb; Ayman al-Zawahiri; TTP (2007) | Takfir of rulers, global *qital* | Armed insurgency against Muslim states |

#### Core Mechanisms of Extremist Thought

1. **Takfir expansion** — branding rulers *taghut* (tyrants) and their societies *jahili*, removing the classical precondition that only an *imam* with authority could declare war.
2. **Selective proof-texting** — Quran 8:39, 9:5 lifted from legal context of defensive warfare; ignored are Quran 2:256 (*la ikraha fi al-din*) and 5:32 (*qatl al-nafs* prohibition).
3. **Rejection of fiqh's ethical apparatus** — classical jihad required declaration by a legitimate authority, non-combatant immunity, proportionality, and termination on offer of peace; modern jihadism discards all four.
4. **Use of media *manhaj*** — Anwar al-Awlaki's English lectures and TTP's *Amrun bil Ma'ruf* pamphlets operationalised this in the KPK recruitment corridor post-2007.

#### 🎯 Exam-Level Worked Problem

**Question:** *"Distinguish between the concepts of jihad as understood in classical Islamic jurisprudence and the reinterpretation used by Salafi-Jihadist groups like TTP. Which Quranic verses are most cited by each side and why?"* (15 marks, Paper II, KPK PMS)

#### Solution:

- *Classical fiqh definition*: Jihad is armed struggle (*qital*) under a legitimate Muslim authority (*imam*), governed by ethical rules — non-combatant immunity, proportionality, prohibition of treachery, and automatic cessation if peace is offered. The "greater jihad" (*jihad al-akbar*) refers to the inner struggle against the ego (nafs), documented in the Hadith of returning warriors.
- *TTP / Salafi-Jihadist redefinition*: Jihad is offensive *qital* declared by individuals against any ruler deemed *kafir*; takfir legitimises the target. Recruitment documents in KP (e.g., Dawat ul-Irshad pamphlets, 2008–2013) drop the classical authority requirement.
- *Verses cited*:
  - **Classical side** — Quran 2:190 (*fight in God's cause against those who fight you, but do not transgress*); 8:61 (*if they incline to peace, incline to it*).
  - **Extremist side** — Quran 8:39 (*fight them until there is no fitnah*); 9:5 (*smiting the mushrikin wherever found*); 9:29 (*fight People of the Book who do not believe*).
- *Counter-citation*: Quran 2:256 (*no compulsion in religion*) and 5:32 (*whoever kills a soul… it is as if he had slain mankind*).

> ⚠️ **Examiner Trap:** Candidates often write only the extremist proof-texts and forget the classical limitations, earning half marks. Always pair every cited verse with its classical *asbab al-nuzul* (occasion of revelation) to show you know that 9:5 addresses a specific post-treaty faction in Madinah, not an eternal command against non-combatants.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Deobandi vs Brelvi vs Salafi — A Tri-Polar Map Relevant to KPK

| Marker | Deobandi | Brelvi | Salafi / Wahhabi |
|---|---|---|---|
| Founder | Muhammad Qasim Nanautawi (1867) | Ahmed Raza Khan (Bareilly, 1902) | Ibn Abd al-Wahhab (1740s) |
| Source hierarchy | Hanafi fiqh + Hadith primacy | Hanafi fiqh + devotion to Prophet's person | Quran & Hadith directly, reject taqlid |
| On Mawlid / shrine visit | Permissible if free of shirk | Strongly affirmed | Bidah, polytheistic |
| On Sufi orders | Accepts but reformist | Centred on orders | Rejects wholesale |
| KP footprint | Largest madrasa network (Haqqania, Jamia Darul Uloom) | Visible in Swabi, Mardan shrines | Saudi-funded channels since 1970s; TTP crossover |

#### KPK-Specific Contemporary Challenges

1. **TTP insurgency (2007–)** — distinct from Afghan Taliban in command (Mehsud, then Noor Wali Mehsud), in target set (Pakistani state institutions, schools), and in ideological strand (Deobandi-takfiri hybrid under Maulana Sufi Muhammad's *Tehrik Nifaz-e-Shariat-e-Mohammadi*, merged 2007).
2. **Madrasa reform** — federal *Madrasa Registration Ordinance 2002* and KP's *KP Darul Uloom / Madaris Regulatory Framework 2018* attempt curriculum audit; resistance framed as anti-Islamic.
3. **Zarb-e-Azb (June 2014) and Rah-e-Nijat (2009)** — counter-operations whose post-conflict *de-radicalisation* debate draws on Maqasid al-Shariah (preservation of *nafs*).
4. **Paigham-e-Pakistan (January 2018)** — ulema decree signed by 1,800+ scholars from Barelvi, Deobandi, Shia, and Salafi schools declaring terrorism *haram*; an institutional answer to takfir ideology that examiners reward.

#### Edge Cases and Conceptual Limits

- **Fiqh al-Aqalliyyat** (Jurisprudence of Muslim Minorities) — developed by Yusuf al-Qaradawi and Taha J al-Alwani; relevant to migrants, refugees (1.4 million documented Afghan refugees in KP alone), and citizenship debates.
- **Iqbal's *ilham* vs *wahy*** distinction (Lecture 7, *Reconstruction*) — direct intuition versus prophetic revelation — underpins his rejection of clerical monopoly.
- **Fazlur Rahman's "double movement"** — historical contextualisation first, ethical re-application second — is the methodological key examiners expect for any "Islamic solution to modern problem" essay.
- **Maqasid hierarchy in extremis** — preservation of *Din* does NOT override *Nafs* (life); suicide attacks fail this test because the agent destroys both *Din* (commits suicide, a major sin) and *Nafs* simultaneously.

1. *"Evaluate Muhammad Iqbal's *Reconstruction of Religious Thought in Islam* (1930) as a critique of taqlid. In what specific ways does Iqbal differ from Sir Syed Ahmad Khan while sharing his modernist goal?"* (15 marks)
2. *"The Paigham-e-Pakistan (2018) ulema decree is sometimes cited as proof that takfir has been defeated in Pakistan. Critically examine this claim with reference to TTP's post-2018 propaganda and the KP madrasa ecosystem."* (20 marks)
