# Sports & Awards

> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)

Trophy-sport mapping is the single highest-yield fact cluster on this topic. Commit it cold.

- **FIFA World Cup** → Football (soccer), first held 1930, won by Uruguay.
- **Davis Cup** → Men's lawn tennis (teams, national).
- **Billie Jean King Cup** (formerly Fed Cup) → Women's lawn tennis teams.
- **Wimbledon** → Lawn tennis, oldest Grand Slam, played on grass.
- **Ashes** → Test cricket between England and Australia.
- **Ranji Trophy** → First-class domestic cricket in India.
- **Thomas Cup** → Men's badminton team championship.
- **Uber Cup** → Women's badminton team championship.
- **Stanley Cup** → Ice hockey, awarded in the NHL (USA/Canada).
- **Sultan Azlan Shah Cup** → International field hockey.
- **Santosh Trophy & Durand Cup** → Indian domestic football; Durand is the oldest football tournament in Asia.

Award hierarchy, top to bottom:

- **Bharat Ratna** — India's highest civilian honour (open to all fields, sportspersons like Sachin Tendulkar, Mary Kom, and Dhyan Chand are among recipients).
- **Padma Vibhushan → Padma Bhushan → Padma Shri** — descending civilian honours.
- **Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna** — highest sporting honour in India (first awarded 1991–92 to Viswanathan Anand).
- **Arjuna Award** — sporting excellence; **Dronacharya Award** — coaches; **Dhyan Chand Award** — lifetime achievement in sport.

> 💡 **High-Yield Memory Hook:** "B-P-V-B-P-S" reads down as Bharat → Padma Vibhushan → Padma Bhushan → Padma Shri. For sport trophies, lock the gender split: **Thomas = men, Uber = women, Davis = men, Billie Jean King = women**. Snore-proof pairings.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)

#### Trophy-Sport Associations and Their Traps

Most MAT questions on this topic offer four trophy names and ask which matches which sport. The trap is rarely the obvious one; it is a near-neighbour. Ranji and Irani are both domestic cricket, but only Ranji is first-class cricket among states; Irani pits the Ranji champion against the Rest of India. Similarly, the Ashes is a bilateral England–Australia Test series, while the ICC World Test Championship is the multi-team event that began in the 2019–21 cycle.

| Trophy | Sport | Format / Scope |
|---|---|---|
| FIFA World Cup | Football | Men's national teams, every 4 years since 1930 |
| Wimbledon | Lawn Tennis | One of four Grand Slams, grass court, since 1877 |
| Davis Cup | Lawn Tennis | Men's national teams |
| Billie Jean King Cup | Lawn Tennis | Women's national teams (renamed from Fed Cup in 2021) |
| Ashes | Cricket | England vs Australia, Test format |
| Ranji Trophy | Cricket | Indian first-class domestic |
| Thomas Cup | Badminton | Men's national teams |
| Uber Cup | Badminton | Women's national teams |
| Stanley Cup | Ice Hockey | NHL playoffs (USA/Canada clubs) |
| Santosh Trophy | Football | Indian state teams |
| Durand Cup | Football | Indian clubs, oldest in Asia (started 1888) |
| Sultan Azlan Shah Cup | Hockey | International invitational, Malaysia |
| Khelo India Youth Games | Multi-sport | Annual domestic, launched 2018 |

#### Civilian and Sporting Award Stack

Bharat Ratna sits above every Padma award, but it is not restricted to sport — recipients span science, literature, public service, and sport alike. Among sport-specific honours, Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna (renamed in 2021 from Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna) leads; Arjuna recognises current playing excellence, Dronacharya honours coaches, and Dhyan Chand is for lifetime contribution. A typical MCQ pairs "highest sporting honour" with "Khel Ratna" — many students wrongly pick Arjuna.

| Award | Domain | Selection Body |
|---|---|---|
| Bharat Ratna | Civilian (all fields) | Government of India |
| Padma series (Vibhushan/Bhushan/Shri) | Civilian (all fields) | Government of India |
| Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna | Sport (lifetime/peak) | Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports |
| Arjuna Award | Sport (excellence) | Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports |
| Dronacharya Award | Sport (coaching) | Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports |

#### Global Awards Worth Knowing

The Nobel Prize has six fields: Physics, Chemistry, Physiology or Medicine, Literature, Peace, and the Sveriges Riksbank Prize in Economic Sciences in Memory of Alfred Nobel — the last was added in 1969, first awarded in 1969 to Ragnar Frisch and Jan Tinbergen. Indian laureates include Rabindranath Tagore (Literature, 1913), C.V. Raman (Physics, 1930), Mother Teresa (Peace, 1979), Amartya Sen (Economics, 1998), Kailash Satyarthi (Peace, 2014), and Abhijit Banerjee (Economics, 2019). At the Oscars, Indian winners are Bhanu Athaiya (Costume Design, 1983), A.R. Rahman (Original Score and Original Song for *Slumdog Millionaire*, 2009), and Resul Pookutty (Sound Mixing, 2009).

#### 🎯 Exam-Level Worked Problem

**Question:** Which trophy is awarded for women's national team badminton?
(A) Thomas Cup  (B) Uber Cup  (C) Sudirman Cup  (D) Davis Cup

#### Step-by-step resolution:
1. Identify the sport: women's national team event restricts options to badminton or tennis cups.
2. Davis Cup is tennis (men's), so option (D) is eliminated immediately.
3. Thomas Cup is badminton but for men; eliminate (A).
4. Sudirman Cup is a mixed-team badminton event (men and women together), so it does not fit a "women's only" criterion. Eliminate (C).
5. Uber Cup is the women's team badminton championship, held alongside Thomas Cup at the same venue. Pick (B).

> ⚠️ **Examiner Trap:** The Sudirman Cup is the most common distractor. It is badminton, but mixed-gender. A student who rushes past "national team" without checking the gender rule will mark (C).

#### Edge Cases and First-Edition Facts

First-edition memory is a separate scoring pocket in MAT. #### Common Confusions Worth Drilling

A handful of pairings generate 70 percent of wrong answers in this topic.

1. **Ballon d'Or vs Golden Boot.** The Ballon d'Or (France Football) is for the best overall player, not the top scorer. The Golden Boot (FIFA) goes to the top scorer at a World Cup.
2. **Bharat Ratna vs Khel Ratna.** Bharat Ratna is civilian and field-agnostic; Khel Ratna is sport-only and ranks below it in the sporting hierarchy but is the highest *sporting* honour.
3. **Nobel Economics.** It is officially the Sveriges Riksbank Prize, added in 1968 and first awarded in 1969 — calling it "founded by Alfred Nobel" is wrong.
4. **Ashes vs World Test Championship.** Ashes is a bilateral Anglo-Australian Test series; the WTC is a multi-team ICC league final.
5. **Thomas Cup vs Uber Cup vs Sudirman Cup.** Thomas (men), Uber (women), Sudirman (mixed). Same sport, three different formats.

#### Two Advanced Practice Prompts

1. The Ashes urn is reputed to contain the burnt bails of a match played in 1882 at The Oval. Which two countries contest the Ashes today, and what is the format? *(Answer: England and Australia, Test cricket.)*
2. The Bharat Ratna was first awarded in 1954. Name three sportspersons who have received it and confirm whether the award is restricted to sport. *(Answer: Sachin Tendulkar, Dhyan Chand (posthumous 2024 honour refers to renaming of Khel Ratna, not Bharat Ratna), Mary Kom among sport-recipients — but the award itself is open to all fields.)*
