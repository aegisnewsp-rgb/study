# Cluster 4 — Business

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your KCSE placement cycle.

Cluster 4 is the KUCCPS Business cluster. It groups degree and diploma programmes in commerce, accounting, economics, finance, marketing, management, actuarial science, insurance, real estate, co-operative management, and office administration offered by CUE-accredited Kenyan universities and colleges. Placement is by **Weighted Cluster Points**, calculated from your four cluster subjects plus overall KCSE mean grade. Degree entry needs KCSE mean grade **C+**; diploma entry needs **C−**.

- Minimum subject prerequisites: Mathematics **C+**, English **C+**, plus two business-relevant KCSE subjects (e.g., Business Studies, Commerce, Economics, Accounting, or Geography).
- Apply via the KUCCPS online portal and list the **maximum allowed programme choices** in genuine preference order.
- Mathematics C+ is mandatory for B.Com, Economics, Actuarial Science, and Finance — most common disqualification error.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months before applying.

#### What Cluster 4 Covers

KUCCPS clusters related degree programmes by subject affinity so candidates with comparable KCSE combinations compete for comparable places. Cluster 4 — Business — consolidates B.Com, BBA, B.Economics, B.Actuarial Science, B.Finance, B.Marketing, B.HRM, B.Hospitality, B.Insurance, B.Real Estate, B.Co-operative Management, and Office Administration degrees, plus diplomas in business management, accounting, supply chain, and sales/marketing.

#### Eligibility and Prerequisites

| Requirement | Degree | Diploma |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overall KCSE mean grade | C+ | C− |
| Mathematics | C+ | C− (varies) |
| English | C+ | C− |
| Cluster subjects chosen | 4 from approved list | 4 from approved list |
| Application channel | KUCCPS portal | KUCCPS portal |

#### How Placement Works

KUCCPS computes **Weighted Cluster Points** from your four cluster subjects (each scaled and summed) plus your overall mean grade. Programmes have a **capacity** and a **cut-off** derived from previous cycles. Candidates are placed in the order of their cluster points for each programme, limited to those who listed it as their first available choice. Capacity fills first-come by points; unmet cut-offs move applicants down their choice list.

- Always list the **maximum number of choices** the portal allows.
- Put your **most preferred realistic programme first**, not the most competitive one.
- Track the **revision window** — KUCCPS publishes start/end dates annually on kuccps.net.
- Diploma and degree Cluster 4 programmes are **not interchangeable**: thresholds and subject requirements differ.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Cluster Points Mechanics in Detail

A candidate's Weighted Cluster Points = weight × (sum of the four cluster subject points) + mean-grade bonus. Each KUCCPS subject is mapped to a points band (A=12 down to E=1 in older cycles, scaled since 2024 reforms). The four cluster subjects for Cluster 4 typically include Mathematics, English, plus two from Business Studies, Commerce, Economics, Accounting, Geography, or Agriculture. Universities cannot waive Mathematics C+ for B.Com, B.Economics, B.Actuarial Science, and B.Finance because these programmes are accredited by CUE with quantitative prerequisites.

#### Common Traps and Edge Cases

| Mistake | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Listing only one choice | Zero placement if cut-off missed |
| Skipping Mathematics assuming "business" | Disqualified from quantitative degrees |
| Confusing KCSE subject codes with KUCCPS clusters | Wrong subject pick at Form 4 stage |
| Missing revision window | Locked into poor-fit programme |
| Not confirming offer | Admission revoked automatically |

#### Worked Example

Suppose a candidate scores KCSE mean grade **B** (cluster points bonus ≈ 36) with Mathematics B+, English B, Business Studies A−, and Economics B+. Their approximate weighted cluster points land in the **high 40s**, competitive for top B.Com programmes at University of Nairobi, Kenyatta, and Strathmore. A peer with C+, C, C+, C+ in the same subjects falls into the **low 30s** and should target diploma, KMTC-equivalent business diplomas, or mid-tier private universities.
