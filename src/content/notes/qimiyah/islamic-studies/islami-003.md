# Usool Scholars Treat Shar'i Knowledge As Falling Along

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

Usool al-Fiqh scholars grade **shar'i knowledge** (العلم الشرعي) along two intersecting axes: **Thubut** (transmission/authentication of the source) and **Dalala** (indication of the ruling). Each axis runs from **qat'i** (قطعي, definitive certainty) to **dhanni** (ظني, probable). This produces a 2×2 matrix governing every ruling in fiqh.

- **Qat'i al-Thubut**: text proven beyond doubt (mutawatir Qur'an, mutawatir Sunnah).
- **Dhanni al-Thubut**: text proven by single/limited chains (ahad hadith) but from a trustworthy narrator.
- **Qat'i al-Dalala**: wording points unambiguously to one meaning (e.g., the five pillars).
- **Dhanni al-Dalala**: wording permits several readings (e.g., the verse on fasting six days of Shawwal).

> 💡 **High-Yield Memory Hook:** **"Q-D / Q-D"** — picture a **Two-by-Two Grid (ثبت / دلالة)** crossed by **Qat'i × Dhanni**. The **five pillars** sit in the top-left box (Qat'i-Qat'i). **Ahad hadith rulings** land in the bottom row. Whenever the exam lists a ruling, ask: *Is the chain certain or probable? Is the wording clear or ambiguous?*

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### Source Hierarchy in Usool al-Fiqh
Usool manuals (al-Wajiz, Luma' al-Sunnah al-Iraqi) rank shar'i sources so that *higher* sources outrank *lower* ones in evidentiary weight. The Qur'an is qat'i al-thubut in its wording; mutawatir Sunnah matches it in transmission certainty; ahad Sunnah, Ijma', and Qiyas follow. Each rank translates to a specific Thubut-Dalala combination that determines how a mujtahid applies the ruling.

| Source | Thubut | Dalala | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Qur'an (mutawatir wording) | Qat'i | Qat'i or Dhanni | "Establish prayer" — qat'i-dalala; verses on riba — dhanni-dalala |
| Mutawatir Sunnah | Qat'i | Qat'i or Dhanni | Mass-transmitted practice of Hajj rites |
| Ahad Sunnah (thiqa narrator) | Dhanni | Qat'i or Dhanni | Hadith on Shawwal fasting (dhanni-thubut, dhanni-dalala) |
| Ijma' | Qat'i (when sound) | Qat'i or Dhanni | Consensus on the obligation of the five prayers |
| Qiyas | Dhanni | Dhanni | Analogy of wine onto narcotics |

#### The Three-Grade Classification of Knowledge Itself
Usool scholars split *all* knowledge into **yaqin** (يقين, certainty), **zann** (ظن, preponderant probability), and **shakk/wahm** (شك/وهم, doubt or suspicion). Only yaqin and zann legally count as *ilm* (knowledge). Shakk is excluded because it produces no operative ruling — a key Hanafi principle states: *"al-zann fi mujibi al-'amal ka'l-yaqin"* (probable knowledge suffices for action), whereas philosophical doubt does not.

1. Identify the source and its transmission channel.
2. Place the source on the Thubut axis (mutawatir vs ahad).
3. Read the wording and place it on the Dalala axis (unambiguous vs ambiguous).
4. Cross the two axes to assign the ruling's Usool grade.
5. Apply the ruling according to its grade — qat'i rulings bind all; dhanni rulings bind the mujtahid and his followers.

#### 🎯 Exam-Level Worked Problem
**Question:** The Prophet's hadith, *"Whoever fasts six days of Shawwal has fasted the entire year,"* is narrated by Abu Ayyub al-Ansari through a single chain via trustworthy narrators. A Qimiyah MCQ asks: **Under Usool classification, what is the ruling's Thubut-Dalala grade?**

**Solution:** Step 1 — Source: ahad Sunnah narrated by one companion (thiqa). Step 2 — Thubut axis: an ahad hadith is **dhanni al-thubut**, because its chain does not reach tawatur level. Step 3 — Dalala axis: the phrase *"fasted the entire year"* admits more than one interpretation (reward-multiplication vs literal completion), making it **dhanni al-dalala**. Step 4 — Combined grade: **dhanni al-thubut / dhanni al-dalala**. Step 5 — Operative weight: still a valid basis for ijtihad and the recommended act of fasting Shawwal.

> ⚠️ **Examiner Trap:** Students often answer *"qat'i because the ruling is well-known"* — confusing the **rulings' familiarity** with its **transmission certainty**. Usool grades the *chain*, not the popularity of the practice.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Edge Cases and Common Mistakes

| Trap | Correct Usool Position |
|---|---|
| Confusing qat'i with the wording itself | Qat'i is an attribute of *knowledge (ilm)*, not of *alfaz*; the wording is qat'i al-wurud |
| Treating all ahad hadith as weak | A sound ahad hadith yields dhanni-thubut that is *binding (mu'tabar)* for the mujtahid |
| Equating dhanni with shakk | Dhanni is *operative* (muhattam); shakk is inoperative — only dhanni creates a duty to act |
| Demanding "modern polling" for Ijma' | Ijma' is the agreement of all mujtahids of one era after the Prophet's death, transmitted by tawatur or sound method |
| Collapsing the four grades into "strong/weak" | The four cells of the matrix are distinct: a qat'i-thubut/dhanni-dalala hadith is not the same as a dhanni-thubut/qat'i-dalala ruling |

#### Connection to Al-Shatibi's Argument
Al-Shatibi in *al-Muwafaqat* argues shar'i knowledge outranks *'aqli* (rational) knowledge because it issues from the Lawgiver (al-Shari'), whose knowledge encompasses every human interest (masalih). The Qur'anic verse *"And We sent down unto you the Book that no one will have any doubt after that…"* (4:113) anchors this hierarchy. A mujtahid therefore ranks an Usool-graded hadith above a purely rational inference when both address the same ruling.

#### Advanced Practice Prompts
1. The verse on riba (*al-Baqarah* 275–279) is mutawatir in wording yet debated in scope. Identify its position on the matrix and justify why Hanafi, Maliki, and Shafi'i schools still derive divergent rulings from the same wording.
2. A *fatwa* based on Qiyas alone arrives at a dhanni-thubut/dhanni-dalala grade. Explain, citing Usool principles, why the Hanafi principle *"al-zann ka'l-yaqin fi mujibi al-'amal"* permits the follower to act on it, and how Shafi'i Usool would qualify this acceptance through stricter conditions on the qarinah (corroborating evidence).
