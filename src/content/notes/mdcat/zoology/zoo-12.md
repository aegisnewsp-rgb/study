# Reproductive System

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)

> The single most testable fact here is *where* events happen, not just *what* happens.

- **Gametogenesis** in males (spermatogenesis) runs inside the **seminiferous tubules** of the testes and yields **4 functional sperm** from one primary spermatocyte.
- **Gametogenesis** in females (oogenesis) occurs in the ovarian cortex and produces **1 ovum + 3 polar bodies** from one primary oocyte.
- **Fertilisation** happens in the **ampulla of the fallopian tube**, never in the uterus.
- **Implantation** of the blastocyst occurs in the **endometrium**, ~6–7 days after fertilisation.
- HPG axis: **GnRH** (hypothalamus) → **FSH and LH** (anterior pituitary) → gonadal steroids.

| Structure | Primary function | Hormonal trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Seminiferous tubules | Spermatogenesis | FSH (Sertoli support) |
| Leydig cells | Testosterone secretion | LH |
| Graafian follicle | Ovulation on day ~14 | LH surge |
| Corpus luteum | Progesterone in luteal phase | hCG rescue in pregnancy |

> 💡 **High-Yield Memory Hook:** **"Sperm = 4, Egg = 1 + 3 trash"** — spermatogenesis is wasteful for gametes only in females; the polar bodies are the discarded set. And **FALlopian = Fertilisation site**, **Uterus = Implantation site** — two distinct anatomical events MDCAT loves to swap.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)

#### Spermatogenesis vs Oogenesis

| Feature | Spermatogenesis | Oogenesis |
|---|---|---|
| Site | Seminiferous tubules of testes | Ovarian cortex (follicles) |
| Duration of one cycle | ~64 days continuous | Years (arrested at prophase I until puberty) |
| Cells produced per primary cell | 4 functional sperm | 1 ovum + 3 polar bodies |
| Meiotic arrest points | None after puberty | Prophase I (until LH surge) → Metaphase II (until fertilisation) |
| Hormonal drivers | FSH on Sertoli cells, LH on Leydig cells | FSH on granulosa, LH on theca → ovulation |

#### The 28-Day Menstrual Cycle

The cycle divides into two phases separated by ovulation.

1. **Follicular phase (day 1–14):** FSH recruits ovarian follicles; granulosa cells convert androgens to **oestradiol**, which rebuilds the endometrium.
2. **Ovulation (day ~14):** A rising oestradiol from the mature Graafian follicle flips into **positive feedback**, producing the **LH surge** that ruptures the follicle. Ovulation follows within 24–36 hours.
3. **Luteal phase (day 15–28):** The ruptured follicle becomes the **corpus luteum**, which secretes **progesterone** to maintain the endometrium.
4. **Menstruation:** Without fertilisation, the corpus luteum involutes, progesterone falls, and the functional layer of the endometrium sheds.

#### Worked Problem

A 26-year-old has a regular 28-day cycle. Her last menstrual period began on 1 March. **When is ovulation expected, and which hormone peaks at that moment?**

- Count 14 days back from the start of the next period, or 14 days forward from 1 March → **expected ovulation: 15 March**.
- The hormone that peaks at ovulation is **LH** (the mid-cycle surge), preceded by a sustained **oestradiol** rise.
- In a typical cycle, fertilisation is only viable in a ~24-hour window after ovulation, with sperm surviving up to 5 days in the female tract.

> ⚠️ **Examiner Trap:** MDCAT options often swap **ovulation** with **implantation** timing, or name **oestrogen** as the *peak* hormone instead of **LH**. Oestradiol is rising; LH is the *peak*.

#### From Fertilisation to Placenta

- **Fertilisation** in the ampulla → cortical reaction blocks polyspermy → zygote begins cleavage.
- **Implantation** of the blastocyst into the endometrium ~6–7 days after fertilisation.
- **First trimester:** corpus luteum maintains pregnancy via progesterone, rescued by **human chorionic gonadotrophin (hCG)** from the trophoblast.
- **~8–12 weeks gestation:** the **placenta** takes over progesterone production. This handover is a classic MCQ pivot.

> 📌 **Formula Check (no numbers to memorise, only durations):** Gestation ≈ 40 weeks (280 days) from last menstrual period, or ≈ 38 weeks (266 days) from fertilisation. Standard cycle = 28 days; normal range 21–35 days.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)

#### Hormone Targets Often Confused

- **Male:** FSH binds **Sertoli cells** → spermatogenesis support (A BP). LH binds **Leydig cells** → testosterone.
- **Female:** FSH acts on **granulosa cells**; LH acts on **theca interna** (androgen production) and later triggers **ovulation** on the mature follicle.

#### Menstrual vs Oestrus Cycle

| Feature | Human (menstrual) | Most mammals (oestrus) |
|---|---|---|
| Endometrium if no pregnancy | Shed (bleeding) | Reabsorbed (no bleeding) |
| Sexual receptivity | Continuous across cycle | Restricted to oestrus ("heat") |
| Behavioural signs | Subtle | Obvious (lordosis, swelling) |

#### Common Pitfalls and Edge Cases

1. **"Progesterone comes from the placenta"** — only after ~8–12 weeks gestation. Before that, the corpus luteum is the source; in early pregnancy, **hCG** keeps the corpus luteum alive.
2. **"Spermatogenesis and oogenesis both yield 4 cells"** — only spermatogenesis yields 4 functional gametes; oogenesis discards three as polar bodies.
3. **"FSH and LH do the same job in both sexes"** — wrong. Their target cells and downstream products differ.
4. **"Implantation occurs in the fallopian tube"** — ectopic pregnancies *can* implant there, but normal implantation is endometrial, ~6–7 days post-fertilisation.
5. **"Parturition is driven only by oxytocin"** — foetal **cortisol** initiates the cascade; **oxytocin** and **prostaglandins** then drive the positive-feedback contractions.

#### Advanced Practice Prompts

- A woman with a 35-day cycle ovulates on which day, and how does this shift her fertile window compared with a 28-day cycle?
- If the corpus luteum fails to be rescued at week 5, what hormone falls first, and what clinical sign follows?

#### Exam Strategy

Reproductive System accounts for ~3% of MDCAT Zoology, usually 1–2 MCQs. Highest-yield targets: the **menstrual cycle phase–hormone map**, the **site of fertilisation vs implantation**, and the **spermatogenesis/oogenesis product count**. Memorise the LH surge trigger (positive feedback from sustained high oestradiol) — it appears as a 2-statement assertion–reason MCQ almost every year.
