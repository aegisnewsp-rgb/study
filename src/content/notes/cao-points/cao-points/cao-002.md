# Higher Level Maths Bonus Points

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your CAO application.

**HL Maths Bonus Points** — Quick Facts for Irish Leaving Certificate students

**What is the bonus?**
If you take Higher Level Mathematics in the Leaving Certificate and achieve grade H6 or above, CAO adds 25 bonus points to your aggregate — exactly 25, applied once, regardless of whether you score H6, H5, H4, H3, H2, or H1. The bonus is not multiplied, not stacked, and not available at Ordinary Level.

**At a glance:**
- Bonus amount: +25 points, applied once
- Threshold: grade H6 or above in HL Maths (H6, H5, H4, H3, H2, H1 all qualify)
- Subject level: Higher Level Maths only (Ordinary Level Maths does NOT trigger the bonus)
- Cap: your total aggregate is capped at 625 points even after the bonus
- Application order: bonus is added before the six-best-subjects rule (see topic cao-003)

**Why it matters for offers:**
The 25-point bonus is the single biggest predictable points swing in the Irish admissions system. It can move you from below a course cut-off to comfortably above it without changing any of your other grades. Many Level 8 STEM programmes (engineering, science, computing, pharmacy, and some health-science programmes) expect applicants to have the bonus; some courses use it as a published course-specific requirement.

⚡ **Quick rule:** If you can realistically aim for H6 or above in HL Maths, the 25-point bonus is essentially free 25 points. The same 25 points would require moving an H3 to an H1 in another subject, or an O1 to an H2 — both substantially harder.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

**HL Maths Bonus Points** — CAO Points Study Guide

**Who qualifies for the bonus?**
You qualify if and only if you meet ALL THREE of these conditions:

1. **Subject level:** you sat Mathematics at **Higher Level** in the Leaving Certificate (not Ordinary Level, not Foundation Level).
2. **Grade threshold:** your final HL Maths grade is **H6 or above** — meaning H1, H2, H3, H4, H5, or H6.
3. **Application timing:** you apply to CAO by the published deadline and list at least one Level 7 or Level 8 undergraduate course in your course choices.

**The bonus is exactly +25, regardless of grade band:**
| HL Maths grade | Bonus points |
|----------------|-------------:|
| H1 | +25 |
| H2 | +25 |
| H3 | +25 |
| H4 | +25 |
| H5 | +25 |
| H6 | +25 |
| H7 | 0 |
| H8 | 0 |

The bonus does NOT scale with grade — a H6 in HL Maths earns the same +25 as a H1. CAO deliberately structured the bonus this way so that the threshold (H6 or above) is the only thing that matters. There is no "bonus for bonus" for higher grades.

**What the bonus is added to:**
The +25 is added to your raw six-best-subjects total BEFORE the 625-point cap is applied. The sequence is:

1. Convert each of your Leaving Certificate subject grades into CPS values (see topic cao-001).
2. Pick your **six best** subjects (excluding Foundation Level Maths entirely — see cao-004).
3. Sum those six CPS values into a raw aggregate.
4. **Add the +25 HL Maths bonus IF** you achieved H6 or above in HL Maths.
5. Cap the result at **625** (the maximum aggregate; see the cap section below).

**What the bonus is NOT:**
- It is **not** added at Ordinary Level. OL Maths at any grade does NOT trigger the bonus.
- It is **not** added at Foundation Level. F1, F2, F3 — no bonus, no points, and not counted in the six best.
- It is **not** added per-subject. It is exactly +25, applied once across the entire aggregate, regardless of how many other maths modules you take.
- It is **not** added to international applicants whose maths is presented under a non-Leaving Certificate scheme (GCE A-Level Maths, GCSE Maths, QQI FET Maths — see the international-applicants section below).

**The 625-point cap:**
The maximum CAO aggregate is 625 points. This is the sum of six H1 grades (6 × 100 = 600) PLUS the +25 HL Maths bonus (600 + 25 = 625). The cap means:

- If your six best subjects before the bonus are already 600 points or higher, the cap absorbs the bonus entirely and your aggregate stays at 600.
- If your six best subjects before the bonus are below 600, the cap does not apply, and your aggregate is six-subject-sum + 25.
- If your six best subjects are exactly 600 before the bonus, the bonus does NOT push you to 625 unless your grade spread allows.

**Worked example 1:** Grades: H1, H1, H2, H2, H3, H3, H4 in seven subjects (six best counted). Six-best CPS = 100 + 100 + 88 + 88 + 77 + 77 = 530. HL Maths is H1 — bonus applies. Aggregate = 530 + 25 = 555. Cap does not apply (555 < 625).

**Worked example 2:** Grades: H1, H1, H1, H1, H1, H1 in six subjects. Six-best CPS = 6 × 100 = 600. HL Maths is H1 — bonus applies. Raw aggregate = 600 + 25 = 625. **Capped at 625.**

**Worked example 3:** Grades: H1, H1, H1, H1, H1, H1, O2 in seven subjects. Six-best CPS = 100 × 5 + H6-of-best-six = O2 (46). Six-best = 500 + 46 = 546. HL Maths not in the six best, so the bonus is NOT applied to the six-best-subjects sum. CAO includes HL Maths in your general points-earning set even if it is not in the six best, so the bonus still applies. Aggregate = 546 + 25 = 571.

(Note: the worked example above assumes the six best include a non-Maths subject at O2 = 46 points; CAO's published six-best rule ALWAYS counts the HL Maths grade if it would improve the aggregate. If HL Maths H1 = 100 is not in your six best because other subjects are higher, the bonus STILL applies. See topic cao-003 for the full six-best-subjects rule.)

**When the bonus was introduced:**
The +25 HL Maths bonus was introduced alongside the 2017 Common Points Scale reform. Before 2017, the bonus was smaller (an older rule for some standard grades) and the points scale was structured differently. Since 2017, every CAO applicant has been scored on the same fixed +25 HL Maths rule — there is no in-year variation.

**Course-specific treatment:**
Many Level 8 STEM programmes (engineering, science, computing, pharmacy, some health-science programmes) use the HL Maths bonus as a course-specific entry requirement. Some programmes require that the bonus be applied to the published course cut-off — meaning the published cut-off already has +25 baked in for HL Maths applicants. The CAO Handbook publishes the course-specific requirements each year; always read the handbook for your specific course.

**Foundation Level Maths applicants:**
Foundation Level Maths does NOT earn the bonus and does NOT count toward the six-best-subjects aggregate. Some Level 8 STEM courses require either a pass in OL Maths at O6 or above, OR a minimum grade in Foundation Maths, as a matriculation requirement (see cao-005). Foundation Level Maths applicants who do not meet the OL/HL requirement for a STEM course are not eligible for the bonus under any pathway.

**International applicants:**
The HL Maths bonus applies ONLY to applicants who took the Irish Leaving Certificate with Mathematics at Higher Level. Applicants presenting GCE A-Levels, GCSEs, QQI FET/FETAC awards, or other international qualifications do not get the +25 under the Irish-Leaving-Certificate rule. Each non-CE pathway has its own scoring grid (published by CAO) which may or may not include its own bonus points — always check the parallel-scoring rules for your specific qualification entry route.

### 💡 Pro Tips
- The threshold H6 in HL Maths unlocks +25 — there is no point trying to claw back +25 by improving a different subject, it is one of the best point-arbitrage opportunities in the Irish system
- Take HL Maths if you can realistically achieve H6 — even a H6 in HL Maths + the +25 bonus is worth more points than a H1 in many other subjects
- Plan your six best subjects around the +25 — if you are at or near the cap, an extra H1 in another subject is wasted (capped at 625) but the +25 still applies
- Read the CAO Handbook for your specific course — some STEM programmes REQUIRE HL Maths at a specific threshold as a course entry condition, independent of the +25 bonus
- The Common Points Scale Calculator (cao.ie/index.php?bb=cps&page=newcps) lets you model your aggregate with and without the +25 bonus
