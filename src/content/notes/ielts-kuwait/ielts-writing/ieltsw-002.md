# Task 1 — Data Comparison and Selection — IELTS Writing Notes

Task 1 of IELTS Academic Writing rewards candidates who select and group the most significant features from the data, ignore minor detail, and signal comparison with appropriate cohesive devices. Listing every data point is a common failure mode.

> Verify the live Writing format on https://ielts.org/ before planning revision.

---

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)

#### Core facts in one pass

- **Selection:** choose the most significant features — not every data point.
- **Grouping:** organise data into 2-3 logical categories.
- **Comparison vocabulary:** whereas, in contrast, similarly, on the other hand, while, compared to.
- **Overview:** state the key trend or comparison in the overview paragraph.
- **Cohesion:** one cohesive device per paragraph is enough.

#### Examiner traps

- Listing every data point without selecting the key features.
- Forgetting the overview (Task Achievement band 7+ requires an overview).
- Using the same cohesive device repeatedly ("and," "and," "and").

---

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)

#### Selecting key features

For a graph with 5 categories over 5 time periods, the candidate cannot describe all 25 data points. Instead, select:

- The highest and lowest values.
- The biggest increase or decrease.
- Any anomalies or crossovers.

#### Grouping data

Group data by:

- **Time period:** early years vs. late years.
- **Category:** type A vs. type B vs. type C.
- **Trend:** rising vs. falling vs. stable.

Two body paragraphs is the standard. Three body paragraphs are appropriate for complex data with three clear groups.

#### Cohesion in Task 1

Cohesive devices used in Task 1:

- **Sequencing:** first, second, finally, then, next.
- **Comparison:** whereas, in contrast, similarly, while, on the other hand.
- **Cause/effect:** as a result, consequently, due to, owing to, leading to.
- **Emphasis:** particularly, especially, notably, in particular.

Use them sparingly — one or two per paragraph is enough. Overuse makes the writing mechanical.

---

### 🔴 Deep — Long-Term Mastery (1mo–6mo)

#### Where data comparison appears across IELTS Writing

Data comparison is the core of Task 1. The Task Achievement band descriptor rewards "a clear overview" and "key features supported by data."

#### Common misconceptions (and the correction)

- "Every data point must be mentioned." No — only the key features.
- "Cohesive devices are required on every sentence." No — they should be used sparingly.
- "Overview must be at the start." It can be after the introduction, before the body paragraphs.

#### Exam technique

- Spend 3-5 minutes planning: identify 2-3 key features and group them.
- Write the overview second, after the introduction.
- Use specific data in the body paragraphs to support the key features.
