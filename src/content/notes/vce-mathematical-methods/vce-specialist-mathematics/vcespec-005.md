# Vectors and Vector Algebra — VCE Specialist Mathematics (VCAA 2023) Notes

Vectors and Vector Algebra is a Year 12 topic in VCE Specialist Mathematics Units 3 and 4. The 2023 Study Design covers vector notation, vector operations (addition, scalar multiplication, dot product, cross product), unit vectors, projection and resolution of vectors. The topic is tested in Examination 2 and is foundational for Mechanics and Kinematics questions.

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)

#### Core facts in one pass

- **Vector notation.** a = (a₁, a₂, a₃) or a = a₁ i + a₂ j + a₃ k. Magnitude |a| = √(a₁² + a₂² + a₃²).
- **Addition.** (a₁, a₂) + (b₁, b₂) = (a₁ + b₁, a₂ + b₂).
- **Scalar multiplication.** λ (a₁, a₂) = (λa₁, λa₂).
- **Dot product.** a · b = a₁ b₁ + a₂ b₂ = |a||b| cos θ.
- **Cross product (3D).** a × b = (a₂b₃ − a₃b₂, a₃b₁ − a₁b₃, a₁b₂ − a₂b₁) = |a||b| sin θ n̂.
- **Unit vector.** a/|a|.

#### Examiner traps

- Confusing the dot product (scalar) with the cross product (vector).
- Mixing up 2D and 3D vector operations.
- Forgetting the right-hand rule for the cross product direction.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)

#### Vector arithmetic

In 2D: a = (a₁, a₂), b = (b₁, b₂). Addition: a + b = (a₁ + b₁, a₂ + b₂). Subtraction: a − b = (a₁ − b₁, a₂ − b₂). Scalar multiplication: λ a = (λa₁, λa₂).

In 3D: extend with third component.

#### Magnitude and direction

|a| = √(a₁² + a₂²) in 2D; √(a₁² + a₂² + a₃²) in 3D. Direction: angle θ = arctan(a₂/a₁) (with quadrant adjustment).

Unit vector in the direction of a: â = a/|a|.

#### Dot product

a · b = a₁ b₁ + a₂ b₂ + a₃ b₃. Also = |a||b| cos θ where θ is the angle between a and b. Therefore cos θ = (a · b)/(|a||b|).

The dot product is zero iff a ⊥ b.

#### Cross product (3D)

a × b = (a₂b₃ − a₃b₂, a₃b₁ − a₁b₃, a₁b₂ − a₂b₁). Magnitude |a × b| = |a||b| sin θ. Direction is perpendicular to both a and b by the right-hand rule.

#### Resolution and projection

Component of a along b: (a · b̂) where b̂ = b/|b|. Projection vector: (a · b̂) b̂.

### 🔴 Deep — Long-Term Mastery (1mo–6mo)

#### Why Vectors underpins Mechanics

- **Mechanics questions use vectors for position, velocity, acceleration, force and momentum.** Each quantity has both magnitude and direction.
- **Projectile motion** is described by a position vector with horizontal and vertical components.
- **Newton's laws** are vector equations: F = ma, where F and a are vectors.

#### Exam technique

- For "find the angle between two vectors", use cos θ = (a · b)/(|a||b|).
- For "find the unit vector in the direction of a", compute a/|a|.
- For "find the component of a perpendicular to b", subtract the parallel component: a − (a · b̂) b̂.

#### Common misconceptions (and the correction)

- "a · b = b · a always." Yes, but a × b = −b × a.
- "The cross product is commutative." No — a × b = −b × a.
- "Cross product is only for 2D." Cross product is defined only in 3D and 7D. In 2D use the perpendicular vector.

#### Specification reference

#### Vector Resolution, Dot Products, and Linear Dependence

In VCE Specialist Mathematics Exam 1 (technology-free) and Exam 2 (CAS-active), vectors are frequently used to prove geometric theorems. The scalar dot product directly yields perpendicularity when zero. The vector projection of vector u onto vector v is given by standard projection formulas. Always verify linearly independent sets when solving 3D coordinate geometry problems involving lines and planes.
