# Biomolecules: Carbohydrates, Proteins, and Lipids

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

Biomolecules are the organic building blocks of life, and HAAD Chemistry tests three classes: **carbohydrates** (polyhydroxy aldehydes/ketones with empirical pattern Cₙ(H₂O)ₙ, e.g. glucose C₆H₁₂O₆), **proteins** (α-amino acid polymers joined by peptide bonds −CO−NH−), and **lipids** (hydrophobic esters such as triglycerides plus amphipathic phospholipids).

#### Carbohydrate essentials

- **Monosaccharides** (glucose, fructose) → **disaccharides** (maltose, lactose, sucrose) → **polysaccharides** (starch, glycogen, cellulose).
- Glycosidic bonds link units; sucrose is *non-reducing*, maltose is *reducing*.

#### Protein essentials

- 20 standard amino acids, ~9 **essential** (must come from diet).
- Peptide bond forms by **condensation**, broken by **hydrolysis**.
- Levels: 1° sequence → 2° helix/sheet → 3° folding → 4° subunits.
- **Denaturation** disrupts 2°/3°/4°, never 1°.

#### Lipid essentials

- Saturated → solid fats (palmitic, stearic); unsaturated → oils (oleic, linoleic).
- **Phospholipids** (not triglycerides) build cell membranes.

**High-yield tests:** Benedict's (reducing sugar → brick-red Cu₂O), Iodine (starch → blue-black), Biuret (peptide bonds → violet), Sudan III (lipids → red).

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### Carbohydrate Structure and Reactions
Glucose exists as an open-chain polyhydroxy aldehyde (one −CHO + five −OH) and, in solution, as α- and β-pyranose **anomers** that interconvert through **mutarotation**. Disaccharides form via a **glycosidic bond** with loss of water; sucrose uses an α-1,β-2 linkage making it *non-reducing*, while maltose (α-1,4) and lactose (β-1,4) remain reducing. Polysaccharides differ in branching: starch (amylose + amylopectin) is the plant storage form, glycogen is the animal storage form, and cellulose is a β-1,4 structural polymer.

| Test | Reacts with | Positive result |
|---|---|---|
| Benedict's | Reducing sugars | Brick-red Cu₂O precipitate |
| Fehling's | Reducing sugars | Same as Benedict's |
| Iodine | Starch (helical coil) | Blue-black complex |
| Molisch | All carbohydrates | Violet ring |

#### Proteins: Bond and Structure
The **peptide bond** −CO−NH− is an amide formed by condensation; it has ~40% double-bond character (C–N ≈ 1.32 Å), forcing planarity and restricting rotation, which is why the peptide backbone is rigid while rotation occurs around the adjacent Cα. **Denaturation** by heat, pH extremes, urea, or heavy metals (Hg²⁺, Pb²⁺) unfolds 2°/3°/4° structure while preserving 1° sequence — egg-white coagulation is the textbook example and clinically relevant in HAAD pharmacy and nursing questions.

> HAAD trap: any answer claiming denaturation changes the amino-acid sequence is wrong; 1° structure is covalent and survives denaturation.

#### Lipids: Classes and Functions
Lipids are classified as **simple** (triglycerides, waxes), **compound** (phospholipids, glycolipids), or **derived** (steroids, terpenes). A **triglyceride** is a triester of glycerol with three fatty acids; saponification (KOH hydrolysis) yields glycerol + 3 fatty-acid salts ("soap").

| Index | Formula | Units | What it measures |
|---|---|---|---|
| Saponification value | (V₍blank₎ − V₍sample₎) × N(KOH) × 56.1 / m | mg KOH/g fat | Average chain length |
| Iodine number | (V₍blank₎ − V₍sample₎) × N(Na₂S₂O₃) × 126.9 / m | g I₂/100 g fat | Degree of unsaturation |

**Phospholipids** are *amphipathic*: a hydrophilic phosphate head + two hydrophobic fatty-acid tails. This dual nature drives the lipid bilayer that forms every cell membrane.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Edge Cases and Stereochemistry
Acetic acid C₂H₄O₂ fits Cₙ(H₂O)ₙ yet is not a carbohydrate — the formula is empirical, not a true hydrate. Likewise, **inositol** (cyclohexane-1,2,3,4,5,6-hexol) is a sugar alcohol but lacks a carbonyl. D- and L-glucose enantiomers differ biologically: only D-sugars are metabolised by human enzymes. Sucrose's α-1,β-2 linkage blocks a free anomeric −OH, eliminating reducing power until invertase hydrolyses it.

#### Protein Folding and Disease
**Prion diseases** (Creutzfeldt-Jakob) result from misfolded 3° structure — 1° sequence identical, yet function lost. A disulfide bridge (−S−S−) stabilises tertiary folds (insulin, antibodies); reduction with β-mercaptoethanol cleaves it without hydrolysing peptide bonds. **Essential amino acids** (histidine, isoleucine, leucine, lysine, methionine, phenylalanine, threonine, tryptophan, valine) must be supplied dietetically; in renal/liver failure, clinicians restrict phenylalanine — a HAAD pharmacy-relevant fact.

#### Lipid Quality and Clinical Links
A high **iodine number** signals polyunsaturation — drying oils (linseed, fish oil) absorb O₂ and polymerise, useful in paints but unstable in food. Low iodine number (coconut, palm) → saturated → solid at room temperature. **Phospholipid bilayer** assembly is driven by the **hydrophobic effect**, not by chemical bonds; cholesterol inserts between fatty-acid tails to modulate fluidity.

> Common exam trap: acid value (mg KOH/g to neutralise free fatty acids) ≠ saponification value (mg KOH/g to hydrolyse esters). Confusing them costs marks.

#### Common Mistakes (Compact List)
- Calling Cn(H2O)n "hydrated carbon" — it is only an empirical pattern.
- Treating sucrose as reducing — it is non-reducing until hydrolysed.
- Saying denaturation alters 1° structure — false.
- Reporting iodine number in mg instead of g I₂/100 g fat — factor-of-100 error.
- Claiming triglycerides form membranes — only phospholipids do.

#### Practice Prompts
1. A urine sample gives a brick-red precipitate with Benedict's reagent but a negative Clinistix (glucose oxidase). Explain which carbohydrate classes can produce this discrepancy and why.
2. Cooking an egg white denatures ovalbumin. State which structural levels are lost and which covalent feature (if any) is broken, justifying the answer with a one-sentence mechanism.
