# Functions, Relations and Graphs (Specialist) — VCE Specialist Mathematics (VCAA 2023) Notes

Functions, relations and graphs is one of the four Areas of Study in VCE Specialist Mathematics Units 3 and 4. The 2023 Study Design covers circular functions, combinations of functions and the connection between trigonometric and exponential forms, plus the introduction of vectors as functions of a scalar parameter. The strand is assessed in both Examination 1 and Examination 2.

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)

#### Core facts in one pass

- **Circular functions in radians.** sin x, cos x, tan x with x in radians. Exact values for 0, π/6, π/4, π/3, π/2, π.
- **Identities.** sin² x + cos² x = 1. tan x = sin x / cos x. sin(A ± B), cos(A ± B), sin 2A, cos 2A, tan(A ± B), tan 2A.
- **Combinations of functions.** Sums, products, compositions. Composite (f ∘ g)(x) = f(g(x)).
- **Vectors as functions.** Position vector r(t) = x(t) i + y(t) j. Velocity v(t) = r'(t). Acceleration a(t) = r''(t).
- **Inverse functions.** f⁻¹(x); exists if f is one-to-one.
- **Transformations.** Translate, stretch, compress, reflect.

#### Examiner traps

- Using degrees instead of radians — Specialist Mathematics uses radians throughout.
- Forgetting that the inverse function is the reflection in y = x, not 1/f(x).
- Confusing scalar and vector functions — scalar functions output a number; vector functions output a vector.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)

#### Circular functions

sin x and cos x have period 2π. tan x has period π. Exact values:
- sin 0 = 0, cos 0 = 1.
- sin π/6 = 1/2, cos π/6 = √3/2.
- sin π/4 = √2/2, cos π/4 = √2/2.
- sin π/3 = √3/2, cos π/3 = 1/2.
- sin π/2 = 1, cos π/2 = 0.

For angles in other quadrants, use the ASTC rule.

#### Compound angle identities

sin(A + B) = sin A cos B + cos A sin B.
sin(A − B) = sin A cos B − cos A sin B.
cos(A + B) = cos A cos B − sin A sin B.
cos(A − B) = cos A cos B + sin A sin B.
tan(A + B) = (tan A + tan B) / (1 − tan A tan B).
tan(A − B) = (tan A − tan B) / (1 + tan A tan B).

Double angle (set A = B):
sin 2A = 2 sin A cos A.
cos 2A = cos² A − sin² A = 1 − 2 sin² A = 2 cos² A − 1.
tan 2A = 2 tan A / (1 − tan² A).

#### Combinations of functions

Sum: (f + g)(x) = f(x) + g(x). Product: (fg)(x) = f(x) × g(x). Composite: (f ∘ g)(x) = f(g(x)).

Domain restrictions are applied at every step. (f ∘ g)(x) = √(g(x)) requires g(x) ≥ 0.

#### Vectors as functions

Position vector r(t) = x(t) i + y(t) j describes a particle's position at time t. Velocity v(t) = dr/dt = x'(t) i + y'(t) j. Acceleration a(t) = dv/dt = x''(t) i + y''(t) j.

Constant velocity motion: r(t) = r₀ + v t. Uniform circular motion: r(t) = R cos(ωt) i + R sin(ωt) j where ω is angular velocity.

### 🔴 Deep — Long-Term Mastery (1mo–6mo)

#### Why this Area of Study underpins Mechanics and Complex Numbers

- **Circular functions** appear in the parametric equations for circular and oscillating motion.
- **Combinations of functions** appear throughout Specialist — in complex number arithmetic (combining real and imaginary parts) and in mechanics (combining velocity components).
- **Vector functions** are the foundation for kinematics. Velocity and acceleration are derivatives of position; position is the integral of velocity.

#### Exam technique

- For circular function equations, work in radians throughout.
- For vector function problems, identify the position vector and take derivatives (or integrate) to find velocity and acceleration.
- For trigonometric identity questions, work on one side and transform it into the other.

#### Common misconceptions (and the correction)

- "Degrees are interchangeable with radians." No — formulas like sin x ≈ x − x³/6 only hold in radians.
- "Velocity is the same as speed." Velocity is a vector; speed is the magnitude of velocity.
- "r(t) and r are the same." r(t) is a position vector function; r is its magnitude.
