# B. Firms and Production — HKDSE Economics Notes

This topic covers Topic B of the Compulsory Part of HKDSE Economics. It addresses the firm and its objectives (profit maximisation, alternative objectives); production (total, average and marginal product); costs in the short run and the long run (fixed, variable, average and marginal cost); and revenue and profit (total, average and marginal revenue).

> Verify the live HKDSE Economics Assessment Framework on https://www.hkeaa.edu.hk/ before planning revision.

---

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)

#### Core facts in one pass

- **The firm**: an economic unit that combines inputs to produce outputs.
- **Objectives of the firm**:
  - **Profit maximisation**: producing where MR = MC.
  - **Sales revenue maximisation**: producing where MR = 0 (Baumol's theory).
  - **Sales growth maximisation**: producing where the growth rate of sales is maximised (Marris's theory).
  - **Satisficing**: producing a satisfactory level of profit rather than the maximum (Simon).
  - **Managerial utility maximisation**: managers pursue their own goals (perks, status, job security).
  - **Corporate social responsibility (CSR)**: balancing profit with social and environmental goals.
- **Production**: TP (total product), AP (average product = TP/L), MP (marginal product = ΔTP/ΔL).
- **Short run**: at least one input is fixed; (typically capital K is fixed, labour L is variable).
- **Long run**: all inputs are variable.
- **Costs**:
  - **Total fixed cost (TFC)**: cost of fixed inputs; independent of output.
  - **Total variable cost (TVC)**: cost of variable inputs; increases with output.
  - **Total cost (TC)**: TFC + TVC.
  - **Average fixed cost (AFC)**: TFC / Q.
  - **Average variable cost (AVC)**: TVC / Q.
  - **Average total cost (ATC)**: TC / Q.
  - **Marginal cost (MC)**: ΔTC / ΔQ.
- **Revenue**:
  - **Total revenue (TR)**: P × Q.
  - **Average revenue (AR)**: TR / Q = P.
  - **Marginal revenue (MR)**: ΔTR / ΔQ.
- **Profit**: TR − TC. Maximised where MR = MC.

#### Examiner traps

- Confusing MP with AP — MP is the change from one more unit of input; AP is the average over all units.
- Forgetting that profit maximisation requires MR = MC AND the MC curve must be rising at the point.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)

#### Production function

The production function shows the maximum output achievable from each combination of inputs: Q = f(L, K, ...).

**Short-run production**: at least one input is fixed. As L increases:

- **Increasing marginal returns**: MP increases (specialisation, division of labour).
- **Decreasing marginal returns**: MP decreases (eventually).
- **Negative marginal returns**: MP becomes negative (overcrowding, fatigue).

**Long-run production**: all inputs variable. Returns to scale:

- **Increasing returns to scale (IRS)**: doubling inputs more than doubles output (specialisation, bulk discounts).
- **Constant returns to scale (CRS)**: doubling inputs doubles output.
- **Decreasing returns to scale (DRS)**: doubling inputs less than doubles output (managerial diseconomies).

#### Cost curves

Short-run cost curves have a U-shape:

- AFC decreases continuously as Q increases (TFC / Q).
- AVC and ATC are U-shaped (due to the law of diminishing marginal returns).
- MC is U-shaped and intersects AVC and ATC at their minimum points.

Long-run cost curves (LRAC) are the envelope of all possible short-run ATC curves. They can show IRS, CRS and DRS.

#### Revenue and profit

For a perfectly competitive firm (price taker): P = AR = MR. The firm sells all output at the market price.

For an imperfectly competitive firm (price setter): P > MR. To sell more, the firm must lower the price on all units.

**Profit maximisation**: produce where MR = MC, AND MC must be rising (the second-order condition). Profit at that output: π = TR − TC.

**Shutdown decision**: in the short run, the firm continues to produce if TR ≥ TVC (i.e. P ≥ AVC). Otherwise, shut down.

**Exit decision**: in the long run, the firm exits if TR ≤ TC (i.e. P ≤ ATC).

### 🔴 Deep — Long-Term Mastery (1mo–6mo)

#### Cross-topic links

- **A. Basic Economic Concepts**: cost and revenue calculations.
- **C. Market and Price**: market structures determine MR vs P.
- **D. Competition and Market Structure**: imperfect competition and pricing power.

#### Exam technique

- **For production questions**: distinguish MP from AP; identify the law of diminishing marginal returns.
- **For cost questions**: identify which cost is fixed vs variable; compute AC, MC, AFC, AVC.
- **For profit questions**: identify MR = MC for max profit; compute π = TR - TC.

#### Common misconceptions (and the correction)

- "Firms always maximise profit." Many firms pursue other objectives (sales growth, manager utility, satisficing).
- "Average cost and marginal cost are the same." MC is the cost of one more unit; AC is the cost per unit averaged over all units.
- "Lower costs mean lower prices." Lower costs reduce the minimum AC; prices depend on the market structure and demand elasticity.
