# Indian Economy & Finance

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

- **GDP at market price** = C + I + G + (X − M), where C is private consumption, I is investment, G is government spending, X is exports, and M is imports.
- **GNP = GDP + Net Factor Income from Abroad (NFIA)**, while **NNP = GNP − Depreciation**; NNP at market price is the formal definition of National Income.
- **Fiscal Deficit** = Total Expenditure − Total Receipts (excluding borrowings); **Revenue Deficit** is the gap between revenue expenditure and revenue receipts; **Primary Deficit** = Fiscal Deficit − Interest Payments.
- **RBI's main tools**: Repo Rate (short-term lending to banks), Reverse Repo Rate, Bank Rate (long-term), CRR (cash reserve), SLR (statutory liquidity ratio), MSF, and the 6-member **Monetary Policy Committee (MPC)** that targets CPI inflation at 4% (±2%).
- **SEBI** regulates capital markets (BSE, NSE, IPOs, FPIs); **NITI Aayog** (est. 1 Jan 2015) replaced the Planning Commission as policy think tank.
- **GST** launched on 1 July 2017 with four slabs — CGST, SGST, IGST, UTGST; petroleum, alcohol, and electricity remain outside its ambit.
- **CPI** is the official inflation target, not WPI; **NPA** classification reverts to the 90-day norm post-COVID.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### Measuring National Output
India's macro aggregates sit on a strict hierarchy. **GDP at market price** uses actual transaction prices and includes net indirect taxes. **GDP at factor cost** strips those taxes out, giving the real cost of production. **GNP** adds Net Factor Income from Abroad (income earned by Indians abroad minus income earned by foreigners in India). **NNP at market price**, after subtracting depreciation on capital stock, is the textbook **National Income**.

> Exam tip: SNAP MCQs frequently ask "what is the difference between GDP at market price and GDP at factor cost?" — the answer is **net indirect taxes**.

#### Fiscal Architecture of the Union Budget
The government splits its books into **Revenue Account** (day-to-day, non-asset-creating) and **Capital Account** (asset-creating, loans given, debt discharged). Deficits must be read in this light:

| Deficit Type | Formula | What it Reveals |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue Deficit | Revenue Expenditure − Revenue Receipts | Shortfall after routine income vs spending |
| Fiscal Deficit | Total Expenditure − Total Receipts (ex-borrowings) | Total borrowing requirement |
| Primary Deficit | Fiscal Deficit − Interest Payments | Borrowing after interest burden is netted out |

#### RBI and Monetary Policy
The **Reserve Bank of India**, established 1935 and nationalised in 1949, is an autonomous statutory body — not a government department. Its **MPC** (3 external + 3 RBI members, chaired by the Governor) decides the **policy repo rate** bi-monthly. Other tools include **CRR** (cash parked with RBI, currently 4%), **SLR** (gold/govt-securities banks must hold), **MSF** (overnight emergency window), and the **Open Market Operations**.

#### Capital Markets and SEBI
**SEBI** was set up in 1988 as a non-statutory body and given statutory powers via the SEBI Act, 1992. It regulates stock exchanges (**BSE, NSE**), mutual funds, IPOs, FPIs, and insider trading. A foreign portfolio investor holding below 10% in a company is an **FPI**; **FDI** crosses the 10% threshold and signals long-term control.

#### Government Schemes
Key flagships: **GST** (1 July 2017), **Jan Dhan Yojana** (financial inclusion, 2014), **MUDRA** (micro-finance), **Startup India** (2016), **Make in India** (2014), and **UPI** (NPCI's real-time payment system, 2016).

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Inflation: CPI vs WPI
**WPI** tracks wholesale prices of goods at the producer level and is published by the Office of the Economic Adviser. **CPI** measures retail prices paid by consumers and is computed by NSO across rural and urban baskets. Since 2014, the **RBI's MPC targets CPI** (4% ± 2%), not WPI — a frequent SNAP trap.

#### Balance of Payments and Forex
The **BoP** has two halves. The **Current Account** records trade in goods, services (invisibles), and transfers. The **Capital Account** records FDI, FPI, external commercial borrowings, and banking capital. India's foreign exchange reserves have four components: **Foreign Currency Assets, Gold, SDRs, and the Reserve Tranche in the IMF**. A widening **Current Account Deficit** financed by short-term debt is the classic 1991 crisis pattern that triggered LPG (Liberalisation, Privatisation, Globalisation) reforms.

#### Banking Structure and NPA Norms
**Scheduled Commercial Banks**, **Regional Rural Banks (RRBs)**, **Cooperative Banks**, **Small Finance Banks**, and **Payment Banks** form India's banking architecture. **Priority Sector Lending (PSL)** mandates 40% of ANBC (Adjusted Net Bank Credit) toward agriculture, MSMEs, education, and housing. An asset becomes an **NPA** when interest/installment is overdue for **90 days**; sub-categories are sub-standard, doubtful, and loss asset.

#### Common Traps in the Exam Hall

> Repo rate is **not** the rate RBI lends to the public; it lends to commercial banks overnight. The Bank Rate is the long-term penal rate.

- NITI Aayog is **advisory** — it does not allocate plan funds like the old Planning Commission did.
- **Finance Commission** (constitutional, every 5 years) ≠ NITI Aayog (executive think tank).
- **Cryptocurrency** is not legal tender; taxed at 30% under Section 115BBH (Finance Act 2022).
- **Basel III** prescribes a minimum **Capital Adequacy Ratio** with Common Equity Tier 1 at 5.5% (including the 2.5% conservation buffer).

#### Practice Prompts
1. A country's GDP is ₹260 lakh crore, NFIA is −₹5 lakh crore, and depreciation is ₹20 lakh crore. Calculate NNP at market price.
2. Distinguish between CRR and SLR — state who maintains them, with whom, and what form the reserves take.
