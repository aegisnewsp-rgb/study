# Current Affairs — Recent Important Events (2024–2026)

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

- **Scope**: events, awards, summits, sports results, and Bangladesh national developments from roughly the last 2–3 years, tested as short factual MCQs worth about 2–3 of the 100 GK questions on the DU Admission paper.
- **Bangladesh focus**: Padma Bridge rail link (opened October 2024), Dhaka Metro MRT Line-6 extension, National Budget 2024–25 and 2025–26 highlights, and the post-August-2024 transition in which **Muhammad Yunus** became Chief Adviser of the interim government.
- **Sports**: ICC Men's T20 World Cup 2024 won by **India** (USA/West Indies hosts); ICC Champions Trophy 2025 hosted by **India**; Paris 2024 Olympics topped by **USA**; FIFA World Cup 2026 will be hosted across **USA, Canada, Mexico**.
- **Awards**: track the Nobel Prizes for 2024 and 2025, Booker Prize, Aga Khan Award for Architecture, and Indian Padma Awards.
- **International bodies**: confirm current heads of the **UN, WHO, IMF, World Bank, WTO, ICC** as of the exam month.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### Reading Method
Maintain a dated register divided into five columns: **Date, Event, Person/Place, Category, Source**. Read the *Daily Star* and *Prothom Alo* (English editions) at least three times a week, and verify with **bdnews24** or Bangladesh government press releases before locking an answer.

#### Bangladesh-Centric Events
- **Muhammad Yunus** assumed the role of Chief Adviser of the interim government on 8 August 2024 following the political transition, distinct from his 2006 Nobel Peace Prize.
- **Padma Bridge rail link** was inaugurated in October 2024, connecting Dhaka with the southern districts.
- **Dhaka Metro MRT Line-6** extended its operational stretch through 2024–2025; track station additions.
- **National Budgets 2024–25 and 2025–26** were presented in June of each year; remember headline tax-revenue targets and any major VAT changes.
- **Bangladesh Bank** revised its policy (repo) rate multiple times in 2024–2025 in response to inflation.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Edge Cases and Transliteration Traps
Bengali names transliterate inconsistently in English, so DU papers often test whether you can recognise the same person under two spellings. **Sheikh Hasina** and **Sheikh Rehana** are sisters, not the same person. **Muhammad Yunus** appears in two distinct roles separated by 18 years — the 2006 Nobel laureate and the 2024 Chief Adviser. Watch for questions that swap these deliberately. Similarly, do not confuse the **ICC Men's Cricket World Cup 2023 (ODI, won by Australia)** with the **T20 World Cup 2024 (won by India)** or the **Champions Trophy 2025**.

#### Adjacent Topics Worth Linking
- **Dhaka International Film Festival (DIFF)** and **Prothom Alo Person of the Year** recur annually.
- **SAARC** remains dormant; questions may test whether you know BIMSTEC has functionally absorbed regional cooperation.
- **IMEC (India–Middle East–Europe Corridor)** was signed at the G20 New Delhi summit (Sept 2023) and continued to feature in 2024–2025 trade-route discussions.
- Track **Bangabandhu Shoad** (Bangladesh's highest civilian award) recipients for 2024 and 2025.

#### Common Exam Traps
- Confusing the year an event was **held** with the year it was **reported**.
- Quoting a cabinet minister or MD from 2023 as if still in office in 2025.
- Treating the 2020 Tokyo Olympics (held 2021) as the most recent Games.
- Listing the wrong G20 host (rotates annually; verify the current cycle).

#### Practice Prompts
1. Who won the ICC Men's T20 World Cup 2024, and which two countries co-hosted it?
2. Name the current Chief Adviser of Bangladesh's interim government and the year he originally received the Nobel Peace Prize.
