# History

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

The CUET UG General Test **History** section spans three blocks: **Ancient, Medieval, and Modern India**, plus **Indian heritage sites**. Of the 4% weight in the General Test, you can expect 5–8 factual MCQs in roughly **60 minutes** total test time.

- **Modern India carries the heaviest PYQ weight** (~50%), followed by Medieval (~25%) and Ancient (~15%); heritage and monuments get 1–2 standalone questions.
- Revise NCERT Class 6–12 themes: dates of movements, founders of dynasties, and the site → dynasty → monument mapping (e.g., **Khajuraho → Chandela dynasty → UNESCO 1986**).
- High-yield one-liners: **1857 Revolt causes**, **Rowlatt Act 1919 → Jallianwala Bagh**, **Dandi March 12 March 1930**, **Quit India 8 Aug 1942**, **15 Aug 1947 Independence**, **26 Jan 1950 Republic**.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### Ancient India — Sites and Empires
The **Indus Valley Civilization (c. 2600–1900 BCE)** flourished at Harappa, Mohenjo-daro, Lothal (dockyard), Dholavira (reservoir), and Kalibangan (fire altars). The **Vedic Period** introduced the varna framework and the *shruti* canon. **Ashoka's Dhamma** (3rd century BCE) spread Buddhism across Sri Lanka and Central Asia via rock edicts at Girnar and Shahbazgarhi. The **Gupta Empire (c. 320–550 CE)** patronised Kalidasa (*Abhijnana Shakuntalam*), Aryabhata (zero and π), and Samudragupta's conquests.

#### Medieval India — Sultanates, Mughals, Bhakti
**Qutb-ud-din Aibak** founded the Slave Dynasty in 1206; Iltutmish consolidated it. **Akbar (r. 1556–1605)** built the mansabdari ranking, abolished jaziya in 1564, and promulgated **Din-i-Ilahi (1582)**. Bhakti saints Kabir, Tulsidas, and Mirabai preached in vernaculars; **Chishti and Suhrawardi** Sufi orders ran *khanqahs*. **Shivaji** founded the Maratha kingdom in 1674, later expanded by **Peshwa Baji Rao I**.

#### Modern India — Freedom Struggle
The British reforms: **Regulating Act 1773**, Charter Acts 1813/1833/1853, **Indian Councils Act 1909 (Morley–Minto)** introduced separate electorates; **Government of India Act 1919 (Montagu–Chelmsford)** introduced dyarchy. The **1857 Revolt** began at Meerut (10 May 1857) and spread under Rani Lakshmibai, Mangal Pandey, Tantia Tope, and Nana Saheb. **INC was founded in 1885**.

| Movement | Year | Trigger / Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Champaran Satyagraha | 1917 | Indigo planters' issue |
| Non-Cooperation | 1920 | Rowlatt + Jallianwala Bagh |
| Civil Disobedience (Dandi March) | 1930 | Salt Law broken 6 April 1930 |
| Quit India | 1942 | "Do or Die" — 8 Aug 1942 |

- **Trap**: Dandi March belongs to **Civil Disobedience**, not Non-Cooperation.
- **Trap**: Morley–Minto ≠ Montagu–Chelmsford — separate electorates vs. dyarchy.
- INA trials at **Red Fort (1945–46)** electrified the freedom movement under Subhas Chandra Bose.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Heritage Sites and Architectural Styles
India has **42 UNESCO World Heritage Sites** (as of 2024) — 34 cultural, 7 natural, 1 mixed (Khangchendzonga). They differ from **ASI protected monuments** under the Ancient Monuments and Archaeological Sites and Remains Act 1958. Key pairings for MCQs: **Taj Mahal (Agra, Shah Jahan, 1632–1653)**, **Qutb Minar (Delhi, Iltutmish, 1199)**, **Hampi (Vijayanagara, 1565 ruined)**, **Khajuraho (Chandela, 950–1050)**, **Konark Sun Temple (Eastern Ganga, 1250)**, **Ajanta–Ellora (Rashtrakuta/Gupta patronage)**.

#### Common Mistakes to Avoid
- Treating 1857 as a pre-planned pan-India war — it began as a sepoy mutiny at Meerut using greased cartridges and spread through regional grievances.
- Calling the Cabinet Mission (1946) the Mountbatten Plan (3 June 1947) — they are distinct.
- Mixing UNESCO **listing year** with construction date — examiners test both.
- Confusing **Vijayanagara** (Sangama, Saluva, Tuluva dynasties) with the Bahmani Sultanate, a contemporary rival.

#### Cross-Topic Linkages
- History ↔ **Polity**: Constituent Assembly debates, Drafting Committee chaired by B.R. Ambedkar (1947).
- History ↔ **Geography**: Indus sites lie in present-day Pakistan (Mohenjo-daro) and Gujarat (Dholavira/Lothal).
- History ↔ **Culture**: Bhakti–Sufi synthesis influenced regional literature (Bengali Vaishnavism, Urdu poetry).

#### Practice Prompts
1. "Arrange the following in chronological order: Dandi March, Quit India, Non-Cooperation, Rowlatt Act, Cripps Mission."
2. "Match the monument to its patron: Khajuraho, Qutb Minar, Taj Mahal, Hampi, Konark."

> **Scoring tip**: Allocate ~45 seconds per History MCQ. Skip–mark heritage questions whose UNESCO year you cannot recall; Modern India questions yield faster recall.
