# National symbols and ideology

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

UNDANA's General Knowledge Topic 5 clusters four pillars you must keep separate in memory: **national symbols** (Garuda Pancasila emblem + the motto *Bhinneka Tunggal Ika*), **national ideology** (Pancasila's five principles + UUD 1945 as the constitutional basis), **administrative geography** (Kupang as the capital of Nusa Tenggara Timur province, where UNDANA's main campus stands), and the **university's own facts** (founded in 1962 as a public university under Kementerian Pendidikan Tinggi, admissions now run through SNPMB/SNBT alongside UNDANA's mandiri pathway).

- **Must-know constants**: Lambang Negara = Garuda Pancasila; semboyan = Bhinneka Tunggal Ika; ibu kota NTT = Kota Kupang.
- **Quick weight cue**: this set sits inside the ~3% General Knowledge band of the UNDANA admission test, mostly as 1–2 short multiple-choice items.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### National symbols and ideology

The **Lambang Negara Garuda Pancasila** is the official emblem of the Republic of Indonesia, ratified alongside the four national identity pillars. The eagle carries a shield bearing the five sila of **Pancasila**: Ketuhanan Yang Maha Esa, Kemanusiaan yang Adil dan Beradab, Persatuan Indonesia, Kerakyatan yang Dipimpin oleh Hikmat Kebijaksanaan dalam Permusyawaratan/Perwakilan, dan Keadilan Sosial bagi Seluruh Rakyat Indonesia. The national motto **Bhinneka Tunggal Ika** ("Unity in Diversity") comes from Old Javanese and is paired with **UUD 1945** as the constitutional foundation, together forming the *empat pilar kebangsaan* alongside **NKRI** (Negara Kesatuan Republik Indonesia).

#### Indonesian government structure

Indonesia runs a **presidential system** at the centre: presiden serves simultaneously as head of state and head of government, with the DPR, DPD and MPR providing legislative checks. At the regional level, each provinsi is led by a **gubernur**, while each kabupaten/kota has a **bupati** or **walikota**. For NTT specifically, the gubernur sits in Kupang and coordinates the 22 kabupaten/kota of the province — a fact UNDANA tests because the university's home province is itself a frequently-quizzed administrative unit.

#### UNDANA institutional facts

Universitas Nusa Cendana was **established in 1962** in Kupang and operates as a *Perguruan Tinggi Negeri* (PTN) under the Kementerian Pendidikan Tinggi, Sains, dan Teknologi. The **main campus is in Kupang**, the capital of NTT, not Jakarta or Bali.

#### Admissions pathways

Since the national policy shift, UNDANA admits new students through **SNPMB** — the umbrella covering **SNBT** (national selection based on test scores) and any internal **jalur mandiri UNDANA** selection run by the university itself, each with its own schedule, quota, and registration portal (pmb.undana.ac.id).

#### High-yield pointers

- Expect a contrastive MCQ: Garuda Pancasila vs general eagle icon, Bhinneka Tunggal Ika vs other nationalist mottos, Kupang vs other NTT cities.
- The exam rewards distinguishing *empirically similar* items, not blind memorisation.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Edge cases examiners exploit

The most common traps sit at the *borders* between adjacent concepts. **SNBT vs SNMPTN confusion**: SNBT replaced the UTBN-style national test model in 2024-era admissions, so labelling it as "SNMPTN" is outdated. **Lambang vs semboyan swap**: students mix the emblem (Garuda) with the motto (Bhinneka Tunggal Ika) or with the national anthem (Indonesia Raya), all three of which appear as separate distractor options. **Province vs city confusion**: Kupang is both a *kota* (municipality) and the *ibu kota* of Provinsi NTT — examiners frame answer choices around this dual status. Finally, **UNDANA's location**: candidates occasionally guess Bali or Surabaya because of regional university brand confusion.

#### Connections to adjacent topics

Topic 5 dovetails with **history of higher education in eastern Indonesia** (the 1962 founding sits within a broader wave of *PTN* establishment under regional autonomy legislation) and with **constitutional studies** through the *empat pilar kebangsaan* framework used in sekolah materials nationwide. It also links to **admissions logistics**, since the choice between SNBT and jalur mandiri UNDANA determines registration portal, payment method, and selection instrument.

#### Common mistakes to avoid

- Writing "Universitas Cendana" or "Universitas Nusa Tenggara" — both are wrong; the legal name includes "Nusa".
- Pairing the Garuda Pancasila with the wrong date (the emblem was formalised by PP No. 43/1958, not a 1945 decree).
- Assuming the rector is elected by students — rectorship follows the ministry's statuta mechanism.
- Treating *gubernur NTT* as appointed directly by the president; the governor is elected through a *Pemilukada* alongside the wakil gubernur.

#### Practice prompts

1. A multiple-choice question lists "Burung Rajawali", "Garuda Pancasila", "Elang Jawa", and "Cendrawasih" as possible national emblems. Only one is correct, and the distractor logic tests whether you can name the right symbol, not just identify a bird.
2. A scenario asks: "If UNDANA's main campus sits in the capital of NTT, which city hosts the rectorate and the central administration?" The correct response ties together the geographic hierarchy: **Province of NTT → Kupang** as capital → UNDANA main campus location.
