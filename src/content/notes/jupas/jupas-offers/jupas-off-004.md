# Re-application and Late-cycle Decisions — JUPAS Notes

This topic covers re-application and late-cycle decisions in JUPAS. Candidates who decline or forfeit a Main Round offer can re-apply in a subsequent cycle, including through the non-JUPAS admissions route for the following year. International qualifications and non-HKDSE applicants generally apply directly to the institutions rather than through JUPAS. Re-admission rules and lateral-entry policies vary by institution.

> Verify the live re-application policies on https://www.jupas.edu.hk/ and each university's admissions page before planning revision.

---

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)

#### Core facts in one pass

- **Re-application in a subsequent JUPAS cycle**: candidates who did not receive an offer in the current cycle can apply in a subsequent cycle (subject to the live eligibility rules).
- **Non-JUPAS admissions**: candidates with non-HKDSE qualifications (e.g. IB, GCE A-Level, mainland Gaokao) apply directly to the institutions rather than through JUPAS.
- **Lateral entry**: some institutions accept transfers from sub-degree programmes (e.g. HKMU, SSSDP) to degree programmes.
- **Year 2 or Year 3 entry**: some institutions admit candidates directly to Year 2 or Year 3 based on prior qualifications.

#### Examiner traps

- Assuming non-JUPAS routes have the same deadlines as JUPAS — they don't.
- Assuming lateral entry is automatic — it requires the institution's approval.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)

#### Re-application in a subsequent JUPAS cycle

Candidates who do not receive a JUPAS offer in the current cycle can apply in a subsequent cycle, subject to the live eligibility rules:

- **HKDSE candidates in the current cycle**: can apply for the next cycle (typically after retaking HKDSE).
- **HKDSE candidates in a previous cycle**: can apply as private candidates; some institutions accept HKDSE results from previous cycles.

**Source**: JUPAS Eligibility Rules, https://www.jupas.edu.hk/

#### Non-JUPAS admissions

Candidates who do not hold HKDSE qualifications apply directly to the institutions through non-JUPAS routes. Examples:

- **IB (International Baccalaureate)**: candidates with IB apply through non-JUPAS routes.
- **GCE A-Level**: candidates with GCE A-Level apply through non-JUPAS routes.
- **Mainland China Gaokao**: candidates with Gaokao apply through non-JUPAS routes (the "national universities" recruitment scheme).
- **Other qualifications**: SAT, ACT, etc.

**Source**: each university's Non-JUPAS Admissions page. Examples:

- HKU: https://admissions.hku.hk/
- HKUST: https://join.hkust.edu.hk/
- CityU: https://www.cityu.edu.hk/admo/

#### Lateral entry

Some institutions accept lateral entry from sub-degree programmes (associate degrees, higher diplomas). Candidates with a good academic record in a sub-degree programme may apply for Year 2 or Year 3 entry to a degree programme.

**Source**: each university's Lateral Entry / Senior Year Intake page.

#### Year 2 or Year 3 direct entry

Some institutions admit candidates directly to Year 2 or Year 3 based on prior qualifications (e.g. a completed sub-degree programme, an associate degree, or transfer from another university). This is sometimes called "advanced standing" or "senior year entry".

**Source**: each university's Admissions page.

#### Strategic considerations

**After declining a JUPAS offer**: candidates may seek admission in a subsequent cycle. The acceptance fee is non-refundable.

**After forfeiting a JUPAS offer**: candidates who fail to pay the acceptance fee forfeit the offer. They may participate in Clearing Round (subject to availability) or re-apply in a subsequent cycle.

**Switching programmes after enrolment**: rare; usually requires withdrawal and re-application.

### 🔴 Deep — Long-Term Mastery (1mo–6mo)

#### Cross-topic links

- **Main Round Offer**: the basis for declining or forfeiting.
- **Clearing Round**: an alternative for the current cycle.
- **Programme Choices**: subsequent cycle choices may differ.

#### Exam technique

- **For "describe the re-application options" questions**: state the options (JUPAS next cycle, non-JUPAS, lateral entry).
- **For "what should a candidate consider" questions**: cite timing, fees, eligibility.

#### Common misconceptions

- "JUPAS is the only route." No — non-JUPAS routes exist.
- "Lateral entry is automatic." No — it requires the institution's approval.
