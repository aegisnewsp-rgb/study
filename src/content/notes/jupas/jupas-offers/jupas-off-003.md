# Acceptance Fee, Registration and Enrolment — JUPAS Notes

This topic covers the post-offer procedures in JUPAS. Successful applicants must pay the acceptance fee within the published window to hold the offer. Payment channels include bank counters, PPS, JPP and the JUPAS online system (check the live channels list on jupas.edu.hk). Failure to pay the acceptance fee by the deadline forfeits the offer. After payment, candidates must enrol at the admitting institution during the published registration window.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)

#### Acceptance fee

The acceptance fee is a non-refundable fee paid to confirm acceptance of a JUPAS Main Round or Clearing Round offer. For the 2026 cycle, the acceptance fee is HK$5,000.

**Purpose**: confirms the candidate's commitment to the programme and reserves the place.

**Refundability**: non-refundable under most circumstances. Special cases (e.g. cancellation of the programme, deferral) may allow refunds.

#### Payment channels

**PPS (Payment by Phone Service)**: registered users can pay via the PPS system. Requires registration with PPS in advance.

**JPP (JUPAS Payment Portal)**: online payment via the JUPAS website using credit or debit cards.

**Bank counters**: in-person payment at designated banks in Hong Kong.

**JUPAS online system**: integrated payment for some payment types.

Re-check the live channels list on jupas.edu.hk for the current cycle.

#### Payment deadline

The payment deadline is typically 24-48 hours after the offer day. For the 2026 cycle:

- Main Round offer day: 5 August 2026, 9:00 am.
- Acceptance fee deadline: 6 August 2026, 5:00 pm (check the live calendar).

Re-check the live calendar on jupas.edu.hk for the current cycle's deadline.

#### Registration and enrolment

After paying the acceptance fee, the candidate must:

- **Register** at the admitting institution during the published registration window. This typically involves submitting identity documents, academic records, and other required documents.
- **Enrol** for courses during the institution's published enrolment window.

Each institution has its own registration and enrolment procedures and deadlines. Candidates should check the admitting institution's website for details.

**Failure to register or enrol** forfeits the place even after payment.

#### Withdrawal and deferral

After accepting an offer, the candidate may withdraw or apply for deferral:

- **Withdrawal**: the candidate gives up the place. The acceptance fee is non-refundable. The candidate may seek admission in a subsequent cycle.
- **Deferral**: the candidate postpones admission to the following year (subject to the institution's policies).

Re-check the policies of the admitting institution.
