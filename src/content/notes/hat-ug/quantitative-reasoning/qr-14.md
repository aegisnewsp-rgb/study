# Data Interpretation from Tables and Charts

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

Data Interpretation (DI) tests your ability to read structured numerical information from tables, bar charts, line graphs, pie charts, and stacked columns, then answer MCQs on percentages, ratios, averages, and growth. On HAT-UG, DI sits inside the Quantitative Reasoning section and carries roughly 4% weight, usually appearing as one shared-data set feeding 4–5 MCQs.

- **Percentage change** = ((New − Old) / Old) × 100% — always divide by the *old* value.
- **Pie-chart value** = (Sector angle / 360°) × Total — convert degrees to a fraction first.
- **Average** = Sum ÷ Count — recompute the sum every time; do not reuse earlier answers blindly.

#### Exam pointers:
- Scan the axis label, legend, and unit (thousands, millions, %) before calculating.
- Convert raw values ↔ percentages using the row/column total as the conversion base.
- Watch for cumulative vs. marginal figures in stacked columns.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### What examiners actually test

HAT-UG DI items come from a single shared table or chart (bar/line/pie/stacked) and ask you to compute, compare, or infer. The recurring operations are percentage change, ratio-to-whole conversion, average, and ranking. Because the data is shared, a misread unit contaminates every dependent answer in the set.

#### Core formulas with units

| Concept | Formula | Variable meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Percentage change | ((New − Old) / Old) × 100% | New, Old = same-unit quantity |
| Pie value | (Angle / 360°) × Total | Angle in degrees, Total in source units |
| Average | Σxᵢ ÷ n | Σxᵢ = sum, n = count of items |
| Ratio → percent | (Part / Total) × 100% | Part, Total same unit |
| CAGR | (End / Begin)^(1/n) − 1 | n = number of years |

#### Standard problem types

- **Year-on-year change:** compute % rise or fall between two columns; mind the denominator year.
- **Pie-to-raw conversion:** a 72° sector in a Total = 500 set gives 500 × 72/360 = 100.
- **Ranking and comparison:** sort categories by value, then compute share-of-total.
- **Average over a subset:** sum the relevant rows, divide by the count of rows only.
- **Data sufficiency:** judge whether the shown data uniquely answers the question.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Edge cases and traps

The most expensive errors in HAT-UG DI are *unit-based*, not arithmetic. A bar chart labelled "Revenue (PKR millions)" read as raw rupees multiplies your answer by 1,000,000. Always write the unit next to any number you extract. The second trap is the **wrong-base percentage**: when asked "by what percent did sales grow from 2022 to 2023?", the denominator is 2022, not 2023. A third trap is **stacking confusion** — in a stacked column, the *top* segment is marginal, while the *running total* at the top of the bar is cumulative.

#### Worked micro-example

A table shows Company X revenue for 2021 = 800, 2022 = 1,000, 2023 = 1,250. CAGR over 2 years = (1250/800)^(1/2) − 1 = √1.5625 − 1 = 1.25 − 1 = 0.25, i.e. **25% per year**. Simple % change from 2021 → 2023 = (1250 − 800)/800 × 100% = 56.25% — note this is *not* annualised.

#### Strategy for HAT-UG

Allocate ~45 seconds per DI MCQ; skim the full chart first, mark the total, then answer in order. If a set looks calculation-heavy, flag and return — the shared data means later items often reuse an earlier subtotal.

#### Practice prompts:
1. A pie chart shows Transport = 90°, Food = 120°, Rent = 100°, Others = 50° out of total monthly expense PKR 60,000. Find the Food share in rupees and the ratio of Rent to Others.
2. Sales (units): 2019 = 400, 2020 = 500, 2021 = 550, 2022 = 605. Compute the CAGR and the year-on-year % change for 2022.
