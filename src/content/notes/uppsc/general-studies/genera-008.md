# Topic 8

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

**General Science** for UPPSC RO/ARO covers Physics, Chemistry and Biology at the **NCERT Class 6–12 level**, split across Paper I (General Studies). Expect ~15–20 factual MCQs that reward clarity of basic definitions over advanced derivation. High-yield areas: **Newton's three laws**, **Ohm's law (V = IR)**, **pH = −log[H⁺]**, **Mendel's monohybrid cross (3:1 ratio)**, and the **cell organelles (mitochondria = powerhouse)**. Memorise SI base units (m, kg, s, A, K, mol, cd) and the seven derived quantities they spawn. Distinguish **mass (kg) vs weight (N)**, **reflection vs refraction**, **convex vs concave lens image type**, and **homozygous (TT/tt) vs heterozygous (Tt)** genotypes. Heat equation **Q = mcΔT**, kinetic energy **(1/2)mv²**, and power **P = VI = I²R** appear almost every year.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### Scope of the Paper
Paper I of the UPPSC RO/ARO Preliminary Examination devotes a sizeable slice to **General Science** (sometimes labelled General Knowledge of Science). Difficulty stops at the higher-secondary textbook; no engineering-level thermodynamics or organic reaction mechanisms are tested.

#### Physics Essentials
**Units and Motion.** Kinematics rests on three equations for uniformly accelerated motion:
- **v = u + at**
- **s = ut + ½at²**
- **v² = u² + 2as**
where *u* is initial velocity (m/s), *v* final velocity, *a* acceleration (m/s²), *s* displacement, *t* time. Newton's second law **F = ma** ties force, mass and acceleration; **weight = mg** uses g ≈ 9.8 m/s².

**Work, Energy, Power.** Work **W = F·s·cosθ**, kinetic energy **KE = ½mv²**, potential energy **PE = mgh**, power **P = W/t = Fv**. Mechanical energy is conserved in absence of friction.

**Heat.** Specific heat capacity enters **Q = mcΔT** (Q in joules, c in J kg⁻¹ K⁻¹). Three modes of heat transfer: **conduction, convection, radiation**.

**Light and Sound.** Reflection obeys *angle of incidence = angle of reflection*; refraction follows **Snell's law: n₁ sinθ₁ = n₂ sinθ₂**. Sound needs a medium; speed in air ≈ 343 m/s.

**Electricity.** **Ohm's law V = IR**; power dissipated **P = VI = I²R = V²/R**. In **series**, R_total = R₁ + R₂ + …; current is the same. In **parallel**, 1/R_total = Σ(1/Rᵢ); voltage is the same.

#### Chemistry Essentials
**Atomic structure:** nucleus contains protons (+1) and neutrons (0); electrons (−1) orbit in shells (2, 8, 8, 18…). Atomic number Z defines the element.

**Bonding:** ionic (metal + non-metal, electron transfer), covalent (non-metals share pairs), coordinate (one atom donates both electrons).

**Acids, Bases, Salts:** **pH = −log₁₀[H⁺]**; pH 7 neutral, <7 acidic, >7 basic. Common acids: HCl (stomach acid), H₂SO₄, CH₃COOH (vinegar). Common bases: NaOH, KOH, NH₄OH.

#### Biology Essentials
**Cell:** basic structural unit; **mitochondria** generate ATP, **ribosomes** synthesise protein, **nucleus** stores DNA. Plant cells add **chloroplasts** (photosynthesis) and a **cell wall** (cellulose).

**Genetics:** Mendel monohybrid cross → **phenotypic ratio 3:1**, genotypic 1:2:1. DNA is a double helix (Watson–Crick, 1953); RNA is single-stranded.

**Human Physiology:** blood circulates via heart → arteries → capillaries → veins. Oxygenated blood is in the left side. Digestion begins in the **mouth (salivary amylase)**, protein digested in **stomach (pepsin)**, absorption mainly in the **small intestine**.

#### Common Question Formats
- **Direct factual MCQ:** "SI unit of force is ___"
- **Statement-based:** two statements, choose correct combination
- **Match-the-following:** scientists ↔ discoveries (Newton, Mendel, Watson-Crick)
- **Assertion–Reason** style in mains screening

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Edge Cases and Examiner Traps

**Sign conventions in thermodynamics.** Use **Q = ΔU + W**, where Q is heat *added to* the system, W is work done *by* the system, ΔU is change in internal energy. Mixing these signs is a frequent distractor.

**pH versus pOH.** At 25 °C, **pH + pOH = 14**. A solution with [H⁺] = 10⁻³ M has pH = 3 and pOH = 11 — students often invert.

**Series–parallel hybrids.** A household circuit is parallel (each appliance gets 220 V); fuses and ammeters are in **series**. Voltmeters are always connected in **parallel** with the component.

**Reflection vs Refraction.** Reflection returns light to the same medium; refraction bends it at the boundary. A **convex lens** converges (forms real, inverted image beyond 2F; virtual magnified at object distance <F). A **concave lens** always forms a **virtual, erect, diminished** image on the same side as the object.

**Genetic confusions.** *Dominant* allele expresses even in heterozygous state; *recessive* needs homozygosity. *Phenotype* = observable trait; *genotype* = genetic makeup. Sickle-cell carriers (HbAS) are heterozygous — phenotype normal, genotype carrier.

#### Connection to Adjacent Topics
General Science overlaps with **Indian Polity** (scientific temper, Article 51A(h)), **Geography** (weather, climate, ocean currents), and **Current Affairs** (ISRO missions, ICMR health advisories, COP environmental decisions). A question on "mitochondria" may sit beside one on "Green Hydrogen" — keep NCERT Science revision tight for both.

#### Micro-Worked Example
A 2 Ω and 4 Ω resistor are connected in **parallel** across a 12 V battery. Find total current.
- 1/R = 1/2 + 1/4 = 3/4 → R_eq = 4/3 Ω ≈ 1.33 Ω.
- Total current I = V/R = 12 ÷ (4/3) = 9 A.
- Power dissipated by 2 Ω resistor: P = V²/R = 144/2 = 72 W.

#### Practice Prompts
1. A homozygous tall pea plant (TT) is crossed with a homozygous dwarf (tt). Work out the F₂ phenotypic and genotypic ratios from the F₁ self-cross.
2. Calculate the pH of a solution whose [H⁺] = 4 × 10⁻⁵ M and state whether it is acidic, neutral, or basic.
