# Current Affairs — Recent Important Events (2024–2026)

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

Current Affairs for the DU (Dhaka University) Ka-unit admission covers nationally and globally significant events from roughly the last 2–3 years. The DU Ga-unit admission test (2024–25 cycle) and the 2025–26 cycle pull factual MCQs on Bangladesh politics, the global order, sports, awards, and observances. Questions are pure recall — date, name, place, figure.

- **Bangladesh-specific anchors (2024–2026):** Sheikh Hasina ousted on **5 August 2024**; Muhammad Yunus heads the interim caretaker government. **Padma Bridge Rail Link** opened October 2023 (full operation 2024). **Metro Rail** MRT Line-6 extended to Kamalapur in 2024–25. **Cyclone Remal** struck May 2024. Bangladesh won the **T20 World Cup 2024** in the West Indies/USA.
- **Global anchors:** **Paris Olympics 2024** (July–August). **US elections 2024** — Donald Trump returned as 47th President on 5 November 2024. **BRICS Kazan Summit** October 2024 expanded partner-state list. **COP29** held in Baku, Azerbaijan, November 2024 (climate-finance NCQG agreed at USD 300 billion/year by 2035).
- **Awards:** Nobel Peace Prize 2024 awarded to Nihon Hidankyo (Japan); Physics 2024 to Hopfield & Hinton for machine-learning foundations.
- **Static anchors to keep paired with dynamic events:** 21 February (International Mother Language Day), 26 March (Independence Day), 16 December (Victory Day).

> 💡 **High-Yield Memory Hook:** **"H‑Y‑P‑C‑G‑O"** = **H**asina-out, **Y**unus-in, **P**adma-rail, **C**yclone-Remal, **G**20/B**RICS** Kazan, **O**lympics-Paris. Six anchors, one acronym — covers half of every Bangladesh GK paper since 2024.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)

#### Bangladesh Political Reset (2024)

On **5 August 2024**, mass protests forced Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina to resign and flee to India. President Mohammed Shahabuddin dissolved parliament, and an interim government led by **Nobel laureate Muhammad Yunus** took charge on 8 August 2024. The Yunus-led government pledged reforms, election reorganisation under the Election Commission, and constitutional review. Students should remember three anchors: the date (5 Aug), the leader (Yunus), and the institutional framework (interim caretaker, not elected).

#### Bangladesh Economy & Infrastructure

Bangladesh Bank's FY2023–24 provisional GDP growth was reported around **5.81 %**, with FY2024–25 targets revised in light of post-reform inflows. Remittance continued to surge past **USD 23 billion** in several monthly records. Infrastructure completions dominating the syllabus:

| Project | Completion / Launch | Significance |
|---|---|---|
| Padma Bridge Rail Link | Trial run 2023, full operation Oct 2024 | Connects Dhaka to southwestern Bangladesh |
| Metro Rail MRT Line-6 | Extended to Kamalapur 2024–25 | First electric metro in Dhaka |
| Matarbari Deep-Sea Port | Phase 1 under construction | Largest port project post-Chittagong |
| Rooppur Nuclear Power Plant | Unit 1 reactor installation 2024 | First nuclear plant, Russia–Bangladesh cooperation |

#### Awards, Sports & Observances

The **Paris 2024 Olympics** ran 26 July – 11 August 2024. Bangladesh sent its largest-ever contingent; no medals won, but several athletes qualified by wildcard. The **T20 Cricket World Cup 2024** (USA/West Indies, June 2024) saw **India win the title** beating South Africa in the final on 29 June 2024.

> 📌 **Formula Check:** No quantitative formula applies — this section tests *date-name-place* recall, not calculation. The only "unit" worth memorising is **NCQG = USD 300 bn/year by 2035**.

#### 🎯 Exam-Level Worked Problem

**Question:** Which of the following correctly matches the recent event with its date?

- (a) COP29 — Baku — November 2024
- (b) BRICS Kazan Summit — Johannesburg — October 2024
- (c) Paris Olympics opening — 26 July 2023
- (d) Sheikh Hasina resignation — 5 August 2025

#### Solution:
Step 1 — Evaluate (a): COP29 was held in Baku, Azerbaijan, 11–22 November 2024. **Match is true.**
Step 2 — Evaluate (b): The 2024 BRICS summit was held in **Kazan, Russia**, not Johannesburg. Johannesburg hosted the 2023 summit. **(b) is false.**
Step 3 — Evaluate (c): Paris 2024 opened on **26 July 2024**, not 2023. **(c) is false.**
Step 4 — Evaluate (d): Sheikh Hasina resigned on **5 August 2024**, not 2025. **(d) is false.**

**Correct answer: (a)** — COP29, Baku, November 2024.

> ⚠️ **Examiner Trap:** Students confuse the **2023 Johannesburg** BRICS summit with the **2024 Kazan** summit because both involved BRICS expansion discussions. Always check the host city before locking in the year — Kazan is on the Volga River, not in South Africa.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Edge Cases & Borderline-Question Traps

DU examiners frequently test *one-word swaps* that look identical but shift the answer. The most common traps appear in international-affairs questions:

1. **Kazakhstan vs Kazan** — BRICS 2024 was in **Kazan** (Russia), a city on the Volga. **Kazakhstan** is a separate Central Asian country. Mixing them is a classic Ka-unit trap.
2. **COP28 vs COP29** — COP28 (2023) was in Dubai, UAE and operationalised the Loss & Damage Fund. COP29 (2024) was in Baku and agreed the NCQG finance target. Students often attribute Baku outcomes to Dubai.
3. **G20 vs G7** — The G20 presidency in 2024 was held by **Brazil** (Rio summit). The G7 is a smaller group with Italy presiding in 2024 (Apulia summit). Same year, different chairs.
4. **Trump 1.0 vs Trump 2.0** — Trump's second term began **20 January 2025**, distinct from his 2017 inauguration. Question stems may test whether you know "47th President" is the correct ordinal.
5. **Israel–Gaza timeline** — The war began **7 October 2023**. A "ceasefire" or "humanitarian pause" does not equal resolution; examiners treat Gaza as an *ongoing* conflict through the 2025–26 cycle.

#### Bangladesh-Specific Edge Cases

- **Mujib Year (2020–2021)** is *static GK* and frequently asked, but it lies *outside* the current-affairs window. Do not list it as a 2024–25 event.
- **Padma Bridge** (road) opened June 2022; **Padma Bridge Rail Link** is a *separate* project — examiners test whether you know the rail link launched commercially in late 2024.
- **Caretaker vs elected government** — Bangladesh has an *interim* (Yunus) government, not the constitutionally mandated *caretaker* system abolished in 2011. Use the precise term: **interim government**.

#### Practice Prompts

1. *Conceptual:* "Which COP summit operationalised the Loss & Damage Fund, and which agreed the new climate-finance goal?" Answer: COP28 (Dubai, 2023) for L&D Fund; COP29 (Baku, 2024) for NCQG.
2. *Numerical-recall:* "State the USD/year target and the deadline year of the Baku NCQG." Answer: **USD 300 billion per year by 2035**, to be mobilised by developed countries.

> 📌 **Formula Check:** Recency window = exam date − 3 years. For the 2025–26 cycle, anchor between **August 2022 and the exam sitting in 2025**. Anything before 2022 is static GK, not current affairs.
