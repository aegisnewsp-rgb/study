# Science & Technology

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your MAT exam.

Science & Technology in MAT GK covers current developments across physics, biology, space, defence, IT, and emerging tech — roughly 3% of the paper. MCQs draw heavily from Indian programmes (ISRO, DRDO) and recent global breakthroughs.

#### Must-know fact blocks for MAT:

- **Space — ISRO launchers:** PSLV (Polar Satellite Launch Vehicle) carries up to ~1,750 kg to LEO in sun-synchronous polar orbit; GSLV (Geosynchronous Satellite Launch Vehicle) and the heavier LVM3 place 2–4 tonne class payloads into GTO using cryogenic upper stages.
- **Defence:** BrahMos is a supersonic **cruise missile** (not ballistic), INS Vikrant is India's first indigenously built aircraft carrier, and Agni-V is an ICBM-class surface-to-surface missile developed by DRDO.
- **Recent laureates:** Track the last two cycles of Nobel Prizes in Physics, Chemistry, and Physiology/Medicine — MAT repeatedly asks the winner and the field.

**High-yield pointers:** memorise the difference between **AI**, **ML**, and **Deep Learning**; know that **CRISPR-Cas9** is a gene-editing tool; remember India's **500 GW non-fossil fuel target by 2030** at COP26; UPI is a payment system, while **PM-WANI** is the public Wi-Fi access framework.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months of prep time.

#### Indian Space Programme

ISRO operates three operational launch vehicles. **PSLV** is the workhorse for remote-sensing and sun-synchronous orbits. **GSLV Mk-II** uses an indigenous cryogenic upper stage for 2-tonne GTO payloads, and **LVM3** (formerly GSLV Mk-III) is the heavy-lifter capable of placing ~4 tonnes in GTO — it launched Chandrayaan-3.

The Chandrayaan series deserves close attention because MAT regularly tests outcomes. Chandrayaan-1 (2008) confirmed water molecules on the Moon. Chandrayaan-2 (2019) deployed an orbiter (still functional) but the Vikram lander crashed. Chandrayaan-3 (2023) successfully soft-landed near the south pole, making India the fourth country to land on the Moon.

#### Defence and Strategic Systems

DRDO laboratories develop missiles, radars, and platforms. **BrahMos** is a ramjet-powered supersonic cruise missile jointly developed with Russia, range ~290–450 km depending on variant. **Agni-V** is an MIRV-capable intercontinental-range ballistic missile (~5,000+ km). **Tejas LCA** is India's indigenous light combat aircraft. **INS Vikrant** (commissioned 2022) is the first indigenously designed and built aircraft carrier.

#### Emerging Technologies

- **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** — broad field of machines simulating human cognition.
- **Machine Learning (ML)** — subset of AI using statistical learning from data.
- **Deep Learning** — subset of ML based on multi-layer neural networks.
- **Quantum computing** uses qubits with superposition and entanglement; India launched the **National Quantum Mission (2023)** with ₹6,003 crore outlay.
- **Green hydrogen** is produced via electrolysis powered by renewables; India targets 5 MMT annual production by 2030.

| Initiative | Ministry / Nodal body | Objective |
| --- | --- | --- |
| National Supercomputing Mission | MeitY + DST | Build 70+ supercomputers for research |
| India Semiconductor Mission | MeitY | Fab and display manufacturing incentives |
| PM-WANI | DoT | Public Wi-Fi hotspots without licence |
| National Quantum Mission | DST | Quantum computing, communication, materials |

**Exam trap:** do not confuse UPI (a payments protocol by NPCI) with PM-WANI (a Wi-Fi access framework).

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Three-Stage Nuclear Programme and Energy Targets

India's nuclear roadmap, designed by Homi Bhabha, proceeds in three stages. **Stage 1** uses Pressurised Heavy Water Reactors (PHWRs) fuelled by natural uranium, producing plutonium-239 as a by-product. **Stage 2** deploys Fast Breeder Reactors (FBRs) that use the plutonium to breed uranium-233 from a thorium blanket. **Stage 3** introduces thorium-based reactors (AHWR being a test bed) because India holds ~25% of global thorium reserves, particularly in the monazite sands of Kerala.

#### Recent Nobel Context and Biotechnology

MAT frequently asks the latest Physics and Chemistry Nobel laureates. Track themes: 2023 Chemistry honoured quantum-dot research; 2023 Physiology/Medicine went to mRNA vaccine modifications enabling COVID-19 vaccines. **CRISPR-Cas9**, adapted from bacterial immune systems, allows precise DNA cuts and is widely used in gene therapy, agriculture, and diagnostics. India's **Genomics and Bio-Authentication** programmes apply similar tools to DNA fingerprinting.

#### Climate and Environmental Indicators

India has committed to **500 GW of non-fossil installed capacity by 2030** and **net zero by 2070**. The Montreal Protocol (1987) phased out CFCs to protect the ozone layer; the Kigali Amendment extends controls to HFCs. **COP summits** shape climate finance, loss-and-damage funds, and Just Energy Transition Partnerships — MAT may ask India's targets or NDC updates.

#### Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. Calling BrahMos a ballistic missile — it is a cruise missile.
2. Mixing Chandrayaan-2 (failed landing) with Chandrayaan-3 (successful south-pole landing on 23 Aug 2023).
3. Treating AI, ML, and Deep Learning as interchangeable.
4. Confusing UPI with PM-WANI.
5. Assigning India's 500 GW renewable target to the wrong year or ministry (it is MNRE, announced at COP26, Glasgow, 2021).

#### Practice Prompts

1. Which ISRO launcher placed Chandrayaan-3 into lunar transfer orbit, and what propellant does its cryogenic stage use?
2. Differentiate the three stages of India's nuclear programme and identify which stage uses thorium as the primary fuel.
