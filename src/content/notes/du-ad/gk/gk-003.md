# Economy and Development of Bangladesh

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

Bangladesh's economy has transformed from a war-ravaged agrarian base in 1971 into a lower-middle-income, garments-and-remittance-driven economy. The country graduates from **Least Developed Country (LDC)** status effective **24 November 2026**, after meeting UN Committee for Development Policy thresholds for the second time in 2018 and 2021. Fiscal-year planning runs on **Five-Year Plans (FYP)**; the **8th FYP covers 2021–2025**, and the long-horizon **Perspective Plan 2041** targets upper-middle-income status by 2041.

|---|---|---|

| GDP composition | Agri ~11% / Industry ~35% / Services ~53% | BBS |
| RMG share of exports | ~84% | Bangladesh Bank, EPB |

> 💡 **High-Yield Memory Hook:** **"A-I-S-R"** for the four growth engines — **A**griculture (declining share), **I**ndustry (RMG-led), **S**ervices (largest sector), **R**emittances (foreign-currency lifeline). For LDC thresholds, remember **"GNI ≥ USD 1,388, HAI ≥ 66, EVI ≤ 32"** — Bangladesh crossed all three.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### Structural Shift Since Independence
At independence (1971), agriculture contributed roughly **55% of GDP** and employed over 80% of the labour force. Five decades later, agriculture has fallen to about **11% of GDP** yet still employs ~40% — a classic dual-economy signature. Industrialisation was catalysed by the **MFA (Multi-Fibre Arrangement) quota system** until 2004, after which Bangladeshi exporters retained competitiveness through low unit labour cost. Services now dominate value-added, propelled by transport, trade, finance, and a fast-rising ICT outsourcing segment.

#### GDP Identity and Core Formulas
The textbook identity governs national accounts:

**GDP = C + I + G + (X − M)**, where C = private consumption, I = gross private investment, G = government consumption, X = exports, M = imports. All four flows are reported in BDT (Bangladesh Taka) at constant 2015–16 prices by BBS.

**Real GDP Growth Rate** = [(Real GDP<sub>current</sub> − Real GDP<sub>previous</sub>) / Real GDP<sub>previous</sub>] × 100, expressed in % per fiscal year (dimensionless ratio).

**CPI-based Inflation Rate** = [(CPI<sub>current</sub> − CPI<sub>previous</sub>) / CPI<sub>previous</sub>] × 100, computed monthly by BBS using the 2021–22 base year basket.

#### RMG and Remittance — Twin Pillars
The **Readymade Garments (RMG)** sector accounts for ~84% of merchandise exports and employs around 4 million workers, predominantly women. Key destinations are the EU, USA, UK, and Canada. **Worker remittances** — primarily from Gulf states (Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Kuwait) and Malaysia — exceeded USD 23 billion in FY 2023–24, financing roughly the entire import bill in some years.

#### Development Planning Timeline
1. **1st FYP: 1973–1978** — reconstruction priority.
2. **2nd FYP: 1980–1985** — liberalisation under Zia era.
3. **5th FYP: 1997–2002** — private-sector-led growth.
4. **8th FYP: 2021–2025** — LDC transition, SDGs, delta plan.
5. **Perspective Plan 2021–2041** — Vision 2041: Upper-middle-income, per capita ~USD 12,500.

#### 🎯 Exam-Level Worked Problem
**Question:** Bangladesh crossed all three UN CDP thresholds for LDC graduation in which year(s), and on what exact date will graduation become effective?
(a) 2018 and 2021; effective 24 November 2026
(b) 2020 and 2023; effective 24 November 2026
(c) 2018 only; effective 24 November 2024
(d) 2021 and 2024; effective 12 December 2026

#### Solution:
The UN Committee for Development Policy assesses graduation every three years. Bangladesh first met **two of three** criteria in 2015, then **all three** in **2018** (confirmed in 2021). On UN General Assembly endorsement, the **smooth-transition period** runs until **24 November 2026**. The threshold values are: GNI per capita ≥ USD 1,388, Human Assets Index (HAI) ≥ 66, Economic Vulnerability Index (EVI) ≤ 32. Correct answer: **(a)**.

> ⚠️ **Examiner Trap:** Option (c) is the most common wrong answer — candidates confuse the **assessment year (2018)** with the **graduation effective date (2026)**. The five-year smooth-transition cushion after UNGA endorsement is what delays graduation. Also remember: Bangladesh is "graduating", not yet "graduated" until November 2026.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### SDG Alignment and Human Development
Bangladesh is among the **top performers** in SDG implementation among South Asian LDCs, achieving gender parity in primary education and reducing under-5 mortality below 30 per 1,000 live births. The **HDI rank ~129/193** (UNDP HDR 2023/24) reflects sustained gains in life expectancy (~73 years) and mean schooling years (~6.6 years), but GNI per capita remains the binding constraint.

#### Sectoral Priority Under Industrial Policy 2022
1. **RMG and textiles** — moving up the value chain to man-made fibres.
2. **Pharmaceuticals** — exporting to 150+ countries; ~98% domestic demand met.
3. **Leather and footwear** — addressing environmental compliance (CETP).
4. **Shipbuilding** — small ocean-going vessels, growing order book.
5. **IT services and BPO** — government offers tax holidays until 2027.

#### Banking-Sector Stress and Macroeconomic Risks
**Non-Performing Loans (NPLs)** have hovered around 9–11% of total disbursement despite repeated write-off drives by Bangladesh Bank. **Foreign exchange reserves** crossed USD 46 billion in 2021 but have since trended down to ~USD 19–25 billion due to import pressure on dollar demand. **Current account deficit** widened as the post-Covid import surge outpaced remittance growth.

#### Advanced Traps and Edge Cases

| Trap | Correct Fact | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| Conflating Vision 2041 with Perspective Plan 2041 | They are the **same** document | DU MCQs reverse the wording |
| Stating RMG share = 60% | It is **~84%** | Outdated 1990s data still circulates |
| Citing Ministry of Finance for repo rate | Repo rate = **Bangladesh Bank** | Monetary vs fiscal confusion |
| Naming NBR as tax authority | NBR collects; **Ministry of Finance** frames policy | DU tests institutional nuance |
| GDP base year as 2005–06 | BBS revised to **2015–16** constant price | Old figures inflate apparent growth |

#### Climate-Economy Nexus
Bangladesh loses roughly **1.7% of GDP annually** to climate shocks per the World Bank (2023). The **Bangladesh Delta Plan 2100** allocates USD 37 billion across three phases for flood management, salinity intrusion, and urban drainage — a critical fiscal commitment for sustaining garment supply chains centred in Gazipur, Narayanganj, and Chattogram.

2. **Quantitative**: Using GDP ≈ USD 460 billion and remittance ≈ USD 23 billion, compute remittance as a percentage of GDP to one decimal place. If remittances fell by 15% next year with no GDP change, by how many percentage points would this ratio drop?
