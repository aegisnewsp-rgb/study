# History

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

NDA GAT History covers Indian History (Ancient, Medieval, Modern) and select World History events, mapped to UPSC's "History of India and the Indian National Movement" syllabus. Expect 15–25 factual-recall MCQs per paper with −1/3 negative marking.

- **Ancient India:** Harappan grid cities (Mohenjo-daro, Harappa, Lothal, Dholavira), Vedic shift from pastoral to settled life, Jainism (Mahavira) and Buddhism (Four Noble Truths), Mauryan peak under Ashoka after Kalinga War, Gupta "Golden Age" (zero, decimal, Kalidasa).
- **Medieval India:** Delhi Sultanate (1206–1526, five dynasties), Mughal mansabdari, Bhakti saints (Kabir, Tulsidas, Mirabai, Chaitanya), Sufi silsilas (Chishti, Suhrawardi, Naqshbandi).
- **Modern India:** British land systems (Permanent, Ryotwari, Mahalwari), 1857 Revolt, INC phases (Moderates → Extremists → Gandhian), Quit India 1942.
- **World History (light):** French Revolution 1789, Industrial Revolution, World Wars I & II, UNO 1945.

> 💡 **High-Yield Memory Hook:** "**H**arappan-**V**edic-**M**auryan-**G**upta-**D**elhi Sultanate-**M**ughal-**B**ritish-**N**ational Movement" → track dynasties chronologically; mnemonic **"Lal-Bal-Pal"** = Lala Lajpat Rai, Bal Gangadhar Tilak, Bipin Chandra Pal (Extremists, not Moderates).

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### Ancient and Medieval Foundations

Harappan sites sit on river banks (Indus, Ravi, Ghaggar-Hakra). Lothal (Gujarat) has a dockyard; Dholavira has a water-management system; Kalibangan (Rajasthan) shows a ploughed field. Vedic society moves from early (Rigvedic, pastoral, tribal *jana*) to later (settled agriculture, *janapada* states, *gana-sangha* republics). Buddhism's **Four Noble Truths** and **Eightfold Path** are the most-tested doctrinal items.

Ashoka's **Dhamma** is moral law based on tolerance — not Buddhism's Dhamma, a frequent conflation in MCQs. Gupta-era India contributes the concept of **zero**, the decimal system, iron-pillar metallurgy, and Dashavatara temple architecture.

Delhi Sultanate chronology: **Mamluk → Khilji → Tughlaq → Sayyid → Lodi**. Alauddin Khilji's market-price controls and Iltutmish's "**Group of Forty**" nobles are boardroom favourites. Mughal administration runs on the **mansabdari** ranking (zat and sawar ranks); Akbar's **sulah-i-kul** (universal tolerance) and **Din-i-Ilahi** (spiritual order, not a new religion) are commonly swapped in questions.

#### Modern India and National Movement

British land reforms: **Permanent Settlement 1793** (zamindari, Bengal), **Ryotwari** (directly with peasant, Madras/Bombay), **Mahalwari** (village, North-Western Provinces). Social-reform milestones: **Brahmo Samaj 1828** (Raja Ram Mohan Roy), **Arya Samaj 1875** (Dayananda Saraswati), **Sati abolition 1829**, **Widow Remarriage 1856**.

The 1857 Revolt fuses sepoy grievances (greased cartridges) with political ones; major leaders include Mangal Pandey, Rani Lakshmibai, Tantia Tope, Nana Sahib. INC formed 1885 (A.O. Hume). **Lahore Session 1929** declares Purna Swaraj under **Jawaharlal Nehru** as President — not C. Rajagopalachari. The **Salt March 1930**, **Civil Disobedience**, **Round Table talks**, and **Quit India 1942** are the Gandhian milestones leading to 1947 Independence.

#### 🎯 Exam-Level Worked Problem

**Question:** Which of the following correctly matches the land-revenue system with its year and founder?
(a) Permanent Settlement — 1793 — Lord Cornwallis
(b) Ryotwari — 1793 — Lord Wellesley
(c) Mahalwari — 1856 — Lord Dalhousie
(d) Subsidiary Alliance — 1813 — Lord Hastings

#### Solution:
- Permanent Settlement → **1793, Lord Cornwallis** ✓ (option a)
- Ryotwari → 1820, Thomas Munro (not Wellesley) → (b) wrong
- Mahalwari → 1822, Holt Mackenzie (not Dalhousie) → (c) wrong
- Subsidiary Alliance → 1798–1800, Lord Wellesley → (d) wrong by both date and category

> ⚠️ **Examiner Trap:** Land-revenue systems and alliance treaties share overlapping years (1793, 1798, 1820). Students lose marks pairing Cornwallis with Wellesley — match the *policy* (land vs alliance) before matching the year.

#### Edge Cases and Map-Based Traps

NDA History questions frequently test **site-vs-river** and **leader-vs-movement** pairings. The table below lists the highest-value traps.

| Trap | Correct Pairing | Why Students Slip |
|---|---|---|
| Lothal vs Kalibangan | Lothal = Gujarat + dockyard; Kalibangan = Rajasthan + ploughed field | Both are Harappan; map-blind guesswork |
| Kanishka's capital | Purushapura (Peshawar), Gandhara art | Mixed with Kushana trade routes |
| Bhakti saint state | Kabir (Banaras), Tulsidas (Awadh), Mirabai (Rajasthan), Basava (Karnataka), Chaitanya (Bengal) | Regional identities blurred in options |
| Cripps Mission 1942 | Offered Dominion Status post-war | Rejected because no immediate transfer |
| Cabinet Mission 1946 | Proposed United India with federal structure | Confused with Mountbatten Plan (June 1947) |
| Poona Pact 1932 | Gandhi–Ambedkar agreement on separate electorates | Swapped with Round Table (1930–32) |
