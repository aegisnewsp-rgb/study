# Units and Measurement

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)

Units and measurement provide the mathematical language and empirical foundation for all physical laws tested in JEE Main.

The International System of Units (SI) defines seven fundamental base quantities:
- Length: metre (m)
- Mass: kilogram (kg)
- Time: second (s)
- Electric Current: ampere (A)
- Thermodynamic Temperature: kelvin (K)
- Amount of Substance: mole (mol)
- Luminous Intensity: candela (cd)

Two supplementary dimensionless units measure angular geometry: plane angle (radian, rad) and solid angle (steradian, sr).

The Principle of Homogeneity of Dimensions states that in any physically valid equation, every additive term on both sides must have identical dimensions:
$$[A] = [B] = [C] \quad 	ext{in} \quad A + B = C$$

Arguments of transcendental functions (trigonometric, exponential, logarithmic) must be strictly dimensionless ($[	heta] = [1]$, $[e^{kx}] \implies [k] = [L^{-1}]$).

Significant figures dictate measurement reporting precision:
- All non-zero digits are significant ($123.4$ has 4).
- Zeros between non-zero digits are significant ($1002$ has 4).
- Leading zeros are never significant ($0.0025$ has 2).
- Trailing zeros after a decimal point are significant ($2.500$ has 4).
- In addition and subtraction, round the final result to the least number of decimal places. In multiplication and division, round to the least number of significant figures.

⚡ **JEE Main Trap:** A physical quantity can have units while remaining completely dimensionless (e.g. angle in radians, strain in $	ext{m/m}$). However, no physical quantity can possess non-trivial dimensions while lacking a unit.

#### 3. Step-by-Step Worked Example: Significant Figures Arithmetic

**Problem:** A laboratory technician records three masses: $m_1 = 24.32	ext{ g}$, $m_2 = 4.1	ext{ g}$, and $m_3 = 0.086	ext{ g}$. (a) Calculate the total mass with correct significant digits. (b) If a rectangular plate has length $L = 12.4	ext{ cm}$ (3 sig figs) and width $W = 3.2	ext{ cm}$ (2 sig figs), calculate its surface area with correct precision.

**Solution Steps:**
1. For addition, align numbers by the decimal point:
$$m_{tot} = 24.32 + 4.1 + 0.086 = 28.506	ext{ g}$$
2. Inspect decimal place precision: $m_1$ has 2 decimal places, $m_2$ has 1 decimal place, and $m_3$ has 3 decimal places.
3. The least precise measurement is $m_2$ (1 decimal place). Round $28.506$ to one decimal place:
$$m_{tot} = 28.5	ext{ g}$$
4. For multiplication of length and width:
$$	ext{Area} = L 	imes W = 12.4	ext{ cm} 	imes 3.2	ext{ cm} = 39.68	ext{ cm}^2$$
5. Inspect significant figures: $L$ has 3 significant figures, whereas $W$ has 2 significant figures.
6. The product must be rounded to 2 significant figures. Round $39.68$ to 2 significant figures:
$$	ext{Area} = 40	ext{ cm}^2 = 4.0 	imes 10^1	ext{ cm}^2$$

#### 3. Step-by-Step Worked Example: Screw Gauge with Zero Error

**Problem:** A screw gauge has a pitch of $0.5	ext{ mm}$ and $50$ divisions on its circular scale. When the spindle and anvil are brought into contact without any object, the 45th circular division coincides with the reference line, and the zero mark of the main scale is barely visible (negative zero error). When measuring the diameter of a wire, the main scale reads $2.5	ext{ mm}$ and the 20th circular division coincides with the reference line. Determine the true diameter of the wire.

**Solution Steps:**
1. Calculate the least count of the screw gauge:
$$	ext{LC} = rac{	ext{Pitch}}{	ext{Total divisions}} = rac{0.5	ext{ mm}}{50} = 0.01	ext{ mm}$$
2. Determine the zero error: The 45th division coincides before reaching zero, meaning the zero mark lies 5 divisions ahead:
$$	ext{Zero Error} = -(50 - 45) 	imes 	ext{LC} = -5 	imes 0.01	ext{ mm} = -0.05	ext{ mm}$$
3. Calculate observed reading:
$$	ext{Observed} = 	ext{Main Scale Reading} + (	ext{Coinciding Division} 	imes 	ext{LC})$$
$$	ext{Observed} = 2.5	ext{ mm} + (20 	imes 0.01	ext{ mm}) = 2.5	ext{ mm} + 0.20	ext{ mm} = 2.70	ext{ mm}$$
4. Apply zero error correction:
$$	ext{True Diameter} = 	ext{Observed Reading} - (	ext{Zero Error})$$
$$	ext{True Diameter} = 2.70	ext{ mm} - (-0.05	ext{ mm}) = 2.70	ext{ mm} + 0.05	ext{ mm} = 2.75	ext{ mm}$$
