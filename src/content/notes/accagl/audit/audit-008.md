# Audit Reports & Communications

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

The auditor's report under ISA 700–706 is the written opinion on whether financial statements give a **true and fair view** (IFRS) or **present fairly** (CA Pakistan/ICAP framework). The opinion is *unmodified* unless misstatements or evidence limitations are material; *modifications* fall into three grades — qualified, adverse, disclaimer.

- **Unmodified opinion**: financial statements give a true and fair view; ISA 700 wording used.
- **Qualified opinion**: material but **not pervasive** misstatement OR limitation on audit scope.
- **Adverse opinion**: misstatement is both material **and pervasive**.
- **Disclaimer of opinion**: inability to obtain sufficient appropriate audit evidence and the effect is pervasive.

> 💡 **High-Yield Memory Hook:** **"MAP-QAD"** = Material + Pervasive → **A**dverse; Material only → **Q**ualified; Evidence only → **Q**ualified; Evidence + Pervasive → **D**isclaimer. Skip the P and you misclassify the opinion.

Two parallel communication channels exist: the **audit report** (to users of the FS) and the written communication to **Those Charged with Governance (TCWG)** under ISA 260/ISA 265. Both must be drafted; omitting either loses marks.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### The Opinion-Modification Matrix (ISA 705)

The single highest-yield concept in this topic is matching the situation to the correct opinion wording. Examiners build scenarios around borderline cases — material vs pervasive, evidence limitation vs identified misstatement, going-concern disclosed vs undisclosed.

| Situation | Materiality | Opinion Type | Wording Used |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identified misstatement | Material but NOT pervasive | **Qualified** ("except for") | "Except for the effects of the matter described…" |
| Identified misstatement | Material AND pervasive | **Adverse** | "The financial statements do not give a true and fair view" |
| Unable to obtain sufficient appropriate evidence | Material but NOT pervasive | **Qualified** | "Except for any adjustments that might have been found necessary" |
| Unable to obtain sufficient appropriate evidence | Material AND pervasive | **Disclaimer** | "We do not express an opinion" |

> 📌 **Formula Check:** There is no numerical formula here — the test is qualitative. *Pervasive* means the misstatement affects the FS as a whole and is not confined to specific elements, accounts, or items.

#### Communication With TCWG (ISA 260 / ISA 265)

The auditor must communicate **in writing** for listed entities and document the following for all audits:

1. **Planned scope and timing** of the audit.
2. **Independence** confirmation plus any threats and safeguards.
3. **Significant findings** — qualitative judgments, corrected/unadjusted misstatements, internal control deficiencies.
4. **Auditor's responsibilities** for forming the opinion.

ISA 265 specifically requires written communication of **significant deficiencies in internal control** to TCWG on a timely basis.

#### Key Audit Matters (ISA 701)

KAMs are selected from matters communicated with TCWG that required **significant auditor attention** during the audit. They appear in a dedicated section *after* the Basis for Opinion. KAMs are **prohibited** when the opinion is qualified, adverse, or disclaimer — a frequently tested rule.

#### 🎯 Exam-Level Worked Problem

**Question:** Zafar Ltd's draft financial statements contain an unrecorded liability of Rs 45 million — 6% of total liabilities. The auditor has obtained sufficient appropriate evidence for all other balances, and the misstatement is not pervasive. Identify the required opinion type and state the opinion paragraph wording.

#### Solution:
1. Identify the issue type → **identified misstatement** (not an evidence limitation).
2. Assess materiality → Rs 45m is material relative to the balance sheet but confined to liabilities; not pervasive.
3. Apply ISA 705 decision tree → **Qualified opinion**.
4. Draft wording: "*In our opinion, except for the effects of the matter described in the Basis for Qualified Opinion paragraph, the financial statements give a true and fair view…*"
5. Include a **Basis for Qualified Opinion** paragraph describing the unrecorded liability and its effect.

> ⚠️ **Examiner Trap:** Students reach for an *adverse* opinion because Rs 45m looks large. Adverse requires the misstatement to be **pervasive** — affecting the FS as a whole, not just one liability account. A single omitted accrual affecting only liabilities is qualified, not adverse.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Edge Cases and Limiting Conditions

The 3% syllabus weight concentrates fire on four traps. Mastering the boundary between them is the difference between pass and fail in the auditing paper.

| Trap | Boundary Rule | Exam Cue |
|---|---|---|
| EoM vs Modification | EoM **supplements** an unmodified opinion; it cannot cure a misstatement | "FS disclose the matter adequately and are fairly presented" → EoM possible; otherwise modify |
| MUGC vs Disclaimer | MUGC section used when going-concern uncertainty is **adequately disclosed**; disclaimer only if disclosure inadequate **and** going concern basis inappropriate | Examiner gives fact pattern: "adequately disclosed in Note X" triggers MUGC, not modification |
| KAM in Modified Opinions | ISA 701 prohibits KAM section in qualified / adverse / disclaimer | Modified opinion + KAM = automatic mark loss |
| TCWG vs Management | ISA 260 communication goes to the **board / audit committee**, not to executive management | If the question names an audit committee, write to TCWG |

#### Going Concern Mechanics (ISA 570)

The auditor evaluates management's assessment of the entity's ability to continue as a going concern for **at least 12 months from the date of approval of the financial statements**. Three reporting outcomes exist:

1. Going concern basis appropriate, no material uncertainty → **unmodified opinion**, no extra section.
2. Going concern basis appropriate, **material uncertainty adequately disclosed** → **unmodified opinion + "Material Uncertainty Related to Going Concern" section** under a separate heading.
3. Going concern basis inappropriate, or uncertainty not adequately disclosed → **adverse or disclaimer** depending on pervasiveness.

#### Other Information (ISA 720)

The auditor reads the **Other Information** (annual report narrative, chairman's statement, governance report) for **material inconsistency** with the audited FS or **material misstatement of fact**. A separate section "Other Information" appears in the audit report; failure to address it loses easy marks.

#### Advanced Practice Prompts

1. A bank's FS include an Emphasis of Matter paragraph on a regulatory investigation. The investigation is also material and pervasive. Draft the opinion and explain whether EoM is permissible.
2. A not-for-profit entity has a going-concern uncertainty disclosed only in the trustees' report, not in the notes to the FS. Identify the required ISA 570 reporting action.
