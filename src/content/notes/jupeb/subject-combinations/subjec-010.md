# Direct Entry Requirements by University

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute decision-making and registration deadlines.

**JUPEB Direct Entry (DE) Scoring and Pathways**
- **JUPEB Grading Scale**: Each registered subject is scored on a 5-point scale:
  - **A = 5 points**, **B = 4 points**, **C = 3 points**, **D = 2 points**, **E = 1 point**, **F = 0 points**.
  - **Bonus Point**: Candidates who score at least an 'E' across all three subjects with zero 'F' grades receive **+1 bonus point**.
  - **Maximum Possible Score**: $5 + 5 + 5 + 1 = 16$ points. Minimum pass threshold: 5–6 points (depending on university).
- **Direct Entry (DE) Privilege**: Enables admission into **200 Level** of Nigerian federal, state, and private universities through the Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board (JAMB) DE portal.
- **Top Faculty Minimum Score Benchmarks (Indicative)**:
  - **Medicine and Surgery (MBBS)**: 12–15 points (UNILAG: 14–15 pts; UI: 13–15 pts; UNIBEN: 13–14 pts).
  - **Pharmacy / Dentistry / Nursing**: 10–13 points.
  - **Engineering (Mechanical, Electrical, Civil, Petrochemical)**: 9–13 points.
  - **Law (LL.B)**: 12–14 points (Literature in English is mandatory).
  - **Accounting / Economics / Finance**: 9–12 points.
  - **Computer Science / Software Engineering**: 9–12 points.
- ⚡ **Critical Admission Trap**: Passing JUPEB with 15 points does NOT exempt you from O'Level prerequisite criteria. You must possess at least 5 credit passes in relevant subjects (including English Language and Mathematics) in WASSCE/NECO/NABTEB at not more than two sittings.

2. **Faculty of Engineering & Technology**:
   - Required Combination: **Mathematics + Physics + Chemistry** (MPC).

3. **Faculty of Law (LL.B)**:
   - Required Combination: **Literature-in-English + Government + CRS/IRS/History/Economics**.

4. **Faculty of Management & Social Sciences**:
   - Required Combination: **Economics + Mathematics + Accounting/Government/Business Studies**.
   - Core Threshold: 8 to 11 points. For Economics, Mathematics is required; for Banking & Finance, Economics and Accounting form the primary backbone.

#### Formal JUPEB Point Computation Formula

The overall Direct Entry point total is evaluated using the standardized Joint Universities Preliminary Examinations Board algorithm:

$$\text{Total JUPEB Points} = \sum_{i=1}^{3} P(S_i) + \text{Bonus}$$

Where:
- $S_i$ represents the candidate's three official subjects.
- Point mapping: $P(\text{A}) = 5,\; P(\text{B}) = 4,\; P(\text{C}) = 3,\; P(\text{D}) = 2,\; P(\text{E}) = 1,\; P(\text{F}) = 0$.
- Bonus point condition:
  $$\text{Bonus} = \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if } \forall i \in \{1,2,3\},\; P(S_i) \ge 1 \\ 0 & \text{if } \exists i \text{ such that } P(S_i) = 0 \end{cases}$$

**Worked Example**:
A candidate scores:
- Chemistry: **B** (4 points)
- Physics: **A** (5 points)
- Biology: **B** (4 points)
- Bonus: +1 point (no 'F' grade)
- **Total Direct Entry Score**: $4 + 5 + 4 + 1 = 14\text{ points}$. This comfortably satisfies the 200-level Medicine threshold at UNIBEN, OAU, and UNN.

#### Institutional Case Studies and Quota Realities

1. **University of Lagos (UNILAG)**:
   - Only accepts candidates who completed their JUPEB programme within recognized, accredited affiliated centres.
   - Requires candidates to choose UNILAG as first choice in the JAMB Direct Entry registration.
   - Releases annual department-specific cut-offs. If Medicine cut-off is 14 points, candidates with 13 points are automatically offered alternative health science courses (e.g., Physiology, Anatomy, or Medical Laboratory Science).
2. **University of Ibadan (UI)**:
   - Strict adherence to faculty prerequisites. For Agriculture and Veterinary Medicine, UI requires Biology or Agricultural Science, Chemistry, and Physics/Mathematics.
   - Requires candidates to present both the official JUPEB transcript and original O-Level certificates during physical clearance.
3. **Ahmadu Bello University (ABU, Zaria)**:
   - Direct Entry admission allocates designated quotas across Northern and catchment states alongside the national merit list.
   - IJMB and JUPEB are both evaluated concurrently under standardized equivalency matrices.

#### Common Pitfalls and Strategic Mitigation

- **Mismatched Subject Combinations**: Registering for Geography, Economics, and Government while seeking admission into Architecture. Architecture at Nigerian universities requires Mathematics and Physics.
- **Transcript Forwarding Delays**: Relying solely on a physical copy of the result statement. Many federal institutions only process admission files once an authenticated transcript is received directly from the examination board.
- **Neglecting JAMB Regularization**: Failure to ensure your JUPEB admission into the preparatory centre was properly registered with JAMB can cause severe delays during final NYSC mobilization after graduation.

⚡ **Admissions Director Insight**: Never discard lower-tier backup universities. When completing the JAMB DE portal entries, select one competitive federal institution as your first choice and a progressive state or recognized private university as your contingency institution.
