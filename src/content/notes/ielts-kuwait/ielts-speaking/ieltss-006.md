# Grammatical Range and Accuracy — IELTS Speaking Notes

Grammatical Range and Accuracy is one of the four official IELTS Speaking band descriptors. It rewards a variety of complex sentence structures and high accuracy. Strong grammatical range helps the candidate reach band 7+.

> Verify the live Speaking band descriptors on https://ielts.org/ before planning revision.

---

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)

#### Core facts in one pass

- **Range:** variety of complex sentence structures (conditionals, comparatives, modals, passive voice, relative clauses).
- **Accuracy:** few grammatical errors.
- **Band 7+:** requires both range and accuracy.
- **Common distractor:** complex sentences with errors lose marks; accuracy counts more than range at band 7+.

#### Examiner traps

- Forcing complex sentences that you are not sure of — risk losing the band.
- Repeating the same sentence structure — limits range.
- Subject-verb agreement errors — common and penalised.

---

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)

#### Sentence structures

Mix simple and complex sentences:

- **Simple:** "I like reading."
- **Compound:** "I like reading and I also enjoy writing."
- **Complex:** "Although I like reading, I don't always have time for it."
- **Compound-complex:** "Although I like reading, I don't always have time for it, but I try to read at least once a week."

#### Specific structures to use

- **Conditionals:** "If I had more time, I would read more." (second conditional)
- **Comparatives:** "Reading is more relaxing than watching television."
- **Modals:** "I could read more if I made time for it."
- **Passive voice:** "The book was written by a famous author."
- **Relative clauses:** "I enjoy reading books that are set in different countries."
- **Reported speech:** "She said she was reading a new novel."

#### Common error categories

- **Subject-verb agreement:** "He go" → "He goes." "The list of items are long" → "The list of items is long."
- **Article usage:** "I bought a book" / "the book I bought" — articles are common errors for non-native speakers.
- **Prepositions:** "interested on" → "interested in." "depend of" → "depend on."
- **Verb tense:** past vs present vs future.
- **Word form:** "decision" (noun) vs "decide" (verb). "Beauty" (noun) vs "beautiful" (adjective).

### 🔴 Deep — Long-Term Mastery (1mo–6mo)

#### Where Grammatical Range appears across IELTS Speaking

Grammatical Range and Accuracy is one of the four band descriptors and contributes to the Speaking band. Strong grammatical range helps the candidate reach band 7+.

#### Common misconceptions (and the correction)

- "Complex sentences always score higher." No — accuracy matters more than range at band 7+.
- "Native-like grammar is the goal." No — clear and accurate communication is the goal.
- "Error-free grammar is required." No — band 7 allows for occasional errors.

#### Exam technique

- Use a variety of sentence structures across the three parts.
- Aim for accuracy over range.
- Check for subject-verb agreement, articles, prepositions, and verb tense.
- Practice common structures (conditionals, comparatives, modals) until they are automatic.
