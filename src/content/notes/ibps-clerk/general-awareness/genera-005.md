# Government Banking Schemes

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

**Government Banking Schemes** are central financial-inclusion, social-security, credit and digital-payment initiatives run by the Government of India through public-sector banks, RBI, NABARD, SIDBI and NPCI. For IBPS Clerk General Awareness, questions test scheme name, target beneficiary, key benefit and implementing agency.

- **PMJDY (2014)** — zero-balance RuPay account, accident cover ₹2 lakh (for accounts opened on/after 28 Aug 2018), overdraft up to ₹10,000.
- **PMJJBY (2015)** — life insurance 18–50 yrs, sum assured ₹2 lakh, premium ₹436/year.
- **PMSBY (2015)** — accident insurance 18–70 yrs, ₹2 lakh death/full disability, premium ₹20/year.
- **APY** — pension from age 60 (slabs ₹1,000–₹5,000), run by PFRDA.
- **MUDRA (PMMY)** — collateral-free loans up to ₹10 lakh in Shishu (≤₹50k), Kishore (₹50k–₹5L), Tarun (₹5–10L).
- **Stand-Up India** — loans ₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore for SC/ST and women entrepreneurs.
- **PM SVANidhi** — street-vendor loans of ₹10,000 → ₹20,000 → ₹50,000.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### The Jan Dhan–Suraksha–Pension Trifecta
PMJDY provides the account, PMJJBY provides life cover and PMSBY provides accident cover — together they form the financial-inclusion core launched 2014–2015. PMJDY's RuPay debit card works on NPCI rails; the overdraft (₹10,000) is reserved for one account per household, preferably the woman's account. PMJJBY's ₹436 annual premium is age-banded, not risk-banded, because it is a group micro-insurance product underwritten through LIC and private insurers via banks. PMSBY uses the same distribution channel at just ₹20/year — a deliberately low price to maximise enrolment.

> Mnemonic: "J**J**B for **J**eevan = life = ₹436; S**S**B for **S**uraksha = accident = ₹20."

#### Credit Schemes and Their Slabs

| Scheme | Target | Loan / Benefit | Refinance / Nodal Agency |
|---|---|---|---|
| MUDRA (PMMY) | Non-farm micro enterprises | Shishu ≤₹50k; Kishore ₹50k–₹5L; Tarun ₹5–10L | MUDRA Ltd via NABARD/SIDBI |
| Stand-Up India | SC/ST or Woman entrepreneur | ₹10 lakh – ₹1 crore greenfield | SIDBI |
| Kisan Credit Card (KCC) | Farmers, fishers, animal husbandry | Up to ₹3 lakh @ 4% effective | NABARD / RBI |
| PM SVANidhi | Street vendors | ₹10k → ₹20k → ₹50k (7% interest subsidy) | MoHUA |
| PM Vishwakarma | 18 traditional artisan trades | Tool kit ₹15k + credit up to ₹3 lakh @ 5% | MoMSME |

APY is the pension counterpart — contributory until age 60, then fixed monthly pension, with spouse continuation and corpus return to nominee.

#### Direct Benefit Transfer and Digital Vouchers
DBT uses NPCI's **APBS** (Aadhaar Payment Bridge System) to push subsidies into Aadhaar-linked bank accounts. **e-RUPI** (2021) is a purpose-specific digital voucher redeemable without a bank account or card, built on UPI, used for health and education grants.

In IBPS Clerk Prelims, expect 2–4 MCQs per shift on scheme name, tagline, beneficiary and benefit value — not derivations.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Edge Cases and Exact-Number Traps
PMJDY's accident cover switches between ₹1 lakh and ₹2 lakh depending on the **account opening date**, not the customer's age. Similarly, APY enrolment closes at age 40 — after that, no fresh subscriber can join, though existing subscribers continue. KCC's "4% effective" rate is conditional on timely repayment: it stacks a 2% prompt-repayment incentive on top of the 3% government interest subvention on short-term crop loans up to ₹3 lakh.

> Numbers to lock: PMSBY ₹20 premium → ₹2 lakh cover; PMJJBY ₹436 → ₹2 lakh life cover; MUDRA top slab ₹10 lakh (not ₹20 lakh); Stand-Up India floor ₹10 lakh (not ₹1 lakh).

#### Mechanism: Why DBT Uses Aadhaar
APBS maps Aadhaar to the beneficiary's bank account through the **National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI)** mapper, so the government pushes funds to the account linked to that Aadhaar. It removes intermediary layers used by older welfare delivery and plugs leakage — a recurring General Awareness reasoning point.

#### Connections to Adjacent Topics
This topic overlaps heavily with **NPCI products** (RuPay, UPI, AePS, APBS), **RBI master directions** on KYC and digital lending, and **budget announcements**. Tagline-based questions in IBPS Clerk Mains (Banking Awareness) often pair MUDRA with "**Fund the Unfunded**" and Stand-Up India with "**Promoting Entrepreneurship**".

#### Common Mistakes
- Confusing PMJJBY (life, ₹436) with PMSBY (accident, ₹20).
- Calling MUDRA's max limit ₹20 lakh — it is ₹10 lakh.
- Stating PM-Kisan as a pension scheme — it is income support of ₹6,000/year in three ₹2,000 instalments.
- Mis-attributing launch years: PMSBY/PMJJBY and MUDRA are 2015; Stand-Up India is 2016; e-RUPI is 2021; PM Vishwakarma is 2023.

#### Practice Prompts
1. A 35-year-old wants a fixed monthly pension from age 60 with spouse continuation and is willing to contribute periodically. Which scheme fits, who administers it, and what are the pension slabs?
2. A street vendor needs her first working-capital loan collateral-free with an interest subsidy. Name the scheme, the three loan tranches, the subsidy rate, and the nodal ministry.
