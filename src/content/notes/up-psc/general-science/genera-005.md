# General Awareness - Current Events & Issues

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

**Current Affairs + Static GK** is how UPPSC frames this section: every recent event must be tied back to an article, scheme, report, or appointment. The Prelims GS Paper I carries roughly 15–20 questions here, drawn from the last 12–18 months of news.

#### Must-know anchors:
- **RBI Repo Rate** (verify current value day-before-exam), **India's GDP growth** %, and **CPI inflation** rate.
- **UP-specific flagships:** Mukhyamantri Kanya Sumangala Yojana, UP Startup Policy 2020/2024 revision, Jewar Airport (Noida International), UP Defence Industrial Corridor, Film City project.
- **National milestones:** Chandrayaan-3 soft-landing date, Aditya L1 launch, G20 New Delhi Leaders' Declaration, India's COVID-vaccine record, 5G/6G vision.
- **Awards:** Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna (renamed from Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna), Padma ranks (Bhushan/Bhushan/Vibhushan), Nobel Prizes of the current cycle.

Link every scheme to its implementing ministry and eligibility rule — that is what makes a current-affairs answer non-throwaway.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### Core Architecture of the Section

The section is **not** random news scanning. UPPSC tests it as **applied awareness** — current events layered onto static General Studies fundamentals (Polity, Economy, Geography, Environment). Questions typically arrive as MCQs in Prelims GS Paper I, and short notes in Mains GS II/III. The 3% weight converts into roughly 15 questions out of 150 in Prelims — losing marks here directly hits cutoff.

1. Read **PIB Daily** (press releases filtered for UP) and the **UP CMO daily bulletin**.
2. Maintain a **single-page tracker**: date / event / static-GK link / ministry.
3. Prioritise UP schemes above national news of the same date.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Edge Cases & Examiner Traps

- **Award-name traps:** Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna was officially renamed to **Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna** (announcement 2021). Writing "Arjuna Award is the highest sports honour" is wrong — Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna is the top; Arjuna is the next tier.
- **Scheme attribution:** Aspirants regularly credit **Mukhyamantri Kanya Sumangala Yojana** (₹25,000 transferred in tranches to girl child; launched **2019**) to the Centre. It is a **UP state** scheme funded from the state social welfare budget.
- **Index successions:** World Bank discontinued **Ease of Doing Business**; the successor is **B-READY (Business Ready)**. MBA-level coaching material may still cite EoDB ranks — do not.
- **Governor vs. CM vs. Speaker:** When President rule was discussed in UP context, examiners test whether you know Article 356 invocation requires a Cabinet recommendation reported to Parliament.
- **RBI terminology:** Repo rate is the *single* policy rate; reverse repo and MSF are corridor rates. Numerical questions ask "by how many basis points" — memorise both direction and magnitude.

#### Worked Examples of MCQ Style

> "With reference to Mukhyamantri Kanya Sumangala Yojana, consider:
> (a) It is a centrally sponsored scheme ✔/✘
> (b) The transfer is ₹25,000 in tranches ✔
> (c) It is implemented by the UP Social Welfare Department ✔"

Correct: (b) and (c). The yojana is **state-sponsored**, not centrally sponsored — eliminating (a).

> "Consider: (1) Aditya L1 orbits L1 (1) (2) Chandrayaan-3 landed near the south pole of the Moon (✔) (3) Gaganyaan first crewed flight was launched in 2023 (✘; launch is subsequent to the recent cycle)."

Use these stems to verify ranks, dates, and bodies — the **micro-detail** is exactly where UPPSC separates toppers from the cut-off zone.

#### Connections to Adjacent Topics in GS Paper I

Indian Polity: every Cabinet reshuffle links to Article 77, the **anti-defection** law (10th Schedule), and parliamentary committees. Economy questions on repo rate extend to **monetary policy committee** composition (3 RBI internal + 3 external + 1 RBI Governor as chair). Geography: Jewar Airport links to the Yamuna Expressway corridor and Western UP logistics. Environment: MISHTI scheme (mangrove initiative) links to CO₂ sequestration and SDG 13, 14, 15.

#### Practice Prompts

1. Frame the **Minister, Ministry, and Static Article** for any three recent Cabinet decisions affecting Uttar Pradesh.
2. Prepare a one-page **Governor, CM, Chief Secretary** sheet — current incumbents, swearing-in dates, and earlier posts — and link each to a constitutional article.
