# Sound, Light and Atomic Structure — MCAT Chem/Phys

Sound, light, and atomic structure span AAMC Foundational Concepts 4 and 5 of the Chem/Phys section. The MCAT tests wave properties (sound and light), geometric optics, photon emission and absorption, and the basics of atomic structure and electron configuration. Items often pair a passage describing a physical setup (a lens, a sound intensity measurement, an emission spectrum) with a multiple-choice item.

### Lite — Quick Review (1h-1d)

#### Core formulas and concepts

- **Wave speed:** v = f × λ (frequency × wavelength).
- **Sound intensity level:** β = 10 × log₁₀(I / I₀), measured in decibels (dB); I₀ = 10^(-12) W/m².
- **Doppler effect:** the observed frequency shifts when source or observer moves.
- **Snell's law:** n₁ × sin(θ₁) = n₂ × sin(θ₂). Index of refraction n = c / v.
- **Lens equation:** 1/f = 1/d_o + 1/d_i. Focal length in metres; object and image distances in metres.
- **Photon energy:** E = h × f = (h × c) / λ. Planck's constant h ≈ 6.626 × 10^(-34) J·s.
- **Bohr model:** E_n = -13.6 / n² eV for the hydrogen atom.
- **Electron configuration:** Aufbau principle, Pauli exclusion, Hund's rule.

#### One-pass drill

1. Identify the topic: sound, geometric optics, photon, or atomic structure.
2. List the known quantities.
3. Pick the formula or rule.
4. Solve algebraically; plug in numbers last.

#### Examiner traps

- Confusing frequency and wavelength. They are inversely related at constant wave speed.
- Forgetting the speed of light c ≈ 3 × 10⁸ m/s when computing photon energy.
- Mixing up the Bohr model and the quantum-mechanical model. The Bohr model is for hydrogen-like atoms only.

### Standard — Regular Study (2d-2mo)

#### Sound waves

The MCAT tests sound intensity (in W/m²) and sound intensity level (in dB). A doubling of intensity adds 3 dB; a tenfold increase adds 10 dB. The threshold of hearing is I₀ = 10^(-12) W/m².

The Doppler effect: when the source moves toward the observer, the observed frequency is higher; when the source moves away, the observed frequency is lower. The MCAT frames this for ambulance sirens, ultrasound, and blood-flow measurement.

#### Geometric optics

**Snell's law:** n₁ × sin(θ₁) = n₂ × sin(θ₂). The angle is measured from the normal. Total internal reflection occurs when light goes from a higher to a lower index of refraction at an angle greater than the critical angle.

**Lens equation:** 1/f = 1/d_o + 1/d_i. For a converging lens, f is positive. For a diverging lens, f is negative. Real images have positive image distances; virtual images have negative image distances.

The MCAT often frames optics in the context of the eye (cornea, lens, retina) and corrective lenses.

#### Photons and atomic structure

A photon is a quantum of light. Its energy is E = h × f. The MCAT tests:

- The relationship between photon energy and wavelength (higher energy = shorter wavelength).
- The Bohr model for hydrogen: E_n = -13.6 / n² eV. Transitions between levels emit or absorb photons of specific energies.
- Atomic orbitals (s, p, d, f) and electron configurations. Aufbau principle: fill lower-energy orbitals first. Pauli exclusion: two electrons per orbital with opposite spins. Hund's rule: fill degenerate orbitals singly before pairing.

#### Practice routine

- Take 5 sound items. Mix intensity, frequency, and Doppler.
- Take 5 optics items. Mix Snell's law, total internal reflection, and the lens equation.
- Take 5 photon items. Mix photon energy, Bohr transitions, and the photoelectric effect.
- Take 5 electron-configuration items. Fill the orbitals for the first 36 elements.

### Deep — Long-Term Mastery (1mo-6mo)

#### How this topic connects to the other Chem/Phys topics

- **Spectrophotometry** uses photon absorption and the Beer-Lambert law, building on photon-energy concepts.
- **Bonding and molecular geometry** depend on electron configurations of the bonding atoms.
- **Imaging techniques** (X-ray, MRI, ultrasound) use sound and light concepts.

#### Common errors and the correction

- **"Higher frequency means more energy."** For electromagnetic waves, yes (E = h × f). For sound waves, frequency is pitch, not energy.
- **"The image distance is always positive."** Real images have positive image distances; virtual images have negative image distances.
- **"The Bohr model works for all atoms."** The Bohr model is for hydrogen and hydrogen-like (single-electron) atoms only. Multi-electron atoms require the quantum-mechanical model.

#### Exam technique

- Identify the topic before picking the formula.
- Use the speed of light c ≈ 3 × 10⁸ m/s for photon calculations.
- For the lens equation, draw a ray diagram to confirm the sign of the image distance.
- For electron configurations, fill the orbitals in Aufbau order: 1s, 2s, 2p, 3s, 3p, 4s, 3d, 4p, 5s, 4d, 5p, 6s, 4f, ...
- Use the platform's calculator for arithmetic.
