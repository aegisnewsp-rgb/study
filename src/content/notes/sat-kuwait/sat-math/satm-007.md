# Geometry and Trigonometry — SAT Math Notes

Geometry and Trigonometry is one of the four SAT Math content domains. The SAT tests area and volume of plane and solid figures, coordinate geometry, circle properties, similarity, right-triangle trigonometry, and the unit circle.

> Verify the live SAT specifications on https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/ before planning revision.

---

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)

#### Core facts in one pass

- **Area of a triangle:** A = ½ × b × h.
- **Area of a circle:** A = πr².
- **Volume of a rectangular solid:** V = l × w × h.
- **Volume of a cylinder:** V = πr²h.
- **Pythagoras:** a² + b² = c² in a right triangle.
- **Similarity:** corresponding angles are equal, corresponding sides are proportional.
- **Trig ratios:** sin θ = opposite/hypotenuse, cos θ = adjacent/hypotenuse, tan θ = opposite/adjacent.
- **Unit circle:** the circle of radius 1 centred at the origin; useful for finding trig values.

#### Examiner traps

- Forgetting to convert units (cm to m, etc.) before calculating.
- Using the wrong formula (perimeter vs area, area vs volume).

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)

#### Coordinate geometry formulas

- Slope: m = (y₂ - y₁) / (x₂ - x₁).
- Distance: d = √((x₂ - x₁)² + (y₂ - y₁)²).
- Midpoint: M = ((x₁ + x₂)/2, (y₁ + y₂)/2).
- Equation of a line: y = mx + b (slope-intercept) or Ax + By + C = 0 (standard).

#### Circle properties

- Area: A = πr².
- Circumference: C = 2πr.
- Angle at the centre is twice the angle at the circumference.
- Angle in a semicircle is 90°.

#### Similar triangles

For similar triangles, corresponding angles are equal and corresponding sides are proportional. The ratio of corresponding sides is the same for all pairs.

#### Right-triangle trigonometry

In a right triangle with angle θ:

- sin θ = opposite / hypotenuse
- cos θ = adjacent / hypotenuse
- tan θ = opposite / adjacent

The standard angles (30°, 45°, 60°) have known trig values.

### 🔴 Deep — Long-Term Mastery (1mo–6mo)

#### Where Geometry and Trigonometry appears across the SAT Math section

Geometry and Trigonometry items appear throughout the SAT Math section. They test the candidate's ability to apply geometric and trigonometric formulas.

#### Common misconceptions (and the correction)

- "Area and perimeter are the same." No — area is the space inside; perimeter is the boundary.
- "All triangles are similar." No — only those with the same angles.

#### Exam technique

- For area/volume problems, identify the shape and apply the formula.
- For Pythagoras problems, identify the right triangle and the hypotenuse.
- For similarity problems, set up a proportion.
- For trig problems, identify the angle and the sides.

#### Step-by-step worked College Board test example

**Problem**:
A retail warehouse offers a 20% discount off the original price of an electronics item during a seasonal sale. An additional clearance coupon applies an extra 15% discount off the already discounted sale price. If the final price paid is $136.00, what was the original list price?

**Solution**:
1. Let P be the original list price.
2. After the first 20% discount, the price is P * (1 - 0.20) = 0.80P.
3. After the second 15% discount, the price is (0.80P) * (1 - 0.15) = (0.80P) * 0.85 = 0.68P.
4. Equate to the final price paid:
   0.68P = 136.00 -> P = 136.00 / 0.68 = 200.00.
5. Common distractor error: adding the percentages (20% + 15% = 35%) and computing 136 / 0.65 = $209.23. Successive percentage changes multiply; they never add.
