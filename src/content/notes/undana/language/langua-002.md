# Reading Comprehension and Text Analysis

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

Reading Comprehension and Text Analysis is the UNDANA admission component that checks how you decode short functional texts in both **Bahasa Indonesia** and **English**. Typical items ask you to name the **main idea (gagasan utama)**, identify the **communicative purpose (tujuan komunikatif)**, infer an unstated meaning, and resolve **vocabulary in context** from passages of 150–300 words.

- **Skim first, then scan.** Read the opening sentence for genre cues (*Diumumkan*, *We are pleased to invite*) before tackling the questions.
- **Locate signal words.** Wh-questions (who/what/when/where/why/how) map to specific parts of the text; 5W1H answers are usually paraphrased, not copied.
- **Watch the negators.** UNDANA items in English frequently use *NOT*, *EXCEPT*, and *INCORRECT* to flip the correct option.

| Skill | What it tests | Quick tactic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main idea | Topic sentence recognition | Read first + last paragraph |
| Purpose | Genre function | Check header (announcement, invitation, ad) |
| Inference | Implied meaning | Combine two stated facts |

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### What the items actually measure

UNDANA examiners design Topic 2 around three cognitive layers. The first layer is **literal comprehension (pemahaman literal)** — locating facts, dates, names, and stated reasons directly in the passage. The second is **inferential comprehension (pemahaman inferensial)** — combining two stated sentences to draw a conclusion the author never spelled out, such as the writer's attitude or the implied audience. The third layer is **critical reading**, where you judge whether an option is supported, contradicted, or unrelated to the text.

#### Skimming and scanning as procedure

Skimming means reading quickly to absorb register and genre. For an Indonesian *pengumuman*, skimming reveals whether the text informs, invites, warns, or persuades. Scanning means moving the eye for keywords that answer each question stem. Always underline signal words in the question — *dimaksudkan*, *tujuan*, *the word "X" in line 3 most nearly means* — before re-reading the relevant sentence.

#### Functional text markers

A reliable first step is recognising the **text type (jenis teks)** from its layout. Announcements begin with *Diumumkan kepada…* or *Dengan ini kami sampaikan…*; invitations list date, time, place, and dress code; advertisements foreground price, contact, and benefit; short dialogues use speaker tags like *Andi:*, *Rina:*.

| Text type | Typical purpose | Key fields to scan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Announcement / Pengumuman | To inform | Date, who, what, deadline |
| Invitation / Undangan | To invite | Date, time, venue, dress code |
| Advertisement / Iklan | To persuade | Product, price, contact |
| Letter / Surat | To request or inform | Sender, recipient, subject |

#### Common traps in this section

- Choosing an answer because its words appear in the passage rather than because it answers the question.
- Confusing *siapa yang mengumumkan* (who announces) with *kepada siapa* (to whom it is addressed).
- Translating English items word-for-word and missing idiomatic meaning such as *out of the blue*, *by and large*, *come up with*.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### How Topic 2 fits the UNDANA paper

Topic 2 contributes roughly 3% of the total UNDANA admission score. Candidates typically face **5–8 multiple-choice items** drawn from one Indonesian passage and one English passage, plus 2–3 standalone vocabulary-in-context items. Because the section is short but discriminative, it is a high-return area: every correct answer costs less than one minute, and careless reading on even three items can lower your overall rank band noticeably.

#### Worked micro-example (Indonesian functional text)

> **Bacalah teks berikut!**
> "Diumumkan kepada seluruh mahasiswa baru Universitas Nusa Cendana bahwa registrasi ulang akan dilaksanakan pada tanggal 5–7 Agustus 2026 secara daring melalui pmb.undana.ac.id. Mahasiswa yang tidak melakukan registrasi dalam waktu yang ditentukan dianggap mengundurkan diri."
>
> **Pertanyaan:** Apa tujuan utama teks tersebut?
> A. Menghibur pembaca.  B. Menginformasikan jadwal.  **C. Mengumumkan kebijakan.**  D. Membujuk pembaca berlibur.  E. Menceritakan pengalaman.
>
> **Jawaban: C.** The opener *Diumumkan kepada* signals an **announcement (pengumuman)** whose communicative purpose is to broadcast a policy. Option B is tempting but narrower — the text announces *what and when*, but the broader act is policy announcement.

#### Strategy checklist for the exam room

1. Read the **first sentence only** to lock in genre and purpose before scanning questions.
2. For each question, **circle the signal word** in Indonesian (*tujuan*, *saran*, *kelemahan*) and in English (*NOT*, *EXCEPT*, *according to*).
3. Match the option to the **text type logic**: an invitation cannot primarily *warn*, an advertisement cannot primarily *regulate*.
4. When two options look true, pick the one that **directly answers the stem**, not the one that is merely true elsewhere in the text.
5. Use **process of elimination**: in English passages, three of four options are usually paraphrases of single sentences, and only one is supported by two combined lines — that one is the inferential answer.

#### Connection to adjacent topics

Strong Topic 2 performance feeds directly into **Topic 1 (Vocabulary and Grammar)** because vocabulary-in-context items test the same lexical bank, and into **Topic 3 (Writing)** because recognising communicative purpose in input texts sharpens your ability to choose the right register and structure on output tasks. Treating Topic 2 as standalone under-prepares you; treat it as a reading engine for the whole Language component.

#### Practice prompts:
1. Given an English announcement ending with *Failure to comply will result in penalties*, identify whether the dominant purpose is to inform, to warn, or to instruct, and justify in one sentence.
2. From a 200-word Indonesian *surat pembaca*, distinguish the literal main idea from the inferred main idea and explain which one UNDANA is most likely to test.
