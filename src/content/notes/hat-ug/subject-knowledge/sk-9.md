# Biology: Human Physiology

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

Human physiology studies how the body's organ systems function, from **cells** through tissues, organs, and whole systems. HAT-UG Subject Knowledge tests it with roughly 4% weightage, so mastery of **homeostasis**, the **cardiac cycle**, and **gas exchange** delivers the highest payoff.

- **Cardiac Output:** CO (L/min) = SV (mL/beat) × HR (beats/min) ÷ 1000.
- **Mean Arterial Pressure:** MAP = Diastolic BP + ⅓ × (Systolic − Diastolic).
- **Vital Capacity:** VC = IRV + TV + ERV; residual volume is excluded.
- **Gas exchange** occurs at the **alveoli**, not bronchi; oxygen rides on **hemoglobin**.
- **Insulin** (β-cells) lowers blood glucose; **glucagon** (α-cells) raises it.

| System | Core function |
| --- | --- |
| Respiratory | O₂/CO₂ exchange across alveolar walls |
| Circulatory | Pumps blood via pulmonary + systemic loops |
| Nervous | Transmits impulses via neurons and synapses |

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### Levels of organisation and homeostasis
The body is organised from cell → tissue → organ → system → organism. **Homeostasis** keeps internal variables (temperature, pH, glucose) within narrow limits using **negative feedback**: a deviation triggers a response that reverses it. Insulin–glucagon balance and thermoregulation via sweat and shivering are classic HAT-UG examples.

#### Circulation and the cardiac cycle
The heart has four chambers driving two circuits: pulmonary (right heart to lungs) and systemic (left heart to body). **Systole** is ventricular contraction and ejection; **diastole** is relaxation and filling. Stroke volume (≈70 mL at rest) times heart rate (≈72 bpm) yields cardiac output near 5 L/min.

#### Respiration and gas transport
Breathing moves air into alveoli, where **diffusion** follows partial-pressure gradients. Hemoglobin binds O₂ to form oxyhemoglobin; **carbonic anhydrase** in RBCs converts CO₂ to HCO₃⁻ for transport. Vital capacity sums inspiratory reserve, tidal, and expiratory reserve volumes.

#### Renal excretion
The nephron performs **glomerular filtration**, then tubular **reabsorption** and **secretion**, producing urine. ADH adjusts water reabsorption in the collecting duct.

- Recall: filtration precedes secretion — most MCQs invert this.
- Lung volumes: TLC = VC + RV; never add RV when asked for VC.
- MAP uses one-third pulse pressure — examiners test this weighting.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Quantitative physiology and clinical links
**BMI** = mass (kg) ÷ height² (m²), classifying underweight (<18.5), normal (18.5–24.9), and overweight (≥25). **eGFR** approximates kidney filtration: ≈ (140 − age) × mass × 0.85 (if female) ÷ (72 × serum creatinine). A drop signals renal impairment relevant to Pakistani curricula.

#### Endocrine and reproductive integration
The anterior pituitary secretes **TSH, ACTH, FSH, LH, GH, prolactin**; the posterior pituitary releases **oxytocin and ADH**. The adrenal **cortex** produces cortisol and aldosterone, while the **medulla** secretes adrenaline. In the menstrual cycle, **FSH** drives follicle growth, **LH** triggers ovulation, and **progesterone** maintains the endometrium.

#### Muscle contraction and metabolism
The **sliding-filament model** uses ATP and Ca²⁺ to cycle myosin heads along actin. **Cellular respiration** oxidises glucose: C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + ≈38 ATP. Aerobic metabolism dominates at rest; anaerobic glycolysis fires during sprints.

#### Common traps
- Confusing **insulin** (β-cells) with **glucagon** (α-cells).
- Naming bronchi as gas-exchange sites — only alveoli qualify.
- Stating the **reflex arc** requires the brain; most spinal reflexes bypass it.
- Equating hormones of anterior and posterior pituitary lobes.

**Practice prompts:** (1) A patient has BP 120/80 mmHg — calculate MAP and explain each term. (2) A spirometer trace shows IRV = 3.1 L, TV = 0.5 L, ERV = 1.2 L, RV = 1.2 L — find VC and TLC.
