# Sports & Culture

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### Player–Sport and Tournament Links

CUET frequently frames questions as "Player X is associated with which sport?" or "Trophy Y is awarded in which event?" Memorise both halves of the link. Cricket follows a layered structure: the **ICC** governs international cricket, the **BCCI** runs India's domestic game, and the **IPL** is the franchise league. Football funnels into **FIFA**, the World Cup, and continental cups like the Euros and Copa América. Tennis rotates through four **Grand Slams** per year (Australian Open, French Open/Roland Garros, Wimbledon, US Open), each with distinctive surface and tradition.

#### Awards Hierarchy and Categories

The Padma Awards are India's highest civilian honours and follow a strict ascending order. Sports awards operate on a parallel ladder: the **Arjuna Award** recognises current sportspersons, the **Dronacharya Award** honours coaches, and the **Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award** is the country's lifetime sporting honour. Many students confuse Khel Ratna with Arjuna — the former is a single lifetime award, while Arjuna is given annually to multiple athletes.

#### Heritage and Cultural Forms

India has over 40 UNESCO **World Heritage Sites** (tangible) and a separate list of **Intangible Cultural Heritage** entries such as Kumbh Mela, Yoga, and Vedic chanting. Classical dances are eight in number, each tied to a specific state tradition.

| Classical dance count | 8 forms officially recognised by the Sangeet Natak Akademi |

| Padma order (low→high) | Padma Shri → Padma Bhushan → Padma Vibhushan → Bharat Ratna |

- **GI tag traps:** Darjeeling Tea → West Bengal (not Assam); Madhubani Painting → Bihar; Banarasi Saree → Uttar Pradesh; Pashmina Shawl → Kashmir.
- **Recent laureates worth memorising:** Amitav Ghosh (Jnanpith 2018), Geetanjali Shree (Booker International 2022 for *Tomb of Sand*), Vaidya Sushila Nayak (Padma Shri 2024 for documentation of Chhau dance).

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Edge Cases and Common Traps

The most-tested trap is **dance-name confusion**: Kathak, Kathakali, and Kuchipudi sound similar but belong to North India, Kerala, and Andhra Pradesh respectively. Another recurring trap is the **Padma rank order** — students swap Shri with Bhushan, costing marks on otherwise easy questions. In sports, mixing up **Olympics vs Asian Games vs Commonwealth** is common; remember the Commonwealth Games are held every four years and India hosted them in 2010 (Delhi) and will host in 2030 (Gandhinagar).

A second cluster of errors surrounds **GI tags and heritage lists**. Sites on UNESCO's *tentative* list (e.g., forts of Rajasthan were on it before inscription) are often wrongly cited as full World Heritage Sites. Intangible heritage entries are also frequently mislabelled as tangible sites — Kumbh Mela is *intangible*, while the Taj Mahal is *tangible*.
