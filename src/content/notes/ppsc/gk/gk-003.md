# Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Province

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (KP) is the northwestern province of Pakistan, renamed from the **North-West Frontier Province (NWFP)** on **19 April 2010** through the **18th Constitutional Amendment**. **Peshawar** is the capital. The province shares a western border with **Afghanistan** along the **Durand Line (≈2,670 km)** and absorbed the former **FATA** tribal belt on **31 May 2018** via the **25th Amendment**. Official short form used by the Government of KP is "KP", not "KPK".

- **Capital:** Peshawar
- **Rename amendment:** 18th (April 2010), NWFP → KP
- **FATA merger:** 25th Amendment, 31 May 2018
- **Western border:** Durand Line with Afghanistan
- **Major passes:** Khyber, Kurram, Tochi, Gomal
- **Largest earth-filled dam:** Tarbela (Indus River, KP)
- **Dominant ethnicity:** Pashtun; dominant language: Pashto

> 💡 **High-Yield Memory Hook:** **"Peshawar-Khyber-Pashto-2010-2018"** — recite this chain to recall capital, the iconic pass, language, NWFP→KP renaming year, and FATA merger year in one breath. Pair with **"D Line is Durand, R Line is Radcliffe"** to instantly separate the Afghan border from the India-Pakistan one.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### Historical Evolution

The territory has been renamed twice in modern Pakistani history. From independence (1947) until 2010, the province was the **North-West Frontier Province (NWFP)**, a label inherited from British India. Through the **18th Constitutional Amendment (passed 19 April 2010)**, the name was changed to **Khyber Pakhtunkhwa** to reflect the demographic identity of the Pashtun majority. On **31 May 2018**, the **25th Amendment** merged the seven agencies and six frontier regions of **FATA** into KP, adding seven new "merged districts" and creating a new administrative division.

#### Geography and Borders

KP lies between roughly 31°N–36°N latitude and 69°E–74°E longitude. It does **not** border India; its neighbours are **Afghanistan** (west/northwest, Durand Line), **Punjab** (southeast), **Islamabad Capital Territory** (east), **Azad Jammu & Kashmir** (northeast) and **Gilgit-Baltistan** (north). The **Durand Line**, agreed in 1893 between British India and Afghanistan, is internationally recognised as the border but disputed by Afghanistan.

#### Major Passes and Rivers

The province is traversed by the **Indus** and its tributaries — **Kabul, Swat, Chitral (Kunar), Bara** — which feed **Tarbela Dam**, the world's largest earth-filled dam by structural volume. Strategic mountain passes into Afghanistan include **Khyber, Kurram, Tochi, and Gomal**.

#### 🎯 Exam-Level Worked Problem

**Question:** Khyber Pakhtunkhwa was formerly known as NWFP. The renaming and the FATA merger occurred through which constitutional amendments?
A) 17th (2003) and 23rd (2016)
B) 18th (2010) and 25th (2018) ✅
C) 19th (2010) and 26th (2019)
D) 20th (2012) and 25th (2018)

**Solution:** The **18th Amendment**, assented on **19 April 2010**, renamed NWFP to Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. The **25th Amendment**, effective **31 May 2018**, merged FATA into KP. Option B matches both years and amendment numbers.

> ⚠️ **Examiner Trap:** Students often pick "19th Amendment" because 2010 is remembered as the renaming year. The 19th Amendment of 2010 dealt with judicial appointments, not renaming. Always verify the amendment number alongside the year.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Administrative Reorganisation After FATA Merger

Before 2018, KP had **7 divisions**: Peshawar, Mardan, Hazara, Malakand, Kohat, Bannu and Dera Ismail Khan, comprising **24–25 settled districts**. FATA contributed **7 agencies** (Bajaur, Mohmand, Khyber, Orakzai, Kurram, North Waziristan, South Waziristan) and **6 frontier regions**. After merger these became 7 merged districts, and a new division (initially styled "Tribal Districts Division") was created. Safest exam answer: *"Originally 7 divisions; restructured after FATA merger in 2018."*

#### Cultural and Historical Heritage

- **Gandhara Buddhist civilisation**: flourished in the Peshawar valley (1st century BCE – 5th century CE). **Takht-i-Bahi** (UNESCO World Heritage, inscribed 1980) is a monastic complex near Mardan.
- **Pashtunwali**: an ancient tribal code governing hospitality (*melmastia*), refuge (*nanawatai*), and justice (*badal*).
- **Bacha Khan (Abdul Ghaffar Khan)**: founded the **Khudai Khidmatgar (Red Shirt)** movement in 1929; advocated non-violent resistance against British rule.

#### Advanced Traps and Exceptions

1. **"KP borders India"** — false; KP does not touch India. Only Punjab and Sindh have working Indian borders.
2. **"Peshawar is Pakistan's second-largest city"** — false; Karachi (#1), Lahore (#2), Faisalabad (#3) all exceed Peshawar in census counts.
4. **"KPK abbreviation is official"** — outdated; the Government of KP uses "KP". Old textbooks still print "KPK".
5. **"Tarbela is the largest dam in the world"** — false; it is the **largest earth-filled dam by volume** (≈153 Mm³), not the tallest or longest overall.
6. **"Khyber Pass is the only crossing"** — false; Kurram, Tochi, Gomal, and the Lowari Tunnel (Chitral) also connect KP with Afghanistan.
7. **"Cadet College Razmak is in settled KP"** — false; it sits in the merged tribal district of North Waziristan (former FATA).

#### Key Institutions and Economy

- **Universities**: University of Peshawar (1950), Khyber Pakhtunkhwa University of Engineering & Technology (Peshawar), Agriculture University Peshawar, COMSATS Abbottabad.
- **KPPSC** headquartered in Peshawar handles provincial recruitment, separate from Punjab's PPSC in Lahore.
- **Economy**: agriculture (wheat, maize, sugarcane, tobacco, peach, persimmon), forestry (Kala-Shitta and Miandam forests), hydropower (Tarbela, Allai Khwar, Dubair), and minerals (chromite, marble, natural gas at Kohat).
