# World History, Major Civilizations, and Contemporary Global Affairs

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

- **River-valley civilizations** — Tigris–Euphrates (Mesopotamia), Nile (Egypt), Indus (Harappa/Mohenjo-daro), Yellow River (Shang) — all emerged with irrigation, writing, and centralized rule.
- **Greco-Roman foundations** — Greek city-states (Athens = direct democracy) and Roman law/republic shaped Western governance, later revived by the **Renaissance** (14th–17th c. Italy).
- **Islamic Golden Age** — Abbasid Baghdad (not Umayyad Damascus) hosted the **Translation Movement**, advancing algebra, optics, and medicine.
- **Industrial Revolution** — began in **Britain** c. 1760, not Germany; later spread to Europe and the U.S.
- **World Wars** — WWI (1914–18) triggered by alliance/treaty systems; WWII (1939–45) caused the **Holocaust**; **UN founded 1945** after the League of Nations failed.
- **Cold War** — USA vs. USSR, ideological + proxy, ended with USSR collapse (1991).
- **Organizations to recognize**: **UN (1945), OPEC (1960, Saudi founding member), G20, WTO, EU, OIC**.
- **Quick mnemonic**: *MEGIC* — Mesopotamia, Egypt, Greece, India, China — the five cradles.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### Cradles of Civilization

The earliest states formed along four great river systems where irrigation enabled surplus agriculture, taxation, and scribe classes. **Mesopotamia** (Sumer, Akkad, Babylon) gave cuneiform writing and the Code of Hammurabi. **Ancient Egypt** unified under the **Pharaoh** c. 3100 BCE and built a state around Nile flooding cycles. The **Indus Valley** civilization (c. 2500 BCE) featured grid-planned cities like Mohenjo-daro with standardized weights. **Shang China** produced oracle-bone script and bronze ritual vessels along the Yellow River.

| Civilization | River | Core Legacy |
|---|---|---|
| Mesopotamia | Tigris–Euphrates | Cuneiform, Hammurabi's Code |
| Egypt | Nile | Pyramids, pharaonic statecraft |
| Indus Valley | Indus | Urban grid planning, drainage |
| Shang China | Yellow River | Oracle-bone script, bronze |

#### Classical and Islamic Eras

**Greek city-states** pioneered philosophy and political experimentation — Athens developed direct democracy, Sparta a military oligarchy. **Rome** contributed republican institutions, codified law, and an engineering/road network that unified the Mediterranean. After 622 CE, the **Caliphate** (Rashidun → Umayyad, capital **Damascus** → **Abbasid**, capital **Baghdad**) translated Greek, Persian, and Indian works, producing advances in algebra (al-Khwarizmi), medicine (Ibn Sina), and optics (Ibn al-Haytham).

#### Modern Transformations

**European colonial expansion** (15th–20th c.) reshaped the Americas, Africa, and Asia. **WWI** (1914–18) erupted from alliance entanglements after the assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand; the Treaty of Versailles humiliated Germany. **WWII** (1939–45) brought the Holocaust and the atomic bombings, ending with the **UN Charter signed 1945**. The **Industrial Revolution** (Britain, c. 1760) mechanized textiles, steam, and iron — fueling both prosperity and imperial reach.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Cold War, Decolonization, and the Saudi Dimension

The **Cold War** (1947–1991) was a bipolar contest waged through nuclear deterrence, NATO/Warsaw Pact alliances, and proxy conflicts (Korea, Vietnam, Afghanistan). It ended with Soviet economic exhaustion and the 1991 USSR dissolution. Parallel **decolonization** (1945–1975) created dozens of new states in Africa and Asia, reshaping the UN General Assembly.

Saudi Arabia is a **founding member of OPEC (1960)** and the **UN (1945)**, and plays a central role in OIC, G20 (hosted the 2020 Riyadh Summit), and the Gulf Cooperation Council. For the Qimiyah exam, expect questions linking Saudi foreign policy to these bodies.

#### Common Traps and Quick-Fix Tips

- Abbasid capital = **Baghdad**; Umayyad capital = **Damascus** — don't swap.
- OPEC = Organization of the Petroleum Exporting Countries (1960, Vienna HQ, 13 members); OAPEC = Arab-only offshoot (1968, Cairo). Saudi Arabia is in **both**.
- The **Holocaust** is a **WWII** event — never attach it to WWI causes.
- The UN was founded **after** WWII in 1945, replacing the failed League of Nations.
- The Industrial Revolution started in **Britain** (late 18th c.), not Germany.

> **Exam tactic**: Qimiyah MCQs often test chronology — rehearse dates for Treaty of Versailles (1919), UN founding (1945), OPEC founding (1960), USSR collapse (1991), and 9/11 (2001).

#### Practice Prompts

1. **Chronology**: Arrange these events in order — Treaty of Versailles, UN founding, OPEC formation, USSR dissolution, 9/11 attacks. Identify one cause-and-effect chain between them.
2. **Organization matching**: For each body (UN, OPEC, G20, WTO, OIC), state the founding year, headquarters, and one current Saudi-relevant policy concern it addresses.
