# What Topic 13 actually measures

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

**Topic 13 – Subject Knowledge** (Pengetahuan Bidang Studi) tests your command of academic substance specific to the study cluster you chose on SIMAK UI: **Saintek**, **Soshum**, or **Humaniora**. The subtest contributes only **3%** of the total score, but it is scored independently from the verbal, quantitative, and Bahasa Indonesia sections.

- **Mapping rule:** choose the soal paket that matches your intended **Kelompok Ujian** — mismatching the cluster invalidates your answers.
- **Curriculum basis:** content draws from **Kurikulum Merdeka** SMA/SMK/MA **Capaian Pembelajaran (CP)** for grades 10–12, not the legacy UN syllabus.
- **Question style:** **literasi akademik** passages with applied concepts — no rote recall traps.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### What Topic 13 actually measures
SIMAK UI splits its paper into independent components, and Topic 13 is the component that checks whether a candidate can read an academic-style stimulus and apply discipline-specific vocabulary correctly. For a Saintek candidate this means biology, chemistry, physics, or mathematics reasoning applied to a short passage; for Soshum it means economics, sociology, geography, or history interpretation; for Humaniora it means philosophy, literature, or cultural studies argumentation.

#### Why the 3% weight still matters
The total SIMAK UI paper runs around 60–100 items depending on the year and program. A 3% slice on a paper of 80 questions equals roughly two to three items. On competitive programs such as **FK**, **FT**, **FH**, or **Akuntansi** where the rank difference between the last admitted and the first rejected candidate is one or two points, surrendering free items from Topic 13 directly costs a seat.

#### Standard problem patterns
Most items present a 3–6 sentence passage, then ask you to identify a **konsep**, infer a **konsekuensi logis**, or select the **istilah** that best fills a blank. The trap is vocabulary that looks familiar but belongs to the *wrong* cluster — for example, "inflasi" appearing in a Saintek paket where only physics/biology terms are scored.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Curriculum anchoring and Kompetensi Dasar
Each cluster on Topic 13 maps to a defined set of **Kompetensi Dasar (KD)** within Kurikulum Merdeka. Saintek items lean on KD that emphasize **penerapan konsep** rather than **perhitungan berulang**; Soshum items emphasize **interpretasi data** (tables, short infographics) rather than date recall; Humaniora items emphasize **argumen teks** rather than author biography. Knowing which KD feeds which item lets you eliminate distractors that drift outside the CP scope.

#### Edge cases and traps
The most common miss is **cluster mismatch**: candidates who pick Soshum paket but applied to a Saintek program auto-fail this section regardless of how well they answer. The second is **assumption carry-over** from UTUL UGM or SNBT — Topic 13 is *not* penalaran umum, so you cannot solve it with logic alone; you must know the term. The third is **timing**: under pressure, students skip Topic 13 expecting it to be "easy", then misread the academic passage and lose points that were obtainable by elimination.

#### Connection to adjacent topics
Topic 13 sits alongside **TPA (Topics 1–8)**, **Bahasa Indonesia (Topics 9–11)**, and **Bahasa Inggris (Topic 12)**. Strong readers outperform on Topic 13 even with average content knowledge, because the question stem is passage-based — improving your skim-and-locate speed on Topic 12 directly raises your Topic 13 ceiling.

#### Practice prompts
1. A Saintek passage describes enzyme activity changing with substrate concentration. Which term — *denaturation*, *Michaelis-Menten*, *osmosis*, *entropy* — fills the blank asking for the kinetic model name?
2. A Soshum passage shows a 2024 BPS inflation table and asks which **kebijakan fiskal** response is *kontradiktif* with the data trend.
