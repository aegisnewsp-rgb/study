# Important Indian Acts and Reforms

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

Landmark Indian Acts form the statutory backbone of banking, finance, governance, and consumer rights. SBI PO consistently tests three facts per Act: the **enactment year**, the **parent regulator or ministry**, and one **defining provision**.

- **Banking Regulation Act, 1949** — regulates banking companies; applies to co-operative banks since the 1965 amendment.
- **RBI Act, 1934** — establishes the Reserve Bank; do not confuse it with the 1949 Act.
- **SARFAESI Act, 2002** — enables secured-asset recovery for non-performing loans of ₹20 lakh and above.
- **IBC, 2016** — enacted 28 May 2016; created NCLT/NCLAT for insolvency resolution.
- **Consumer Protection Act, 2019** — replaced the 1986 Act; set up the CCPA.
- **RTI Act, 2005** — operationalised citizen's right to information.

Memorise demonetisation (8 Nov 2016), GST rollout (1 July 2017), PMJDY launch (28 Aug 2014), and the first Lokpal appointment (2019).

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### Why these Acts matter for SBI PO

The General Awareness section blends static banking law with current-affairs reforms. Roughly 1–2 questions per shift ask either the parent body (RBI, SEBI, MCA, CBDT, CBIC), the year, or the landmark feature. Questions on PMJDY, IBC, GST, SARFAESI, and demonetisation appear every cycle.

#### Core Banking and Finance Acts

| Act | Year | Parent Body | Key Provision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RBI Act | 1934 | Ministry of Finance | Establishes the Reserve Bank of India |
| Banking Regulation Act | 1949 | RBI | Defines "banking", "banking company", demand and time liabilities |
| SARFAESI | 2002 | RBI | Secured-asset enforcement for NPAs ≥ ₹20 lakh |
| PMLA | 2002 | ED under Finance Ministry | Attachment of money-laundering proceeds |
| IBC | 2016 | MCA / NCLT | Time-bound corporate insolvency resolution |
| SEBI Act | 1992 | Ministry of Finance | Regulates the securities market |

#### Pre-Independence Statutes Still in Force

- **Indian Contract Act, 1872** — governs contracts and agreements.
- **Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881** — covers promissory notes, cheques, bills of exchange (Section 138 deals with cheque dishonour).
- **Indian Evidence Act, 1872** — defines admissible evidence.
- **Indian Penal Code, 1860** — replaced by Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 from 1 July 2024.
- **CrPC, 1973** — replaced by Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023.

#### Recent Reforms Worth Tracking

- Demonetisation of ₹500 and ₹1000 notes: 8 November 2016.
- GST rollout: 1 July 2017 under the 101st Constitutional Amendment.
- PMJDY launch: 28 August 2014; MUDRA Yojana: 8 April 2015.
- Lokpal appointment: February 2019 under the Lokpal and Lokayuktas Act, 2013.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Connections to Adjacent Topics

Link each Act with its operational mechanism. SARFAESI hands secured creditors enforcement powers without court intervention; IBC funnels large corporate defaults into NCLT within a 330-day window; PMLA targets the *proceeds* of crime, while the Fugitive Economic Offenders Act, 2017 targets the *person* evading prosecution for offences worth ₹100 crore or more. Together they form a layered recovery-plus-deterrence framework that SBI PO questions often combine.

#### Common Mistakes Examiners Exploit

1. Marking IBC's year as 2017 — it received Presidential assent on 28 May 2016.
2. Stating GST commenced on 1 April 2017 — the correct date is 1 July 2017.
3. Confusing the RBI Act (1934) with the Banking Regulation Act (1949).
4. Treating PMJDY, MUDRA, and PMFBY as interchangeable financial-inclusion schemes.
5. Crediting the wrong ministry — SEBI comes under the Finance Ministry, not the Ministry of Corporate Affairs.

#### Worked Example: Multi-Act Question

> "The Act that empowers banks to take possession of secured assets without court order, enacted in 2002, is administered by which regulator?"

Answer: **SARFAESI Act, 2002, administered by the Reserve Bank of India** (with the Debts Recovery Tribunal handling appeals).

#### Practice Prompts

1. Which 2019 statute replaced the Consumer Protection Act, 1986, and what body did it create?
2. The 101st Constitutional Amendment relates to which reform, and on what date was it operationalised?
