# Topic 2

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

**Pancasila** is the state philosophy of Indonesia, proclaimed on **18 Agustus 1945** and commemorated every **1 Juni** (Hari Pancasila). Its **five sila** must be memorised in exact order: (1) **Ketuhanan Yang Maha Esa**, (2) **Kemanusiaan yang Adil dan Beradab**, (3) **Persatuan Indonesia**, (4) **Kerakyatan yang Dipimpin oleh Hikmat Kebijaksanaan dalam Permusyawaratan/Perwakilan**, (5) **Keadilan Sosial bagi Seluruh Rakyat Indonesia**.

**NKRI** (Negara Kesatuan Republik Indonesia) rests on four constitutional pillars: **Pancasila, UUD 1945, Bhinneka Tunggal Ika, NKRI** itself. The national motto **Bhinneka Tunggal Ika** ("unity in diversity") comes from the Kakawin *Sutasoma* by **Mpu Tantular**.

- National symbol: **Garuda Pancasila** (not the merah putih flag).
- Geography: archipelago of **17,000+ islands** between the **Indian and Pacific Oceans**, straddling the equator.
- Key dates: **28 Oktober** (Sumpah Pemuda), **17 Agustus** (Kemerdekaan).

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### The Five Sila and Their Sequence

The sila are not interchangeable. Indonesian civic tests treat the order as a fixed sequence, and the wording of each sila is tested verbatim. **Sila 1** establishes belief in one God; **Sila 2** translates that belief into humanitarian fairness; **Sila 3** elevates national unity above ethnic or regional identity; **Sila 4** institutionalises deliberation rather than majoritarian rule; **Sila 5** demands social justice for the entire population, including those not yet economically mobile.

#### The Four Constitutional Pillars of NKRI

| Pillar | Function | Tested As |
|---|---|---|
| Pancasila | Grundnorm / state philosophy | Foundation of all law |
| UUD 1945 | Written constitution | Supreme legal source |
| Bhinneka Tunggal Ika | National motto | Unity across suku, agama, ras |
| NKRI | Form of the state | Unitary republic |

#### Bhinneka Tunggal Ika — Origin

The phrase is a line of Old Javanese: *"Bhinneka tunggal ika, tan hana dharma mangrwa"* — different yet one, there is no dual truth. Mpu Tantular composed it in the 14th-century *Sutasoma* to teach religious tolerance in the Majapahit era, a context examiners may surface as a literature question.

#### Exam Pattern at UNDANA

General Knowledge contributes only about **3%** of the entrance score, but that 3% is low-hanging fruit: the items are factual MCQs. Expect match-the-date, identify-the-symbol, and complete-the-sila questions. UNDANA draws these items from the **Kemdikbud** civic-literacy framework, so Kemdikbud's official phrasing is the safest reference.

#### Common Traps

- The proclamation date (18 Agustus 1945) is **not** the commemorated holiday — that is **1 Juni**.
- "Five major islands" is a misconception; Indonesia has **five largest** islands (Sumatera, Jawa, Kalimantan, Sulawesi, Papua) inside an archipelago of 17,000+.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Why Sila 1 Comes First

The sequencing is ideological, not alphabetical. By placing **Ketuhanan Yang Maha Esa** at the head, the drafters signalled that Indonesian civic life flows from a theistic premise into humanitarian, communal, deliberative, and economic values. Exam questions occasionally ask *"Which sila underlies deliberation?"* — the answer is Sila 4, but the reasoning chain runs Sila 1 → Sila 4 because deliberation in the Indonesian conception presumes a moral community grounded in belief.

#### Garuda Pancasila vs. Bendera Merah Putih

Students often lose marks by selecting *"red-and-white flag"* when asked for the national symbol. **Garuda Pancasila** is the symbol (lambang negara); the **Sang Saka Merah Putih** is the flag. The shield on Garuda's chest carries the five sila in pictogram form — a star, a chain, a banyan tree, a buffalo head, and rice-and-cotton. The exam may show the shield and ask which sila the banyan tree represents (Sila 3, Persatuan Indonesia).

#### Wawasan Nusantara and Geography

Indonesia sits between the **Indian Ocean** and the **Pacific Ocean**, and between the **Asian** and **Australian** continents, with the equator cutting through Sumatra, Kalimantan, and Sulawesi. UNDANA applicants in particular benefit from knowing that **Nusa Tenggara Timur (NTT)** lies in the eastern half of the archipelago, which shapes UNDANA's regional-recruitment priorities.

#### Adjacent Topics to Cross-Study

- **UUD 1945 amendments** (especially 1945 → 2002 MPR reform) — ties into civic-literacy MCQs.
- **Sumpah Pemuda (28 Oktober 1928)** — birth of the *bangsa Indonesia* concept.
- **Trikora** and the integration of West Papua — relevant because UNDANA is a Papuan university.

#### Common Mistakes at Extended Level

- Substituting *"Ketuhanan Yang Maha Esa"* with *"Ketuhanan"* — examiners mark the full phrase as correct.
- Confusing the Garuda symbol with provincial emblems (e.g., the Bali-style *Singa Ambara Raja*).
- Assuming *"Bhineka Tunggal Ika"* is Papua New Guinean — PNG's motto is *"Unity in Diversity"* in English; the Javanese phrase belongs to Indonesia.

2. Identify the Mpu Tantular source of Bhinneka Tunggal Ika and explain in one sentence how the original context (Majapahit) relates to modern Indonesian pluralism.
