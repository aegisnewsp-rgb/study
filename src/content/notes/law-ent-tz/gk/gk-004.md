# Tanganyika's Independence and the Formation of Tanzania

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Lock these two dates first — they carry the most marks in the General Knowledge paper.

Tanganyika became independent on **9 December 1961** under Prime Minister **Julius Kambarage Nyerere**, then merged with Zanzibar on **26 April 1964** to form the **United Republic of Tanzania**. Tanganyika had been a British-administered **UN Trust Territory**, not a direct colony — that trusteeship status is the reason the 1961 transfer of power was unusually orderly and is also a frequent TLS question stem.

| Fact | Detail | Year |
|---|---|---|
| Internal self-government | Nyerere becomes PM of Tanganyika | 1 May 1961 |
| Full Uhuru (independence) | Tanganyika sovereign state | 9 December 1961 |
| Republican constitution | Nyerere becomes President | 1962 |
| Zanzibar Revolution | Sultan overthrown; ASP / Abeid Karume | 12 January 1964 |
| Articles of Union signed | Tanganyika + Zanzibar pact | 22 April 1964 |
| United Republic formed | "Tanzania" officially created | 26 April 1964 |

> 💡 **High-Yield Memory Hook — "May-Dec-Jan-April-April":** May 1961 (self-gov) → December 1961 (Uhuru) → January 1964 (Zanzibar Revolution) → 22 April 1964 (Articles) → 26 April 1964 (Tanzania born). Five dates, one rhythm: 5-12-1-22-26. Recite the five numbers in order before walking into the exam hall.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)

#### The Trusteeship Background
After World War II, Tanganyika was placed under British administration as a **UN Trust Territory** supervised by the **Trusteeship Council** — a legal status distinct from a Crown Colony. The UN framework required Britain to prepare the territory for self-rule, which produced a structured handover rather than a contested one. The **Tanganyika African National Union (TANU)**, founded in 1954, became the dominant nationalist party and swept the 1958–1960 legislative elections.

On **1 May 1961** Tanganyika gained internal self-government with Nyerere as Prime Minister under a dominion-style constitution. Full sovereignty — Uhuru — followed on **9 December 1961**, confirmed by the UK Parliament and recorded in the US Department of State foreign-relations series as a recognized transfer of authority.

#### The Zanzibar Side of the Union
Zanzibar was a **separate British protectorate** ruled by the Sultan. The **Zanzibar Revolution of 12 January 1964** overthrew the Sultan, and the **Afro-Shirazi Party (ASP)** under **Abeid Karume** established the People's Republic of Zanzibar. Karume's government then negotiated a union with Nyerere's Tanganyika.

The **Articles of Union were signed on 22 April 1964**, and the union took legal effect on **26 April 1964**, creating the United Republic of Tanzania. Under the deal, Zanzibar retained semi-autonomy: its own **Revolutionary Council**, a separate parliament (the House of Representatives), and a separate judicial structure.

#### Comparison Matrix — Easily Mixed-Up Facts

| Common Confusion | Correct Distinction |
|---|---|
| Tanganyika was a Crown Colony | It was a **UN Trust Territory**, not a Crown Colony |
| Nyerere was President at Uhuru | He was **Prime Minister** at independence; President came with the 1962 Republican constitution |
| Zanzibar joined Tanganyika voluntarily and peacefully | It joined after a **revolution** (12 Jan 1964) overthrew the Sultan |
| Tanzania's name existed before 1964 | The name **"Tanzania"** is a 1964 portmanteau of *Tan*ganyika + *Zan*zibar |
| Tanganyika and Zanzibar were one territory | They were **two separate British-administered units** until the 1964 union |
| Nyerere led the Zanzibar Revolution | **Abeid Karume / ASP** led it; Nyerere was Tanganyika's leader |

#### 🎯 Exam-Level Worked Problem

**Question:** *"On what date was the United Republic of Tanzania officially formed, and which two territories united to create it?"*

**Step-by-step working.** The United Republic of Tanzania was officially formed on **26 April 1964**, when the Articles of Union between Tanganyika and Zanzibar (already signed on 22 April 1964) came into effect. The two uniting territories were the **sovereign Republic of Tanganyika** (independent since 9 December 1961 under Nyerere) and the **People's Republic of Zanzibar** (established 12 January 1964 after the revolution that overthrew the Sultan, led by the Afro-Shirazi Party under Abeid Karume). The Articles were negotiated between Nyerere and Karume; Julius Nyerere became the first President of the unified state, while Karume became First Vice-President with Zanzibar retaining its own Revolutionary Council.

> ⚠️ **Examiner Trap:** the question deliberately omits the year — many candidates write "1961" because that is the date they remember for "independence." That is Tanganyika's independence, not Tanzania's formation. The two events are separated by nearly three years.

#### Quick-Reference List — Must-Know Names
- **Julius Kambarage Nyerere** — first PM (1961), first President of Tanzania (1964).
- **Abeid Karume** — leader of the Zanzibar Revolution; First Vice-President.
- **TANU** — Tanganyika African National Union, the independence movement.
- **ASP** — Afro-Shirazi Party, the Zanzibar counterpart.
- **UN Trusteeship Council** — the UN body that supervised the handover.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)

#### Edge Cases and Constitutional Nuance
Three points test the depth of a candidate's understanding and frequently catch students who only memorise dates:

1. **Internal self-government vs independence.** On 1 May 1961 Tanganyika became internally self-governing but the Queen remained head of state, with a Governor-General acting on her behalf. Full sovereignty — including the right to conduct foreign policy and leave the Commonwealth on its own terms — was acquired only on **9 December 1961**. A TLS question worded as "When did Tanganyika become *fully independent*?" expects December, not May.

2. **Two Nyerere presidencies.** Nyerere held the presidency twice: first under the **1962 Republican constitution** (after Tanganyika became a republic within the Commonwealth), and again from **26 April 1964** as the first President of the United Republic. Some reference texts compress these into one; examiners will not.

3. **Zanzibar's retained powers.** The union was asymmetric. Zanzibar kept its **own government, legislature and courts**, and the Articles of Union carved out specific areas — including some residual foreign-affairs representation — where Zanzibar's voice is constitutionally distinct. This is why Zanzibar remains a recurring political topic in Tanzania's news and why TLS questions sometimes test whether the student knows that "union" did not mean absorption.

#### Advanced Practice Prompts
1. **Compare-and-contrast question.** *"Compare Tanganyika's constitutional status before 1961 with Zanzibar's. Why did the two territories follow different constitutional paths into the United Republic?"* — answer should distinguish UN Trust Territory (Tanganyika) from protectorate (Zanzibar) and link each to the post-1964 union structure.

2. **Cause-and-effect question.** *"Explain how the Zanzibar Revolution of January 1964 made the formation of Tanzania possible by April of the same year."* — answer should walk through: Sultan overthrown → ASP-led republic → Karume's government sought continental solidarity and protection from rival political factions → union with Nyerere's Tanganyika negotiated and signed within three months.

> 📌 **Trap worth flagging in revision:** Zanzibar's protectorate status is routinely written off as "a colony." It was not. The British held it under a separate treaty arrangement, which is part of why the 1964 union retained so much Zanzibari autonomy.
