# Akhlaq — Islah Dhawat al-Bayn and al-Shura — Qatar Secondary Certificate Islamic Studies Notes

The MoEHE Grade 12 Islamic Studies Term 2 Akhlaq (Ethics, الأخلاق) domain covers "Islah Dhawat al-Bayn" (reconciliation between people, إصلاح ذات البين) and "al-Shura wa-qaymatuha" (consultation and its values, الشورى وقيمتها). These are the ethical modules of the Term 2 curriculum.

### Lite — Quick Review (1h-1d)

#### Core facts in one pass

- **Islah Dhawat al-Bayn:** reconciliation between people — restoring relations between Muslims who are in dispute. A duty of every Muslim, not just scholars or judges.
- **Hadith basis:** "ألا أخبركم بأفضل من درجة الصلاة والصيام والصدقة؟ إصلاح ذات البين، فإن فساد ذات البين هي الحالة" — Shall I not tell you of what is better than prayer, fasting, and charity? Reconciliation between people, for the corruption of relations is the destructive one (Abu Dawud, al-Tirmidhi).
- **Al-Shura:** consultation — the Quranic principle of mutual decision-making in the Muslim community. Surah Ash-Shura 42:38 — " وأمرهم شورى بينهم " — their affairs are decided by mutual consultation.
- **Scope of shura:** governance, family decisions, community matters, and dispute resolution.

#### Examiner traps

- Reducing islah to a "feel-good" activity — it is a religious obligation with specific rules.
- Confusing shura with democracy — shura is consultation grounded in the Sharia; democracy is majority rule without a religious anchor.

### Standard — Regular Study (2d-2mo)

#### Islah Dhawat al-Bayn — the duty of reconciliation

The Quran commands reconciliation: " وإن طائفتان من المؤمنين اقتتلوا فأصلحوا بينهما " (Al-Hujurat 49:9) — if two parties of believers fight, make peace between them. The verse establishes reconciliation as a community obligation (fard kifayah).

The Prophetic tradition reinforces the duty. The hadith "الصلح خير" (reconciliation is best, Sahih al-Bukhari 1357) sets the principle that disputes should be resolved through dialogue, not through escalation.

Steps of islah:

1. Listen to both sides without prejudice.
2. Identify the root cause (often misunderstanding or pride).
3. Propose a solution grounded in fairness and the Sharia.
4. Get agreement from both parties.
5. Follow up to ensure the reconciliation is maintained.

The role of the islah mediator: a person of wisdom, knowledge of the Sharia, and the trust of the community. The classical scholars noted that the mediator must be acceptable to both parties.

#### Quranic verses on shura

Surah Ash-Shura 42:38 — " والذين استجابوا لربهم وأقاموا الصلاة وأمرهم شورى بينهم ومما رزقناهم ينفقون " — those who respond to their Lord, establish prayer, whose affairs are decided by mutual consultation, and who spend of what We have provided them.

Surah Al-Imran 3:159 — " فبما رحمة من الله لنت لهم ولو كنت فظا غليظ القلب لانفضوا من حولك فاعف عنهم واستغفر لهم وشاورهم في الأمر " — by the mercy of Allah you were gentle with them; had you been harsh, they would have dispersed. So pardon them, consult them in affairs, and rely on Allah.

The Quranic basis establishes shura as (1) a community obligation, (2) a Prophetic practice, and (3) a foundation for Islamic governance.

#### Scope and limits of shura

**In governance:** the ruler consults the scholars, the commanders, and the notables on major decisions (war, peace, taxation, appointment). The decision is the ruler's, but it must be informed by consultation.

**In family:** parents consult each other and, at appropriate ages, the children on family decisions.

**In community:** the mosque, the school, the workplace — consultation is the mode of decision-making.

**Limits:** shura cannot override a clear Quranic ruling or a definitive Prophetic hadith. Consultation is on the means and the application, not the principles.

#### Application in contemporary context

Umar ibn Abd al-Khattab's shura committee (the six who selected the third caliph) is the classical model. The shura principle is reflected in modern constitutions across the Muslim world — the Shura Council in Saudi Arabia, the Majlis in Iran, the parliament in many Muslim-majority countries.

### Deep — Long-Term Mastery (1mo-6mo)

#### Why islah and shura carry marks at the MoEHE terminal

The MoEHE Akhlaq domain (Term 2) connects the ethical teachings of Islam to the social order — islah as the repair of disputes, shura as the mode of decision-making. These modules equip the Grade 12 student with the ethical tools for community life.

#### Exam technique

- For islah questions, cite the Quranic verse (49:9) and the Prophetic hadith, then list the steps of reconciliation.
- For shura questions, distinguish shura from democracy and cite the Quranic basis (42:38, 3:159).

#### Common misconceptions (and the correction)

- "Shura is the same as democracy." No — shura is consultation within the Sharia; democracy is majority rule.
- "Islah is just mediation." Islah is a religious obligation with specific Quranic and Prophetic basis.
