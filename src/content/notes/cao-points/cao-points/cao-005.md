# Matriculation Minimum Requirements

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your CAO application.

**Matriculation Minimum Requirements** — Quick Facts for Irish Leaving Certificate students

**What is matriculation?**
Matriculation is the **separate eligibility gate** that a candidate must pass before CAO looks at their points aggregate. Even with 625 points, a candidate who fails matriculation cannot receive an offer for that course.

**Standard matriculation minimum (most Level 7/8 courses):**
- **English:** O6/H7 (Ordinary Level grade 6 or Higher Level grade 7 or above)
- **Mathematics:** O6/H7 (or F6 pass in Foundation Maths for some courses)
- **Two other subjects:** O6/H7 in two additional Leaving Certificate subjects
- **Course-specific:** each course can add its own requirements (e.g., H6 in Chemistry for science programmes)

**At a glance:**
- Application order: matriculation check FIRST, then aggregate cut-off check
- Pass matriculation = eligible for that course regardless of aggregate
- Fail matriculation = ineligible, regardless of aggregate (even 625 points)
- Course-specific: many courses publish additional requirements beyond the standard 4 subjects
- Language requirement: some courses require a third language (typically a modern European language)

**Why it matters for offers:**
Many applicants focus exclusively on the aggregate and miss a course-specific matriculation requirement that blocks their offer. Always read the course handbook for your specific course's matriculation minimum AND English list.

⚡ **Quick rule:** Check the **matriculation requirements** for every course you list on your CAO application — not the aggregate alone. A course that requires H6 in Chemistry will reject you if your Chemistry is below H6, regardless of your other 5 H1 grades.

**Matriculation Minimum Requirements** — CAO Points Study Guide

**What is the matriculation minimum?**
The matriculation minimum is a set of **separate subject-and-grade requirements** that a candidate must satisfy in addition to meeting the aggregate cut-off. The matriculation is checked independently and is NOT a function of your six-best-subjects aggregate.

For example, a candidate with 580 points who fails the matriculation (e.g., scored H8 in English) is NOT eligible for a course that requires O6/H7 in English, even though their aggregate exceeds the 450 cut-off. Conversely, a candidate with 380 points who meets ALL matriculation requirements (O6 in English, O6 in Maths, O6 in two other subjects, plus course-specific) IS eligible for a course with a 400 cut-off... wait, no — they still need to meet the aggregate. The point is: matriculation is a NECESSARY but not SUFFICIENT condition for an offer.

**The standard matriculation minimum:**
Most Level 7 (ordinary bachelor degree) and Level 8 (honours bachelor degree) courses publish a **standard matriculation minimum**:

- **English:** O6/H7 (or higher) — Ordinary Level grade 6 or Higher Level grade 7 or above
- **Mathematics:** O6/H7 (or higher) — Ordinary Level grade 6 or Higher Level grade 7 or above; some courses accept Foundation Maths F6 pass as an alternative
- **Two other subjects:** O6/H7 in two additional Leaving Certificate subjects (typically at OL or HL level)
- **Irish (Gaeilge):** O6/H7 in some courses (especially Irish-medium programmes and education degrees)
- **A modern European language:** O6/H7 in French / German / Spanish / Italian for some courses (especially arts and humanities)
- **Course-specific:** each course can add its own subject and grade requirements

The standard minimum is published by each higher education institution in its prospectus and on the CAO website (cao.ie/courses). The exact requirements vary by institution and programme.

**Why foundations differ between O6, H7, and Foundation Maths:**
The grades O6 (Ordinary Level grade 6) and H7 (Higher Level grade 7) are equivalent in CPS terms:
- O6 = 23 points
- H7 = 0 points (HL grade 7)

Wait — that doesn't match the typical matriculation minimum. Let me restate: O6 and H7 are PASS grades at the respective levels. H7 is the minimum pass at HL; O6 is the minimum pass at OL.

- O6 in Ordinary Level Maths = pass at OL with grade 6 or above (CPS value: 23-56)
- H7 in Higher Level Maths = pass at HL with grade 7 (the lowest HL pass grade; CPS value: 0)

The standard matriculation requirement for Maths is typically **O6/H7** meaning either:
- O6 or above at OL, OR
- H7 or above at HL

Some courses use a different notation:
- "O6 in Maths" = exactly O6 at OL (not H7)
- "H6 in Maths" = exactly H6 at HL
- "Pass in Foundation Maths" = F1/F2/F3 (a pass at Foundation level)

Always read the specific requirement for your course.

**Course-specific matriculation requirements:**
Many courses publish additional requirements beyond the standard 4 subjects. Common course-specific requirements include:

| Course type | Common additional matriculation requirement |
|---|---|
| Engineering | O6/H7 in Maths (often HL preferred) + O6/H7 in English + O6/H7 in one science |
| Science | O6/H7 in Maths + O6/H7 in English + O6/H7 in one science |
| Computing | O6/H7 in Maths (often HL preferred) + O6/H7 in English |
| Medicine | H6 in Chemistry + H6 in Biology (or Physics) + H6 in Maths + H6 in English (or Irish) |
| Nursing | O6/H7 in Maths + O6/H7 in English + O6/H7 in one science (Biology/Chemistry/Physics) |
| Pharmacy | H6 in Chemistry + H6 in Biology + H6 in Maths + H6 in English |
| Veterinary | H6 in Chemistry + H6 in Biology (or Physics) + H6 in Maths + H6 in English |
| Teaching (Primary) | O6/H7 in Maths + O6/H7 in English + O6/H7 in Irish (if teaching through Irish) |
| Teaching (Secondary) | H6 in the subject you plan to teach + O6/H7 in Maths + O6/H7 in English |
| Arts / Humanities | Standard 4-subject minimum (English + Maths + 2 others); language requirement varies |
| Business / Commerce | Standard 4-subject minimum (English + Maths + 2 others); some require H6 in Maths |

The specific requirements for your course are published in:
- The CAO handbook entry for the course (cao.ie/handbook)
- The higher education institution's prospectus (e.g., UCD, Trinity, UCC, UL, NUIG, Maynooth, DCU, TU Dublin)
- The university's own course webpage

**Worked example 1:** Candidate wants Engineering at UCD. Course requirements:
- O6/H6 in Maths (HL preferred, but OL accepted)
- O6/H7 in English
- O6/H7 in one of Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Applied Maths, or Engineering
- O6/H7 in one other subject

If candidate has H6 in Maths, H7 in English, H6 in Physics, H7 in Irish, H5 in Chemistry = 5 subjects at O6/H7 or above. Matriculation met. Aggregate check is next.

**Worked example 2:** Candidate wants Medicine at RCSI. Course requirements:
- H6 in Chemistry
- H6 in Biology OR Physics
- H6 in Maths (third language can substitute)
- H6 in English OR Irish
- Plus the standard 4-subject minimum

If candidate has H5 in Chemistry (satisfied), H6 in Biology (satisfied), H6 in Maths (satisfied), H7 in English (satisfied). Matriculation met. Aggregate check is next.

**Worked example 3:** Candidate wants Arts at Trinity. Course requirements:
- O6/H7 in English
- O6/H7 in Maths (or Foundation Maths pass)
- O6/H7 in two other subjects (typically a language + one more)
- A third language is recommended but not required

If candidate has O1 in English, O2 in Maths, O2 in Irish, O1 in French, O1 in History. Matriculation met. Aggregate check is next.

**How matriculation interacts with the aggregate:**
The order of evaluation for a CAO offer is:

1. **Matriculation check:** Does the candidate meet all subject-and-grade minimums for the course?
2. **Aggregate check:** Does the candidate's aggregate (six-best + 25 bonus) meet or exceed the course's published cut-off?
3. **Random number allocation:** If the candidate is tied with others at the cut-off, the random number breaks the tie (see cao-006).

A candidate who fails step 1 is NOT considered for an offer, regardless of their aggregate. A candidate who passes step 1 but fails step 2 is NOT considered for an offer because their aggregate is too low.

**The O6 vs H6 distinction in matriculation:**
Some courses specify O6 in Maths while others specify H6. The distinction matters:

- O6 = Ordinary Level grade 6 minimum (e.g., a student who took OL Maths and achieved O6 satisfies O6)
- H6 = Higher Level grade 6 minimum (e.g., a student who took HL Maths and achieved H6 satisfies H6)

A student who took OL Maths and achieved O1 (56 points) does NOT satisfy an H6 requirement, because H6 means HL grade. Always check whether the requirement is O6 (any level) or H6 (HL only).

The combined notation "O6/H6" means either O6 or H6 — i.e., the requirement is satisfied by either OL or HL grade 6 or above.

**The language requirement:**
Some courses require a third language, typically a modern European language (French, German, Spanish, Italian) at O6/H7 or above. The language requirement is more common in:
- Arts / Humanities programmes (often required)
- Social Science programmes (sometimes required)
- Education programmes (often required for primary teaching)

The language requirement is less common in:
- STEM programmes (rarely required)
- Business / Commerce programmes (sometimes required for international programmes)
- Health Science programmes (rarely required)

**Foundation Maths and matriculation:**
Foundation Maths (F1, F2, F3) is accepted as the Maths matriculation by SOME Level 7 and Level 8 courses. The typical language is:

- "O6/H7 in Mathematics (or F6 in Foundation Mathematics)" — accepts either OL Maths O6 or HL Maths H7 or Foundation Maths pass
- "Pass in Foundation Maths may be acceptable for some courses" — course-by-course check

Not all courses accept Foundation Maths. Engineering, science, and many health-science courses typically require OL/HL Maths and do NOT accept Foundation Maths. Always check the specific course requirements.

**Frequently asked questions:**
- "Can I use my six-best aggregate to satisfy the matriculation minimum?" — No. The matriculation is a separate subject-specific check. Your aggregate uses your six-best CPS, but the matriculation requires specific subjects at specific grades. A candidate whose six-best includes English, Maths, Irish, French, Biology, and Chemistry has aggregate-grade material, but the matriculation requires specific grades in English and Maths.
- "What happens if my grade is one below the requirement?" — You fail the matriculation for that course. CAO does NOT round up, apply waivers, or accept "nearly passing." The minimum is a pass grade.
- "Can I substitute a different subject for the matriculation?" — Only if the course handbook explicitly allows it. Most courses are strict about the specific subject and grade. Some courses allow substitutions (e.g., H6 in Physics OR Biology for a science programme).
- "Does my +25 HL Maths bonus affect matriculation?" — No. The bonus affects the aggregate, not the matriculation. The matriculation is a separate check.
- "Does my LCVP link modules count for matriculation?" — Generally no. LCVP does not count as a matriculation subject in most courses. Check your specific course requirements.

**When the matriculation minimum was introduced:**
The current matriculation minimum framework was introduced in the 1990s and has been refined over time. The specific requirements vary by institution and course. Always check the current CAO handbook for your course, as requirements can change from year to year.

**The matriculation role for non-standard applicants:**
- **Mature students:** Have separate matriculation requirements (typically a relevant qualification or experience)
- **QQI FET applicants:** Have a separate matriculation via the QQI FET framework
- **International applicants:** Have separate matriculation requirements per their qualification (GCE A-Levels, GCSEs, etc.)
- **HEAR / DARE applicants:** May have additional supports but the standard matriculation still applies

### 💡 Pro Tips
- Check the **matriculation requirements** for every course you list on your CAO application — not the aggregate alone
- A course that requires H6 in Chemistry will reject you if your Chemistry is below H6, regardless of your other 5 H1 grades
- Read the COURSE-SPECIFIC requirements (H6 in Chemistry for medicine, H6 in Biology for pharmacy, etc.) — these are stricter than the standard 4-subject minimum
- The matriculation is checked BEFORE the aggregate — if you fail matriculation, your aggregate is irrelevant
- Foundation Maths satisfies the matriculation for SOME courses but not all — check the specific course handbook
- The matriculation minimum does NOT move on the +25 HL Maths bonus — the bonus is aggregate-only
- For non-standard applicants (mature, QQI FET, international, HEAR/DARE), the matriculation is handled separately through the relevant pathway
