# Banking Products and Services

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

**Banking Products and Services** are the financial instruments — deposits, loans, payment systems, and investment products — offered by Scheduled Commercial Banks in India under RBI regulation (Banking Regulation Act, 1949). IBPS Clerk Mains typically asks 3–5 questions from this area.

- **Deposits:** Savings (interest ~3–4%, transaction limits), Current (no interest, for businesses), Fixed Deposit (locked tenor, premature penalty), Recurring Deposit (monthly instalments).
- **Payment Systems:** NEFT (batch, no upper cap, 24×7), RTGS (real-time, min ₹2 lakh), IMPS (instant, up to ₹5 lakh), UPI (interoperable, NPCI).
- **Regulatory levers:** CRR (cash with RBI), SLR (govt securities/gold), Repo Rate (RBI lends to banks), Reverse Repo Rate (RBI borrows from banks).
- **Key schemes:** Jan Dhan Yojana (zero-balance accounts), MUDRA (Shishu ≤₹50k / Kishore ≤₹5 lakh / Tarun ≤₹10 lakh), e-RUPI (purpose-specific digital voucher).

> 💡 **Mnemonic:** "**C**ash **R**eserve, **S**tatic **L**iquid" — CRR is cash, SLR is securities.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### Deposit Products

Banks mobilise funds through deposit accounts governed by RBI's interest rate directives. **Savings Accounts** serve individuals with no cheque-book restrictions on number but RBI limits free transactions to 3–5 per month in some bank variants. **Current Accounts** serve businesses — no interest is paid, unlimited over-the-counter withdrawals, and higher minimum balance requirements.

**Fixed Deposits** lock funds for a chosen tenor (7 days to 10 years) at a contracted rate; premature withdrawal attracts a penalty of 1% (for deposits below ₹2 lakh on some bank variants per RBI directive of June 2023). **Recurring Deposts** accept monthly instalments and pay out the corpus with interest at maturity.

#### Loan Products

Loans are priced off the **Repo Rate** (RBI's policy rate at which it lends short-term funds to banks under the Liquidity Adjustment Facility) plus a spread, or linked to **MCLR** (Marginal Cost of Funds-based Lending Rate). Secured loans (home, vehicle, gold) are collateral-backed; unsecured loans (personal, education) carry higher rates. NPA classification kicks in at 90+ days overdue.

#### Payment and Settlement Systems

| System | Settlement | Min Amount | Max Amount | Hours |
|--------|-----------|------------|------------|-------|
| NEFT | Half-hourly batches | ₹1 | No cap | 24×7 |
| RTGS | Real-time gross | ₹2,00,000 | No cap | 24×7 |
| IMPS | Instant | ₹1 | ₹5,00,000 | 24×7 |
| UPI | Instant | ₹1 | ₹1,00,000* | 24×7 |

*UPI per-transaction cap revised upward for select P2M categories.

#### Exam Patterns

Questions appear as direct recall (NRE vs NRO repatriability, DICGC ₹5 lakh limit) or as one-line assertions. Recent weightage: 3–5 questions in IBPS Clerk Mains GA, mostly in the 2022–25 papers.

> ⚠️ **Trap:** NEFT has *no upper limit* — students confuse it with IMPS's ₹5 lakh cap.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### NRI Account Distinctions

**NRE (Non-Resident External)** holds repatriable foreign currency earnings; interest is fully tax-exempt in India but principal and interest are freely repatriable. **NRO (Non-Resident Ordinary)** holds Indian income (rent, dividends); interest is taxable at 30% plus surcharge/cess, and repatriation is capped at USD 1 million per financial year. **FCNR(B)** is a foreign currency term deposit — no exchange-rate risk because both principal and interest are paid in the same foreign currency.

#### Priority Sector Lending (PSL)

RBI mandates that 40% of **ANBC** (Adjusted Net Bank Credit) or Credit Equivalent of Off-Balance Sheet Exposure, whichever is higher, be deployed under PSL. The eight weaker sections include small/marginal farmers, artisans, and SC/ST borrowers. Targets within PSL:

- Agriculture: 18% (13.5% direct + 4.5% indirect)
- Micro Enterprises (manufacturing ≤₹1 cr service ≤₹5 cr investment)
- Education loans up to ₹10 lakh
- Housing loans up to ₹35 lakh in metros, ₹25 lakh elsewhere

#### Digital Lending and Recent RBI Norms

The **Digital Lending Guidelines (September 2022)** mandate that all loan disbursals and repayments flow directly between the borrower and the regulated lender's bank account — no pass-through to third-party FLDG (First Loss Default Guarantee) arrangements. **e-RUPI**, launched in 2021, is a purpose-specific digital voucher redeemable without a bank app, used in government welfare delivery.

#### Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Believing DICGC insures only deposits — it covers principal **and** interest up to ₹5,00,000 per depositor per bank (commercial + cooperative separately).
- Confusing MCLR with Base Rate — Base Rate was replaced by MCLR from April 2016.
- Treating Reverse Repo as a deposit scheme — it is the rate at which RBI *absorbs* surplus liquidity from banks.

#### Practice Prompts

1. A non-resident wants to deposit foreign currency earnings, needs full repatriation of both principal and interest, and wants zero Indian tax on interest. Which account fits — NRE, NRO, or FCNR(B)? Justify.
2. State the mandatory PSL percentage, name two of the eight weaker sections, and compute the agriculture sub-target split (direct vs indirect).
