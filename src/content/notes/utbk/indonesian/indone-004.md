# Topic 4

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

**Topic 4** of Literasi Bahasa Indonesia tests higher-order reading comprehension on academic, argumentative, and informational passages of roughly 200–500 kata. The non-negotiable skill is locating the **ide pokok** of each paragraph and the whole text, then layering inference on top.

- **Ide pokok** is the kalimat inti that can stand alone; supporting sentences contain fakta, contoh, angka, or reasoning that prove or illustrate it.
- **Struktur teks** signals tell you where the main idea sits: deduktif (general → specific), induktif (specific → general), or campuran.
- **Makna tersurat** is printed in the text; **makna tersirat** is what the writer implies through tone, word choice, or omission — always ground it in a specific sentence.
- **Fakta vs opini**: a fakta is verifiable, an opini uses value-laden markers like "sebaiknya", "harus", or comparative-superlative judgments.
- **Inferensi** combines two explicit clues to reach a third, unstated point. **Simpulan** is the text-wide conclusion, not a paragraph-level restatement.

> Trap: selecting the longest sentence as ide pokok — length does not equal centrality.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### Reading the passage architecture
Every UTBK/SNPMTN Literasi passage opens with a **tujuan komunikatif** cue in the first paragraph: "Tujuan artikel ini adalah…", "Berikut ini akan diuraikan…", or a definitional frame. Identify it before chasing detail, because every later paragraph must justify it.

#### Locating ide pokok accurately
Use the **kohesi** markers — *pertama, selain itu, dengan demikian, oleh karena itu* — to track how ideas accumulate. The ide pokok is usually:

| Position in paragraph | Signal words (Indonesian) | Test |
|---|---|---|
| Opening (deduktif) | "Pada dasarnya…", "Menurut X…" | Can the rest of the paragraph be deleted without losing meaning? |
| Closing (induktif) | "Jadi…", "Oleh sebab itu…", "Dengan demikian…" | Does the final sentence absorb the prior examples? |
| Embedded | No explicit marker | Look for the sentence that other sentences reference back to. |

#### Makna tersurat vs makna tersirat
**Makna tersurat** answers questions beginning with *apa, siapa, kapan, di mana* using information stated verbatim. **Makna tersirat** answers *mengapa, bagaimana perasaan, apa tujuan* by reading between sentences.

> A correct inference always cites two textual anchors; a wrong one invents information the passage never supplies.

#### Inferensi and simpulan
**Inferensi** is paragraph-scoped. **Simpulan** is passage-scoped and must reconcile every paragraph — if one section argues *for* and another *against*, the simpulan captures the tension, not one side.

#### Exam traps specific to LTBB
- Conflating **opini** (the author's stance) with **fakta** (cited evidence).
- Misreading **kata rujukan** like *hal ini, hal tersebut, ia, mereka* — trace each pronoun to its nearest plausible antecedent, not the most recent noun.
- Treating the soal "ide pokok paragraf ke-3" as a whole-text question; stay scoped.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Discourse markers as diagnostic tools
Indonesian connectors are not interchangeable; they predict the logical relation you will be asked about.

| Kata hubung | Logical relation | Typical soal phrasing |
|---|---|---|
| *karena, sebab, akibatnya* | Sebab-akibat | "Apa yang menyebabkan…?" |
| *meskipun, namun, akan tetapi* | Kontras / konsesi | "Perbedaan utama antara X dan Y…" |
| *agar, supaya, untuk* | Tujuan | "Tindakan X dilakukan dengan tujuan…" |
| *jika, apabila, asalkan* | Syarat | "Pernyataan yang benar jika…" |
| *selain itu, di samping itu* | Tambahan | "Informasi yang tidak termasuk dalam teks…" |

When a connector shifts mid-paragraph (e.g., from *karena* to *namun*), the paragraph is almost certainly **campuran**, and the ide pokok will sit at the structural pivot.

#### Worked micro-example
Suppose a 350-kata editorial passage argues that micro-credentialing (sertifikasi mikro) risks fragmenting the national curriculum. Paragraph 1 defines micro-credentialing; Paragraph 2 cites three university pilots; Paragraph 3 quotes a critic (*"akan melemahkan integritas pendidikan"*), then closes with *akan tetapi, jika dirancang bersama pemangku kebijakan, fragmentasi dapat diminimalkan*.

- **Ide pokok paragraf 3**: desain kolaboratif dapat menekan risiko fragmentasi kurikulum. The opposing quote is supporting evidence, not the main claim.
- **Inferensi valid**: penulis menerima premis kritikus tetapi menolak fatalisme. Anchor 1 = *risiko fragmentasi*; Anchor 2 = *jika dirancang bersama*.
- **Simpulan teks**: micro-credentialing layak diuji dengan desain kebijakan kolaboratif, bukan ditolak seluruhnya.

#### Adjacent topics and AKM framing
This topic shares inference machinery with **penalaran teks** in UTBK Saintek and with the **literasi numerasi** reading sub-component. Treat the same passage-mapping skill (tujuan → struktur → bukti → simpulan) as transferable.

#### Practice prompts
1. A 400-kata teks eksposisi opens with *Tujuan penulisan artikel ini adalah…* then lists three solusi. One paragraph later shifts to *kritik terhadap solusi kedua*. What is the ide pokok of that critical paragraph, and why is the longest sentence in it not the answer?
2. A discussion passage presents dua sisi equally. State one inferensi about the author's posisi, naming the two explicit anchors you used.
