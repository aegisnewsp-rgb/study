# CEFR B2 Communicative Competence — Qatar Secondary Certificate English Notes

The MoEHE English Curriculum Standards publish the Grade 12 exit level as CEFR B2 Low-Mid. The CEFR (Common European Framework of Reference for Languages) describes B2 as "upper intermediate" or "vantage" — the learner can understand the main ideas of complex text, interact with a degree of fluency, and produce clear, detailed text on a wide range of subjects. The MoEHE standards align to the CEFR's three components of communicative competence: linguistic, pragmatic, and sociolinguistic.

### Lite — Quick Review (1h-1d)

#### Core facts in one pass

- **Linguistic competence:** range and accuracy of vocabulary, grammar, pronunciation.
- **Pragmatic competence:** using language to achieve communicative goals (persuading, agreeing, refusing, requesting).
- **Sociolinguistic competence:** register, politeness, formality, cultural appropriateness.
- **B2 listening:** understand extended speech, follow complex argument, recognise speaker's viewpoint.
- **B2 reading:** read articles, contemporary literature, technical instructions.
- **B2 speaking:** interact with fluency, present clear descriptions, argue a point.
- **B2 writing:** clear, detailed text on a wide range of subjects; essays highlighting personal significance.

#### Examiner traps

- Confusing fluency with accuracy — fluency is flow; accuracy is correctness. B2 requires both.
- Speaking only in short sentences — B2 expects sustained, developed responses.
- Limiting vocabulary to high-frequency words — B2 requires range.

### Standard — Regular Study (2d-2mo)

#### Linguistic competence

Vocabulary range: 5,000-6,000 words (B1: 2,500-3,250, B2: 5,000-6,000, C1: 8,000-9,000). Academic Word List (AWL) covers the most common academic vocabulary; the General Service List (GSL) covers the most common general vocabulary.

Grammar range: simple and complex sentences, conditionals, modals, passive voice, reported speech, relative clauses, gerunds and infinitives. Error rate stays below the threshold that interferes with communication.

Pronunciation: clear articulation, appropriate stress and intonation, intelligible at natural speed even when discussing abstract topics.

#### Pragmatic competence

Speech acts: requesting (Could you tell me...?), refusing (I'm afraid I can't, but...), agreeing (I completely agree...), disagreeing (I see your point, but...), persuading (The key issue is...), suggesting (Have you considered...?).

Discourse competence: organising ideas logically, signalling structure (firstly, on the other hand, in conclusion), maintaining topic, taking turns in conversation.

Functional competence: describing, narrating, comparing, expressing opinion, hypothesising, evaluating, summarising.

#### Sociolinguistic competence

Register: formal (essay, presentation, interview), neutral (classroom discussion, email to teacher), informal (chat with friends, social media).

Politeness markers: indirect requests (Would you mind if I...), hedging (perhaps, it seems, I think), deference (please, thank you, excuse me).

Cultural awareness: idioms, proverbs, references that are specific to the language and culture. At B2, the learner understands common cultural references and uses them appropriately.

#### B2 descriptors in the four skills

Listening: "I can understand extended speech and lectures and follow even complex lines of argument provided the topic is reasonably familiar."

Reading: "I can read articles and reports concerned with contemporary problems in which the writers adopt particular attitudes or viewpoints."

Speaking: "I can interact with a degree of fluency and spontaneity that makes regular interaction with native speakers quite possible."

Writing: "I can write clear, detailed text on a wide range of subjects related to my interests."

### Deep — Long-Term Mastery (1mo-6mo)

#### Why B2 is the exit target

The MoEHE curriculum standards align the Grade 12 exit to CEFR B2 Low-Mid. This is the level required for undergraduate study at most international universities (the Education City branch campuses typically require IELTS 6.0-6.5, which corresponds to B2). The competency framework is drawn from the Qatar National Curriculum Framework (Supreme Education Council, 2016).

#### Exam technique

- For listening, read questions first and listen for the keywords, not for every word.
- For speaking, plan a 60-second monologue on a familiar topic with three points and an example for each.
- For writing, write a thesis-driven essay with clear paragraphing and supporting evidence.
- For reading, practice under timed conditions — B2 requires both comprehension and speed.

#### Common misconceptions (and the correction)

- "B2 = native speaker." No — B2 is upper intermediate. Native speakers typically operate at C2.
- "B2 = perfect grammar." No — B2 allows for occasional errors that do not impede communication.
