# Science & Technology

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your SPSC exam.

Science & Technology for SPSC covers Pakistani scientific organisations, milestones in space, nuclear and defence technology, and current IT awareness. Most questions are one-line MCQs testing recall of names, years and acronyms.

#### Must-Know Facts

- **SUPARCO** — Pakistan Space and Upper Atmosphere Research Commission, established **1981**; launched **Badr-I in 1990** (first indigenous satellite); operates **PRSS-1** (remote sensing, launched 2018 from China).
- **PAEC** — Pakistan Atomic Energy Commission, founded **1956**; oversees peaceful nuclear use in energy, agriculture, and medicine; reports to the **National Command Authority (NCA)**.
- **PNRA** — Pakistan Nuclear Regulatory Authority; the *safety regulator*, distinct from PAEC.
- **Chagai nuclear tests** — Chagai-I on **28 May 1998**, Chagai-II on **30 May 1998**.
- **Abdus Salam** — Pakistani-origin **1979 Nobel Prize in Physics** for electroweak unification theory.
- **Missile families** — Ballistic: *Ghaznavi, Shaheen, Ghauri*; Cruise: *Babur*.
- **IT awareness** — Know generations 3G/4G/**5G**, basics of **AI** and **cybersecurity**, and Pakistan's IT export growth (over USD 2 billion in recent fiscal years).

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

Science & Technology in the SPSC General Knowledge paper sits inside the *current affairs & general awareness* cluster and rewards candidates who can match a Pakistani institution to its mandate, and a year to a milestone. Roughly **3–6 questions** appear on this area in the combined competitive examination.

#### Pakistani Scientific Organisations

| Organisation | Full Name | Mandate |
|---|---|---|
| SUPARCO | Space and Upper Atmosphere Research Commission | Space research, satellites, remote sensing |
| PAEC | Pakistan Atomic Energy Commission | Nuclear energy, reactors, agri-medicine |
| PNRA | Pakistan Nuclear Regulatory Authority | Safety regulation of nuclear facilities |
| PCSIR | Pakistan Council of Scientific & Industrial Research | Industrial / chemical R&D |
| NARC | National Agricultural Research Centre | Crop science, soil, livestock research |
| NIH | National Institute of Health, Islamabad | Vaccines, disease surveillance |

#### Space Technology Milestones

Badr-I (1990) was a small **low-Earth-orbit relay** launched from China, not a geostationary satellite. PakSat-1 (2011) became Pakistan's first **geostationary communications** satellite. PRSS-1 and EO-1 (2018) marked entry into **optical remote sensing** capability for crop, flood and urban planning.

#### Nuclear and Defence Technology

PAEC runs research reactors such as **PARR-1** and **PARR-2** at Nilore and supports cancer hospitals nationwide. The **NCA** is the apex policy body, while **PNRA** issues licences and enforces safety. Indigenisation is led by **HIT Taxila**, **Kamra** (PAC), and **KRL**, producing the Babur cruise missile and Shaheen ballistic series.

#### IT and Current Awareness

Pakistan's IT exports have crossed the **USD 2 billion** mark under the Digital Pakistan framework. Examiners test basic definitions: **AI** as machine-performed cognition, **5G** as the fifth-generation mobile standard with peak speeds above 1 Gbps, and **cybersecurity** as protection of CIA — *confidentiality, integrity, availability*.

> **Trap:** SPSC frames options so that "PCSIR" and "SUPARCO" are swapped. Remember — *PCSIR = industry; SUPARCO = space*.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

Mastery of this section requires recognising how SPSC frames distractor options and how current official notifications reshape the answer key year to year.

#### Edge Cases and Common Mistakes

- **Badr-I vs PakSat-1:** Many candidates wrongly date Pakistan's first satellite to 2011. Correct: Badr-I in 1990.
- **Abdus Salam's nationality:** Although born in Jhang and a Pakistani citizen at independence, his later career was in the UK. He is still credited as Pakistan's Nobel laureate in Physics (1979).
- **NCA vs PNRA vs PAEC:** NCA = policy, PAEC = operator, PNRA = regulator. Options often rotate these three acronyms.
- **Missile class confusion:** Babur is a **subsonic cruise missile**, while Ghaznavi, Shaheen and Ghauri are **ballistic**. Ghauri is also known as Hatf-V.

#### Environmental Science Linkages

Questions increasingly reference Pakistan's **climate vulnerability** — the 2022 super-floods, smog in Lahore, and glacier retreat in the Karakoram. Know the **Paris Agreement (2015)** and the **COP** summit cycle. Pakistan launched the **Living Indus** initiative to restore the Indus basin.

#### Exam Strategy

- Read the *Pakistan Annual Yearbook* or SUPARCO/PAEC press releases before the exam — they seed new MCQs.
- Skip calculations; this section is **100% recall-based**.
- Budget **≤30 seconds** per question; flag and move if unsure.

#### Practice Prompts

1. Which organisation regulates nuclear safety in Pakistan — PAEC, PNRA, or NCA?
2. Name the year Badr-I was launched and the year SUPARCO was established.
3. Identify which is a cruise missile: Ghaznavi, Shaheen, Ghauri, or Babur.
