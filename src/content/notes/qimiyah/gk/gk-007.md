### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Calendar and Date Traps

A recurring examiner tactic pairs the Hijri founding date of a Saudi institution with its Gregorian counterpart in a single MCQ. Saudi Unification is 1932 Gregorian = 1351 AH; National Day coincides. Founding Day, reinstated as an official holiday in 2022, is 22 February 1727 Gregorian ≈ 1139 AH. Memorising both calendars together blocks the most common wrong-answer pattern.

#### Regional Geography Edge Cases

| Largest administrative region | Riyadh Region | Eastern Province (pop. larger, area smaller) |
| Capital | Riyadh | Mecca or Jeddah |
