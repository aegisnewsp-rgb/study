# Banking Awareness - RBI and Monetary Policy

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

The **Reserve Bank of India (RBI)**, constituted on **1 April 1935** under the **RBI Act, 1934**, is India's central bank. It issues currency under **Section 22** of the Act, acts as banker to the government and to banks, and regulates the banking system including NBFCs and cooperative banks.

Monetary policy is decided by the **Monetary Policy Committee (MPC)**, a 6-member body (3 RBI + 3 external) chaired by the RBI Governor, operational since **August 2016**. The MPC fixes the **Policy Repo Rate** — the rate at which RBI lends to commercial banks against government securities under the **Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF)**. The statutory inflation target is **4% CPI with a ±2% tolerance band**.

Key rates: **Bank Rate (6.50%)**, **MSF at Repo + 25 bps**, **CRR 4%**, **SLR 18%**. **OMOs** (Open Market Operations) are used to manage durable liquidity. UPPSC frequently asks the count of MPC members, the inflation target figure, and the current repo rate band.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### Institutional Architecture

The **RBI** was nationalised on **1 January 1949** and currently has a central board, 4 local boards, and 19 regional offices plus the central office in Mumbai. Its main functions are **monetary authority, issuer of currency, regulator of the banking system, manager of foreign exchange, developmental role, and banker to the government**. Section 22 of the RBI Act empowers it to issue banknotes of all denominations; the one-rupee note is issued by the Ministry of Finance.

#### The Monetary Policy Committee

The **MPC** was set up under the RBI (Amendment) Act, 2016, with the **first meeting on 5–6 October 2016**. It has **6 members**:
- Governor (Chair), Deputy Governor in charge of monetary policy, one RBI officer nominated by the Central Board.
- **Three external experts** appointed by the Central Government.

Decisions are by majority; if tied, the Governor has a casting vote. The committee meets **at least four times a year** and publishes a **Monetary Policy Report (MPR)** after each meeting.

#### Inflation Targeting Framework

Under **Section 45ZA**, the statutory target for CPI inflation is **4%**, with a tolerance band of ±2% (i.e. 2%–6%). If inflation stays outside the band for three consecutive quarters, the RBI must submit a report to the government explaining the reasons and corrective action. The target is **reviewed every 5 years** by the Central Government in consultation with the RBI.

#### Policy Instruments

- **Repo Rate**: RBI's policy rate; banks borrow short-term funds against G-Secs (LAF).
- **Reverse Repo Rate**: Used now mainly for **Fixed Rate Reverse Repo (FRRR)** auctions, the floor of the LAF corridor.
- **Marginal Standing Facility (MSF)**: Banks can borrow overnight at Repo + 25 bps against SLR securities — the **ceiling** of the corridor.
- **Bank Rate**: Stands at the MSF rate since 7 August 2023 (6.50% in current cycle) and is charged on RBI's long-term advances.
- **Standing Deposit Facility (SDF)**: Introduced on **8 April 2022** at 25 bps below the Bank Rate; allows banks to park excess funds without collateral.
- **CRR**: Cash that banks must park with RBI as a % of net demand and time liabilities.
- **SLR**: % of NDTL banks must hold in liquid assets (gold, G-Secs, cash).
- **Open Market Operations (OMO)**: Outright purchase or sale of G-Secs to manage durable liquidity.

#### Money Supply Formulas

- **M1 (Narrow Money)** = Currency with public + Demand deposits with banks + Other deposits with RBI.
- **M3 (Broad Money)** = M1 + Time deposits with banks.
- **Money Multiplier** ≈ 1 / (CRR + other leakage ratios).

#### Exam Patterns in UPPSC

The UPPSC PCS Prelims commonly ask: number of MPC members (6), inflation target (4% CPI ±2%), repo rate current value, MSF spread (+25 bps), headquarters of RBI (Mumbai), RBI Act year (1934), and operational date of MPC (2016). Mains GS Paper III may expect a 150-word note on "Inflation Targeting in India".

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Corridor System and Liquidity Management

The **LAF corridor** is asymmetric: Reverse Repo at –25 bps and MSF at +25 bps around the Repo Rate. The SDF (–25 bps from Bank Rate) was added in April 2022 as a collateral-free absorption tool. **Variable Rate Reverse Repo (VRRR)** auctions of longer tenors (14-day, 28-day) are used to soak up durable surplus liquidity without changing the policy rate — a key distinction examiners test.

#### Stance, Forward Guidance, and Reports

The MPC also states a **stance** — *Accommodative* (e.g. rate cuts/holds with bias to ease), *Neutral*, or *Withdrawal of Accommodation*. The **Monetary Policy Report (MPR)** replaces the older quarterly Monetary Policy Review and provides a forward path. The **Financial Stability Report (FSR)**, published twice a year, presents stress tests on banks.

#### Exchange Rate and External Sector

RBI manages the rupee on a **market-determined exchange rate with no fixed target**, intervening only to curb excessive volatility. The balance sheet identity is: **Net Domestic Assets (NDA) + Net Foreign Assets (NFA) = Currency + Deposits of the government with RBI** (simplified). RBI also maintains the **Foreign Exchange Reserves** (forex, gold, SDRs, IMF reserve tranche).

#### Common Traps and Mistakes

- Confusing **Bank Rate** with Repo Rate (Bank Rate applies to long-term refinance; Repo is the LAF rate).
- Saying MPC has 4 members (it is 6, including 3 external).
- Stating the inflation target as 5% (it is 4% ± 2% band).
- Treating SDF as a *lending* tool — it is an *absorption* facility.
- Citing 1935 as the year RBI was *nationalised* — that is **1 January 1949**.

#### Worked Quick Check

If CRR is 4% and a bank has ₹100 crore of NDTL, required CRR cash with RBI = **₹4 crore**. If the policy rate is 6.50% and the corridor spread is ±25 bps, the Reverse Repo = 6.25% and MSF = 6.75%. If real growth requires positive real rate, and CPI inflation is 5%, the **real policy rate = 6.50% − 5% = 1.50%**.
