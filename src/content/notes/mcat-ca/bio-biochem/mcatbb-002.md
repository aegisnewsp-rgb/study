# Biomolecules: Carbohydrates, Lipids and Nucleotides — MCAT Bio/Biochem

Carbohydrates, lipids, and nucleotides complete AAMC Foundational Concept 1 of the Bio/Biochem section. The MCAT tests monosaccharides and glycosidic bonds, polysaccharides, fatty acids, triglycerides, phospholipids, sterols, nitrogenous bases, and nucleotides. These biomolecules underpin energy metabolism, membrane structure, and genetic information storage.

### Lite — Quick Review (1h-1d)

#### Core concepts

- **Monosaccharides:** simple sugars (glucose, fructose, galactose). Aldoses (aldehyde group) and ketoses (ketone group). Trioses, tetroses, pentoses, hexoses (3, 4, 5, 6 carbons).
- **Glycosidic bonds:** covalent bonds between monosaccharides. α-1,4 (starch, glycogen); β-1,4 (cellulose); α-1,6 (branch points in glycogen).
- **Polysaccharides:** starch (energy storage in plants), glycogen (energy storage in animals), cellulose (structural in plants), chitin (structural in animals).
- **Fatty acids:** saturated (no double bonds, solid at room temperature); unsaturated (one or more double bonds, liquid at room temperature).
- **Triglycerides:** glycerol + 3 fatty acids. Energy storage in adipose tissue.
- **Phospholipids:** glycerol + 2 fatty acids + phosphate group. Amphipathic (hydrophobic tails, hydrophilic head). Form bilayers in water.
- **Sterols:** four fused rings (cholesterol, steroid hormones).
- **Nucleotides:** nitrogenous base + pentose sugar + phosphate. DNA (deoxyribose, A/T/G/C); RNA (ribose, A/U/G/C).

#### One-pass drill

1. Identify the biomolecule: carbohydrate, lipid, or nucleotide.
2. Identify the structural feature: glycosidic bond, fatty acid saturation, base type.
3. Apply the relevant rule.

#### Examiner traps

- Confusing α and β glycosidic bonds. α-1,4 bonds are digestible by humans; β-1,4 bonds are not.
- Treating all lipids as hydrophobic. Phospholipids are amphipathic.
- Mixing up DNA and RNA bases. DNA has thymine (T); RNA has uracil (U).

### Standard — Regular Study (2d-2mo)

#### Carbohydrates in detail

- **Monosaccharides:** glucose (blood sugar, the primary energy source for most cells); fructose (fruit sugar); galactose (part of lactose).
- **Disaccharides:** sucrose (glucose + fructose); lactose (glucose + galactose); maltose (glucose + glucose).
- **Polysaccharides:** starch (energy storage in plants); glycogen (energy storage in animals, branched); cellulose (plant cell walls, β-1,4 bonds, indigestible by humans); chitin (arthropod exoskeletons, fungal cell walls).

The MCAT tests carbohydrate structure in the context of digestion, energy metabolism, and cell signalling.

#### Lipids in detail

- **Fatty acids:** saturated (no C=C, straight chain, high melting point); monounsaturated (one C=C); polyunsaturated (multiple C=C). The double bond position (ω-3, ω-6) is tested in nutritional contexts.
- **Triglycerides:** glycerol esterified to three fatty acids. The body's main energy storage. Hydrolysed by lipases to release fatty acids for energy.
- **Phospholipids:** glycerol + 2 fatty acids + phosphate group. The phosphate head is polar; the fatty acid tails are nonpolar. Form bilayers in aqueous environments (cell membranes).
- **Sterols:** cholesterol (membrane fluidity, precursor to steroid hormones); steroid hormones (testosterone, oestrogen, progesterone, cortisol).

#### Nucleotides in detail

- **Structure:** nitrogenous base + pentose sugar (ribose or deoxyribose) + one or more phosphate groups.
- **Bases:** purines (adenine, guanine — two rings); pyrimidines (cytosine, thymine, uracil — one ring). DNA uses A, T, G, C. RNA uses A, U, G, C.
- **Pairing:** A pairs with T (or U in RNA) via two hydrogen bonds; G pairs with C via three hydrogen bonds.
- **Functions:** DNA (genetic information storage); RNA (messenger, transfer, ribosomal); ATP (energy currency); cAMP (second messenger); NAD⁺/NADH (electron carrier); FAD/FADH₂ (electron carrier).

#### Practice routine

- Take 5 carbohydrate items. Identify the monosaccharide, the bond type, and the resulting polysaccharide.
- Take 5 lipid items. Identify the fatty acid saturation, the lipid class, and the biological role.
- Take 5 nucleotide items. Identify the base type, the sugar (ribose or deoxyribose), and the function.

### Deep — Long-Term Mastery (1mo-6mo)

#### How this topic connects to the other Bio/Biochem topics

- **Metabolism** depends on glucose (glycolysis), fatty acids (beta-oxidation), and nucleotides (ATP, NADH).
- **Cellular biology** depends on phospholipid bilayers and membrane proteins.
- **Genetics** depends on DNA structure and base pairing.

#### Common errors and the correction

- **"Starch and cellulose are interchangeable."** Starch has α-1,4 bonds; cellulose has β-1,4 bonds. Humans can digest starch but not cellulose.
- **"All fatty acids are bad."** Saturated and trans fats are linked to cardiovascular disease; monounsaturated and polyunsaturated fats are generally considered healthier.
- **"DNA and RNA have the same bases."** DNA has thymine (T); RNA has uracil (U). Otherwise, both have A, G, C.

#### Exam technique

- For carbohydrate items, identify the bond type (α or β) before identifying the polysaccharide.
- For lipid items, identify the fatty acid saturation before identifying the lipid class.
- For nucleotide items, identify the sugar (ribose or deoxyribose) and the base pairing before identifying the function.
