# Aqidah — Athar al-Aqidah and Mawaqif al-Islam min al-Sihr — Qatar Secondary Certificate Islamic Studies Notes

The MoEHE Grade 12 Islamic Studies Aqidah (Islamic Creed, العقيدة الإسلامية) domain opens in Term 1 with "Athar al-Aqidah al-Islamiyya fi bina' al-shakhsiyya al-muslima" (The impact of Islamic creed on building the Muslim personality, أثر العقيدة الإسلامية في بناء الشخصية المسلمة) and continues in Term 2 with "Mawaqif al-Islam min al-Sihr" (The Islamic position on sorcery, موقف الإسلام من السحر).

### Lite — Quick Review (1h-1d)

#### Core facts in one pass

- **Aqidah:** the six pillars of Islamic belief — belief in Allah, the angels, the holy books, the prophets, the Day of Judgment, and divine decree (qadar).
- **Athar al-Aqidah:** the impact of correct belief on the individual and the community — stability, purpose, ethical conduct, social cohesion.
- **Sihr (sorcery):** the Islamic doctrinal position is that sihr is real, haram, and one of the major sins. It is distinct from miracle (mu'jizah), grace (karamah), and natural causation.
- **Ruqya:** the Quranic and prophetic method of seeking refuge in Allah from sihr and evil — distinguished from sihr itself.

#### Examiner traps

- Conflating sihr with miracle — both are supernatural, but sihr is from Shaytan, miracle is from Allah.
- Treating aqidah as abstract theology — the MoEHE module emphasises the practical effect on character.

### Standard — Regular Study (2d-2mo)

#### The six pillars of Islamic belief

1. **Belief in Allah:** His oneness (tawhid), His names and attributes (asma' wa sifat), His exclusive right to worship (uluhiyyah).
2. **Belief in the angels:** created from light, they worship Allah without disobedience. Jibril, Mika'il, Israfil, and Malik are the four best-known.
3. **Belief in the holy books:** the Quran (the final revelation, unchanged), the Tawrah, the Zabur, the Injil (revealed to previous prophets, now abrogated by the Quran in matters of law).
4. **Belief in the prophets and messengers:** from Adam to Muhammad (peace be upon them all). The Quran mentions 25 by name; the hadith tradition affirms 124,000.
5. **Belief in the Day of Judgment:** the resurrection, the reckoning, the sirat, the paradise and hell.
6. **Belief in divine decree:** the good and the evil of it, the qadar written in al-Lawh al-Mahfuz, the compatibility with free will.

#### Athar al-Aqidah on the Muslim personality

Correct aqidah produces (1) inner peace and stability (al-taqwa, al-tawakkul); (2) ethical conduct grounded in divine accountability; (3) social cohesion through shared belief and practice; (4) resistance to despair through belief in divine mercy and wisdom; (5) courage and sacrifice through belief in the eternal reward; (6) humility before Allah and kindness to creation.

The MoEHE Term 1 module traces these effects across the personal, family, and social domains. Examples: how belief in Allah's watchfulness deters theft; how belief in the Day of Judgment deters injustice; how belief in angels and divine decree gives patience in hardship.

#### Sihr in the Islamic tradition

Classical scholars (al-Bukhari, Ibn al-Qayyim, Ibn Taymiyyah) distinguish:

- **Sihr (sorcery):** using jinn or unseen means to harm others — haram, a major sin.
- **Tahara (amulets, talismans):** some are shirk (associating partners with Allah), some are permissible if they contain only Quran.
- **Ruqya shar'iyyah:** recitation of Quran, the names of Allah, and prophetic supplications to seek refuge or healing — permissible and recommended.
- **Kahanah (fortune-telling):** haram — claiming knowledge of the unseen.

#### Quranic basis on sihr

Surah Al-Baqarah 2:102 — " واتبعوا ما تتلو الشياطين على ملك سليمان وما كفر سليمان ولكن الشياطين كفروا يعلمون الناس السحر " — They followed what the devils taught during Solomon's reign; Solomon did not disbelieve, but the devils disbelieved, teaching people sorcery.

Surah Al-Falaq 113:4 — " ومن شر النفاثات في العقد " — and from the evil of those who blow on knots (a reference to a common form of sihr in the prophetic era).

The hadith on the seven destructive sins (Sahih al-Bukhari 2767) includes السحر (sihr) — confirming its status as a major sin.

### Deep — Long-Term Mastery (1mo-6mo)

#### Why aqidah matters at the MoEHE terminal

The MoEHE Islamic Studies curriculum places aqidah as one of the five domains, alongside Quran and Hadith. The Grade 12 modules connect correct belief to the practice of Islam — the ethical, social, and spiritual implications of tawhid and the pillars of belief.

#### Exam technique

- For Athar al-Aqidah, organise by domain (personal, family, social) and by pillar of belief.
- For Mawaqif al-Islam min al-Sihr, distinguish the categories clearly (sihr, ruqya, kahanah, tahara).

#### Common misconceptions (and the correction)

- "Sihr is just illusion." No — the Islamic tradition affirms that sihr has real effects (with divine permission) and is a major sin.
- "Aqidah is only for scholars." Aqidah is the practice of every Muslim; the Grade 12 modules make it accessible to all students.
