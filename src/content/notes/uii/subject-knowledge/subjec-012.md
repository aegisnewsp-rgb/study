# Typical Content Domains

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your Universitas Indonesia (SIMAK UI) examination.

In the Universitas Indonesia entrance examination (Seleksi Masuk Universitas Indonesia / SIMAK UI), **Subject Knowledge** encompasses the academic sub-tests administered alongside the core scholastic aptitude battery (Kemampuan Dasar / TKPA). It targets foundational competency across natural sciences (*Saintek* / Kemampuan IPA) and social sciences (*Soshum* / Kemampuan IPS) depending on your target faculty. Review these essential structural facts:

- **Exam Architecture**: SIMAK UI divides into two main sessions:
  1. *Kemampuan Dasar (KD)*: Bahasa Indonesia, Bahasa Inggris, and Matematika Dasar.
  2. *Kemampuan IPA (Saintek)*: Matematika IPA, Fisika, Kimia, and Biologi.
  3. *Kemampuan IPS (Soshum)*: Ekonomi, Sejarah, Geografi, and Sosiologi.
- **Scoring Formula & Penalty Mechanics**: SIMAK UI applies a differential scoring formula:
  $$	ext{Score} = (	ext{Correct} 	imes +4) + (	ext{Incorrect} 	imes -1) + (	ext{Unanswered} 	imes 0)$$
  Because incorrect answers incur a $-1$ penalty, guessing without eliminating at least two distractors damages aggregate rank.
- **Weightage Context**: While general reasoning and language modules establish baseline eligibility, advanced subject knowledge represents the primary differentiator for high-demand faculties like Fakultas Kedokteran (FK), Fakultas Teknik (FT), and Fakultas Ilmu Komputer (FASILKOM).
- **Core Strategy**: Target high-certainty questions first. A candidate scoring $+4$ on 8 confident items outscores a candidate attempting all 15 items with multiple negative deductions.

#### 1. Set Theory & Inclusion-Exclusion Principles

In SIMAK UI Matematika Dasar and logical reasoning modules, set theory problems test multi-variable overlapping sets:

##### Principle of Inclusion-Exclusion
- For two finite sets $A$ and $B$:
  $$|A \cup B| = |A| + |B| - |A \cap B|$$
- For three finite sets $A$, $B$, and $C$:
  $$|A \cup B \cup C| = |A| + |B| + |C| - (|A \cap B| + |B \cap C| + |C \cap A|) + |A \cap B \cap C|$$

##### De Morgan's Laws for Sets
1. $(A \cup B)^c = A^c \cap B^c$ (The complement of the union equals the intersection of the complements).
2. $(A \cap B)^c = A^c \cup B^c$ (The complement of the intersection equals the union of the complements).

#### 2. Negative Marking Optimization & Risk Utility Model

Let an exam section consist of $N$ questions, each having 5 options ($A, B, C, D, E$).
- Points awarded for a correct answer $= +4$.
- Points deducted for an incorrect answer $= -1$.
- Points for omitting $= 0$.

##### Expected Value of Pure Random Guessing
If a candidate has zero knowledge and picks randomly among 5 options:
$$E = \left(\dfrac{1}{5} 	imes (+4)ight) + \left(\dfrac{4}{5} 	imes (-1)ight) = \dfrac{4}{5} - \dfrac{4}{5} = 0.00$$
Pure random guessing yields an expected mathematical return of zero, but introduces severe statistical variance that endangers rank.

##### Expected Value After Eliminating Distractors
1. *Eliminating 1 wrong option (4 choices remain)*:
   $$E = \left(\dfrac{1}{4} 	imes (+4)ight) + \left(\dfrac{3}{4} 	imes (-1)ight) = 1.0 - 0.75 = +0.25\,	ext{points}$$
2. *Eliminating 2 wrong options (3 choices remain)*:
   $$E = \left(\dfrac{1}{3} 	imes (+4)ight) + \left(\dfrac{2}{3} 	imes (-1)ight) = \dfrac{4}{3} - \dfrac{2}{3} = +0.67\,	ext{points}$$
3. *Eliminating 3 wrong options (2 choices remain)*:
   $$E = \left(\dfrac{1}{2} 	imes (+4)ight) + \left(\dfrac{1}{2} 	imes (-1)ight) = 2.0 - 0.50 = +1.50\,	ext{points}$$

**Golden Rule of SIMAK UI Guessing**: Never guess blindly. If you can definitively rule out at least two false options, the positive expected value ($+0.67$) makes attempting the item statistically advantageous over leaving it blank.

#### Problem 1: Propositional Logic and Implication Negation
**Question**: What is the logical negation of the proposition: *"If an applicant passes the SIMAK UI exam, then they are admitted to Fakultas Kedokteran"*?
- (A) If an applicant fails SIMAK UI, they are not admitted to Fakultas Kedokteran.
- (B) An applicant passes SIMAK UI and is not admitted to Fakultas Kedokteran.
- (C) An applicant fails SIMAK UI and is admitted to Fakultas Kedokteran.
- (D) If an applicant is admitted to Fakultas Kedokteran, then they passed SIMAK UI.

**Solution**:

#### Problem 2: Three-Set Cardinality Calculation
**Question**: In a survey of 100 prospective SIMAK UI candidates:
- 50 prepare for Matematika IPA ($M$).
- 40 prepare for Fisika ($F$).
- 35 prepare for Kimia ($K$).
- 20 prepare for both $M$ and $F$.
- 15 prepare for both $F$ and $K$.
- 18 prepare for both $M$ and $K$.
- 10 prepare for all three subjects ($M \cap F \cap K$).

How many candidates prepare for *none* of these three subjects?

**Solution**:
1. Use the Principle of Inclusion-Exclusion for three sets:
   $$|M \cup F \cup K| = |M| + |F| + |K| - (|M \cap F| + |F \cap K| + |M \cap K|) + |M \cap F \cap K|$$
2. Substitute the given parameters:
   $$|M \cup F \cup K| = 50 + 40 + 35 - (20 + 15 + 18) + 10$$
   $$|M \cup F \cup K| = 125 - 53 + 10 = 82$$
3. Compute the complement (candidates preparing for none):
   $$|	ext{None}| = 	ext{Total} - |M \cup F \cup K| = 100 - 82 = 18$$
**Answer: 18 candidates**.
