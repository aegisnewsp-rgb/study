# Application Fee and Required Documents — JUPAS Notes

This topic covers the JUPAS application fee and the documents required for the application. The application fee is charged per cycle and changes year to year. Required documents typically include HKDSE results, school reference, predicted grades (if applicable), and any other qualifications.

> Verify the live JUPAS application fee and document requirements on https://www.jupas.edu.hk/ before planning revision.

---

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)

#### Core facts in one pass

- **Application fee**: charged per JUPAS cycle. The fee amount is published on jupas.edu.hk each year.
- **Payment methods**: typically include PPS, JPP, bank counters, and the JUPAS online system (check the live channels list).
- **Required documents**: HKDSE results (entered in the JUPAS system), school reference, predicted grades (if applicable), other qualifications.
- **Late applications**: subject to a late application fee and late-application rules.
- **Withdrawal**: candidates can withdraw from JUPAS; the application fee may not be refunded.

#### Examiner traps

- Forgetting to pay the application fee before the deadline.
- Omitting required documents (e.g. predicted grades for in-progress HKDSE candidates).

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)

#### Application fee

The JUPAS application fee is charged per cycle. The fee amount is published on jupas.edu.hk for each admission cycle (typically in the autumn before the cycle opens).

For recent cycles, the fee has been in the range of several hundred HKD (re-check the live fee schedule on jupas.edu.hk for the current cycle).

**Late application**: candidates who miss the regular application deadline may submit a late application, subject to a late-application fee and late-application rules. The late-application deadline is typically later than the regular deadline but with a higher fee.

**Payment channels** (check the live list on jupas.edu.hk):

- PPS (Payment by Phone Service)
- JPP (JUPAS Payment Portal — online credit/debit card payment)
- Bank counters (in Hong Kong)
- JUPAS online system (for some payment types)

**Withdrawal**: candidates can withdraw their JUPAS application at any time before the Main Round. The application fee is typically not refunded.

#### Required documents

The JUPAS application requires the following documents and information:

- **HKDSE results**: candidates enter their HKDSE results directly in the JUPAS system. HKDSE results are uploaded to the JUPAS system automatically for current HKDSE candidates.
- **Personal information**: name, Hong Kong ID number, contact details, school information.
- **School reference**: provided by the candidate's school. Includes predicted grades (for in-progress HKDSE candidates) and a holistic assessment.
- **Other qualifications**: candidates with non-HKDSE qualifications (e.g. IB, GCE A-Level, mainland China Gaokao) submit these separately.
- **Personal statement**: required for some programmes (typically programmes with strong admissions interview components).
- **Portfolio**: required for some programmes (e.g. Architecture, Design, Visual Arts).

#### Submission deadlines

The submission deadlines for JUPAS vary by component:

- **Application submission**: typically December (regular) or early January (late).
- **School reference submission**: typically December (regular).
- **Other qualifications**: typically December (regular).

Re-check the live calendar on jupas.edu.hk for the current cycle's deadlines.

#### Common misconceptions (and the correction)

- "JUPAS is free." No — there is an application fee per cycle.
- "The application fee is refunded on withdrawal." No — the fee is typically not refunded.
- "Personal statement is required for all programmes." No — only for some programmes (typically those with interview components).
