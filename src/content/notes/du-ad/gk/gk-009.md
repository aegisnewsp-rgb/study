# Geography of Bangladesh — Rivers, Districts, and Natural Resources

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

Bangladesh sits on the **Ganges–Brahmaputra–Meghna (GBM) delta**, the largest river delta in the world at roughly **122,000 km²**. The country has **8 administrative divisions** containing **64 districts (zilas)**, drained by about **700 rivers** — of which the **Padma, Meghna, and Jamuna** carry the largest discharge.

| Term | What it actually means in Bangladesh |
|---|---|
| **Padma** | Combined Ganges + Jamuna, ~120 km inside BD |
| **Jamuna** | Brahmaputra renamed once it enters BD (~240 km) |
| **Meghna** | Surma + Kushiyara + Old Brahmaputra, longest river within BD (~946 km) |
| **Sundarbans** | World's largest single-block mangrove forest, ~6,000 km² (BD share) |

> 💡 **High-Yield Memory Hook:** **"P-J-M, M-P-J"** — **P**adma + **J**amuna form the **M**eghna downstream at Chandpur. Flip the loop when recalling which river joins which: Meghna absorbs Padma, and Padma is born where Jamuna meets the Ganges.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### River System Mechanics

The hydrology tested on DU Admission GK papers is **confluence-based, not length-based**. Bangladesh inherits three river families that meet on its floodplain:

1. **Ganges** enters from the west as the **Padma** after meeting the **Jamuna** (Brahmaputra segment inside BD) near Goalundo.
2. **Brahmaputra** flows east-to-west through Tibet and Assam, then enters BD as the **Jamuna**.
3. **Surma–Kushiyara** drain the Sylhet basin and merge into the **Old Brahmaputra**, which forms the **Meghna** before meeting the **Padma** near Chandpur.

The combined Meghna then discharges into the **Bay of Bengal** through a tidal delta — Bangladesh's southern coastal plain.

#### Comparison Matrix — Easily Confused Items

| Confused Pair | The Correct Distinction |
|---|---|
| Padma vs Ganges | Ganges becomes Padma only inside BD, after absorbing the Jamuna |
| Meghna vs Padma | Meghna is the longest in BD; Padma is shorter but has the highest discharge |
| Brahmaputra vs Jamuna | Same river, different name: Brahmaputra (India) → Jamuna (BD) |
| Chittagong vs Chattogram | Same city; "Chattogram" is the official Bangla spelling |
| Sundarbans (BD) vs Sundarbans (India) | Bangladesh holds ~60% — the **largest single block** |
| Keokradong vs Mowdok Mual | Mowdok Mual (~1,003 m, Rangamati) is the highest peak in BD |

#### 🎯 Exam-Level Worked Problem

**Question:** Which of the following is the longest river *within Bangladesh*?
(a) Padma  (b) Brahmaputra  (c) Meghna  (d) Jamuna

#### Solution:
1. Eliminate **Brahmaputra** — its course inside BD is renamed Jamuna, not Brahmaputra (~240 km inside BD).
2. Eliminate **Padma** — only ~120 km of the Ganges channel runs inside BD before confluence.
3. Eliminate **Jamuna** — ~240 km within BD.
4. **Meghna** — assembled from Surma + Kushiyara + Old Brahmaputra, it measures ~946 km within Bangladesh. **Answer: (c) Meghna.**

> ⚠️ **Examiner Trap:** Many students circle (b) Brahmaputra because the name is famous globally. The DU paper specifically tests *within Bangladesh* length, where the Meghna wins by a wide margin. Always read the qualifier: "inside BD" or "within Bangladesh."

#### Natural Resources Snapshot

- **Natural gas:** Titas (largest producing), Habiganj, Bakhrabad, Rashidpur, Kailashtila — mostly in the **Surma Basin** of Sylhet/Dhaka.
- **Coal:** Barapukuria (Dinajpur) is the only producing mine; Phulbari and Khalaspir hold unmined reserves.
- **Sundarbans mangrove:** shared with India, BD portion ~6,000 km², Royal Bengal Tiger habitat, UNESCO World Heritage.
- **Soils:** alluvial floodplain (most agricultural land), terrace/red soils (Madhupur, Barind Tract), hill soils (CHT), coastal saline (south).

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Edge Cases and High-Value Traps

1. **Division count:** Bangladesh had 6 divisions before 2010. **Rangpur** was added in 2010, and **Mymensingh** in 2015 — total **8**. Papers older than 2015 still cite 6 or 7; modern DU papers require **8**.
2. **Highest peak debate:** Most Bengali textbooks name **Keokradong (~986 m)** as the highest point. Recent surveys place **Mowdok Mual (~1,003 m)** in Rangamati higher — know both and answer "Mowdok Mual" when the option appears.
3. **Sundarbans ownership ratio:** BD ~60%, India ~40%. Saying "Sundarbans is in India" is wrong; saying "largest mangrove in India" is also wrong — the **single largest block** lies in BD.
4. **Cox's Bazar:** ~120 km continuous natural sandy sea beach — **longest in the world**, not just Asia. Marina Beach (Chennai) is urban; Cox's Bazar is natural.
5. **Largest division by area:** **Chittagong (Chattogram) Division**. Smallest: **Mymensingh Division** (added 2015, carved from Dhaka).
6. **Titas vs Barapukuria trap:** Titas = gas. Barapukuria = coal. Never mix them in MCQs asking "largest producing ___ field."
7. **Soil tracts:** Madhupur Tract and Barind Tract are **terrace/red-soil uplands**, not floodplain — Bangladesh's floodplain agriculture does **not** cover them.

#### Advanced Practice Prompts

1. "Name the river formed by the confluence of the Ganges and the Jamuna, and the district where this confluence lies." → **Padma**, near **Goalundo**, **Rajbari District** (under Dhaka Division historically, but check the modern district map for the exact zila).
2. "Which Bangladeshi division was created most recently, and from which parent division?" → **Mymensingh Division** (2015), carved from **Dhaka Division**.
