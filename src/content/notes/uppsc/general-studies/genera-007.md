# Sectoral Structure & National Income

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.
**Indian Economy and Social Development** is one of the highest-scoring GS Paper II sections in UPPSC RO/ARO, regularly contributing 8–12 objective questions. The bedrock identity is **GDP = C + I + G + (X − M)**, expanded from GNP by adding **Net Factor Income from Abroad (NFIA)**. **NNP at Market Price** is the true *National Income* figure after subtracting depreciation. For poverty and inequality, remember the **Tendulkar line (₹32/day rural, ₹33/day urban, 2009–10 base)** and the **Multidimensional Poverty Index (MPI)** that India now uses for SDG tracking. Two high-yield traps: confusing *Fiscal Deficit* with *Revenue Deficit*, and assuming *Current Account Deficit* equals *Trade Deficit* (it doesn't — invisibles matter).

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### Sectoral Structure & National Income
The Indian economy is a **mixed economy** where the **primary sector** (agriculture, ~17–18% of GVA), **secondary sector** (industry, ~25–27%) and **tertiary sector** (services, ~50%) coexist with active public and private participation. National income identities cascade downward:

- **GNP = GDP + NFIA**
- **NDP = GDP − Depreciation**
- **NNP at MP = GNP − Depreciation** ← the operative National Income

**Per Capita Income = National Income / Mid-year Population** is the headline welfare number published by the Central Statistics Office (now NSO, MoSPI).

#### Planning Regime
**Five Year Plans** steered India from 1951 to 2017. The **NITI Aayog** (replacing the Planning Commission in January 2015) is an advisory body — it does **not** allocate funds. Note the shift: plans had binding targets; NITI Aayog issues *vision documents* and *indices* (like the State Health Index).

#### 1991 Reforms (LPG)
Triggered by a **BoP crisis** when forex reserves fell to barely two weeks of imports. Finance Minister **Manmohan Singh** under PM **Narasimha Rao** executed **Liberalisation** (dismantling the licence-permit raj), **Privatisation** (disinvestment of PSUs), and **Globalisation** (FDI openness). Key milestones: devaluation of the rupee, abolition of the Controller of Capital Issues, and entry of private capital in sectors reserved for the public sector.

#### Poverty, Unemployment & Inflation
- **Tendulkar Committee (2009–10)**: poverty line of ₹32/day rural, ₹33/day urban.
- **Rangarajan Committee (2014)**: revised to ₹47/day rural, ₹67/day urban.
- **MPI** captures deprivations in health, education, and living standards.
- **Inflation** is measured by **CPI** (consumer) and **WPI** (wholesale); RBI targets CPI at **4% (±2%)**.
- **Multiplier k = 1/(1 − MPC)** — a rise in investment of ₹100 with MPC = 0.8 generates ₹500 in income.

#### Fiscal & External Sectors
- **Fiscal Deficit = Total Expenditure − Total Receipts (excluding borrowings)**. The **FRBM Act, 2003** mandates its reduction.
- **Current Account = Trade Balance + Net Invisibles**. A **CAD** above 3% of GDP is a red flag.
- India's BoP comprises the **Current Account**, **Capital Account** and **Errors & Omissions**.

#### Exam Patterns
Expect 6–10 MCQs combining conceptual traps with current data: latest GDP growth rate, repo rate, fiscal deficit %, forex reserves, HDI rank, and scheme-based questions on **MGNREGA, PM-KISAN, Jan Dhan, Atmanirbhar Bharat, Startup India**.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Edge Cases & Conceptual Traps
A surprising number of candidates confuse **GNP at Factor Cost** (now called NDP at FC) with **NNP at Market Prices**. They differ by **Net Indirect Taxes (NIT)**: NNP at MP = NNP at FC + NIT. UPSSSC and UPPSC routinely test this distinction. Similarly, **Real GDP** strips out price effects via the **GDP Deflator = (Nominal GDP / Real GDP) × 100**, while **CPI** is what households actually experience.

#### Monetary Policy Architecture
The **RBI's Monetary Policy Committee (MPC)** sets the **Repo Rate** (the rate at which RBI lends short-term funds to banks). Other operative rates: **Reverse Repo**, **Bank Rate**, **Marginal Standing Facility (MSF)**. Recent innovations include the **Standing Deposit Facility (SDF)** introduced in April 2022 as a new floor rate. The **Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF)** is the operating window.

#### Social Sector Schemes — Mapping the 21st Century
- **MGNREGA 2005**: 100 days of guaranteed wage employment per rural household.
- **PM-KISAN**: ₹6,000/year in three instalments to landholding farmer families.
- **PMJDY (Jan Dhan Yojana)**: zero-balance accounts with RuPay cards and overdraft up to ₹10,000.
- **PMJJBY, PMSBY, APY**: insurance and pension trilogy.
- **Atal Innovation Mission**, **Skill India**, **Startup India** — innovation stack.
- **Beti Bachao Beti Padhao** for sex ratio, **POSHAN Abhiyaan** for malnutrition, **Ayushman Bharat (PMJAY)** for ₹5 lakh health cover.

#### Connections to Adjacent Topics
- **Demographic Dividend**: India's median age is ~28; converts to demographic burden if skill and health investments lag.
- **Green Revolution (1966–67)** raised wheat yields via HYV seeds; **White Revolution (Operation Flood, 1970–96)** made India the world's largest milk producer.
- **Inclusive Growth** sits at the intersection of the **Gini Coefficient** (0 = perfect equality, 1 = perfect inequality) and **HDI = ⅓ (Life Expectancy Index + Education Index + Income Index)**.
- **MSP** is announced for 22-23 crops based on the **Swaminathan formula (C2 + 50%)**, though procurement remains largely for wheat and paddy.

#### Common Mistakes
1. Treating **NITI Aayog** as a successor that *allocates* plan funds — it does not; Finance Ministry and line ministries do.
2. Equating **Privatisation** with **Liberalisation** — they are distinct pillars.
3. Assuming **Current Account Deficit = Trade Deficit** — invisibles (services, remittances) can offset goods deficit.

#### Practice Prompts
1. *Numerical:* If MPC = 0.75 and investment rises by ₹200 crore, compute the change in national income and explain the multiplier process.
2. *Conceptual:* Distinguish among Fiscal Deficit, Revenue Deficit, and Primary Deficit. Why does the FRBM framework now use a debt-to-GDP glide path rather than a single fiscal deficit number?
