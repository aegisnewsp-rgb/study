# Static GK

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

**Static GK** is the recall-based slice of CUET UG's General Test — fact questions whose answers do not change with current events. It covers **Indian Polity**, **History**, **Geography**, **Indian Economy**, **General Science**, **Awards**, **Books & Authors**, **National Symbols**, **Important Days**, and **UNESCO sites**.

- **Polity anchors:** Article 17 abolishes untouchability; Article 15 prohibits discrimination; the **10th Schedule** contains the anti-defection law; the **Constituent Assembly** adopted the Constitution on 26 Nov 1949, implemented 26 Jan 1950.
- **History anchors:** Quit India Movement = 1942; Civil Disobedience Movement = 1930; Dandi March = 12 Mar 1930.
- **Economy anchors:** First Five Year Plan (1951–56) focused on **agriculture**; NITI Aayog replaced the Planning Commission in **2015**.

> Mnemonic for polity: "**15 dis, 17 untouch**" — Article 15 = discrimination, Article 17 = untouchability.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### What CUET actually tests
The General Test asks **40 of 50 questions** for 200 marks (+5/-1 marking). Static GK items surface as one-line direct MCQs — for example, *Which schedule deals with anti-defection law?* or *Who authored "Discovery of India"?*. Roughly 12–15 questions come from the GK block, and about two-thirds of those are static.

#### The seven static buckets
- **Indian Polity:** Constitution, Fundamental Rights, DPSP, Schedules, key amendments.
- **History:** Ancient, Medieval, Modern India, and select World History.
- **Geography:** Indian physical/economic geography plus world map facts.
- **Indian Economy:** Five Year Plans, NITI Aayog, RBI, committees.
- **General Science:** Class 6–10 Physics, Chemistry, Biology fundamentals.
- **Awards & Honours:** Bharat Ratna, Padma series, Nobel, sport honours.
- **Books & Authors, National Symbols, Firsts in India, Important Days, UNESCO sites.**

#### High-yield table
| Bucket | Highest-yield facts to memorise |
|---|---|
| Polity | Articles 14, 15, 17, 32, 44; Schedules 1, 2, 8, 10, 12 |
| History | 1857 Revolt, 1919 Jallianwala Bagh, 1930 Dandi, 1942 Quit India |
| Economy | Plan 1 = agriculture; Plan 2 = industry; 1991 LPG reforms |
| Awards | Bharat Ratna (highest civilian); Param Vir Chakra (highest military) |

> Trap: students often quote "highest civilian" without remembering **Param Vir Chakra** sits in a separate military category and is **not** a civilian award.

#### How to prepare
Use **NCERT Class 6–12** (History, Geography, Polity, Economics) as the spine, layer **Lucent's GK** for awards/days/books, then solve **previous-year CUET, SSC, and UPSI papers** — the question templates repeat.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Distinguishing static from dynamic
Static GK is **time-invariant**: the capital of Meghalaya, the author of *Hind Swaraj*, or Article 17's wording never change. Dynamic GK covers recent schemes, appointments, and current affairs. CUET deliberately mixes both inside the GK slot, so label every fact you memorise as either **static** (write once, retain for years) or **dynamic** (refresh monthly).

#### Edge cases examiners exploit

- **Award confusions:** Bharat Ratna (civilian, 1954), Padma series (civilian, three tiers), Param Vir Chakra (military, 1950) — different fields, different precedence.
- **Schedule traps:** The 10th Schedule (anti-defection) is asked as "which schedule" — students guess "9th" or "11th". The **8th** Schedule lists official languages; the **12th** deals with powers of municipalities.
- **Plan focus areas:** Plan I = agriculture, Plan II = industry, Plan III = foodgrains/balanced growth — not interchangeable.
- **Firsts:** *First President* (Rajendra Prasad), *First PM* (Nehru), *First Speaker of Lok Sabha* (G.V. Mavalankar), *First Chief Justice* (Harilal Kania) — easy to swap on exam day.

#### Worked identification example
Question: *Which Article abolished untouchability?*
Mechanism: scan Fundamental Rights → Article 14 (equality), 15 (non-discrimination), 16 (equal opportunity), 17 (untouchability abolition), 18 (abolition of titles). **Answer: 17.**

> Strategy: in CUET, if a polity question says "abolished", it almost always points to **Article 17**, not 15.

#### Common mistakes
- Mixing Quit India (**1942**) with Civil Disobedience (**1930**).
- Treating Kaziranga, Sundarbans, and Hampi as "natural sites" — Sundarbans is natural, Hampi is cultural, Kaziranga is natural; many students miss the category.
- Skipping the Books-and-Authors subset because it "feels unpredictable" — yet it is a 2–3 question goldmine of static recall.

#### Practice prompts
1. *The 10th Schedule of the Indian Constitution deals with which of the following?* (Answer: Anti-defection law, added by the 52nd Amendment, 1985.)
2. *Name the author of "Discovery of India" and the year of its publication.* (Answer: Jawaharlal Nehru, 1946.)

#### Exam-specific weightage
General Test = ~3% of CUET aggregate signal but is the **decisive tie-breaker** for top ranks. Static GK items form ~8–10 questions per paper; 30 minutes of targeted NCERT reading daily is enough to cover 90% of the bank.
