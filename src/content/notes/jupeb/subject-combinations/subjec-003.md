# JUPEB Science Combination (PMB)

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid review of the Physics, Mathematics, and Biology (PMB) subject combination, biomedical engineering eligibility, and Direct Entry cutoffs for JUPEB.

**PMB Science Combination — High-Yield Essentials**

1. **Constituent Subjects:** **Physics**, **Mathematics**, and **Biology** (Botany and Zoology A-Level components).
2. **Distinct Faculty Pathways:**
   - **Biomedical Engineering & Biophysics:** Blends physical mechanics with biological physiology.
   - **Agricultural & Biosystems Engineering:** Farm mechanization, soil conservation, bio-process engineering.
   - **Biotechnology & Bioinformatics:** Computational biology, genetic sequencing, mathematical modeling of biological populations.
   - **Environmental Science & Ecology:** Quantitative environmental tracking and ecological risk modeling.
3. **JUPEB Point Grading Matrix:**
   - A = 5 points, B = 4 points, C = 3 points, D = 2 points, E = 1 point, F = 0 points (+1 bonus point for 0 fails).
   - Maximum points = 16 points.

⚡ **JUPEB Exam Trap:** Many candidates choose PMB hoping to enter Clinical Medicine and Surgery (MBBS). However, **Medicine and Surgery strictly requires Chemistry (PBC: Physics, Chemistry, Biology)** across all Nigerian universities! PMB is tailored for biomedical, computational, and biophysical sciences, not medical doctor direct entry!

## 3. High-Yield Quantitative Problem Solving in PMB

#### Worked Micro-Example: Hardy-Weinberg Population Genetics
In a population of 10,000 individuals, 16% exhibit an autosomal recessive phenotype (such as albinism, $q^2$). Calculate the percentage of heterozygous carriers in this population:
1. Recessive allele frequency ($q$):
   $$q^2 = 0.16 \implies q = \sqrt{0.16} = 0.40$$
2. Dominant allele frequency ($p$):
   $$p + q = 1 \implies p = 1 - 0.40 = 0.60$$
3. Heterozygous carrier frequency ($2pq$):
   $$2pq = 2 \times (0.60) \times (0.40) = 0.48 = \mathbf{48\%}$$
   Out of 10,000 individuals, exactly 4,800 are asymptomatic carriers of the recessive trait.

## 4. JUPEB Examination Diagnostic Questions

**Question 1 (JUPEB Biology):** In a cross between two heterozygous tall pea plants ($Tt \times Tt$), what proportion of the tall offspring is expected to be homozygous dominant?  
*(A) 1/4  
(B) 1/3  
(C) 1/2  
(D) 2/3*  
**Answer:** (B) 1/3 (Explanation: The genotypic ratio is $1\text{ }TT : 2\text{ }Tt : 1\text{ }tt$. Restricting to the *tall offspring* ($TT$ and $Tt$, which make up 3 parts), exactly 1 out of these 3 parts is homozygous dominant $TT$).

**Question 2 (JUPEB Mathematics):** Evaluate the definite integral $\int_0^2 (3x^2 - 2x + 1)\,dx$:  
*(A) 4  
(B) 6  
(C) 8  
(D) 10*  
**Answer:** (B) 6 (Explanation: $\int (3x^2 - 2x + 1)\,dx = x^3 - x^2 + x$. Evaluating from 0 to 2: $[2^3 - 2^2 + 2] - [0] = [8 - 4 + 2] = 6$).
