# Contemporary Pakistan — Current Affairs

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your PPSC paper.

**Contemporary Pakistan — Current Affairs** in the PPSC syllabus covers post-1973 constitutional, economic, security, and foreign-policy developments that examiners test as **factual MCQs** under Pakistan Affairs (≈3% weight).

| Anchor fact | Detail to memorise |
|---|---|
| **18th Amendment** | Passed **2010**; abolished the Concurrent & Legislative Lists, devolved 47 federal subjects to provinces. |
| **25th Amendment** | Passed **May 2018**; merged **FATA** into Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. |
| **7th NFC Award** | Announced **2009**; raised provincial share of federal divisible pool to **57.5%** (later adjusted). |
| **FATF grey list** | Pakistan listed **June 2018**, exited **October 2022**. |
| **CPEC** | Framework agreement **April 2015**; loans routed via **EXIM Bank of China**. |

#### High-yield pointers:
- PPSC asks *amendment number + year + subject*; never confuse the 18th with the 25th.
- The **National Action Plan (NAP)** has a **20-point** agenda, launched December 2014.
- Pakistan is a **non-signatory** of the NPT but observes a unilateral moratorium.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students preparing over weeks.

#### Constitutional and Federal Reforms
The **18th Constitutional Amendment (2010)** is the centrepiece of post-2008 federal restructuring. It removed the **Concurrent and Legislative Lists** from the Fourth Schedule, transferring subjects like education, health, and population planning to the provinces. A **Council of Common Interests (CCI)** mechanism was strengthened to manage inter-provincial disputes. By contrast, the **25th Amendment (2018)** specifically addressed the **FATA–KP merger**, extending the Constitution and Pakistan Penal Code to the tribal belt and creating the new **Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Tribal Districts** (renamed merged districts in 2022).

> Trap: Candidates often attribute FATA's merger to the 18th Amendment — it was the 25th.

#### Economic Indicators and Fiscal Architecture
PPSC frames economic questions around indicators reported by the **State Bank of Pakistan (SBP)** and **Pakistan Bureau of Statistics (PBS)**. The **National Finance Commission (NFC) Award** redistributes the federal divisible pool; the **7th Award (2009)** raised the provincial share from 46.5% to **57.5%**, and successive awards (8th in 2021) recalibrated the ratio along population and poverty/performance criteria.

| Indicator | Reporting body | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| **Policy rate** | SBP Monetary Policy Committee | Bi-monthly |
| **CPI inflation** | PBS | Monthly |
| **GDP growth** | PBS / SBP | Annual, quarterly revisions |
| **Foreign reserves** | SBP | Weekly |

#### CPEC and External Posture
**CPEC** is the flagship project of the **Belt and Road Initiative (BRI)**, with framework signing on **20 April 2015**. Financing is largely **concessional loans** through the **EXIM Bank of China**, not grants. The **Long-Term Plan (LTP)** runs to **2030** and includes **Special Economic Zones (SEZs)** at Rashakai, Dhabeji, Allama Iqbal Industrial City, and Gwadar.

#### Security and Accountability
**Operation Zarb-e-Azb** (June 2014–2017) targeted militant networks in North Waziristan; **Radd-ul-Fasaad** (2017) followed with nationwide de-weaponisation. **NAB** operates under the **National Accountability Ordinance 1999** and has been amended multiple times — a frequent PPSC question.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for thorough PPSC preparation.

#### Edge Cases and Examiner Traps
1. **FATF chronology** — Pakistan was first greylisted in June 2018, briefly moved to the blacklist warning in 2021, and formally exited in **October 2022** following the Plenary in Paris. Mixing 2022 and 2023 announcements is a common error.
2. **NFC provincial share** — the 57.5% figure is the **horizontal** share; *vertical* distribution (centre vs provinces) differs. Some awards added performance-based incentives.
3. **NPT vs CTBT** — Pakistan is a **non-signatory of the NPT** (since it never acceded) but has a voluntary **CTBT moratorium** and maintains Strategic Export Controls through the **Strategic Export Control Division (SECDIV)**.

#### Worked Example — Decoding a PPSC MCQ
Question: *"The Concurrent List of the Constitution of Pakistan was abolished through which amendment?"* — Answer: **18th Amendment, 2010**.
- Cue words: "Concurrent List", "abolished" → 18th, not 17th (Judicial Commission) or 19th (Judicial appointments).
- Year association: 2010 → post-18th-Amendment NFC talks (which produced the 7th Award in 2009 before the amendment).

#### Practice Prompts
- *"Name the body that sets Pakistan's policy rate."* — **State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) Monetary Policy Committee.**
- *"Which amendment merged FATA into KP, and in what year?"* — **25th Amendment, 2018.**

#### Strategic Exam Notes
PPSC typically allocates **1–3 marks** per Current Affairs MCQ. Weightage is light (≈3%), but high scorers score here because questions are factual and memorisable. Prioritise **amendment-number/year pairs**, **NFC percentages**, **CPEC milestones**, and **FATF dates** from the **PPSC syllabus portal** and the **National Assembly of Pakistan** website.
