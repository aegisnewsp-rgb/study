# Seerah — Umm Salama and Makana al-Shabab — Qatar Secondary Certificate Islamic Studies Notes

The MoEHE Grade 12 Islamic Studies Seerah (Prophetic Biography, السيرة النبوية) domain opens in Term 1 with the life of Umm Salama (أم المؤمنين أم سلمة رضي الله عنها) — Mother of the Believers — and continues in Term 2 with the role of youth in the Prophetic biography (مكانة الشباب في السيرة النبوية). Seerah is the foundational Islamic history module at Grade 12.

### Lite — Quick Review (1h-1d)

#### Core facts in one pass

- **Umm Salama (Hind bint Abi Umayya):** one of the Mothers of the Believers (Ummahat al-Mu'minin), wife of the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him). Known for her wisdom, her political counsel, and her role in the Treaty of Hudaybiyyah.
- **Her marriage to the Prophet:** after her first husband Abu Salama died from injuries sustained at the Battle of Uhud, the Prophet proposed and she accepted on condition that her children from her first marriage would stay with her.
- **Role of youth in the Seerah:** the youthful Companions who shaped the early Muslim community — Ali ibn Abi Talib, Usama ibn Zayd, al-Mus'ab ibn 'Umayr, Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas, Zayd ibn Thabit, and others.
- **Quranic reference:** Surah Al-Kahf 18:13 mentions the youthful believers who sought refuge in the cave — " ونبئهم عن الفتية الذين آمنوا بربهم وزدناهم هدى " — a Quranic basis for the spiritual station of believing youth.

#### Examiner traps

- Confusing Umm Salama (Hind bint Abi Umayya) with other Umm Salama narrations.
- Romanticising the youth of the Seerah — they were disciplined, trained, and committed to the mission, not merely enthusiastic.

### Standard — Regular Study (2d-2mo)

#### Umm Salama — her life before Islam

Hind bint Abi Umayya ibn al-Mughira belonged to the Makhzum clan of Quraysh — one of the wealthiest and most influential clans. Her father was a leader of his clan; her brother was Umar ibn Abi Salama. She was known before Islam for her wisdom and her poetry.

She married Abu Salama ibn Abd al-Asad — one of the early converts to Islam, related to the Prophet by alliance (Abu Salama was the Prophet's nephew through breastfeeding). They emigrated to Abyssinia in the first hijrah, then to Medina after the Prophet's hijrah.

Abu Salama was wounded at the Battle of Uhud (3 AH / 625 CE) and died of his injuries. The Prophet visited him before his death and prayed for him. Umm Salama's grief was immense — the Prophet consoled her with the famous hadith: "إنما الصبر عند الصدمة الأولى" (Patience is at the first strike).

#### Umm Salama — her marriage to the Prophet

The Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) proposed to Umm Salama after the death of her husband. She accepted on the condition that her children from her first marriage would remain in her care — a condition the Prophet honoured. The marriage took place in 4 AH.

Umm Salama was known for her political counsel. The famous hadith at the Treaty of Hudaybiyyah (6 AH / 628 CE) records her advice to the Prophet when the Muslims were distressed by the terms of the treaty. She advised him to perform the umrah rites himself and trust that Allah would vindicate the decision. The Prophet accepted her advice and the Muslims followed.

#### Her legacy

Umm Salama is credited with many hadith narrations — her wisdom and her deep engagement with the Quran made her a teacher of the early community. She lived until 59 AH (approximately 680 CE). Her tomb is in al-Baqi' cemetery in Medina.

#### Role of youth in the Seerah

The Quranic verse " وقل رب زدني علما " (And say: my Lord, increase me in knowledge, Ta-Ha 20:114) was reportedly revealed when the Prophet was praying for the youth of Islam. The classical scholars note that the youthful Companions were the most active in the da'wah (call) and the most willing to make sacrifices.

Examples: Ali ibn Abi Talib accepted Islam at age 10 and slept in the Prophet's bed during the Hijrah. Al-Mus'ab ibn 'Umayr was sent as the first ambassador to Medina at age 25. Usama ibn Zayd led the army of Mu'tah at age 18-20. Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas distinguished himself at the Battle of Uhud at a young age.

The lessons: training in Islam begins early, the role of youth is active and central, and the youthful energy must be channeled through discipline and learning.

### Deep — Long-Term Mastery (1mo-6mo)

#### Why Seerah carries marks at the MoEHE terminal

The MoEHE Seerah domain connects the abstract teachings of Islam to the lived example of the Prophet and the early community. The Grade 12 modules (Umm Salama and Makana al-Shabab) emphasise role models for contemporary Muslims — the Mothers of the Believers and the youthful Companions.

#### Exam technique

- For Umm Salama questions, name the events precisely (Treaty of Hudaybiyyah, the death of Abu Salama) and link them to her counsel.
- For Makana al-Shabab questions, cite specific youthful Companions by name and their contribution.

#### Common misconceptions (and the correction)

- "The Mothers of the Believers were passive figures." No — they were active in the community, in scholarship, in political counsel, and in education.
- "The youth of the Seerah were exceptional." They were disciplined and trained; their example shows what is achievable through commitment.
