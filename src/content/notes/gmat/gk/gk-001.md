# Ghana Geography and Physical Features

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

Ghana sits in West Africa between **4°44′N–11°11′N** latitude and **3°11′W–1°14′E** longitude, bordered by Côte d'Ivoire (west), Burkina Faso (north), Togo (east) and the Gulf of Guinea (south). The country covers about **238,533 km²** with a ~550 km coastline, and **Accra** is the capital.

- **Highest point:** Mount Afadjato (~885 m), Akwapim-Togo Ranges.
- **Lowest point:** Sea level, Atlantic coast.
- **Largest artificial lake:** Lake Volta (~8,500 km²), formed by Akosombo Dam (1965).
- **Main river system:** Volta River (Black + White + Red Volta) emptying into the Atlantic.
- **Climate belts:** Tropical rainforest (SW), Guinea savanna (middle), Sudan savanna (north).
- **Regions:** 16 administrative regions (post-2019 reorganisation).

> 💡 **High-Yield Memory Hook:** "**C**ôte d'**I**voire **W**est, **B**urkina **F**aso **N**orth, **T**ogo **E**ast, **A**tlantic **S**outh" → **C I W B F N T A S** walks the borders clockwise. Pair it with "**A**fadjato **A**bove, **A**tlantic **A**t zero" to lock in Ghana's highest and lowest points.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### Physiographic Regions

Ghana's terrain divides into four main physiographic units running roughly parallel to the coast. The **Coastal Plains** (≤100 km wide) are low, sandy and lagoon-dotted (e.g. Keta Lagoon). Inland, the **Volta Basin** forms central lowlands drained by the Volta system. North-west of the basin rise the **Ashanti Plateau** and **Kwahu Plateau** (300–600 m), while the eastern border is marked by the **Akwapim-Togo Ranges**, which contain Mount Afadjato, Ghana's highest point at approximately **885 m**.

#### Drainage and the Volta System

The drainage pattern is dendritic. The **Volta River** is formed by the confluence of the **Black Volta**, **White Volta** and **Red Volta** in the north-central lowlands; it flows south and empties into the Atlantic via the **Volta Estuary**. The **Akosombo Dam**, built across the Volta gorge in 1965, impounds **Lake Volta** — the world's largest artificial reservoir by surface area (~8,500 km²). **Lake Bosumtwi**, near Kumasi, is a famous impact crater lake in the Ashanti Region.

#### Climate vs Vegetation Matrix

| Zone (S → N) | Climate type | Rainfall pattern | Vegetation belt |
|---|---|---|---|
| South-west coast | Equatorial / Tropical rainforest | Double maximum (>1,500 mm/yr) | High Forest (Guinea-Congolian) |
| Middle belt | Wet-and-dry (Guinea) savanna | Single maximum, ~1,000–1,300 mm | Deciduous Forest → Guinea Savanna |
| North | Tropical (Sudan) savanna | Single maximum, ~800–1,000 mm + dry **harmattan** | Sudan Savanna |

> 💡 **High-Yield Memory Hook:** Rainfall drops as you move north — "**R**ainforest → **R**educed → **R**oughly 800 mm" (south to north). The double-maximum coast is sometimes called the **Equatorial** type; the single-maximum north is **Tropical/Sudan**.

#### 🎯 Exam-Level Worked Problem

**Question:** Which of the following correctly pairs a Ghanaian physical feature with its location?
(A) Mount Afadjato — Ashanti Plateau
(B) Lake Volta — Northern Region savanna
(C) Akwapim-Togo Ranges — Eastern border
(D) Volta Estuary — Burkina Faso border

#### Solution:
- (A) Wrong — Afadjato is in the **Akwapim-Togo Ranges**, not the Ashanti Plateau.
- (B) Wrong — Lake Volta lies mainly in the **Eastern/Volta Region**, created by the Akosombo Dam on the Volta River, not in the Northern savanna.
- (C) Correct — The Akwapim-Togo Ranges run along Ghana's **eastern border** with Togo and contain Mount Afadjato (~885 m).
- (D) Wrong — The Volta Estuary opens into the **Atlantic (Gulf of Guinea)** in the south, not Burkina Faso.

#### Answer: (C)

> ⚠️ **Examiner Trap:** Candidates often misread "Northern Region" as the savanna zone and assume Lake Volta sits there because the **Volta River** has northern tributaries. The lake itself is impounded in the south by Akosombo — its tributaries come from the north, but its basin is central-eastern Ghana.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Edge Cases and Adjacent Links

Three traps recur in GAT-style questions on this topic:

1. **Volta Basin ≠ Volta River.** The basin covers roughly 160,000 km² of central Ghana's drainage region; the Volta River is one watercourse within it.
2. **Capital confusion.** **Accra** is the national capital; **Kumasi** is the Ashanti regional capital — examiners exploit this swap frequently.
3. **Region count drift.** Pre-2019 Ghana had 10 regions; six new ones (Ahafo, Bono East, North East, Savannah, Oti, plus Western North) were created in 2019, taking the total to **16**. Older textbooks may still list 10.

#### Natural Resources and Strategic Links

Ghana's resource base historically earned it the name **Gold Coast**; it remains Africa's largest gold producer. **Cocoa** dominates agricultural exports, while **bauxite**, **manganese**, **timber**, **diamonds** and offshore **oil** (Jubilee field, production from 2010) round out the economy. These resources connect directly to neighbouring topics in **Gk**: Economic Geography and Ghanaian History (colonial trade in gold, the 1957 independence transition from Gold Coast to Ghana).
