# Quran — Surah Al-Hashr Memorisation and Surah Aal Imran Tafsir — Qatar Secondary Certificate Islamic Studies Notes

The MoEHE Grade 12 Islamic Studies Term 1 Quran domain pairs the memorisation of Surah Al-Hashr (الحشر) with a Tafsir module on selected verses of Surah Aal Imran (آل عمران) focused on taqwa (تقوى الله) and adherence to the religion of Allah (الاعتصام بدينه سبحانه وتعالى). Memorisation and Tafsir are the two halves of the Grade 12 Quran mastery expected at the MoEHE terminal.

### Lite — Quick Review (1h-1d)

#### Core facts in one pass

- **Surah Al-Hashr:** surah 59, Madani, 24 verses. Themes: the expulsion of the Banu al-Nadir from Medina, the divine attribute of Glorious and Mighty, the Quran as a reminder, and the Awesome. Verses 59:18-21 are widely memorised for daily supplication.
- **Surah Aal Imran:** surah 3, Madani, 200 verses. Themes: the unity of the divine message, the station of the family of Imran, the command to hold fast to the rope of Allah (3:103), the duty of taqwa.
- **Tafsir methodology at MoEHE Grade 12:** the textbook uses tafsir bi-al-ma'thur (interpretation based on the transmitted sources — Quran, Sunnah, sayings of the Salaf) and tafsir bi-al-ra'y (reasoned interpretation within the limits of the Arabic language and the Sharia).

#### Examiner traps

- Confusing Surah Aal Imran (Imran's family) with Surah Al-Imran (the family of Imran) — same surah, different transliteration.
- Mixing up Surah Al-Hashr (59) with Surah Al-Hujurat (49) — both are Madani, both about community relations, but the content is distinct.
- Quoting Tafsir Ibn Kathir without attribution — the MoEHE textbook uses multiple classical sources.

### Standard — Regular Study (2d-2mo)

#### Surah Al-Hashr memorisation plan

The surah has 24 verses. Memorisation strategy:

1. Read the verse aloud three times, applying tajweed.
2. Cover the text and recite from memory.
3. Repeat with the previous verses (rolling review).
4. After three full rolling reviews, recite the chapter without the text.

Daily target: 1-2 new verses plus the rolling review of all previously memorised verses. Surah Al-Hashr memorisation typically takes 2-3 weeks at this pace.

The final ten verses (59:21-24) are recommended for memorisation as a daily supplication: " لو أنزلنا هذا القرآن على جبل لرأيته خاشعا متصدعا من خشية الله ... " — they are part of the morning and evening adhkar in the Sunni tradition.

#### Surah Aal Imran — Tafsir of selected verses on taqwa

3:102 — " يا أيها الذين آمنوا اتقوا الله حق تقاته ولا تموتن إلا وأنتم مسلمون " (O you who believe, fear Allah as He should be feared, and do not die except as Muslims). Classical tafsir: " حق تقاته " is interpreted as obedience without rebellion, remembrance without forgetfulness, gratitude without ingratitude.

3:103 — " واعتصموا بحبل الله جميعا ولا تفرقوا " (And hold fast, all together, to the rope of Allah, and do not become divided). The "rope of Allah" is interpreted as the Quran, the Sunnah, the community of believers, or the covenant — all are valid interpretations in the classical tafsir tradition.

3:110 — " كنتم خير أمة أخرجت للناس " (You are the best nation produced for mankind). The verse establishes the Ummah's responsibility for enjoining good and forbidding evil — the connection between taqwa (individual) and the public mission of the community.

3:159 — " فبما رحمة من الله لنت لهم ولو كنت فظا غليظ القلب لانفضوا من حولك " (It was by the mercy of Allah that you were lenient with them; had you been harsh and hard-hearted, they would have dispersed from around you). A foundational verse on the Prophetic ethics of softness, consultation, and forgiveness.

#### Tafsir methodology

Classical Sunni methodology: (1) Tafsir by the Quran — verses explain other verses. (2) Tafsir by the Sunnah — explanations in hadith. (3) Tafsir by the sayings of the Companions (especially Ibn Mas'ud, Ibn Abbas, Ali). (4) Tafsir by the classical Arabic language. (5) Tafsir by reasoned ijtihad within the methodology of Ahl al-Sunnah wa al-Jama'ah.

### Deep — Long-Term Mastery (1mo-6mo)

#### Why memorisation and tafsir carry marks at the MoEHE terminal

The MoEHE Quran module for Grade 12 tests both fluency of memorisation and depth of understanding through tafsir. Surah Al-Hashr is the assigned memorisation in Term 1; Surah Al-Mujadila is the Term 2 memorisation (see qsscrel-* Term 2 notes). Tafsir of Surah Aal Imran verses on taqwa is the MoEHE Term 1 Tafsir module.

#### Exam technique

- For memorisation: recite slowly with tajweed; pause at every waqf sign; use the mu'dhin or a study partner as the listener.
- For tafsir: state the verse in Arabic first, then the translation, then the classical interpretation, then the contemporary application.
- Link every tafsir point back to taqwa as the unifying theme — the MoEHE module name ("تقوى الله والاعتصام بدينه") is the structural anchor.

#### Common misconceptions (and the correction)

- "Memorisation is just rote." No — MoEHE requires fluency with tajweed, not just memorisation.
- "Tafsir is personal opinion." No — within Ahl al-Sunnah, tafsir must be grounded in the transmitted sources or in the established Arabic language.
