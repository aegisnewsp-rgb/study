# Task 1 — Describing Visual Information — IELTS Writing Notes

IELTS Academic Writing Task 1 asks the candidate to describe some visual information in their own words — a graph, table, chart, or diagram. The candidate must write at least 150 words in about 20 minutes. Task 1 carries one-third of the Writing band score; Task 2 carries two-thirds.

> Verify the live Writing format and Task 1 mark weighting on https://ielts.org/ before planning revision.

---

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)

#### Core facts in one pass

- **Format:** describe a graph, table, chart or diagram in 150+ words.
- **Time:** about 20 minutes.
- **Mark weighting:** Task 1 carries one-third of the Writing band; Task 2 carries two-thirds.
- **Structure:** introduction (paraphrase), overview (key trend), body paragraphs (specific data).
- **Tense:** present simple for current state, past simple for past data.
- **Cohesion:** use appropriate linking devices (whereas, in contrast, similarly, while).

#### Examiner traps

- Writing more than 150 words by copying the question — time spent on padding costs marks on Task 2.
- Listing every data point without selecting the key features.
- Using present simple for past data (or vice versa).

---

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)

#### Structure of a Task 1 response

1. **Introduction (1-2 sentences):** paraphrase the question prompt. Example: "The graph shows the percentage of households with internet access in three countries between 2000 and 2020."
2. **Overview (2-3 sentences):** state the key trend or comparison. Example: "Overall, internet access increased in all three countries, but the rate of increase was highest in country A."
3. **Body paragraph 1:** describe specific data or comparisons.
4. **Body paragraph 2:** describe further specific data or comparisons.

#### Describing trends

Use appropriate vocabulary:

- **Upward trend:** increase, rise, grow, climb, surge, rocket, soar, escalate.
- **Downward trend:** decrease, fall, drop, decline, plunge, plummet, slump, dip.
- **Stable trend:** remain steady, stay constant, level off, plateau.
- **Modifiers:** slightly, gradually, steadily, rapidly, sharply, dramatically, significantly.

#### Describing comparisons

Use comparison vocabulary:

- **Similarity:** similarly, likewise, in the same way, both, also, as well as.
- **Difference:** whereas, while, in contrast, however, on the other hand, unlike.
- **Degree:** much, far, significantly, considerably, slightly, marginally.

### 🔴 Deep — Long-Term Mastery (1mo–6mo)

#### Where Task 1 appears across IELTS Writing

Task 1 is always the first task in IELTS Academic Writing, accounting for one-third of the Writing band. Strong Task 1 performance is the baseline; weak Task 1 performance caps the band even if Task 2 is strong.

#### Common misconceptions (and the correction)

- "More data points score higher." No — selecting the key features scores higher than listing everything.
- "Paraphrasing is not required." Yes — Task 1 expects a paraphrased introduction, not a copy of the prompt.
- "Past data uses present tense." No — past data uses past simple.

#### Exam technique

- Spend 3-5 minutes planning the structure before drafting.
- Group data points into 2-3 categories (e.g. by time period, by region, by trend).
- Use the overview to state the key trend, not the details.
