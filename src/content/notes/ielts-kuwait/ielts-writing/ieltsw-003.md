# Task 1 — Process and Map Description — IELTS Writing Notes

Two less common but regularly appearing Task 1 formats are the process diagram and the map. Process diagrams describe a natural or manufactured process with sequenced steps. Map tasks describe changes between two stages of a location. Each requires specific vocabulary and structure.

> Verify the live Writing format on https://ielts.org/ before planning revision.

---

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)

#### Core facts in one pass

- **Process diagrams:** describe a natural or manufactured process with sequenced steps.
- **Map tasks:** describe changes between two stages of a location.
- **Tense:** present simple passive for processes; past simple or present simple for maps.
- **Sequence markers:** first, then, subsequently, finally, after that, in the next stage.
- **Directional vocabulary:** north, south, east, west, opposite, adjacent, in the corner, in the centre.

#### Examiner traps

- Using past tense for a current process (or vice versa).
- Missing a step in the sequence.
- Confusing the location of items on a map.

---

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)

#### Process diagrams

Structure of a process diagram response:

1. **Introduction (1-2 sentences):** paraphrase the prompt and state what the process shows.
2. **Overview (1-2 sentences):** state the key stages of the process.
3. **Body paragraph 1:** describe the first half of the process.
4. **Body paragraph 2:** describe the second half of the process.

Use present simple passive: "The raw material is heated," "The mixture is then cooled."

#### Map tasks

Structure of a map response:

1. **Introduction (1-2 sentences):** paraphrase the prompt and state the two maps.
2. **Overview (1-2 sentences):** state the key changes between the two maps.
3. **Body paragraph 1:** describe specific changes in one area.
4. **Body paragraph 2:** describe specific changes in another area.

Use appropriate directional vocabulary and compare the two maps systematically.

---

### 🔴 Deep — Long-Term Mastery (1mo–6mo)

#### Where process and map tasks appear across IELTS Writing

Process and map tasks appear occasionally in IELTS Academic Writing Task 1. They are less common than graphs and charts but equally tested.

#### Common misconceptions (and the correction)

- "Process diagrams use active voice." No — present simple passive is the standard.
- "Map tasks describe only one map." No — they describe changes between two maps.
- "Process diagrams can skip steps." No — every step must be described.

#### Exam technique

- For process diagrams, identify each step before writing. Use present simple passive.
- For map tasks, identify the key changes before writing. Use comparative language ("compared to the first map").
