# Indian Economy

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)

> A fast scan of the 1991 LPG turn, the GDP family, and the four RBI levers that show up most in MCQs.

India became a mixed economy in 1947, ran five-year plans through the Planning Commission (1950–2014), then switched in 2015 to NITI Aayog, a think-tank with no fund-allocation power. The defining break was 1991: PM P.V. Narasimha Rao and FM Manmohan Singh pushed Liberalization, Privatization and Globalization, ending the Licence Raj.

- **National income ladder (₹):** GDP → NDP = GDP − Depreciation → NNP = NDP + NFIA. NFIA is factor income from abroad minus income paid abroad.
- **RBI's four hot levers:** Repo Rate (short-term lending to banks), Reverse Repo Rate, CRR (cash with RBI), SLR (gold + govt securities with the bank itself). SLR ≥ CRR always.
- **CPI vs WPI:** CPI = retail, published by NSO-MoSPI; WPI = wholesale, published by DPIIT. RBI watches CPI for policy.
- **GST cut-off:** rolled out 1 July 2017, subsuming central excise, service tax and VAT. Petroleum products and alcohol stayed outside.
- **FDI vs FPI:** FDI carries managerial control (DPIIT-regulated); FPI is passive securities buying (SEBI-regulated).

> 💡 **Memory Hook:** "**G-D-N-N** = Gross→Depreciate→Net, then **A**dd abroad income" — track GDP, NDP, NNP in that order, then slap NFIA on top.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)

> Mechanism-level view: how the four national-income aggregates connect, who controls which lever, and how examiners twist CRR/SLR pairs.

#### National Income Aggregates

Every macro question in CMAT reduces to one of four figures. Build them in this exact order so the units line up:

1. **GDP at Market Price** = C + I + G + (X − M), where C is private consumption, I is investment, G is government spending, X exports, M imports (all in ₹).
2. **NDP** = GDP − Depreciation (consumption of fixed capital, ₹).
3. **NNP at Market Price** = NDP + NFIA, where NFIA = factor income earned abroad − factor income paid abroad (₹).
4. **NNP at Factor Cost (National Income)** = NNP at Market Price − Indirect Taxes + Subsidies (₹).

Per Capita Income (₹/person) = National Income ÷ Mid-year Population. Two countries can share GDP rank 5 yet diverge sharply on per capita — a frequent trap.

#### Fiscal vs Monetary — Who Does What

| Aspect | Fiscal Policy (Government) | Monetary Policy (RBI) |
|---|---|---|
| Instruments | Taxes, subsidies, public spending, borrowing | Repo, Reverse Repo, CRR, SLR, MSF, OMO |
| Document | Union Budget | Monetary Policy Statement (bi-monthly) |
| Deficit measures | Fiscal, Revenue, Primary Deficits | Money multiplier = 1 / CRR |
| Goal | Growth, equity, employment | Price stability, liquidity |

> 📌 **Formula Check:** Money Multiplier = 1 / CRR. With CRR = 4%, the maximum theoretical money creation per ₹1 of fresh reserve is ₹25. Dimensionless ratio.

#### Banking & Market Architecture

- **Scheduled vs Non-Scheduled Commercial Banks:** Scheduled banks have RBI-paid paid-up capital ≥ ₹5 lakh and are eligible for RBI borrowing.
- **Specialised institutions:** NABARD (agriculture), SIDBI (MSME), EXIM Bank (trade finance), NHB (housing).
- **Capital markets:** Primary (IPO, FPO) issues new securities; Secondary (BSE founded 1875, NSE 1992) trades existing ones. SEBI became a statutory body via the SEBI Act, 1992 (non-statutory from 1988).

#### Frequently Twisted Pairs

| Concept A | Concept B | The Line Examiners Cross |
|---|---|---|
| CRR | SLR | CRR = cash with RBI; SLR = gold + govt securities kept by the bank itself |
| WPI | CPI | WPI = wholesale (DPIIT publishes); CPI = retail (NSO-MoSPI publishes) |
| FDI | FPI / FII | FDI = control + lasting interest; FPI = portfolio, no control |
| Fiscal Deficit | Primary Deficit | Primary = Fiscal − Interest Payments |
| Revenue Deficit | Fiscal Deficit | Revenue = Revenue Expenditure − Revenue Receipts only |
| Planning Commission | NITI Aayog | Plans had force + funds; NITI advises, no fund power |

#### Worked MCQ Walkthrough

**Question:** The difference between Gross Domestic Product at Market Price and Net National Product at Factor Cost equals which of the following?  
(A) Depreciation + Indirect Taxes − Subsidies + NFIA  
(B) Depreciation − Indirect Taxes + Subsidies − NFIA  
(C) Depreciation + Indirect Taxes + Subsidies − NFIA  
(D) Depreciation − Indirect Taxes − Subsidies + NFIA

**Step 1.** Start from GDP<sub>MP</sub>. Move to NDP<sub>MP</sub> by subtracting Depreciation.
**Step 2.** Move to NNP<sub>MP</sub> by adding NFIA (since NNP = NDP + NFIA).
**Step 3.** Convert to NNP<sub>FC</sub> by subtracting Indirect Taxes and adding Subsidies.

So GDP<sub>MP</sub> − NNP<sub>FC</sub> = Depreciation − NFIA + Indirect Taxes − Subsidies, giving **Option (A)**.

> ⚠️ **Examiner Trap:** Option (B) inverts the NFIA sign. Students forget that going *from* NDP to NNP adds NFIA, so the reverse subtraction flips NFIA's sign in the difference.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)

> Edge cases, multilateral architecture, and the schemes that quietly replace older five-year-plan vocabulary in CMAT papers.

#### Reforms & Planning — The Sequence That Matters

- 1947: Mixed-economy framing adopted; public-sector dominance in heavy industry.
- 1950: Planning Commission set up by executive resolution (extra-constitutional, hence dissolvable without Parliament).
- 1966–67: Green Revolution under M.S. Swaminathan and C. Subramaniam; foodgrain self-sufficiency follows.
- 1970: Operation Flood (White Revolution) under Verghese Kurien — India becomes the world's largest milk producer.
- 1991: LPG reforms dismantle the Licence Raj; industrial licensing scrapped for most sectors, FIPB route for FDI opens.
- 2014: Jan Dhan Yojana (financial inclusion), Make in India launched.
- 2015: NITI Aayog replaces the Planning Commission; the 12th Plan period (2012–2017) is the last.
- 2017: GST rollout on 1 July with a four-slab structure (5, 12, 18, 28%).
- 2020: Atmanirbhar Bharat package announced during the COVID-19 shock.

#### Inflation — Why Two Indices Coexist

WPI tracks wholesale/bulk transactions and feeds into the GDP deflator arithmetic. CPI is the retail basket that determines real wages and features in most central-government compensation formulas. Because fuel and food weights differ, the two diverge sharply in monsoon years — a classic CMAT "which index moved how much" trap.

| Index | Measures | Publisher | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| CPI (Combined) | Retail prices | NSO, MoSPI | RBI inflation target |
| WPI | Wholesale prices | Office of Economic Adviser, DPIIT | Producer-side inflation, GDP deflation |

#### Multilateral Bodies — Don't Mix Them Up

- **IMF** (Bretton Woods, 1945): balance-of-payments support, exchange-rate stability.
- **World Bank** = IBRD + IDA; development lending, not trade rules.
- **WTO** (1995, succeeded GATT 1947): global trade rules, dispute settlement.
- **AIIB** (2016, Beijing-headquartered) and **NDB / BRICS Bank** (2015, Shanghai-headquartered): newer infra-financing alternatives.

#### Contemporary Schemes CMAT Loves to Ask

| Scheme | Launch Year | Core Target |
|---|---|---|
| Jan Dhan Yojana | 2014 | Zero-balance bank accounts |
| MUDRA Yojana | 2015 | Collateral-free MSME loans |
| Start-Up India | 2016 | Three-year tax holiday for recognised start-ups |
| Atmanirbhar Bharat | 2020 | Local manufacturing + import substitution |
| PMJDY | Re-launched 2014 | Direct Benefit Transfer plumbing |

#### Advanced Traps and Edge Cases

1. SEBI was non-statutory from 1988, statutory from 1992.
2. SLR must stay ≥ CRR at all times; they are independent ratios, not substitutes.
3. GST does not subsume petroleum (crude, aviation fuel, HSD, petrol, diesel) and alcohol for state governments — these remain outside the constitutional 101st Amendment levy.
4. Per capita income uses mid-year population, not census population.
5. "Highest GDP" never implies "highest HDI" — Norway and Switzerland outrank China and India on HDI despite smaller GDP.
6. NITI Aayog has a CEO, Vice-Chairperson, and Governing Council with CMs; the Chairperson is the Prime Minister.
7. Current Account Deficit widens when imports + remittances-out exceed exports + remittances-in; it is *not* the same as Trade Deficit, which excludes services and transfers.
8. Five Year Plans ran from the 1st (1951–56) through the 12th (2012–17); there is no 13th Plan — NITI Aayog replaced the planning framework entirely.

#### Two Practice Prompts

1. A question gives you "primary deficit" and asks for "fiscal deficit." State the relation in words, write the formula, then explain why a government focused on debt reduction targets the primary deficit rather than the headline fiscal deficit.
2. Compare CRR and SLR on five dimensions — issuer of the obligation, form in which the reserve is held, current statutory floor, impact on lending capacity, and which one RBI can vary without legislative change. Conclude which lever hits bank profitability faster when raised by 50 basis points.
