# Notable Pakistanis and World Personalities

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

**Father of the Nation**: **Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah** — first Governor-General of Pakistan, delivered the Constituent Assembly speech on 11 August 1947 defining state principles. **Poet of the East**: **Allama Muhammad Iqbal** — philosopher of Pakistan who delivered the Allahabad Address (1930) conceptualising a Muslim homeland and authored the poem *Khudi*. **First Prime Minister**: **Liaquat Ali Khan** — assassinated 16 October 1951 at Rawalpindi's Company Bagh. **First female head of a Muslim-majority state**: **Fatima Jinnah**, candidate in 1965 presidential election. **First Muslim Nobel physicist**: **Dr. Abdus Salam** (Nobel 1979) for electroweak unification. **Youngest Nobel laureate**: **Malala Yousafzai** (Peace, 2014, age 17). World icons to remember: **Nelson Mandela** (Peace 1993), **Mother Teresa** (Peace 1979), **Albert Einstein** (Physics 1921), **Marie Curie** (Physics 1903, Chemistry 1911 — only person with two Nobels).

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### Pakistani Founders and National Icons
- **Muhammad Ali Jinnah (1876–1948)** — barrister from Lincoln's Inn, led the All-India Muslim League, negotiated Partition through the Mountbatten Plan, became first Governor-General on 14 August 1947. His 11 August 1947 speech pledged equal citizenship regardless of caste or creed.
- **Allama Iqbal (1877–1938)** — bar-at-law from Cambridge, recipient of British knighthood, author of *Bang-e-Dra*, *Bal-e-Jibril*, *Asrar-e-Khudi*; his 1930 Allahabad Address articulated the foundational concept of a separate Muslim state in northwestern British India.
- **Liaquat Ali Khan (1895–1951)** — co-founder of Muslim League with Jinnah; first PM, presented the Objectives Resolution on 12 March 1949.
- **Fatima Jinnah (1893–1967)** — trained dentist from Madras, founded All-Pakistan Women's Association; ran against Ayub Khan in 1965.

#### Pakistani Scientists and Humanitarian Icons
- **Dr. Abdus Salam (1926–1996)** — physicist from Jhang; Nobel Prize 1979 for electroweak unification with Sheldon Glashow and Steven Weinberg; also founded ICTP Trieste.
- **Dr. Abdul Qadeer Khan (1936–2021)** — metallurgist, credited for Pakistan's nuclear and centrifuge enrichment capability (URENCO-era stolen blueprints).
- **Malala Yousafzai (b. 1997)** — Swat activist shot by Taliban in 2012; co-laureate Nobel Peace Prize 2014 with Kailash Satyarthi.
- **Edhi family**: **Abdul Sattar Edhi** — founder of Edhi Foundation (1951), world's largest volunteer ambulance network.

#### International Leaders
**UN Secretaries-General** to memorise: **Trygve Lie** (Norway, 1946–1952), **Dag Hammarskjöld** (Sweden, 1953–1961 — died in plane crash), **U Thant** (Burma/Myanmar, 1961–1971), **Kurt Waldheim** (Austria, 1972–1981), **Javier Pérez de Cuéllar** (Peru, 1982–1991), **Boutros Boutros-Ghali** (Egypt, 1992–1996), **Kofi Annan** (Ghana, 1997–2006 — Nobel Peace 2001), **Ban Ki-moon** (South Korea, 2007–2016), **António Guterres** (Portugal, 2017–present).

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)

#### Chronology of Pakistani Heads of State
**Governors-General**: Jinnah (1947–1948), Khawaja Nazimuddin (1948–1951), Ghulam Muhammad (1951–1955), Iskander Mirza (1955–1956). **Presidents**: Iskander Mirza (1956–1958), Ayub Khan (1958–1969), Yahya Khan (1969–1971), Zulfikar Ali Bhutto (1971–1973), Fazal Ilahi Chaudhry (1973–1978), Muhammad Zia-ul-Haq (1978–1988), Ghulam Ishaq Khan (1988–1993), Farooq Leghari (1993–1997), Muhammad Rafiq Tarar (1998–2001), Pervez Musharraf (2001–2008), Asif Ali Zardari (2008–2013), Mamnoon Hussain (2013–2018), Arif Alvi (2018–2024), Asif Ali Zardari (2024–present). **Prime Ministers** sequence begins with Liaquat Ali Khan and includes: **Benazir Bhutto** (PM 1988–1990, 1993–1996 — first female PM of a Muslim country), **Nawaz Sharif** (three terms), and **Imran Khan** (2018–2022 — Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf founder, 1992 Cricket World Cup captain).

#### Common Traps in MCQs
1. **"Pakistan Resolution"**: passed 23 March 1940 at Lahore's Minto Park (now Iqbal Park) — sometimes wrongly attributed to 1947 or to Iqbal himself; it was moved by **A.K. Fazlul Huq**.
2. **"Poet of the East"** title: given to **Iqbal**, not Faiz Ahmed Faiz or Mirza Ghalib.
3. **Nobel categories**: Abdus Salam received **Physics**, not Peace. Malala received **Peace**, not Literature.
4. **Marie Curie** is the **only person** with Nobel Prizes in two different sciences — not Einstein.
5. **Nelson Mandela** served **27 years in prison** (1962–1990), became President in 1994.
6. **Mother Teresa** was born in Skopje (then Ottoman Empire, now North Macedonia) — not Indian by birth.
