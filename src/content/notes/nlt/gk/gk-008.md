# Pakistan's Administrative System

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

Pakistan runs a **federal parliamentary democracy** under the 1973 Constitution, with power divided between the federal government in Islamabad, four provinces (Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Balochistan), Gilgit-Baltistan, AJK, and the Islamabad Capital Territory. The **President is the ceremonial head of state**; the **Prime Minister is the head of government**, leading the Federal Cabinet and the National Assembly majority. Parliament (Majlis-e-Shoora) is **bicameral**: the National Assembly (342 seats, 5-year term) and the Senate (96 members, 6-year term, equal provincial representation).

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### Constitutional Foundation
The 1973 Constitution defines Pakistan as a **Federation** with parliamentary features. Legislative competence is split through the **Fourth Schedule** into the Federal Legislative List (defence, foreign affairs, currency), the Concurrent Legislative List (criminal law, contracts), and the Provincial Legislative Lists (local government, agriculture, health).

#### Federal Executive
The **President**, elected by both houses of Parliament plus provincial assemblies, signs bills into law and appoints the Prime Minister, Chief Justice, and Governors. The **Prime Minister** commands the National Assembly's confidence and chairs the Federal Cabinet, controlling executive policy. The President acts on the PM's advice in most matters under Article 48.

#### Parliament (Majlis-e-Shoora)
The **National Assembly** has 342 members serving a 5-year term: 272 general seats, 60 reserved for women, and 10 for non-Muslim minorities. The **Senate** has 96 members on 6-year staggered terms, with 23 general seats per province plus seats for FATA-successor areas, ICT, and technocrats.

#### Provincial Structure
Each province has a **Governor** (appointed, ceremonial), **Chief Minister** (executive head), **Provincial Cabinet**, and unicameral **Provincial Assembly**. The Chief Secretary leads the provincial bureaucracy, while the **Divisional Commissioner** supervises clusters of districts.

| Body | Members | Term | Selection |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| National Assembly | 342 | 5 years | Direct adult franchise |
| Senate | 96 | 6 years (staggered) | Equal provincial electorates |
| Provincial Assembly | Varies (Punjab 371) | 5 years | Direct adult franchise |

- The **Deputy Commissioner** is the district's chief civil officer (PAS/CSP cadre) coordinating revenue, magisterial, and development functions.
- The **Election Commission of Pakistan (ECP)** is a constitutionally autonomous body that conducts federal and provincial polls.
- NLT traps include confusing the Governor with the CM, or calling Parliament unicameral.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Judiciary and Constitutional Bodies
The court hierarchy runs **Supreme Court → High Courts → District Courts**, with the **Federal Shariat Court** auditing legislation against Quran and Sunnah. The **Supreme Judicial Council** judges judicial misconduct, while the **Federal Public Service Commission (FPSC)** recruits federal bureaucrats.

#### Special Territories
**AJK** and **Gilgit-Baltistan** hold distinct constitutional positions: AJK has its own president and prime minister under the AJK Interim Constitution Act 1974; Gilgit-Baltistan gained an elected assembly through the Gilgit-Baltistan Order 2018. Neither enjoys full provincial legislative autonomy over the Concurrent or Federal Lists.

#### District and Local Administration
The **Deputy Commissioner** (DC) heads the district, assisted by Additional and Assistant Commissioners, plus a District Police Officer (separate cadre). Local Government Acts in each province add tehsil, district, and city councils below the DC, though structures differ across Punjab, Sindh, KP, and Balochistan.

| Common Mistake | Correction |
| --- | --- |
| President = head of government | PM is head of government; President is head of state |
| Senate has population-based seats | Senate has equal provincial seats (23 each) |
| DC is a police officer | DC is civil/administrative (PAS cadre) |
| AJK/G-B are full provinces | They are special territories with limited legislative scope |
| NA term is 4 years | NA serves a 5-year term |
| Pakistan has unicameral Parliament | Parliament is bicameral (NA + Senate) |
