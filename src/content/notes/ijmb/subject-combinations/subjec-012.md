# IJMB Registration, Examination Process and University Admission Strategy

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

IJMB (Interim Joint Matriculation Board) is a one-year advanced-level programme approved by the Nigerian government and recognised by the **National Universities Commission (NUC)** as a route into **200-level** of Nigerian universities via Direct Entry (DE). To qualify, a candidate needs **five O'Level credit passes** (WAEC, NECO, or NABTEB) including English Language and Mathematics in not more than two sittings, plus successful completion of **three IJMB subjects** matched to the target degree, and a valid **JAMB DE form**.

- **Grade points (NUC scale):** A = 9, B = 8, C = 6, D = 4, E = 2; below E or absent = 0.
- **Typical aggregate:** (IJMB Score × 0.60) + (JAMB DE Score × 0.40).
- **Three-subject minimum average:** (S1 + S2 + S3) ÷ 3, must meet the university's DE threshold.

IJMB alone does not guarantee admission; JAMB DE registration through the **JAMB CAPS portal** is mandatory.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### Eligibility and Subject Combination

A candidate must hold at least five O'Level credit passes including English Language and Mathematics, obtained in no more than two sittings. The three IJMB subjects chosen at registration must **mirror the UTME subject pattern** of the intended university course. Medicine requires Physics, Chemistry, and Biology; Engineering requires Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry; Law commonly takes Government, History, and Literature in English or Economics. A mismatch between IJMB subjects and the target faculty invalidates the DE application even with high grades.

#### Registration Procedure

Registration runs through the **IJMB portal** (ijmb.com.ng). The candidate purchases a scratch card/PIN, fills bio-data, O'Level details, and subject choices, uploads a passport photograph, prints the registration slip, and presents it at an **accredited IJMB affiliated study centre**. Late registration or registration at an unaccredited centre disqualifies the candidate from sitting the examination.

#### Examination and DE Linkage

The IJMB written papers are held at designated centres, typically in March or April after the academic session. After results are released, the candidate must separately purchase and sit the **JAMB Direct Entry form**, selecting IJMB as the A'Level qualification and listing first, second, and third university choices on the JAMB portal. Admission is processed through **JAMB CAPS**.

| Stage | Action | Authority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pre-programme | 5 O'Level credits (≤2 sittings) incl. English & Maths | WAEC / NECO / NABTEB |
| Registration | PIN purchase, portal bio-data, study-centre enrolment | IJMB |
| Examination | Written papers at approved centres | IJMB |
| DE application | JAMB DE form + IJMB as A'Level | JAMB |
| Admission | 200-level placement via CAPS | University + JAMB |

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Score Aggregation Mechanics

IJMB final scores combine grade points across the three principal subjects. The percent-based IJMB Score equals (Grade Points ÷ Maximum Points) × 100, and most universities then compute a final DE aggregate as (IJMB Score × 0.60) + (JAMB DE Score × 0.40). For example, a candidate scoring grade points 24 out of 27 (three A's) earns IJMB Score = 88.9%. Combined with a JAMB DE Score of 70, the aggregate is 81.3, comfortably clearing competitive DE thresholds at federal universities.

#### Recognition Limits and Strategy

IJMB is accepted by federal, state, and most private Nigerian universities, but **not by all institutions**. Military universities, certain professional faculties, and some private universities restrict IJMB admission. Always verify the chosen institution's DE requirements on its official portal before committing to the programme. Apply early through CAPS to capture merit slots before supplementary lists.

#### Common Mistakes and Re-sit Rules

> Skipping JAMB DE because of strong IJMB grades is the single most common reason qualified candidates lose 200-level offers.

Other frequent errors include choosing Art subjects for Engineering, presenting O'Level results with more than two sittings, and patronising fake agents who issue fraudulent transcripts. Candidates who fall short of the cut-off may re-sit at the next IJMB session, subject to the limited re-sit allowance published by IJMB.

1. Verify the affiliated study centre on the IJMB portal before paying any fee.
2. Cross-check that your three IJMB subjects match the UTME combination of your target course.
3. Purchase the JAMB DE form in the same cycle as your IJMB results release.
