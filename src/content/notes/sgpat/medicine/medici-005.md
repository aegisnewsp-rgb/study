# BLS Chain of Survival

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> The Chain collapses the moment any single link is skipped — recall the five-link adult sequence before anything else on this page.

The Chain of Survival is the ERC/AHA sequence that ties bystander action to hospital discharge after cardiac arrest. SCFHS expects candidates to reproduce the adult chain verbatim and contrast it with the pediatric version.

- **Adult chain (5 links):** Recognition → Early CPR → Rapid defibrillation → ALS → Post-cardiac arrest care.
- **Pediatric chain (6 links):** adds **Prevention** at the front and **Recovery** at the back.
- **High-quality CPR (adult):** depth **5–6 cm**, rate **100–120 / min**, ratio **30:2**, full recoil, minimize interruptions.

| Link | What the candidate must know | Single most-tested number |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Recognition | Activate EMS, call for AED | — |
| 2 Early CPR | Compress hard & fast | 5–6 cm, 100–120/min |
| 3 Defibrillation | VF/pVT only; survival falls **7–10 % per minute** of delay | < 3 min ideal |
| 4 ALS | Drugs, airway, 4 H's & 4 T's | — |
| 5 Post-care | PCI for STEMI, **TTM 32–36 °C** ≥ 24 h | 32–36 °C |

> 💡 **High-Yield Memory Hook:** **RCD-AP** — "**R**ecognition, **C**PR, **D**efib, **A**LS, **P**ost-care." Walk the chain left-to-right in OSCE stations; skipping one letter means a station-fail.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard tier summary line: a Saudi GP Board MCQ on this topic almost always tests link order, compression numerics, or the adult/pediatric mismatch.

#### Mechanism — Why the Chain Is Sequential

Each link buys time for the next. Cerebral perfusion pressure (CPP) falls to near zero within seconds of arrest; only external compressions restore a small forward flow (~25–30 % of normal cardiac output). For shockable rhythms (VF/pVT), the myocardium still has metabolic substrate for minutes — defibrillation within the first 3 minutes yields survival of roughly **50–70 %**, dropping by **7–10 percentage points every minute** defibrillation is delayed. CPR bridges that gap by perfusing the myocardium until a shock can be delivered.

#### Comparison Matrix — Adult vs Pediatric Chain

| Feature | Adult Chain | Pediatric Chain |
|---|---|---|
| Number of links | 5 | 6 |
| First link | Recognition & EMS activation | **Prevention** (injury, SIDS, anaphylaxis) |
| Last link | Post-cardiac arrest care | **Recovery** (neuro-rehab, family support) |
| Compression depth | 5–6 cm | ≈ 4 cm (infant) / 5 cm (child) |
| Ratio (2 rescuers) | 30:2 | **15:2** |
| AED pad size | Adult pads ≥ 8 kg | Pediatric pads < 25 kg; adult OK if unavailable |
| Initial rhythm most common | VF/pVT (~25 % of OHCA) | Asystole / bradycardia (~most) |

#### Worked Clinical Scenario

A 58-year-old man collapses at a shopping mall. Bystanders phone EMS at 12:00:00. A security guard begins hands-only CPR at 12:01:30. The mall AED is applied at 12:03:00 and delivers one shock; rhythm converts to sinus. EMS arrives at 12:06:00, obtains ROSC, transports to a PCI-capable centre. 12-lead ECG shows anterior STEMI.

1. **Link 1 (Recognition):** 12:00:00 — bystander activates EMS.
2. **Link 2 (Early CPR):** 12:01:30 — quality CPR running, depth ~5–6 cm, rate 110/min, CCF ~70 %.
3. **Link 3 (Defibrillation):** 12:03:00 — AED shock at **3 min** post-arrest → expected survival still **> 30 %**.
4. **Link 4 (ALS):** 12:06:00 — IV access, epinephrine 1 mg q 3–5 min, advanced airway, search for reversible causes (4 H's, 4 T's).
5. **Link 5 (Post-care):** PCI within 90 min, TTM **32–36 °C** for ≥ 24 h, structured ICU care.

> ⚠️ **Examiner Trap:** candidates often credit the AED alone with restarting the heart. Defibrillation only **defibrillates** — it terminates VF/pVT; it does not restart asystole or PEA. Without preceding CPR to prime the myocardium, late shocks fail.

#### Common Pitfalls the SCFHS Paper Exploits

- "Compression rate ≈ 100/min" — wrong. The upper bound is **120/min**; faster rates reduce stroke volume and depth.
- Confusing **CCF** with chest compression *duty cycle*. CQF monitors time spent on compressions as a fraction of total resuscitation time; **target ≥ 60 %**, ideally **≥ 80 %**.
- Pads placed directly over an implanted pacemaker/ICD or on a wet chest — pads must be offset ≥ 8 cm and the chest dried.
- Stating a single TTM number (e.g., "33 °C") — guideline accepts **32–36 °C**.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Edge-case tier summary line: the chain looks linear, but in real arrests it is a parallel loop — bystander CPR and EMS activation run concurrently, and post-care decisions often begin in the ALS link.

#### Boundary Conditions & Edge Cases

- **Traumatic arrest:** Chain is the same, but thoracotomy within 10 min replaces early defibrillation as the dominant link; survival depends on penetrating vs blunt mechanism.
- **Drowning / asphyxial arrest (pediatric):** Rescue breaths matter more — sequence is **30:2** single-rescuer, **15:2** two-rescuer, and the pediatric chain's **Prevention** link (supervision, pool fencing) is the highest-yield public-health intervention.
- **Pregnancy (>20 weeks):** Left lateral tilt 15–30°, manual uterine displacement, perimortem Caesarean within 4 min of maternal arrest if ROSC not achieved.
- **Opioid-associated arrest:** Naloxone has no proven mortality benefit; treat as standard cardiac arrest — the Chain does not bend for toxidromes.

| Scenario | Chain Modification | Exam-Relevant Point |
|---|---|---|
| Witnessed VF, AED 2 min away | CPR first, defib at 3 min | Survival still > 30 % |
| Unwitnessed arrest, EMS > 8 min | CPR for 2 min, then defib | "Charge while compressions continue" |
| ICD firing repeatedly | Magnet over device, then defib | Magnet suspends ICD shock detection |
| Hypothermic arrest | "Nobody is dead until warm & dead" | Defib max 1 shock before re-warming |
| Mechanical CPR device | Apply without interrupting ≥ 10 s pauses | Devices do not replace quality CPR |

#### Connections to Adjacent SCFHS Topics

- **ACLS algorithms (VF/pVT vs PEA/asystole):** the ALS link operationalises the Chain.
- **Anaphylaxis & asthma emergencies:** the pediatric **Prevention** link depends on recognising these as arrest precursors.
- **STEMI pathways:** the 5th link is functionally the cath-lab activation criterion — door-to-balloon ≤ 90 min.
- **Ethics & DNAR:** Recovery link includes structured family debrief and DNAR reconciliation post-ROSC.

#### Advanced Practice Prompts

1. A 4-year-old drowns in a backyard pool. A single rescuer arrives. State the chain you activate, the compression-to-ventilation ratio you will use, and the compression depth you will target. Justify each choice against the pediatric framework.
2. A 67-year-old with a dual-chamber ICD collapses; ICD has fired 6 times pre-EMS. On EMS arrival rhythm is VF. Sequence your management across all five adult links and explain how the ICD alters the defibrillation link.
