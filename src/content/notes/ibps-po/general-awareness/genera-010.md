# Static GK — Important Facts About India & the World

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

Static GK in IBPS PO covers fixed, non-changing factual knowledge — capitals, currencies, headquarters, firsts, awards, dances, dams, and key dates — that does not shift with current events. Roughly **8–12 questions** appear in the 50-question General Awareness section, drawn mainly from banking awareness, Indian polity, geography, history, and world organizations.

- **First Indian in space:** Rakesh Sharma (1984, aboard Soyuz T-11).
- **First woman PM of India:** Indira Gandhi (1966).
- **Mount Everest height:** 8,848.86 m (revised by the 2020 joint Sino-Nepal survey, not 8,848 m).
- **RBI:** established 1 April 1935 under the RBI Act 1934; HQ Mumbai since 1937 (was originally Kolkata).
- **Longest river (world):** Nile, ~6,650 km.
- **Highest waterfall:** Angel Falls, Venezuela (979 m).

> Memorize the four-column fact triplets: **Person → Role → Year**, **HQ → City → Country**, **Dam → River → State**, **Dance → State**.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### Indian Polity & Economy Anchors
The Constitution was **adopted on 26 November 1949** and **enacted on 26 January 1950**. The first Prime Minister was Jawaharlal Nehru (sworn in 15 August 1947), and the first President was Rajendra Prasad (1950). The Planning Commission was replaced by **NITI Aayog on 1 January 2015**. The RBI was nationalized on **1 January 1949**, distinct from its 1935 founding — examiners exploit this gap.

#### World Organizations Headquarters
Static-GK questions on international bodies are pure recall. The table below locks the most-tested pairings:

#### Indian Geography & Superlatives
- **Highest peak in India:** K2 / Godwin-Austen (8,611 m), in Ladakh — not Everest, which is in Nepal.
- **Longest Indian river:** Ganga (~2,525 km).
- **Largest desert (world):** Sahara; **largest cold desert:** Antarctica.
- **Major passes:** Zoji La, Rohtang, Nathu La, Khardung La.
- **Eight classical dances** with origin states must be memorized: Bharatanatyam (TN), Kathak (UP/North), Kathakali (Kerala), Kuchipudi (AP), Odissi (Odisha), Manipuri (Manipur), Mohiniyattam (Kerala), Sattriya (Assam — added in 2000).

> Trap: Sattriya belongs to **Assam**, not Tamil Nadu. Mohiniyattam and Kathakali both come from Kerala — do not mark them as the same item.

#### Firsts & Awards — Where Mistakes Cluster
- **First census in India:** 1872 (under Lord Mayo); first complete census 1881.
- **Bharat Ratna** is the highest civilian award, instituted in 1954; awarded for exceptional service in any field.
- **Booker Prize:** International Booker since 1969; rebranded Man Booker Prize 1969–2019, reverted to Booker Prize from 2019.
- **Nobel categories:** Physics, Chemistry, Medicine, Literature, Peace, Economics (Sveriges Riksbank Prize, since 1969).
- **First Indian Nobel laureate:** Rabindranath Tagore (Literature, 1913). First Indian in Nobel for Sciences: C. V. Raman (Physics, 1930).

#### Common Mistakes & Exam Strategy
1. Confusing RBI establishment (1935) vs nationalization (1949) vs HQ shift (1937) — three different years, three different facts.
2. Assigning Yen to Beijing instead of Tokyo — Brazil uses Real; Argentina uses Peso.
3. Mt Everest height 8,848 m is **outdated**; the 2020 Sino-Nepal survey settled on **8,848.86 m**.
4. UNESCO HQ is **Paris**, not Geneva; WHO is Geneva, not Paris.
5. **Time budget:** aim for ≤45 seconds per static-GK MCQ. Since the answer is fixed and recall-based, skip reasoning — straight recall or skip-and-return.

#### Practice Prompts
1. Match each dam to its river and state: Bhakra Nangal, Hirakud, Tehri, Sardar Sarovar, Nagarjuna Sagar.
2. State the headquarters and year of formation for: UN, UNESCO, WHO, IMF, WTO, ASEAN, IAEA.
