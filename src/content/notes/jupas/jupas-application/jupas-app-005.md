# Modification of Programme Choices After HKDSE Release — JUPAS Notes

This topic covers the modification window after HKDSE results are released. Each applicant is allocated a 48-hour time slot (announced from late June) during mid-July in which to add, remove, or reorder programme choices. Modification may ONLY be submitted within the allocated time slot — applicants who miss the slot cannot modify afterwards.

> Verify the live modification window dates on https://www.jupas.edu.hk/ before planning revision.

---

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)

#### Core facts in one pass

- **48-hour allocated time slot**: each applicant receives a personal 48-hour window for modification.
- **Time slot allocation announced**: from 24 June 2026 for the 2026 cycle. Candidates check their allocation from this date.
- **Modification window dates**: 16-18 July 2026 for the 2026 cycle.
- **Modification is restricted**: may ONLY be submitted within the allocated time slot — no late modifications.
- **What can be modified**: add, remove, or reorder programme choices within the 20-choice maximum.

#### Examiner traps

- Missing the allocated time slot — no late modifications are allowed.
- Failing to check the time slot allocation — applicants must log in from 24 June 2026 to check.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)

#### The modification process

**Step 1 — Check time slot allocation (from 24 June 2026 for the 2026 cycle)**:

Each applicant logs in to their JUPAS account to find out their allocated 48-hour modification time slot. The slot is published on the JUPAS website from this date.

**Step 2 — Prepare the modified choices**:

Before the modification window opens, the applicant should:

- Compare actual HKDSE results to predicted grades.
- Identify which programmes are now within reach (high admission probability).
- Identify which programmes are out of reach (low admission probability).
- Identify safety programmes (clearly below expected results).
- Plan a new choice list — which programmes to keep, which to add, which to remove, and which to re-band.

**Step 3 — Submit modifications during the allocated time slot**:

Modifications may ONLY be submitted within the allocated 48-hour time slot. The applicant logs in to the JUPAS system during the slot, makes the changes, and submits.

**Step 4 — Confirm the modifications**:

After submission, the applicant should:

- Log in and confirm the modifications were saved.
- Print or save a copy of the modified choice list.
- Contact JUPAS immediately if there is an issue.

#### Strategy for modification

**Strategy 1: Re-rank based on actual results**

If actual HKDSE results are:

- **Higher than predicted**: consider moving safety programmes to a higher band (Band B → Band A).
- **Lower than predicted**: consider moving ambitious programmes to a lower band (Band A → Band B or C).
- **As predicted**: minor adjustments only.

**Strategy 2: Check minimum entrance requirements**

Each programme publishes minimum entrance requirements (e.g. Level 3 or 4 in core subjects). If the actual results fall below a minimum entrance requirement, the programme becomes ineligible.

Candidates should verify that all chosen programmes' minimum entrance requirements are met before submitting modifications.

**Strategy 3: Consider the band structure**

- Band A should contain programmes the applicant has a reasonable chance of admission to (based on results).
- Band B should contain realistic programmes.
- Band C should contain safety programmes.

**Strategy 4: Use expected-score ranges as guidance**

Most programmes publish expected-score ranges from the previous cycle. The applicant should:

- Compare their actual results to the expected-score ranges.
- Use the ranges as guidance, not guarantees (cutoffs vary by cycle).

#### Exam technique

- **For "describe the modification process" questions**: state the time slot allocation, the modification window dates, and the constraint that modifications may only be made during the allocated slot.
- **For "strategy" questions**: cite specific scenarios (results higher than predicted, lower than predicted).

#### Common misconceptions (and the correction)

- "The modification window is open for everyone at the same time." No — each applicant has a personal 48-hour slot.
- "Modifications are accepted after the window closes." No — JUPAS does not accept late modifications.
- "Lower results mean you must accept any programme offered." No — applicants can decline offers or and seek a Clearing Round offer.
