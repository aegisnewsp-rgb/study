# Topic 8

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

The **Kingdom of Saudi Arabia** occupies roughly four-fifths of the Arabian Peninsula, covering about **2,150,000 sq km** in the southwest of Asia. The country is an **absolute monarchy** ruled by the House of Al Saud; the King holds the title *Custodian of the Two Holy Mosques*. The official capital is **Riyadh**, and the official language is **Arabic**.

Administration is divided into **13 regions** (manatiq), each subdivided into governorates. Two cities — **Mecca (Makkah al-Mukarramah)** and **Medina (al-Madinah al-Munawwarah)** — host the Two Holy Mosques and serve as the destinations for **Hajj and Umrah**.

**National Day** falls on **23 September**, commemorating the 1932 unification. The flag carries a green background with the white *shahada* and a sword. **Vision 2030**, launched in 2016, is the diversification roadmap. Saudi Arabia holds the **world's second-largest proven oil reserves** (after Venezuela); the currency is the **Saudi Riyal (SAR)**, pegged near **3.75 SAR = 1 USD**.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### Geography and Location
Saudi Arabia sits at the crossroads of Asia, Africa, and Europe, with coastlines on the **Red Sea** (west) and the **Arabian Gulf** (east). The Rub' al-Khali ("Empty Quarter") in the south is one of the largest continuous sand deserts in the world. Terrain is largely arid plateau, with mountain ranges such as the **Sarawat** running parallel to the Red Sea coast.

#### Administrative Structure
The Kingdom comprises **13 administrative regions**: Riyadh, Makkah, al-Madinah, al-Qassim, the Eastern Province, Asir, Tabuk, Hail, al-Jawf, Jizan, Najran, al-Bahah, and the Northern Borders. Each region contains governorates (*muhafazat*) headed by an emir appointed by royal decree.

#### Government and Symbols
- **System**: Absolute monarchy, governed by **Basic Law of Governance (1992)**.
- **Head of State**: The King, currently **Custodian of the Two Holy Mosques**.
- **Legislative body**: The **Shura Council** (Majlis al-Shura), an appointed consultative assembly.
- **Flag**: Green field, white *shahada* ("There is no god but God; Muhammad is the Messenger of God"), and a white sword.
- **Anthem**: *Nashid al-Istiqlal* (Anthem of Independence).

#### Economy
Saudi Arabia is the **world's largest exporter of crude oil**. **Ghawar Field** is the largest onshore oil field, and **Safaniya** is the largest offshore field. The **Saudi Arabian Oil Company (Saudi Aramco)** is the national petroleum company. **Vision 2030** under Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman targets diversification into tourism, mining, logistics, entertainment, and renewable energy. Landmark projects include **NEOM**, the **Red Sea Global** tourism initiative, and **Qiddiya**.

#### Demographics
The population exceeds **33 million**, of whom roughly **38–40% are non-Saudi residents**. The **Nitaqat (Saudization)** program sets quotas for employing Saudi nationals in the private sector.

#### Heritage
UNESCO World Heritage Sites include **Al-Hijr (Madâin Sâlih)** in Medina Region, **At-Turaif District in ad-Dir'iyah**, **Historic Jeddah (Al-Balad)**, **Rock Art in the Hail region**, and the **Al-Ahsa Oasis**.

#### Typical Exam Patterns
Qimiyah GK items test: matching cities to regions, identifying the correct Hijri-Gregorian founding date, distinguishing Vision 2030 megaprojects, and recognising flag/anthem symbols. **Numerical traps** (e.g. "12 regions" or "largest oil reserves") are common.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Edge Cases and Examination Traps
A frequent trap asks for "the largest proven reserves" — the correct answer is **Venezuela (~304 billion barrels)**, with Saudi Arabia ranked **second (~267 billion barrels)**. Do not confuse **production volume** (Saudi Arabia leads OPEC in output capacity) with **reserves**. Similarly, the Kingdom has the **world's largest desalination capacity**, desalinating more water than any other nation, a fact occasionally tested.

#### Founding and Calendar Conversion
The Kingdom was proclaimed on **21 Jumada al-Awwal 1351 H**, which corresponds to **23 September 1932** by the Gregorian calendar. National Day celebrations use the Gregorian date; Hijri-date questions require conversion using the Umm al-Qura calendar.

#### Demographic and Social Indicators
- Median age is around **29 years**, indicating a youthful population.
- **Saudization bands** in Nitaqat are colour-coded: *Platinum, Green (High/Mid/Low), Yellow, Red*.
- Female workforce participation has risen sharply under Vision 2030 reforms.

#### Vision 2030 Megaprojects
- **NEOM**: a $500-billion planned megacity in Tabuk Province, including *The Line*.
- **Red Sea Global** and **AMAALA**: luxury tourism destinations on the Red Sea coast.
- **Qiddiya**: entertainment and cultural city near Riyadh.
- **Diriyah**: heritage and cultural tourism project at the At-Turaif site.

#### Common Mistakes
1. Writing **12 or 14** regions instead of **13**.
2. Confusing **Mecca** with **Medina** when asked which city hosts the Prophet's Mosque (*Masjid an-Nabawi* — Medina).
3. Saying Saudi Arabia has the **largest reserves** (it is second).
4. Quoting the Hijri founding date as the National Day.

#### Practice Prompts
1. **Multiple choice**: "Which of the following is the official capital of Saudi Arabia?" — Answer: *Riyadh*.
2. **Numeric**: "How many administrative regions does Saudi Arabia have?" — Answer: *13*.
3. **Assertion–Reason**: "Saudi Arabia has the largest proven oil reserves in the world — therefore it is the largest oil exporter." Assess: the *reason* and *conclusion* are linked, but the *premise is false* (Venezuela holds the largest reserves), so the assertion fails.
