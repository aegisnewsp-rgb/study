# Quran — I'jaz al-Quran and Quranic Memorisation Methods — Qatar Secondary Certificate Islamic Studies Notes

The MoEHE Grade 12 Islamic Studies Term 1 Quran domain includes the inimitability of the Quran (i'jaz, إعجاز القرآن الكريم) and the memorisation methods (طريقة الحفظ) used to preserve the Quran through the generations. I'jaz is the theological claim that the Quran cannot be replicated by any human composition, and it is the foundation of the Quran's status as a miracle.

### Lite — Quick Review (1h-1d)

#### Core facts in one pass

- **I'jaz al-Quran:** the inimitability of the Quran — the claim that the Quran's literary, legislative, informative and predictive dimensions are beyond human replication.
- **Forms of i'jaz:** linguistic (balagha, rhetoric), legislative (the Sharia system), informative (historical and scientific knowledge), predictive (prophecies), and psychological (effect on the listener).
- **Quranic memorisation methods:** tahfiz (memorisation), tasmi' (listening to a hafiz recite), tikrar (repetition), talaqqi (receiving from a teacher), muraja'ah (review), and rabt (linking verses to context).

#### Examiner traps

- Treating i'jaz as just the linguistic challenge — it covers all dimensions of the Quran.
- Memorising without review — the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) emphasised review as much as new memorisation.

### Standard — Regular Study (2d-2mo)

#### The challenge of the Quran (التحدي)

The Quran itself challenges humans and jinn to produce a surah like it: " قل لئن اجتمعت الإنس والجن على أن يأتوا بمثل هذا القرآن لا يأتون بمثله ولو كان بعضهم لبعض ظهيرا " (Al-Isra 17:88). The classical scholars interpret this as a perpetual challenge across time — the inability of any literary composition, in any language, to match the Quran.

The pre-Islamic and early Islamic poets (al-Nabighah, al-A'sha, Tarafa, and the Makkan rhetoricians) attempted to match the Quran and failed; the historical record preserves their inability despite their acknowledged mastery of Arabic.

#### Dimensions of i'jaz

**Linguistic i'jaz:** the Quran's unmatched level of Arabic rhetoric — the precision of meaning, the music of sound, the structure of argument, the variety of style (from the shortest verses to the longest surahs). Modern Arabic linguists continue to document features unique to the Quranic register.

**Legislative i'jaz:** the Quran establishes a legal and ethical system that has guided 1,400+ years of community life across diverse cultures, languages, and historical conditions.

**Informative i'jaz:** the Quran contains historical references (the Pharaoh, the people of 'Ad and Thamud, the city of Iram) that pre-date modern archaeological confirmation; it contains statements about cosmology, embryology, and biology whose accuracy has been confirmed by modern science.

**Predictive i'jaz:** the Quran includes prophecies (the conquest of Makkah, the Persian and Roman interactions, the signs of the Last Day) that have been interpreted as evidence of divine knowledge.

#### Memorisation methods

The classical tahfiz curriculum at major institutions (Al-Azhar, the Prophet's Mosque in Madinah, the Islamic University of Madinah) follows these methods:

1. **Sab' (seven) readings:** memorisation in one of the seven canonical qira'at (readings) — Hafs, Warsh, Qalun, Al-Duri, Al-Susi, Ibn Kathir, Abu 'Amr. The MoEHE curriculum uses Hafs 'an Asim as the standard Qatar reading.
2. **Daily wirds (portions):** the student memorises a fixed amount per day (half a page, one page, two pages) and reviews the previously memorised portion.
3. **Tasmi' (recital before a teacher):** the student recites the memorised portion to a qualified teacher who corrects errors.
4. **Muraja'ah (review):** systematic review of older memorisation to prevent forgetting. The Prophet said "تعاهدوا القرآن فإنه أوشك أن يتفلت من أصحابه" — review the Quran, for it is more likely to slip away than a camel from its owner.

### Deep — Long-Term Mastery (1mo-6mo)

#### Why i'jaz and memorisation matter at the MoEHE terminal

The MoEHE Quran module tests understanding of i'jaz as the theological foundation for the Quran's status. Memorisation is part of the Grade 12 continuous assessment. Together, they bridge the linguistic study of the Quran and its spiritual discipline.

#### Exam technique

- For i'jaz answers, organise by dimension (linguistic, legislative, informative, predictive) — a structured answer scores higher.
- For memorisation, recite slowly with tajweed; mark each verse with a finger; pause at every waqf sign.

#### Common misconceptions (and the correction)

- "I'jaz is just about Arabic eloquence." No — the Quran claims i'jaz across linguistic, legislative, informative and predictive dimensions.
- "Memorisation is a talent, not a skill." Memorisation is a skill that improves with consistent practice; the hafidh's ability is built on daily discipline.
