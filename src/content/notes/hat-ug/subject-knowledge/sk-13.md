# Islamic Studies

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

Islamic Studies in HAT-UG carries 3% weightage under Subject Knowledge and tests factual recall through MCQs across Quran, Hadith, Seerah, Fiqh, and Islamic ethics. The exam distinguishes between two anchor frameworks: the **Six Articles of Faith** (Arkan al-Iman) covering belief in Allah, Angels, Holy Books, Prophets, Day of Judgment, and Qadar, and the **Five Pillars of Islam** (Arkan al-Islam) covering Shahadah, Salah, Zakat, Sawm, and Hajj.

- **Zakat rate**: 2.5% on qualifying wealth held for one lunar year above the nisab threshold.
- **Sahih al-Bukhari** was compiled by Imam al-Bukhari; **Sahih Muslim** by Muslim ibn al-Hajjaj al-Naysaburi, distinct scholars.
- **Battle of Badr** = 2 AH (624 CE); **Battle of Uhud** = 3 AH (625 CE); **Conquest of Makkah** = 8 AH (630 CE).

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### Core Frameworks: Belief vs Practice

The Six Articles of Faith describe *what a Muslim believes* (Iman), while the Five Pillars describe *what a Muslim practices* (Islam). Mixing these up is a recurring HAT-UG trap. Tawheed (Oneness of God) anchors the belief side; Salah (five daily prayers) anchors the practice side.

#### Sources of Islamic Law

Islamic jurisprudence (Fiqh) derives rulings from four ranked sources: **Quran** (primary revelation), **Sunnah** (practices of Prophet Muhammad, PBUH), **Ijma** (scholarly consensus), and **Qiyas** (analogical reasoning). When a direct text is absent, scholars apply Qiyas to extend a known ruling to a new case.

#### Hadith Classification

Hadith are graded by chain-of-narration (isnad) integrity. The six canonical collections (Kutub al-Sittah) are Bukhari, Muslim, Tirmidhi, Abu Dawud, Nasa'i, and Ibn Majah.

| Grade | Meaning | Reliability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sahih | Authentic, with unbroken chain of trustworthy narrators | Highest |
| Hasan | Good, with minor weakness but acceptable | Acceptable for practice |
| Daif | Weak, with notable defect in chain | Not used as legal proof |
| Mawdu | Fabricated | Rejected |

#### Common Confusions to Avoid

- **Zakat vs Sadaqah**: Zakat is obligatory at 2.5% on qualifying wealth above nisab; Sadaqah is voluntary.
- **Ramadan** is the **9th** Islamic month, not the 1st; Dhul Hijjah is the 12th (month of Hajj).
- The four Sunni Imams (Abu Hanifa, Malik, Shafi'i, Ahmad ibn Hanbal) founded **madhahib (schools of law)**, not Hadith collections.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Seerah Chronology and the Khulfa-e-Rashideen

Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) was born in 570 CE and passed away in 632 CE after delivering the Farewell Sermon at Hajjat al-Wada'. The four Rightly-Guided Caliphs (Khulfa-e-Rashideen) who led the ummah from 632–661 CE are Abu Bakr, Umar ibn al-Khattab, Uthman ibn Affan, and Ali ibn Abi Talib. HAT-UG MCQs frequently test the order of their rule and the year of the Prophet's migration (Hijrah, 622 CE).

#### Fiqh of Transactions and Family Law

Riba (interest), maysir (gambling), and khamr (alcohol) are categorically prohibited. A valid Nikah requires **ijab** (proposal) and **qabul** (acceptance) by two competent parties in the presence of two Muslim witnesses; **mahr** (dower) is the bride's right. Inheritance shares follow Quran 4:11, where a male heir generally receives twice the female share in corresponding categories.

#### Worked Micro-Example: Zakat Calculation

A person holds cash savings of PKR 200,000 and gold worth PKR 80,000 on the zakat due date, both held for one full lunar year above nisab.

1. Qualifying wealth = 200,000 + 80,000 = **PKR 280,000**
2. Zakat rate = **2.5%**
3. Zakat payable = 280,000 × 0.025 = **PKR 7,000**

#### High-Yield Practice Prompts

1. List the Six Articles of Faith in order and distinguish each from the Five Pillars of Islam.
2. Identify the compiler of Sahih Muslim, the year of the Battle of Badr, and the pillar governing Zakat rate.
