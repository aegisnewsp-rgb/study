# School Principal's Nominations (SPN) — JUPAS Notes

This topic covers the School Principal's Nominations (SPN) scheme. SPN is a separate direct-offer scheme run through participating schools. Each school may nominate up to a quota of students for direct admission to selected programmes before the JUPAS Main Round. SPN offers are made concurrently with JUPAS Main Round offers; recipients must accept or decline SPN offers before the JUPAS Main Round offer day.

> Verify the live SPN scheme rules on https://www.jupas.edu.hk/ before planning revision.

---

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)

#### Core facts in one pass

- **SPN**: a direct-offer scheme that allows school principals to nominate students for direct admission to selected programmes.
- **Quota**: each school has a limited quota of SPN nominations.
- **Concurrent with Main Round**: SPN offers are made at the same time as JUPAS Main Round offers.
- **Decision before Main Round day**: recipients must accept or decline SPN offers before the JUPAS Main Round offer day.
- **Selected programmes**: only certain programmes accept SPN nominations; the school selects which programmes to nominate for.

#### Examiner traps

- Treating SPN as a separate JUPAS offer — SPN is a different offer that overrides the Main Round offer in many cases.
- Missing the deadline — SPN decisions must be made before the JUPAS Main Round offer day.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)

#### How SPN works

**Nomination**: the school principal (or a designated teacher) nominates eligible students for direct admission to selected programmes. Each school has a quota of nominations.

**Eligibility**: schools determine their own criteria for nominating, but typical criteria include academic performance (HKDSE predicted grades or mock exam results), non-academic achievements (leadership, community service, special talents), and the student's fit with the programme.

**Processing**: the nominations are submitted to JUPAS before the HKDSE results release. JUPAS processes them concurrently with Main Round offers.

**Offer**: SPN offers are made on the same day as JUPAS Main Round offers. If a student receives both an SPN offer and a JUPAS Main Round offer, the SPN offer typically takes precedence (the student must decide on the SPN first).

**Decision**: the student must accept or decline the SPN offer by the published deadline (typically the day before the JUPAS Main Round offer day).

**Acceptance**: if the student accepts the SPN offer, they are committed to that programme (subject to meeting minimum entrance requirements based on actual HKDSE results).

**Decline**: if the student declines the SPN offer, they fall back to the JUPAS Main Round offer (if any).

#### Strategy for SPN candidates

**If nominated**:

- Research the SPN programme thoroughly: curriculum, career paths, fit with your goals.
- Compare to your other programme choices: which programme is your strongest preference?
- Consider that accepting the SPN means you cannot change your mind later (subject to the published rules).

**If not nominated**:

- SPN is a competitive scheme; not all schools participate, and not all nominated students receive offers.
- Focus on the JUPAS Main Round and Clearing Round.

#### Differences between SPN and Main Round

| Aspect | SPN | Main Round |
|--------|-----||------------|
| Decision maker | School principal | JUPAS system |
| Criteria | School-set | Published admission-score formula |
| Timing | Concurrent with Main Round | Main Round offer day |
| Acceptance deadline | Before Main Round day | Same day as offer (typically) |
| Override rule | SPN typically takes precedence | Subject to SPN outcome |

#### Exam technique

- **For SPN questions**: describe the nomination process; explain the concurrency with Main Round; state the decision deadline.
- **For strategy questions**: explain the trade-off between SPN and Main Round preferences.

#### Common misconceptions (and the correction)

- "SPN guarantees admission." SPN is a conditional offer; the student must still meet minimum entrance requirements based on actual HKDSE results.
- "SPN candidates can change their mind." No — once an SPN offer is accepted, the commitment is binding (subject to the published rules).
- "SPN is for top students only." SPN criteria vary by school; some schools use SPN for students who excel in specific areas (sports, arts, leadership).
