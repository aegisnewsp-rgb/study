# JUPEB Science Combination (PCM)

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

PCM is the JUPEB subject trio of **Physics**, **Chemistry**, and **Mathematics** that candidates register for to qualify for direct entry into Engineering, Computer Science, Mathematics, and most pure and applied Science degree programmes in Nigerian universities.

- **What it is:** A prescribed three-subject registration combination, not a single exam paper.
- **What it opens:** Engineering, Computer Science, Mathematics, Statistics, Physics, Chemistry, Biochemistry, Microbiology, and Technology-related faculties.
- **What it does NOT open:** Medicine, Pharmacy, Nursing, and most Biological Sciences — those require PCB.
- **Minimum standard:** Credit-level passes (typically graded A–D) across the three subjects; receiving universities commonly demand a total score of **5 points and above with at least two A grades**.
- **Verification source:** The official JUPEB subject combination list on the JUPEB portal (jupeb.veritas.edu.ng) and the chosen affiliated university's JUPEB programme page.

> 💡 **High-Yield Memory Hook:** "PCM = Pure Sciences, no Medicine." If the target course involves a stethoscope, pharmacy counter, or nursing ward, switch to PCB. Also remember the acronym **"P-C-M = Powerful Calculators & Machines"** — Engineering, Computing, Mathematics.

#### What PCM Actually Means in JUPEB

JUPEB (Joint Universities Preliminary Examinations Board) runs a one-year Advanced level programme whose result substitutes for UTME/Post-UTME in granting Direct Entry admission into 200-level in Nigerian universities. Each candidate must register exactly three subjects from the JUPEB-approved combination list. **PCM** pairs the three foundational quantitative-natural subjects and is the most-requested combination for candidates aiming at Engineering, Technology, and the computational sciences.

#### Comparison Matrix — PCM vs Other JUPEB Science Combinations

| Combination | Subjects | Suited Programmes | Unsuitable For |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PCM** | Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics | Engineering, Computer Science, Mathematics, Statistics, Physics, Chemistry, Industrial Chemistry, Biochemistry, Microbiology, Geology | Medicine, Pharmacy, Nursing, Veterinary Medicine |
| **PCB** | Physics, Chemistry, Biology | Medicine, Pharmacy, Nursing, Veterinary Medicine, Microbiology, Biochemistry, Botany, Zoology | Pure Mathematics, most Engineering, Computer Science |
| **PEM** | Physics, Economics, Mathematics | Economics, Accounting (in select universities), Actuarial Science (where allowed) | Pure Chemistry, Medicine |
| **PGM** | Physics, Geography, Mathematics | Geography, Meteorology, Environmental Science (limited universities) | Chemistry-intensive programmes, Medicine |

#### Required Steps for Choosing PCM Correctly

1. Confirm the target degree programme on the receiving university's JUPEB brochure.
2. Check whether the programme accepts PCB as an alternative or insists on strict PCM.
3. Confirm whether **Further Mathematics** is required instead of, or in addition to, Mathematics.
4. Register the three subjects with matching JUPEB subject codes on the portal.
5. Print and keep the registration slip — corrections are rarely allowed after submission.
6. Score at least the receiving university's minimum point threshold (commonly **5 points with at least two A grades**).

#### 🎯 Exam-Level Worked Problem

#### Question:
Adaeze wants to study **Computer Science** through JUPEB at University of Lagos (UNILAG). She has strong grades in Physics and Chemistry but weak in Further Mathematics. Her friend suggests she register PCM (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics) because "all universities accept it for Computer Science." Should Adaeze proceed with PCM, and what is the single most important verification step she must take before paying the JUPEB registration fee?

#### Solution:

- **Step 1 — Identify the target programme:** Computer Science at UNILAG JUPEB programme falls under the Faculty of Science / Faculty of Engineering (varies by session). Most UNILAG brochures classify Computer Science as a **quantitative science** that requires Mathematics and Physics at credit level.
- **Step 2 — Match the JUPEB combination:** PCM (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics) satisfies the *minimum* subject requirement because Mathematics and Physics are the two critical subjects, and Chemistry is an acceptable third science.
- **Step 3 — Check for Further Mathematics demand:** Some Computer Science and Engineering programmes at UNILAG and UNIBEN explicitly list Further Mathematics as a **preferred or required** fourth subject. Mathematics alone is usually accepted, but Further Mathematics strengthens the application.
- **Step 4 — Perform the verification:** Adaeze must open the official UNILAG JUPEB programme page and read the subject combination table line for Computer Science. The single most important action before payment is: **cross-check the JUPEB brochure of the receiving university, not just the national JUPEB portal, because each university has the final say on which combinations map to which degrees.**
- **Step 5 — Apply the minimum grade rule:** UNILAG typically sets a JUPEB cut-off around **5 points minimum with at least two A grades** across the three subjects.

**Answer:** Yes, Adaeze can proceed with PCM, but the critical verification step is to **read the receiving university's specific JUPEB subject combination table for Computer Science** before paying, because university-level rules override generic assumptions.

> ⚠️ **Examiner Trap:** Accepting a friend's casual claim that "PCM works everywhere." The trap is registration-level disqualification — a candidate may score A in all three subjects yet still be denied admission because the combination did not match the programme's stated requirements. Always verify against the receiving university's JUPEB brochure.

#### Edge Cases and Boundary Conditions

PCM is administratively simple, but its execution contains several traps that only surface when candidates interact with the actual JUPEB portal and individual university brochures.

| Scenario | What Looks Right | What Actually Happens | Correct Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Medicine aspirant chooses PCM | "I am good at Physics, Chemistry, Maths" | PCM is rejected; Medicine needs PCB | Switch combination to PCB before registration closes |
| Engineering candidate omits Further Mathematics | "Ordinary Mathematics is enough" | Some universities (e.g. UNIBEN Engineering) require Further Maths at credit level | Add Further Mathematics as a fourth JUPEB subject where allowed |
| Candidate picks PCB instead of PCM for Industrial Chemistry | "Biology is safer" | Some Industrial Chemistry programmes insist on Mathematics, not Biology | Verify programme-specific subject demand |
| Strong student scores only one A grade | "My PCM total is 6 points" | Many universities require **at least two A grades** | Aim for two A's and one B minimum |
| Candidate registers wrong combination code | "I can edit later on the portal" | JUPEB corrections after submission are rare and require formal appeals | Triple-check the subject codes before final submission |
| Candidate waits till final year to verify | "I have time" | JUPEB registration typically closes months before the exam | Verify by the second week of registration |

#### Connections to Adjacent Topics

PCM sits at the intersection of three JUPEB subject syllabuses: Physics (mechanics, electricity, waves, modern physics), Chemistry (physical, inorganic, organic), and Mathematics (pure and applied). Mastery of **trigonometry and calculus** in Mathematics feeds directly into Physics mechanics and Chemistry kinetics, making it the most internally coherent Science combination. Candidates considering **actuarial science or computer engineering** should also evaluate whether **Economics (PEM)** or **Further Mathematics** strengthens their application more than the third slot in PCM.

#### Common Registration-Time Mistakes

1. Treating PCM as a universal "science" ticket — it is not accepted for health-science faculties.
2. Copying a senior colleague's combination without checking the current session's brochure.
3. Ignoring the difference between **credit-level pass** (the minimum) and the actual competitive points needed.
4. Paying the JUPEB registration fee before the combination is fully verified.

#### Advanced Practice Prompts

1. **Scenario A:** A candidate scored 6 points (A A B) in PCB and wants to switch to Computer Science. Can she rewrite JUPEB with PCM in the next session, and how do carry-over rules affect her PCB result?
2. **Scenario B:** Compare the JUPEB PCM requirement for **Mechanical Engineering at UNIBEN** vs **Computer Science at UNILAG**. Which university is more likely to require Further Mathematics, and how should this shape a candidate's subject registration strategy?

> 💡 **High-Yield Revision Hook:** Use the rule of thumb **"P for Physical, C for Chemical, M for Mathematical"** — your target faculty must match all three letters. Engineering and Computing match all three; Medicine matches only P and C; pure Mathematics-heavy faculties lean on P and M.
