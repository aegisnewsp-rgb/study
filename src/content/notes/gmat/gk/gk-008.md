# Ghana Current Affairs and Recent Developments

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)

> Memory Hook for the test: think **"6-3-16-24"** — Independence **6 March 1957**, Republican status **1 July 1960** (often confused), **16 regions** since 2019, and the **2024** election cycle.

Ghana returned to democratic rule under the 1992 Fourth Republican Constitution and has alternated power between the NPP and NDC since 1993. The most recent presidential handover happened on **7 January 2025**, when John Dramani Mahama (NDC) was sworn in after winning the **2024 general election**, succeeding Nana Akufo-Addo (NPP).

| Fact | Specifics |
|---|---|
| Independence | 6 March 1957, under Kwame Nkrumah |
| Republican status | 1 July 1960 |
| Head of State + Government | The President (one person, both roles) |
| Regions | 16 (after the 2019 creation of 6 new regions) |
| Capital | Accra (also hosts the AfCFTA Secretariat) |
| Current President | John Dramani Mahama (since 7 January 2025) |

> 💡 **High-Yield Memory Hook:** "**NPP → NPP → NDC**" — Akufo-Addo won 2016 and 2020; Mahama won 2024. For GAT, just name the winner and the year.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)

#### Political Structure and Governance

Ghana runs a **unitary presidential republic** with three branches. The **President** is both head of state and head of government — unlike the British monarch, the Ghanaian president does not "reign but not rule." Parliament is unicameral, led by a **Speaker**. The **Chief Justice** heads the Judiciary. Since 2019 the country has **16 regions**, after six new ones were carved out of existing territories.

| Branch | Head | Where they sit |
|---|---|---|
| Executive | President (and Vice-President) | Jubilee House, Accra |
| Legislature | Speaker of Parliament | Parliament House, Accra |
| Judiciary | Chief Justice | Supreme Court, Accra |

#### Recent Elections and Democratic Continuity

The 2024 cycle returned the NDC to power through Mahama. The NPP retained some parliamentary seats despite losing the presidency — a familiar **split-ticket pattern** that students often miss. The 2020 cycle re-elected Akufo-Addo. Power has changed hands through the ballot box at every election since 2000, a record few African peers match.

#### Economy and the IMF Programme

In 2023 Ghana signed an **Extended Credit Facility (ECF)** with the IMF worth about **US$3 billion**. The ECF is a *concessional loan*, not a grant — it carries repayment terms. The country also ran the **Domestic Debt Exchange Programme (DDEP)** in 2022–2023, restructuring domestic bonds. Watch for these acronyms on the paper; "ECF" and "DDEP" appear together in past GAT items.

#### Flagship Domestic Programmes

- **Free SHS** — free senior high school tuition since 2017.
- **1D1F (One District One Factory)** — industrialisation initiative.
- **Planting for Food and Jobs** — agricultural input subsidy programme.
- **NHIS** — National Health Insurance Scheme, administered by the NHIA.
- **Ghana Beyond Aid** — policy vision to move Ghana off aid dependence, launched by President Akufo-Addo.

#### Foreign Relations and Continental Role

Ghana was the **first sub-Saharan African country to gain independence** from European colonial rule. It hosts the **AfCFTA Secretariat** in Accra since 2021, making Accra the administrative capital of Africa's continental free-trade area. The United States partners with Ghana through a **Millennium Challenge Corporation (MCC) compact** focused on energy.

> 📌 **Formula Check:** This topic has no numerical formulas — it tests recall of dates, names, and acronyms instead.

#### Worked Example: Identifying the Wrong Statement

A typical GAT item reads: *"Which of the following about Ghana's 2024 election is correct?"*

**Correct reasoning:** The NDC's John Mahama won the presidency; the NPP did not win the presidency in 2024 (they lost it, though they kept some seats). The swearing-in date was 7 January 2025. The IMF ECF was signed in 2023, not 2024. AfCFTA's secretariat sits in Accra, not Addis Ababa.

> ⚠️ **Examiner Trap:** Students pick "NPP won 2024" because they remember NPP from 2020. The trap tests whether you tracked the **most recent** handover, not the party you last studied.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)

#### Historical Anchors That Confuse Candidates

Three dates collide in students' minds:

| Year | Event | Easy-to-confuse trap |
|---|---|---|
| 1957 | Independence, 6 March | Mixing with Republic Day |
| 1960 | Republic declared, 1 July | Mixing with Independence Day |
| 1992 | Fourth Republican Constitution | Mixing with 1993 (first election under it) |

Ghana's **Fourth Republic** began in 1993, *not* 1992. The constitution was approved in 1992; the democratic government it created took office in January 1993.

#### Edge Cases in Government Structure

1. **Head of State vs Head of Government** — in some textbooks these are listed separately even for presidential systems. For Ghana, treat them as the same person.
2. **Speaker of Parliament** — second in the presidential line of succession after the Vice-President, a fact GAT has used as a true/false distractor.
3. **NIA and the Ghana Card** — the National Identification Authority issues the Ghana Card, a foundational identity document linked to banking and SIM registration. Tests sometimes ask which body issues it.

#### Economic Traps Beyond the Headlines

- **Inflation** is measured through the **Consumer Price Index (CPI)**, published monthly by the Ghana Statistical Service. GAT rarely asks for a specific rate — it asks which *index* measures inflation.
- **Debt-to-GDP** was a recurring theme during the DDEP. The mechanism, not the exact ratio, is what GAT tests.
- **"Ghana Beyond Aid"** is a long-term *vision*, not a funded programme with a budget line. Examiners describe it as "completed" to trap you.

#### Foreign-Policy Distinctions

| Body | Headquarters | Ghana's role |
|---|---|---|
| AfCFTA Secretariat | Accra, Ghana | Host nation |
| African Union (AU) | Addis Ababa, Ethiopia | Member state |
| ECOWAS | Abuja, Nigeria | Member state |
| United Nations | New York, USA | Member state |

> ⚠️ **Examiner Trap:** "AfCFTA is a UN agency" is the most common wrong option in this section. AfCFTA is an African Union treaty, administered from Accra but legally separate from the UN system.

#### Advanced Practice Prompts

1. List five facts a candidate must know about the **2024 general election** (date, winner, party, sworn-in date, predecessor).
2. Explain why the **ECF** is not equivalent to a grant, and identify one domestic policy programme designed to reduce aid dependence.
