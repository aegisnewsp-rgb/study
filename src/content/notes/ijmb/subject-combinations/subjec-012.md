# IJMB Registration, Examination Process and University Admission Strategy

### 🟢 Lite — Quick Review (1h–1d)
> Rapid summary for last-minute revision before your exam.

IJMB (Interim Joint Matriculation Board) is a one-year advanced-level programme approved by the **National Universities Commission (NUC)**. It gives successful candidates direct entry into **200-level** of Nigerian universities through the **JAMB Direct Entry (DE)** route.

- **Eligibility:** 5 O'Level credit passes (WAEC/NECO/NABTEB) including **English Language and Mathematics**, in not more than 2 sittings.
- **Subject rule:** Pick **3 IJMB subjects** that match your target faculty (e.g., Physics + Chemistry + Biology for Medicine).
- **Grade scale:** A = 9 points, B = 8, C = 6, D = 4, E = 2 (used for score aggregation).
- **DE formula used by most universities:** Aggregate = (IJMB × 0.60) + (JAMB DE × 0.40).
- **Trap:** IJMB alone does not admit you — you must still buy the **JAMB DE form** or no university can offer admission.

### 🟡 Standard — Regular Study (2d–2mo)
> Standard content for students with a few days to months.

#### Registration Workflow

Registration runs on the official IJMB portal (ijmb.com.ng). You purchase a scratch card/PIN, complete bio-data, enter O'Level details, select your three IJMB subjects, upload a passport photograph, print the registration slip, and report to an **affiliated study centre**. Only centres accredited by the IJMB office can train and present candidates for the examination; unaccredited centres issue results that NUC will not recognise.

#### Eligibility and O'Level Requirement

Every candidate must hold five O'Level credits including English Language and Mathematics in no more than two sittings. The IJMB programme starts only after this requirement is met. Candidates with pending or missing credits at registration are not cleared to sit the final papers.

#### Subject Combination Logic

Your three IJMB subjects are **principal subjects** and must align with the degree you are targeting. Wrong combinations make you ineligible for DE regardless of score.

| Target Course | IJMB Principal Subjects |
| --- | --- |
| Medicine / Pharmacy / Nursing | Physics, Chemistry, Biology |
| Engineering | Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics |
| Law / Political Science | Economics, Government, History |
| Accounting / Business | Mathematics, Economics, Accounting or Government |

#### Examination Process

Written papers are held at designated IJMB examination centres, typically in March/April after the academic session. Use the approved IJMB syllabus for each subject — it differs from WAEC and JAMB UTME syllabuses.

#### Linking IJMB to University Admission

After IJMB releases results, you must still purchase the **JAMB DE form** at jamb.gov.ng, select IJMB as your A'Level qualification, and list first/second/third choice universities. Universities then compute your aggregate using IJMB scores and JAMB DE score, then upload your name on CAPS if you cross their DE cut-off.

### 🔴 Extended — Deep Study (3mo+)
> Comprehensive coverage for students on a longer study timeline.

#### Grade Points and Aggregation

Each IJMB subject carries a maximum of 9 points. A three-subject candidate can earn up to 27 points, rescaled to a percentage for the aggregation formula.

| Letter Grade | Points |
| --- | --- |
| A | 9 |
| B | 8 |
| C | 6 |
| D | 4 |
| E | 2 |
| F / Absent | 0 |

**Worked example:** A candidate scores A (9) in Physics, B (8) in Chemistry, C (6) in Biology. Total = 23 of 27.
IJMB Score = (23 ÷ 27) × 100 = **85.19%**.
Aggregate with JAMB DE = 60% say: (85.19 × 0.60) + (65 × 0.40) = **77.11%**.

#### University Admission Strategy

1. Target **A grades in all three principal subjects** — competitive courses (Medicine, Law, Engineering) rarely admit below B/B/C.
2. Confirm the university's **DE cut-off and faculty-specific subject demands** on its admissions portal before registration.
3. Sit JAMB DE promptly once IJMB results are released; late DE registration pushes admission into the next cycle.
4. Request your **transcript of results** early from the affiliated IJMB centre, as universities demand it during screening.
5. Apply for institutions where IJMB is accepted — federal, state, and most private universities, but always verify on the institution's DE page.

#### Common Mistakes and How to Avoid Them

- **Wrong subject combinations** (e.g., Economics for Engineering) — re-check the JAMB brochure and your target faculty before paying.
- **Skipping JAMB DE** — IJMB results without a DE form cannot be processed by any Nigerian university.
- **Unaccredited centres** — confirm accreditation directly with the IJMB office; agents offering "fast IJMB" results are fraudulent.
- **Re-sit limits** — IJMB permits only a limited number of re-sit attempts; treat each session as binding.
- **O'Level deficiencies at screening** — five credits including Maths and English must be complete before final clearance.

#### Practice Prompts

1. A candidate has 5 O'Level credits including English and Maths, scores A, B, C in IJMB Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and 65% on JAMB DE. Compute the aggregate used by most universities.
2. Explain why a candidate with IJMB A's in Government, History, and CRS cannot use IJMB to gain DE admission into Mechanical Engineering.
